About

I started in biotechnology, where my dissertation turned out to be mostly about data analysis. That pulled me into analytics, and I have spent the five years since turning messy data into dashboards, forecasts and models that teams use to make decisions.

Today I'm at Capital One, in the Refinance line of business, turning large data ecosystems into decisions that help customers. Before that I was a data analyst at Temple University and a product analytics consultant for Unilever, helping global stakeholders see where their money was going and how to make more of it.

I work across machine learning, statistical modeling and presenting findings to leadership, from sizing untapped markets for a Colgate-Palmolive campaign to cost-optimized fraud models. Outside work I build end-to-end ML projects with real data, honest evaluation and an app you can try, several of them from Kaggle competitions.
