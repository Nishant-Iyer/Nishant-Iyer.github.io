About

I'm a simple, nerdy guy who accidentally discovered that data is the world's most interesting puzzle. My background is a bit of a remix: I started in biotechnology, wrote a dissertation that was 10% biology and 90% data obsession, and never looked back.

Today I'm at Capital One, in the Refinance line of business, turning large data ecosystems into decisions that help customers. Before that I was a data analyst at Temple University and a product analytics consultant for Unilever, helping global stakeholders see where their money was going and how to make more of it.

I work across machine learning, statistical modeling and executive storytelling. Whether it's sizing untapped markets for Colgate-Palmolive or building AI tools on the side, I'm happiest between "how does this work?" and "how can we make it work better?"
