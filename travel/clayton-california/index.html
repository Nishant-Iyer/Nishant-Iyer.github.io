Eat

Eat Ed's Mudville Grill

A sports bar located off of Clayton Road in downtown Clayton. The spaghetti is good, the Hefeweizen (in mugs) is generous, but the true star of the establishment is the mini-basketball game ($0.50 for one game, with a free game given for scores over 30) hidden in the back near the arcade games.

📍 6200 Center St☎ +1 925 673-0333
MapsWebsite

Eat Skipolini's Pizza

A great, local pizza place located in downtown Clayton that regularly wins Bay Area awards for its pizza. There is a bocce court for adults, a play structure for children and an arcade for teens.

📍 1033 Diablo St☎ +1 925 672-1111⚠ Last checked Apr 2016 — confirm before you go

Go next

  • Concord - One of the largest cities in the East Bay, offering a variety of shopping and dining options.
  • Mount Diablo State Park - This 20,000 acre (80 km2) park borders Clayton to the south and provides great hiking opportunities on a mountain that dominates the landscape for most of central and eastern Contra Costa County. It offers an incredible viewshed, with views on clear days stretching 181 miles to Mount Lassen.
