Overview

Lake Village is the county seat of Chicot County in the Delta region of Arkansas.

2 sights · 1 things to do · 1 places to stay

Best things about Lake Village

World War II Japanese American Internment Museum

This commemorates the Japanese detention camp that existed here during WWII. Even Japanese-Americans were displaced here.

Lakeport Plantation

A Greek Revival plantation home from 1859 that is the only such remaining property along the Mississippi in Arkansas. It has a rather intriguing presence when surrounded by fields of cotton.

Lake Chicot State Park

This place offers boating, fishing, and bird-watching opportunities on the oxbow lake known as Lake Chicot.

    Understand

    Lake Village is a bit of a sad dying delta town with its empty stores, but it has some interesting attractions nonetheless. Just like Greenville, MS on the other side of the Mississippi River, Lake Village is on an oxbow lake.
