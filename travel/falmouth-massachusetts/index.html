Overview

Falmouth is a town in Massachusetts on Cape Cod's southwest corner. It includes the villages of Woods Hole, West Falmouth, North Falmouth, Hatchville, Silver Beach, East Falmouth, Teaticket, and Waquoit. It is the Cape's second most populous town.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Sep. Summer highs reach about 80°F; winter lows dip to about 24°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Mar and Oct. (climate data from nearby Newport)

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 1,070 miles, roughly 18 h 30 min without stops. Flying is usually faster.
  • HYA Cape Cod Gateway Airport, Hyannis — 19 mi away. Directions
  • EWB New Bedford Regional Airport, New Bedford — 20 mi away. Directions
  • PVD Rhode Island T. F. Green International Airport, Providence/Warwick — 44 mi away (major hub). Directions

Falmouth is best accessed via Route 25 to the Bourne Bridge or Route 3 to the Sagamore Bridge. If coming from the Sagamore Bridge, take Sandwich Road to the traffic circle and take Route 28 south. If coming from the Bourne Bridge, the traffic circle will be on the Cape Cod side of the bridge. Traffic will be heavy at peak travel times during the summer. Falmouth is about a 70-mi drive (112 km) from Boston and will take at least 1 hr 15 min.
