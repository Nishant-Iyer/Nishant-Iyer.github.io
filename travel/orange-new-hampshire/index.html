Overview

Orange is a small town located in New Hampshire that was granted in 1769 and incorporated in 1790. The census of 2000 has the population at 299. The Mt. Cardigan State Park is located inside Orange.

Do

  • Hiking - There are several trails and one to the top of Mt. Cardigan. It is a 3,121 ft treeless granite summit. You can see a panorama views of west central N.H., including Mt. Monadnock and the White Mountains, Camel's Hump near Montpelier, Vermont, and Pleasant Mountain in Maine.
  • Picnicking - The Park also includes picnic tables and a great spot for picnicking.
