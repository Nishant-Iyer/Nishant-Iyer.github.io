Overview

Port Huron is a city in Southeast Michigan, the second-busiest border crossing (with Sarnia, Ontario) in the state.

5 sights · 5 things to do · 2 places to eat · 3 bars & cafés · 1 places to stay

Best things about Port Huron

St. Clair River

a busy waterway. You can watch freighters, sailboats, fishing boats, and fun vessels.

Port Huron Museum

Fort Gratiot Lighthouse

Port Huron to Mackinac sailboat race

one of the longest fresh-water races in the world with over two hundred boats entering the race each year.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Oct. Summer highs reach about 84°F; winter lows dip to about 19°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: May and Jul. (climate data from nearby Detroit)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 340 miles, roughly 6 h without stops.
  • FNT Bishop International Airport, Flint — 66 mi away. Directions
  • DTW Detroit Metropolitan Wayne County Airport, Detroit — 71 mi away (major hub). Directions
