Overview

Central Tennessee, more often called Middle Tennessee, is a region of Tennessee. Besides the obvious destinations like Nashville, there are some lesser known ones, like Carthage, for instance, the hometown of former Vice-President Al Gore.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Mar–May, Sep–Oct. Summer highs reach about 91°F; winter lows dip to about 30°F. Very hot (Jul, Aug) — plan outdoor time for mornings. Wettest: May and Apr. (climate data from nearby Nashville)

Other destinations

  • The Natchez Trace Parkway crosses through Middle Tennessee. It begins in Nashville and continues south through Wayne County where it enters Alabama. The road continues all the way to Natchez, Mississippi where it ends. The stretch through Middle Tennessee is often regarded as the most beautiful portion of the road. There are numerous overlooks, hiking trails, and waterfalls in the beautiful scenery of the rolling, Tennessee hills.
  • Lake Barkley
  • Land Between the Lakes

See

  • Tennessee Wineries. Visit at least one of the more than 20 wineries in Tennessee. Here in Middle Tennessee are these: Beachaven Vineyards & Winery (Clarksville), Sumner Crest Winery (Portland), Red Barn Winery & Vineyards (Lafayette), Beans Creek Winery (Manchester), Keg Springs Winery (Hampshire), Holly Ridge Winery and Vineyard (Livingston), Highland Manor Winery (Jamestown) and Stonehaus Winery, Inc. (Crossville)
  • Meriwether Lewis National Monument or Grinder's Stand, east of Hohenwald along the Natchez Trace, grave and reconstructed inn where it is believed Meriwether Lewis took his life.
  • State Historic Rock House, in Sparta, is a historic way station along a wagon road between Nashville and Knoxville circa the 1830s where Andrew Jackson once stopped, and near the town of Sparta that nearly became Tennessee's state capital.
  • Cumberland Caverns, east of Manchester, is the 2nd largest cave in Tennessee, with a chandelier from the Lowes Metropolitan Theater in New York City.
