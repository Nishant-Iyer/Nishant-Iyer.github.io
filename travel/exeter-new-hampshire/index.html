See American Independence Museum

The Ladd-Gillman House, built in 1721, is now a history museum focusing on New Hampshire's role in the American Revolution. One of the first copies of the Constitution, a Dunlap broadside, was found in the attic in 1985. The Folsom Tavern, built by Colonel Samuel Folsom in 1775, has been moved to museum grounds and restored.

📍 1 Governor's Ln☎ +1 603-772-2622
