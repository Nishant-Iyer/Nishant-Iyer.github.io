Overview

Mount Eaton is a village in Wayne County in Northeast Ohio. The population was 246 at the 2000 census. The village has a total area of 0.2 mi2, all land.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Apr–Oct. Summer highs reach about 84°F; winter lows dip to about 20°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Jun and May. (climate data from nearby Akron)

    Get in

    • Route 94 - southern portion terminates in town connecting the town to destinations north such as Dalton, Wadsworth, North Royalton, Parma, and Cleveland.
    • Route 250 - runs northwest to southeast through town. To the west it connects with Wooster and Ashland, and to the east with Wilmont, Dover, and New Philadelphia.
