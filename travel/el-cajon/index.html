Overview

El Cajon is a city in San Diego County in Southern California. The city is nicknamed "The Big Box".

Best things about El Cajon

San Diego Air & Space Museum Gillespie Field Annex

Aircraft museum and displays.

Water Conservation Garden

Beautifully curated, features informative displays for children and adults

Mother Goose Parade

El Cajon's annual Mother Goose Parade is the largest parade in San Diego County and the largest of its type west of the Mississippi.

The Magnolia

Concert venue.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Jan–Dec. Summer highs reach about 89°F; winter lows dip to about 42°F. Wettest: Feb and Jan.
