Overview

Jeffersonville, often called "Jeff" by locals, is the county seat of Clark County in Southern Indiana, on the opposite side of the Ohio River from Louisville, Kentucky and connected to Louisville by the Kennedy, Lincoln, and Lewis and Clark Bridges. It has a population of 48,000 (2019).

Best things about Jeffersonville

Howard Steamboat Museum

A 22-room, late Victorian mansion features stained and leaded glass windows, intricate hand carved and original furnishings. Artifacts &amp; models of famous boats built in the Howard Shipyards are exhibited.

Charlestown State Park

Hiking trails and camping at the old army ammunition plant.

Vintage Fire Museum

Big Four Station

The area around the Big Four Bridge was transformed into one of Jeffersonville Premier parks that is a destination for many people from all over the community and host to special events.

Bob Hedge Park

A small community park at the site of a former elementary school. Mainly of interest to parents of children with disabilities because of "Lydia's World", a playground featuring disability-friendly equipment.

Covered Bridge Golf Club

18-hole course designed by Fuzzy Zoelle with a driving range. Annually hosts Wolf Challenge featuring pro and celebrity golfers.

Get in

By car

Interstate 65, which crosses the river on the John F. Kennedy and Abraham Lincoln Bridges (respectively carrying southbound and northbound traffic), connects Jeffersonville with Louisville and other points between Chicago and Mobile, AL. Interstate 265, with the 2016 opening of the Lewis and Clark Bridge to Kentucky, offers a bypass around the northern end of the city. The 65 and 265 crossings are tolled, with all-electronic tolling (no toll booths). The lowest toll rates are available only to drivers with transponders issued either by the local tolling authority or the multi-state E-ZPass consortium.

By plane

Small aircraft can fly into Clark County Airport, 5 miles north in Sellersburg. Passenger jet service is available at Louisville International Airport, 8 miles south off of I-65 and I-264.

Amtrak discontinued service to Jeffersonville and Louisville in 2003.

Get around

The main road through Jeffersonville is 10th Street (formally IN 62). Riverside Drive and Utica Pike carry traffic along the Ohio River. Hamburg Pike runs north-south along the western side of the city. Veterans Parkway connects the north side of Jeffersonville with the retail district of Clarksville. Holmans Lane connects Veterans Parkway with 10th Street on the east side, where at that point Allison Lane connects 10th with Utica Pike.
