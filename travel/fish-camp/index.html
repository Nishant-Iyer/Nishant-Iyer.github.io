Get in

By car

The only access to Fish Camp is via SR41.

By bus

Go Yosemite Area Regional Transit System (YARTS)

The YARTS shuttle travels on Highway 41 from Fresno to Wawona and Yosemite Valley, with a stop in Fish Camp at the Tenaya Lodge. Ticket prices include park entry, and can be purchased from the shuttle driver.

🕒 Shuttle operates from late-May through late-September
