Best time to visit & weather

    Get in

    By car

    • U.S. Route 30, Nevada, Ohio can be accessed by U.S. Route 30, about a half mile to the south of Nevada which provides connection to Fort Wayne, Lima, and Upper Sandusky to the west, Bucyrus, Mansfield, and Canton to the east.
    • Ohio State Route 231, goes through Nevada, Ohio. It's the main route to Nevada from U.S. Route 30 which is located to the south of town.

    Get around

    There is no public transportation in Nevada, Ohio. It is located in a rural area, driving is easy and pleasurable.
