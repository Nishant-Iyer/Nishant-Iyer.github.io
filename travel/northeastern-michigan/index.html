Overview

Northeastern Michigan is the part of Michigan that stretches along the shore of Lake Huron from the Straits of Mackinac to Saginaw Bay, and the extensive forested areas inland.

Plan your trip

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Sep. Summer highs reach about 81°F; winter lows dip to about 16°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Oct and Sep. (climate data from nearby Traverse City)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

Cities

  • Thunder Bay National Marine Sanctuary and Underwater Preserve — a 4,300-sq-mi (11,000 km²) area that protects an estimated 116 historically significant shipwrecks
