Best things about Belton

Belton Depot

An old passenger train depot that lost service in the 1960s. Now home to the Ruth Drake Museum and the South Carolina Tennis Hall of Fame.

Belton Center for the Arts

Small art gallery with rotating exhibits.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Mar–Jun, Sep–Nov. Summer highs reach about 90°F; winter lows dip to about 32°F. Very hot (Jul) — plan outdoor time for mornings. Wettest: Jul and Aug. (climate data from nearby Greenville)
