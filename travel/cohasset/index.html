Best things about Cohasset

Cohasset Maritime Museum

Seasonal. It was built about 1750 as a ship chandlery. This museum exhibits Cohasset's maritime history and artifacts.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Sep. Summer highs reach about 82°F; winter lows dip to about 18°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Mar and Oct. (climate data from nearby Quincy)

  • BED Laurence G Hanscom Field, Bedford — 29 mi away. Directions

Cohasset is on Route 3A. From Boston follow Interstate 93 South to Exit 7 (Route 3 South). Follow Route 3 south to Exit 14, Route 228, (Hull, Nantasket, Rockland), at the bottom of the ramp go left. Follow Rt. 228 North about 7 miles then go right onto 3A.
