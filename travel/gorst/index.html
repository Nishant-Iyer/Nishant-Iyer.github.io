Overview

Gorst is on the Kitsap Peninsula of Washington state, at the end of Sinclair Inlet in Puget Sound.

Best things about Gorst

Elandan Gardens

Waterfront gardens with world-class bonsai collection and landscape artistry along with the nursery, gift shop and gallery filled with antiques and treasures from Asia, Europe and North America.

Sinclair Inlet Wildlife Viewing Area

Viewing platform. Shoreline access by steep path, not wheelchair accessible. Left very wild. Winter nesting grounds for several species of sea birds.

Jarstad Park

Reservations required, but can walk in if arriving on foot. Easy access to Gorst Creek and the Suquamish Fish Hatchery. Horseshoes. Picnic tables, shelter, benches. Volleyball/Badminton.

Rodeo Drive-In Theatre

A well-loved and awesome classic drive-in movie theater.

Understand

The hamlet of Gorst was the birthplace of Gorst Air Transport, a tiny carrier that was the basis upon which United Air Transport was built. Aviation magnate William Boeing turned the small air service into the legendary company that now bears the United Air Lines name.

Get in

Gorst is a Kitsap crossroads:

  • State Route 3 (WA-3) leads north to Bremerton and beyond, and south to Belfair and Shelton.
  • State Route 16 (WA-16) leads to Port Orchard, Gig Harbor and across the Narrows Bridge to Tacoma.

Get around

Gorst is more like a business district strip mall kind of area. If you need a tire fixed or are shopping for a car this is the place.
