Overview

Clearlake is a city in Lake County inland in the North Coast region of California. It is located at the southern end of Clear Lake, which is more than 1.5 million years old.

    Do Anderson Marsh State Historic Park

    Historical information about California's native Pomo people and ranching. Good for hiking and bird watching, especially waders and other birds that like the wetlands. Stroll along the easy Cache Creek Nature Trail. Boating is easiest during summer and fall, when the water levels are lower; even then, less-experienced boaters should stay far away from the dam. (Kayaks and canoes can be rented from Clear Lake Campground on the other side of Cache Creek.) Picnic tables and one faucet with drinking water. Wheelchair-accessible portable toilet, if the path isn't too muddy. This wildlife preserve is not a dog-friendly location.

    📍 8400 CA-53, Lower LakeBetween Lower Lake and Clear Lake on Highway 53, north of Calistoga🕒 Sunrise to sunset☎ +1 707 279-2267⚠ Last checked Jan 2021 — confirm before you go
