Overview

Granger is a town on the Columbia River Plateau of Washington State, in the Yakima Valley. The town is decorated with statues of dinosaurs.

Best things about Granger

Hisey Park

The densest cluster of Granger's resident dinosaurs. The public bathrooms are housed in a building shaped like a volcano, and everything in the playground is shaped like dinosaurs.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Jun–Sep. Summer highs reach about 76°F; winter lows dip to about 25°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Nov and Dec. (climate data from nearby Mount Rainier National Park)
