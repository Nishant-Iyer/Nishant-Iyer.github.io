Overview

Seymour is a city in Southern Indiana. It is the "small town" home of rocker John Mellencamp.

3 sights · 1 things to do · 3 places to eat · 1 bars & cafés · 4 places to stay

Best things about Seymour

Southern Indiana Center for the Arts

Housed in a 1851 brick mansion, owned by John Mellencamp, it has two main galleries and Mellencamp prints.

Freeman Army Airfield Museum

Muscatatuck National Wildlife Refuge

Wetland marsh and woodlands that includes 8 miles of hiking trails, two pioneer cemeteries, and a log cabin. Good birding.

Seymour Oktoberfest

Annual festival held downtown in early October, celebrating the city's German history with a carnival, food and crafts booths, and a parade. Opening dates for the year can be found on the festival's website.
