Overview

Marysville is a city in the North Sound region of Washington State, on the north side of the Snohomish River delta. Marysville is across I-5 from Quil Ceda Village, part of the Tulalip Indian Reservation.

Get in

  • Interstate 5 has five exits from I-5 into town, from exits #199 ("Marysville") through #208 ("SR-530 Arlington/Darrington"). Downtown is accessed through exit 199.
  • State Route (SR) 529 serves as the old Hwy 99 through Marysville as State St. It directly connects into Broadway in Everett and is accessed from I-5 SB at exit 198 going into Everett.
  • State Route (SR) 9 parallels I-5 to form the east boundary of Marysville.
