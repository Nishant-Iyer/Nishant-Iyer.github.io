Overview

Hardin is a town in southeast Montana, just north of the Crow Indian Reservation. About 3,800 people lived there in 2020.

1 sights · 2 things to do · 1 places to eat · 3 places to stay

Best things about Hardin

Big Horn County Historical Museum

Focuses on local history. 35 acres and has 24 historic structures, though the structures are closed from October 1 to May 1.

Arapooish Recreation Park

Access to both the Bighorn River and Koyama's Pond. Day use site with concrete boat launch, picnic area, walking trail.

Little Bighorn Days

Parades, Street Dance, PRCA Rodeo, and other activities.

    Get in

    Interstate 90 (I-90) passes the northern part of town.

    Montana Highway 47 (MT-47) connects Hardin to Interstate 94, making a cut across for those travelling from the east.

    The nearest commercial airport is Billings Logan International Airport (BIL).
