Overview

Potsdam (pop 9,000) is a village in the town of Potsdam in northern New York State.

4 sights · 1 things to do · 3 places to eat · 1 bars & cafés · 2 places to stay

Best things about Potsdam

Clarkson University

Private university founded 1896, 3500 undergraduate and postgraduate students on two campuses; arts, science, business and engineering.

SUNY Potsdam

Founded 1816 as the St. Lawrence Academy, which became the Potsdam Normal School to train teachers of music, mathematics and the arts. 4500 students, two libraries and an art gallery.

Orchestra of Northern NY

Live performances at SUNY Potsdam's Hosmer Concert Hall and around the region (Watertown, Massena).

Potsdam Public Museum

Local and civil war history, archives.

Potsdam Town and Country Club

Golf course with restaurant (+1 315 265-1234), pro shop.

Understand

Founded in 1806 and named after the city of Potsdam in what is now Germany. The town, in turn, lent its name to the orange Potsdam sandstone found in Central St. Lawrence County.

Get in

By car

Take US 11 north from Watertown or NY 56 south from Massena.

By bus

Go Trailways Bus Line

Intercity bus from Massena, Potsdam, Canton, Gouverneur, Evans Mills, Watertown and Pulaski to Syracuse. Air and rail connection at Syracuse. Onward bus connection from Syracuse to NYC.

📍 Market Square Mall, 22 Depot St.🕒 Mon-Fri 5:45AM-12:50PM, Sat 5:45-8:30AM & noon-12:50PM, closed Sun/holidays
Google MapsWebsite

By train

There is no Amtrak service in the region. The closest passenger rail is in Brockville (VIA) or Utica-Rome (Amtrak). The closest passenger rail to connect directly to the bus to Potsdam is in Syracuse.

By plane

There is a tiny one-runway Potsdam municipal airport which houses thirteen single-engine planes for general aviation (PTD), but the closest scheduled Essential Air Service is in Massena (MSS) or Ogdensburg (OGS), each with two daily Cape Air flights from Albany. Massena and Ogdensburg are reachable from Canton-Potsdam by county-level commuter bus:

Go St. Lawrence NYSARC

Weekday commuter bus service to Ogdensburg, Massena (three runs daily), Canton and Morristown.
