Best things about Lake Norden

South Dakota Amateur Baseball Hall of Fame

The museum features permanent exhibits on the history and development of amateur baseball in South Dakota, including memorabilia from about a dozen South Dakotans who have played Major League Baseball.

Donald Christman Toy Museum

The museum has a large display of antique dishes and toys.
