Overview

Coarsegold is a city in Madera County in the Sierra Nevada region of California.

2 things to do · 2 places to eat · 1 places to stay

Best things about Coarsegold

Chukchansi Gold Resort & Casino

A resort casino run by the Chukchansi Native American nation.

Tarantula Festival

Coarsegold is full of tarantulas once a year during their mating season, and this festival celebrates the spiders with tarantula races, displays, costumes, and food.

Get in

By car

By car, the best access to Coarsegold is taking California State Route 41 north from Fresno.

By bus

Place Yosemite Area Regional Transit System (YARTS)

The YARTS shuttle travels on Highway 41 between Fresno and Wawona and Yosemite Valley, with stops in Coarsegold at Coarsegold Market and Chukchansi Gold Resort & Casino. Ticket prices include park entry, and can be purchased from the shuttle driver.

🕒 May 14 to September 14, seven days a week
