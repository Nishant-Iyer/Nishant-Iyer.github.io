    Shop Arboretum of South Barrington

    The Arboretum of South Barrington is an open-air shopping center hosting over 60 stores like DSW, Jos. A. Bank, and Victoria's Secret, and the iPic Theater, that offers light dining and drinks in the theater. The Arboretum is home to many different tree and plant species.

    Shop America's Antique Mall

    If you're on the hunt for an eye-catching vintage piece or a gift, this is one of the largest antiques stores around. Hundreds of vendor booths in one enormous room. Wear comfortable shoes, and consider bringing a water bottle or a snack. Restrooms and seating area in the back.
