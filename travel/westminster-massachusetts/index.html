Best things about Westminster

The original Westminster Cracker Factory

The Westminster Cracker Company was established 1828 in Westminster. Westminster Cracker oyster crackers are a New England staple, and the original factory that adorns every package can be seen here.

Westminster Cracker Festival

The town of Westminster celebrates it's cracker making history with this annual festival that takes place in October.
