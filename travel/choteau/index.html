Overview

Choteau is a town in Central Montana. It is the county seat of Teton County. About 1,700 people live there.

4 sights · 2 things to do · 3 places to eat · 2 bars & cafés · 3 places to stay

Best things about Choteau

Montana Dinosaur Center

Part of the Montana Dinosaur Trail. Has model of the longest dinosaur, a diplodocus, and the first baby dinosaur bones collected in North America.

Teton Pass Ski Area

400 skiable acres with 3 lifts.

Freezout Lake

From mid-March to mid-October hundreds of thousands of snow geese and tens of thousands of trumpeter and tundra swans visit the lake. There is a tracker for fall and spring migration

Old Trail Museum

Museum with a bit of everything. Fossils, Blackfeet and Métis histories, history of the region.

Pine Butte Swamp Preserve

Abundant variety of wildlife - grizzlies, beaver, elk, bobcat, and bighorn sheep to name some. Only part of the preserve is open to the public.

Teton Disc Golf

18 Holes. Course info on south side of Pavilion.

    Get in

    Choteau is at the intersection of U.S. Highway 89 (US-89) and U.S. Highway 287 (US-287).

    The nearest commercial airport is Great Falls International Airport (GTF).

    Get around

    You will need a personal vehicle, or bike, to get around Choteau.

    When adventuring outside of town, be sure you plan for fuel and download maps. Wide open country is great to explore, just don't expect to always have service and amenities.
