    Get in

    By car

    Walpole is located off of major highways US Interstate 95 and Route 1

    By plane

    • T.F. Green Airport in Warwick (Rhode Island) and Logan International Airport in Boston.

    By train

    Walpole is accessible via the , which provides service from South Station in Boston.

    Go Walpole MBTA station

    Maps

    By bus

    The MBTA's 34E bus runs all the way to the train station. Can be an alternative, although slower, to the commuter rail. Service operates seven days a week.

    Get around

    Everything is pretty much walking distance from the train station. The 34E bus makes many stops heading north of the train station into Norwood, bus service is every 25-45 minutes weekdays and 50-60 minutes weekends.
