    Do

    Do Children's Museum of Southeastern Connecticut

    Children's museum in the village of Niantic. Contains plenty to keep kids occupied. Inside there is a model ship, a TV weather forecast station, and a large play kitchen. There is also a small, authentic pipe organ which demonstrates the principles of how a pipe organ operates. A complete science section with hands-on exhibits for kids to explore. There is also an outdoor play area complete with a small wall climbing area and zip line.

    📍 409 Main Street, Niantic, CT🕒 See website, hours vary by season💲 $7.00 from ages 1 and up☎ +1-860-691-1111⚠ Last checked Aug 2017 — confirm before you go

    Do Niantic Bay Boardwalk

    A 1.1 mile concrete "boardwalk" along the coast of Long Island Sound. It was originally a true boardwalk which was destroyed by Hurricane Sandy, but the town and Amtrak funded the restoration of the walk. Excellent views of Long Island Sound.

    🕒 Sunrise to Sunset⚠ Last checked Aug 2017 — confirm before you go

    Located in the village of Niantic, this massive used book store (with over half a million volumes) is spread over three locations, each housing a specialized collection. The grounds are quirky, the staff is knowledgeable. Books are bought and sold.

    Eat The Black Sheep

    BurgersAmericanDessert / Ice cream

    An upscale Irish bar and restaurant, from the hardwood floors to the original tin ceiling the bar is sleek, stylish and welcoming. They serve 15 types of draft beer, which you can enjoy sitting at the dark mahogany bar, in a cozy nook, or by the fireplace when the weather turns cold. In the warmer weather, there is an outdoor patio directly on Main Street serving food and drinks daily. The menu consists of a fine selection of both Irish and American cuisine. Favorites include: Traditional Bangers & Mash, Angus Burger, Steamed Mussels and Pan Seared Stonington Sea Scallops. All of the desserts are made in-house.

    Sleep

    Stay Inn at Harbor Hill Marina

    Beautiful bed and breakfast in the marina district of the quaint Connecticut seaside village of Niantic. The property features two buildings: the original Main Inn and the newly opened (as of 2014) Captain Clark House. Guests can enjoy magnificent water and nautical views from any one of the 16 guestrooms, each with its own unique decor and special amenities, some with queen or king beds, soaking tubs, fireplaces and private balconies.
