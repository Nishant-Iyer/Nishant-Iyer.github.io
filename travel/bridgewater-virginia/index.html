Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Jun–Sep. Summer highs reach about 70°F; winter lows dip to about 14°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Mar, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Dec and Jan. (climate data from nearby Snowshoe)

    Sleep

    Stay Crimson Inn

    Crimson Inn offers 24 suites in four different configurations. Each includes a compact kitchen with full size refrigerator, stove top, microwave, coffee maker, granite countertops throughout, and ceramic tile flooring. The living/sleeping areas offer a flat panel television with cable. The Crimson is located in historic Bridgewater, in the heart of Shenandoah Valley.
