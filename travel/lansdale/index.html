Overview

Lansdale is a town of 16,000 people in Montgomery County, Pennsylvania.

This article also covers the nearby communities of North Wales, Souderton, Kulpsville, Harleysville, Doylestown, and Montgomeryville.

6 sights · 8 things to do · 12 places to eat · 3 bars & cafés · 6 places to stay

Best things about Lansdale

Kugel Ball

An interesting sculpture next to the railroad station.

Parkside Place Park

Morgan Log House

Built by Welsh Quaker Edward Morgan, Daniel Boone's grandfather. Features dressed logs with decorative chinking and 18th century furnishings. On the National Register of Historic Places. Hours change seasonally.

Lansdale Public Library

Public library.

Mercer Museum

Museum in Doylestown (about 25 minutes away from Lansdale)

Mennonite Heritage Center

Enjoy exhibits and programs that tell the story of Mennonite faith and life in southeastern Pennsylvania. Research genealogy and local history in the historical library.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Apr–Oct. Summer highs reach about 86°F; winter lows dip to about 23°F. Cold months (Jan) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Jul and Sep. (climate data from nearby Warminster)
