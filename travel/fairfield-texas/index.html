    See Freestone County Historical Museum

    Consists of a gallery building built in 2007 plus four historic buildings, including the old county jail built in 1857 that is original to the site, and two log cabins built in 1845 and 1852 that were moved to the site from elsewhere. The museum contains local artifacts from around the county, along with temporary exhibitions and genealogical records.
