Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Jun–Jul. Summer highs reach about 62°F; winter lows dip to about 19°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Mar, Nov, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Sep and Oct.

    Understand

    Climate

    Daylight hours run from 9:50AM - 3:30PM in the winter, and 4:15AM - 11:15PM in the summer. Typical summer temperatures are around 62°F during the dayand 49°F at night. Some summer days occasionally see temperatures higher at around 69°F with lows around 54°F.

    Get in

    There are no roads into or out of Cordova and no railway either, for that matter. Thus your options of getting in are either boat or plane.

    By boat

    The Alaska Marine Highway has a stop in Cordova

    By plane

    Go Merle K. (Mudhole) Smith Airport (CDV)

    Alaska Airlines serves the city from Anchorage, Juneau, Seattle/Tacoma Airport and Yakutat, a place in the panhandle with no overland access to elsewhere. Ravn Alaska ceased its flights from Anchorage in April 2020 due to coronavirus. You should try to contact your travel agency, or contact the airline.
