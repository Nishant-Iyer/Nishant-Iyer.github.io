Overview

Gulf Coast is a region in Alabama. It consists of Baldwin, Clarke, Conecuh, Escambia, Mobile, Monroe, Washington counties.

Plan your trip

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Oct–May. Summer highs reach about 92°F; winter lows dip to about 42°F. Very hot (Jun, Jul, Aug) — plan outdoor time for mornings. Wettest: Aug and Jul.

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 920 miles, roughly 16 h without stops. Flying is usually faster.
  • MOB Mobile Regional Airport, Mobile — 38 mi away. Directions
  • PNS Pensacola International Airport, Pensacola — 49 mi away (major hub). Directions

Mobile Regional Airport (MOB) offers flights from Atlanta, Charlotte, Dallas-Fort Worth, Houston-Intercontinental.

Get in

Mobile Regional Airport (MOB) offers flights from Atlanta, Charlotte, Dallas-Fort Worth, Houston-Intercontinental.

It is often cheaper to fly into Pensacola International Airport (PNS). If you are staying in the Gulf Shores or Orange Beach areas, it is actually shorter travel times from the airport, but everywhere else is farther.

Get around

The most convenient method to travel the region is by car. There is a bus system in the Mobile, Alabama area, but it runs with very low frequency.
