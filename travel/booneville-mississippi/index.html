If you go towards the stoplight (east), you will come to the east side of town, to a four way intersection. The next major town, Tupelo, is down the highway.     Understand

    It is home to Northeast Community College, and the campus is spread throughout the town. It is located primarily on the east side of the town, and most of the older buildings (such as the old Prentiss Manufacturing Company buildings, now two flea markets and a tile store) sit here. This is also where the several good residential areas are located in the town.
