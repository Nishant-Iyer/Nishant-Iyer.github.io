Overview

The Northtowns of Erie County are in the Niagara Frontier region of New York.

Plan your trip

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Sep. Summer highs reach about 80°F; winter lows dip to about 19°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Sep and Oct. (climate data from nearby Buffalo)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 560 miles, roughly 9 h 30 min without stops.
  • BUF Buffalo Niagara International Airport, Buffalo — 3 mi away (major hub). Directions
  • IAG Niagara Falls International Airport, Niagara Falls — 15 mi away. Directions

Sample 3-day trip

  1. Day 1: Clarence — Tillman Road Wildlife Management Area, Clarence Arboretum, Clarence Historical Society.
  2. Day 2: Amherst — Buffalo Niagara Heritage Village, Buffalo Bulls, Old Home Days.
  3. Day 3: Cheektowaga — Our Lady Help of Christians Chapel, Reinstein Woods Nature Preserve, Tomb of Rabbi Eliyahu Yosef Rabinowitz.

Cities

  • Akron — a small rural village that's home of the majestic Akron Falls
  • Alden — countrified charm at the eastern edge of Erie County
  • Amherst — Buffalo's most populous suburb, with a wide range of things to do; includes the charming historic village of Williamsville
  • Cheektowaga — shopping galore, home of the largest mall on the Niagara Frontier
  • Clarence — a comparatively-high income town known for antique shops
  • Grand Island — where the Niagara River splits in two; once an old summer resort, now an upper-class exurb with a charming old-fashioned amusement park.
  • Lancaster — site of the historic Lancaster Opera House
  • Tonawanda — the western terminus of the present-day Erie Canal; a 19th-century lumber port turned middle-class suburb

Go next

Buffalo, of course, if you haven't already.

The world-famous Niagara Falls is to the north.

Just across the Niagara River (via the Peace Bridge) is Canada, more specifically Ontario. Keep in mind that you do need documents such as a passport to cross.
