Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Sep. Summer highs reach about 81°F; winter lows dip to about 16°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Oct and Sep. (climate data from nearby Traverse City)

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 250 miles, roughly 4 h 30 min without stops.

Honor is a small village located in Benzie County in Northwestern Lower Michigan close to Sleeping Bear Dunes National Lakeshore, the Platte Lakes, Crystal Lake and Lake Michigan. Also the Platte River runs through the outskirts of town which makes Honor a popular canoeing and fishing destination.
