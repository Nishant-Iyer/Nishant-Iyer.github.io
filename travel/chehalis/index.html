Overview

Chehalis is a city in southwestern Washington State. It is a twin city of Centralia to the north.

3 sights · 4 places to stay

Best things about Chehalis

Lewis County Historical Museum

Regional history museum housed in the former railway station.

Veterans Memorial Museum

Claquato Church

One of the oldest standing churches in Washington State, built in 1858.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Sep. Summer highs reach about 78°F; winter lows dip to about 35°F. Wettest: Nov and Jan. (climate data from nearby Kent)
