Overview

Washougal is a charming river community of 17,000 people at the junction of the Washougal and Columbia Rivers in southwest Washington State. Visitors will find the cozy downtown a convenient launch for ample outdoor activities including hiking, fishing, kayaking, inner-tubing, mountain biking, motorsports, shooting-sports and more.

    Understand

    The summer heat attracts locals from throughout Washington and Oregon looking to cool off at the serene riverbanks, swimming holes and beaches while inner tubing, swimming and kayaking along the Washougal River.

    Autumn colors provide a vibrant spectacle along Lewis & Clark Highway with auburn-ribboned vistas of the Columbia Gorge at Cape Horn Overlook.

    Winter Holiday Season brings locals from far & wide to the famous self-cut Christmas Tree farms found along Washougal River Road, where families can experience felling their own yuletide Christmas tree while sipping hot chocolate.

    Springtime bloom signals the start of the outdoor season for hikers to enjoy hundreds of miles of hiking trails and campgrounds nearby.

    Get in

    By plane

    Portland International Airport (PDX) is a major international airport a 20 minute drive from Washougal via Highways I-205 & SR-14.

    By car

    State Route 14 (SR-14), The Lewis & Clark Highway, passes through Washougal. Motorists who approach from the west can see Mount Hood rising above the Cascade Mountains framed by the columnar cliffs that signal the gateway of the Gorge.

    SR-14 may be reached by I-205 and I-5 in Vancouver.

    By train

    Amtrak Cascades operates between Vancouver, BC and Eugene via Bellingham, Mount Vernon, Everett, Edmonds, Seattle, Tukwila, Tacoma, Lacey (Olympia), Centralia, Kelso (Longview), Vancouver, WA, Portland, Oregon City, Salem, and Albany. The nearest station to Washougal is Amtrak Vancouver Train Station (VAN) , accessible by CTRAN bus line or car.

    By bus

    Washougal is accessible from Vancouver's Turtle Place Station via bus routes The Vine on Mill Plain and Line 92 Camas/Washougal.

    Do

    Washougal is renowned for motorcycling each summer, hosting thousands of spectators at the Motocross Nationals at Washougal MX Park.

    Do Washougal MX Park

    Home to motocross events throughout the summer season, including Pro & Amateur national competitions. Amateur riders can participate in a breadth of motorcycle disciplines including motocross, enduro riding, street and offroad motorcycle touring as well as 4x4 off road activities within the ample State and Federal public lands adjacent to the city.
