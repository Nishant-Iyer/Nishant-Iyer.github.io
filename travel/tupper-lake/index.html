    Do The Wild Center

    A nature museum on the shores of the Raquette River, with enough trails and exhibits to keep you busy for several hours—even in the winter! Kids have plenty to get their hands on (and plenty of ways to burn off energy), while adults can choose their preferred pace with activities and education, both indoors and outdoors. The center's Wild Walk is a nature museum on stilts, with trails, interactive exhibits, and observation points up to 40 feet in the air, giving visitors an amazing view above the trees.
