Overview

The Northern Edwards Plateau is the far southern reach of the Panhandle of Texas.

Plan your trip

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 1,250 miles, roughly 21 h 30 min without stops. Flying is usually faster.
  • SJT San Angelo Regional Mathis Field, San Angelo — 44 mi away. Directions
  • MAF Midland International Air and Space Port, Midland — 65 mi away. Directions
  • SAT San Antonio International Airport, San Antonio — 218 mi away (major hub). Directions

Sample 3-day trip

  1. Day 1: San Angelo — Fort Concho National Historic Landmark, San Angelo State Park, Lake Nasworthy.
  2. Day 2: Big Spring — Big Spring State Park, Hangar 25 Air Museum, Heritage Museum of Big Spring.
  3. Day 3: Brownwood — Lake Brownwood State Park, Brown County Museum of History, The Martin & Frances Lehnis Railroad Museum.

Understand

The north portion of the Edwards Plateau is bound by Southeast New Mexico on the west, the higher Llano Estacado Plateau to the north, and the North Central Plains to the east and northeast.
