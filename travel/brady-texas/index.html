Best things about Brady

Heart of Texas Country Music Museum

Honoring 75 country music stars with artist's original costumes and items, and life-size wax replicas.

Heart of Texas Historical Museum

Housed in the restored 1910 county jail, this museum has preserved some of the cells along with prisoner articles. In addition it has exhibits on the pioneer days of the town and region.
