Overview

Coldwater is a city of 14,000 people (2020) in West Central Michigan on the border with Indiana. Coldwater is a quaint community where you can find home-town festivals, boutique shops, and events.

Best things about Coldwater

Tibbits Opera House

The theater was built in 1882 in the French Empire style of architecture. It is the second-oldest theater in Michigan. It is now a venue for a variety of cultural activities.

Children's Museum of Branch County

Wing House Museum

It was built in 1875, and is now a historical museum.

Heritage Park

Tennis courts.

Capri Drive-in Theater

Little River Railroad

It offers rides behind a steam locomotive built in 1911, that leave from the city's historic 1883 depot.
