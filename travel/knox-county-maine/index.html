Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Sep. Summer highs reach about 79°F; winter lows dip to about 15°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Oct and Nov. (climate data from nearby Acadia National Park)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 1,160 miles, roughly 20 h without stops. Flying is usually faster.
  • RKD Knox County Regional Airport, Rockland — 4 mi away. Directions
  • AUG Augusta State Airport, Augusta — 43 mi away. Directions
  • PWM Portland International Jetport, Portland — 69 mi away (major hub). Directions

Sample 3-day trip

  1. Day 1: Rockland — Farnsworth Art Museum, Maine Lighthouse Museum, Owl's Head Transportation Museum.
  2. Day 2: Vinalhaven — Lane's Island, Tiptoe Mountain, Brown's Head Lighthouse.
  3. Day 3: Camden — Mt. Battie, Laite Beach, The Center Green.
