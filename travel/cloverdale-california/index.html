Best things about Cloverdale

Cloverdale History Center & Museum

The Gothic-Revival house is one of the oldest dwellings in town, and contains the holdings of the Cloverdale Historical Society, Craftsman and Victorian furnishings.

River Park

This park features river access and picnic areas.

The Cloverdale Historic Murals

The murals depict early life in the town and were painted by local artists.

Cloverdale River Park

The park runs along the west bank of the Russian River, with opportunities for walking, fishing, paddling, birdwatching, wildlife viewing, and picnicking.

    Stay The Pinschower Inn

    This elegant 1901 Victorian mansion is on a quiet residential street. All rooms have private baths, central heat and A/C, flat screen TVs, and free Wi-Fi access. A multi-course gourmet breakfast is served every morning, including fresh fruits and vegetables grown at the Inn. The Inn features fine period antiques, luxurious linens and upscale amenities. The beautifully landscaped grounds include a demonstration vineyard, Koi ponds and patio, flower and vegetable gardens and a Mediterranean courtyard. Outdoors, guests can enjoy the splendor from a forty-foot veranda, a vintage gazebo or a flower-covered pavilion. Inside, they can relax in the inn's parlor or library.
