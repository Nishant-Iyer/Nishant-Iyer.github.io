Get in and around

Clackamas is on Interstate 205, with I-5 and Oregon City to the south, and I-84 and the Portland Airport to the North. Oregon Route 224 runs as an expressway to Milwaukie and then as a highway to rural Clackamas County, while Oregon Route 212 connects Clackamas to US-26. Clackamas is also the terminus of the MAX Green Line light rail.
