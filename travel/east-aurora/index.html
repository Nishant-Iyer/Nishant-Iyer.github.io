See Knox Farm State Park

Visitors to Knox Farm can observe wildlife, hike, cross-country ski and ride horseback along beautifully scenic nature trails. Nature walks are also offered through a partnership with New York Audubon and the Buffalo Audubon Society, as well as programs offered by state park naturalists. The Knox Farm Visitor Center contains exhibits of historic and natural interest. Biking is allowed in designated areas and horseback riding and driving by permit only. Household pets only; on a leash not more than 6 feet.

📍 437 Buffalo Rd.1/2 mile north from the Circle on Rt 16🕒 Dawn to Dusk, year round☎ +1 716-549-1802

Shop Vidler's

East Aurora's original five-and-ten store opened in 1930 and is located in 4 connected 19th-century buildings. Voted by readers of Buffalo Spree magazine as "the most fun place to shop in Western New York". 75,000 different items in inventory including candy, cards, gifts, school & office supplies, candles, collectables, house & kitchen wares, fabric & yarn, toys, year-round holiday & Christmas items. Popular destination for locals and tourists.

Eat The Dog Bar Restaurant

Dine in a nearly 200-year-old landmark with rural-America decor and a collection of antiques and curios from the past. Popular entrées are Porterhouse steak, thick loin lamb chops, shrimp scampi, and 20-ounce lobster tail. Other menu items include soup, salad, burgers and dessert. Full bar. Slow service as everything is cooked to order. Reservations recommended.
