Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Oct. Summer highs reach about 84°F; winter lows dip to about 22°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Sep and Apr. (climate data from nearby Cleveland)

Buy

Downtown Chagrin Falls boasts plenty of one of a kind boutiques and shops without the bustle of a city. Jewelry, clothing and shoes define the theme to many of the shops. For winter gear, head to Geiger's.

Stay The Hillbrook Inn

A quaint bed and breakfast nestled in the woods by a picturesque river, Hillbrook also serves as a club. The Tudor mansion transports patrons back in time to 16th-century England and its character adds a touch of elegance to any trip. The Inn is very quiet and has very good food.
