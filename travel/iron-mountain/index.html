Overview

Best things about Iron Mountain

Chapin Mine Steam Pump Engine

Largest steam driven engine built in the United States, measuring several stories in height. Now housed in the Cornish Pumping Engine and Mining Museum.

Pine Mountain Jump

One of the largest artificial ski jumps in the world.
