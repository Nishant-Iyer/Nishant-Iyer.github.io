Overview

Orange City is a city in Western Iowa. The population was 6,004 in the 2010 census.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Oct. Summer highs reach about 85°F; winter lows dip to about 10°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Jun and May. (climate data from nearby Sioux City)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 530 miles, roughly 9 h without stops.
  • SUX Sioux Gateway Airport / Brigadier General Bud Day Field, Sioux City — 45 mi away. Directions
  • FSD Sioux Falls Regional Airport, Sioux Falls — 53 mi away. Directions
  • OMA Eppley Airfield, Omaha — 118 mi away (major hub). Directions

For the most part, driving is your best option. If you're a private pilot, then you also have the option of flying into the town's small airport.
