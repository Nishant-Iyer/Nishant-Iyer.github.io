    Do

    Do Homestead-Miami Speedway

    NASCAR track in the Greater Miami area. Before COVID-19, the track had hosted NASCAR's Championship Weekend, featuring the season finales of the organization's three national touring series (Cup, Xfinity, Truck); the season finale has since moved to the Phoenix area. The track's NASCAR date is now in October; the Cup Series races on the last weekend of the month, with the Xfinity and Truck races a week earlier. Current sponsored race names are Dixie Vodka 400 (Cup), Contender Boats 300 (Xfinity), and Baptist Health 200 (Truck).

    📍 1 Ralph Sanchez Speedway Blvd, Homestead🕒 Ticket office: Mon-Fri 9AM-5PMChecked Jul 2023
    MapsWebsite

    Fruit picking

    Do Robert is Here Fruit Stand and Farm

    This fruit stand and farm is popular with tourists and local residents. It offers many of the most sought after varieties of locally-grown tropical fruits and vegetables as well as widely-acclaimed smoothies, ice creams, and other prepared foods. It also features a petting zoo and is located only minutes from one of the main entrances to Everglades National Park as well as an alligator farm.

    📍 19200 SW 344th St, Homestead🕒 Daily 8AM–7PM including holidays; closed September and October☎ +1 305-985-1520
    MapsWebsite

    Do Fruit and Spice Park

    This park features a vast assortment of fruits, spices, and other foods of interest that can be grown in Florida's subtropical climate. The park is also famous for getting a few items to grow that normally can't be grown even in Florida (e.g. mangosteen, cacao, jaboticaba, etc.). After an orientation, visitors are allowed to pick and eat whatever is available at the time of their visit. This park is relatively close to "Robert is Here" Fruit Stand and Farm.

    📍 24801 S.W. 187th Avenue, Homestead🕒 Daily 9AM-5PM, except Christmas💲 $8 adult, $2 child 6-11, and children under 6 are free☎ +1 305-247-5727
