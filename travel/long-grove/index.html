Understand

Long Grove is a village in Lake County, and it has certain ordinances to maintain the town’s country atmosphere.

See

  • Old Long Grove - When someone says they've been to Long Grove, they are usually talking about this historic street filled with moderately expensive yet very interesting stores and restaurants. Different festivals go on there during the year.
