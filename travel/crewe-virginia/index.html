Get in

Crewe is best accessed by road, with US-460 running through the middle of town and US-360 just to its north. Route 49, which extends into North and South Carolina, offers access to Buggs Island Lake.

Get around

Crewe can be traversed by car or on foot, with an extensive albeit aging sidewalk system. Residential streets are safe for bicyclists. The Blackstone Area Bus System also offers bus service on Mondays, Tuesdays, and Thursdays for a $1 fare.

Do

The local library sometimes offers a book club, board games, and yoga classes.

Outdoor recreation

Fishing and a nature trail are available at the (Nottoway) Falls off of Route 49. Locals will swim in the waters below the dam, though they can be perilous due to slick algae and water moccasins.

Twin Lakes State Park, 15 minutes west of Crewe, offers fishing, camping, swimming, a nature trail, a playground, and kayaking. Two picnic shelters are available for reservation for private events.

Buy

Crewe has two florists and a consignment shop, and no grocery stores. Vegetables are available seasonally at a produce stand in the Super Dollar parking lot or at Sunny Slope Orchard past Burkeville on US-360. Meat is available at Meat & Greet in Burkeville. Most residents travel to Farmville or Blackstone for groceries.
