Overview

Danvers is a town in Massachusetts with a population of about 28,000 (2020). A large part of the town used to be called "Salem Village" and was where most of the accusers in the 1692 witch hysteria lived. The trials took place in neighboring Salem.

    See Former Danvers State Insane Asylum

    A very good example of the Kirkbride style asylum that was once extremely common in the United States, and made famous from the 2001 horror film Session 9, which was filmed in the complex when it was abandoned. Much of the asylum complex was demolished in 2007, but the main Kirkbride building remains, and now houses the Bradlee Danvers apartment complex, and thus not open to the public. The cemetery for the asylum remains as well, with its characteristic hexagonal headstones.
