Overview

Moorhead is a city in Minnesota of about 35,000. Moorhead is sometimes referred to as the "twin city" of the larger Fargo, North Dakota, its neighbor across the Red River in North Dakota.

    Understand

    Together Moorhead and Fargo are the hub of the Fargo-Moorhead Metropolitan Area, with suburbs Dilworth, West Fargo, Horace, and Harwood.

    Get in

    By car

    Moorhead is at Minnesota's western border on Interstate 94.

    US Highway 10 has its western terminus in Moorhead, and US Highway 75 passes through the city in a north/south orientation.

    By train

    The Fargo/Moorhead area has rail service via Amtrak (the Empire Builder), with the passenger terminal at 420 4th Street North in Fargo. Trains only come once a day in each direction, in the middle of the night.

    By plane

    Two airports serve Fargo/Moorhead:

    By bus

    Bus service is provided by Jefferson Lines, serving one stop in Moorhead and two across the state border in Fargo, from cities in North Dakota, South Dakota, Minnesota, and Montana. Check the schedules for all three stops in the Fargo-Moorhead area, as different buses serve different stops.

    Get around

    Matbus provides a local bus service around the Fargo/Moorhead area six days a week.
