Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Nov–Apr. Summer highs reach about 100°F; winter lows dip to about 53°F. Very hot (May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep) — plan outdoor time for mornings. Wettest: Sep and Jun. (climate data from nearby McAllen)
