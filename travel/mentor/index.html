Mentor is a city in Lake County, Ohio.

Best things about Mentor

James A. Garfield National Historic Site

The summer farm house of the 20th President of the United States.

Finnish Heritage Museum

A museum cataloging the history of Americans of Finnish decent.

Headlands Beach State Park

Headlands Beach is Ohio's largest beach on Lake Erie. Activities include fishing, swimming and hiking.

Indian Museum of Lake County

Civic Center Park

Includes a waterpark, an ice arena, and a skate park.

Mentor On Tap

Annual beer tasting competition.
