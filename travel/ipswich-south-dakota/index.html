Ipswich is a town in the Glacial Lakes region of South Dakota.

4 sights

Best things about Ipswich

J.W. Parmley Museum

The former home of J.W. Parmley, an early pioneer and developer of north central South Dakota.

Parmley Land Office

Marcus P. Beebe Memorial Library

The town library. The Prayer Rock/Memorial Rock is located on the front grounds.

Memorial Arch

Memorial to the 12 men of Edmunds County who died in World War I.
