Overview

Coulee City is in the Columbia River Plateau of Washington State, at the south shore of Banks Lake in the Grand Coulee.

3 sights · 1 things to do · 1 places to stay

Best things about Coulee City

Lake Lenore Caves

These caves on the Grand Coulee were formed by water erosion on volcanic rock, which makes them geologically interesting.

Sun Lakes-Dry Falls State Park

At the foot of Dry Falls. Offers camping and lakefront shoreline. During the last ice age, Dry Falls occasionally carried more water by volume than all the rivers of the world combined (known as the Missoula Flood).

Banks Lake

A 27 mi reservoir in the northern portion of the Grand Coulee, a formerly dry coulee near the Columbia River, formed by the Missoula Floods.

Summer Falls

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Sep. Summer highs reach about 87°F; winter lows dip to about 19°F. Cold months (Jan, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Dec and Jan. (climate data from nearby Leavenworth)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 1,940 miles, roughly 33 h 30 min without stops. Flying is usually faster.
  • EAT Pangborn Memorial Airport, Wenatchee — 45 mi away. Directions
  • GEG Spokane International Airport, Spokane — 82 mi away (major hub). Directions

US Highway 2 (US-2) passes through Coulee City, between Wenatchee and Spokane.
