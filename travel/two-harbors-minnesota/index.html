Overview

Two Harbors is a town along Lake Superior in Minnesota, about 27 miles north of Duluth.

3 sights · 1 places to eat · 2 places to stay

Best things about Two Harbors

Lighthouse Point and Harbor Museum

The oldest lighthouse in Minnesota. Keepers quarters now used as a bed and breakfast (see below).

Split Rock Lighthouse

Depot Museum

A former train station built by the Duluth and Iron Range Railroad. Today it is maintained by the Lake County Historical Society as a history museum.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Sep. Summer highs reach about 78°F; winter lows dip to about 2°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Mar, Nov, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Jun and Jul. (climate data from nearby Duluth)

Minnesota State Highway 61 is the only route into town. It stretches from Duluth in the south, through Two Harbors, all the way to the Canadian border at Grand Portage. From there it becomes Ontario Highway 61 and continues to Thunder Bay.
