Overview

Kapalua is a resort community located along the west coast of the island of Maui, Hawaii. Situated 20 minutes from Lahaina town and bounded to the North by Honolua Bay.

    Get in

    Kapalua Resort is a world-class destination in West Maui.

    Go Kapalua-West Maui Airport (JHM)

    Flights from Honolulu, Kahului, Kailua-Kona and Moloka'i

    is 5 minutes drive from the resort
    MapsWebsite

    Kapalua Resort offers a free shuttle service to and from this Airport. Car rental services are offered at the Kapalua-West Maui Airport and also at the Ka'anapali Car Rental station on Halawai Drive, off Honoapiilani Highway. Kapalua Resort is located 5 mi from Ka'anapali Beach Resort and 8 mi from historic Lahaina Town.

    Additionally, Maui's main airport Kahului Airport (OGG) is approximately 45-60 minutes' drive from Kapalua Resort. All major car rental companies are offered within the Kahului Airport complex on Mokuea Place.

    See

    From the summit of the mountains at Pu'u Kukui to the Bays of Pi'ilani at the coastline, Kapalua Resort is a natural wonderland of beauty, rich in history and culture. Its 22,000 acres (8,900 ha) are filled with lush valleys, verdant peaks, towering pines, lava rock promontories, picturesque bays and protected coral reefs. The resort is surrounded by working pineapple fields and a diversified farm.

    Kapalua Resort is encircled by a necklace of five pristine bays and beaches. All offer a variety of water sports activities, general beach relaxation and some picnic facilities.

    Do

    From world-class golf, tennis and spa to one-of-a-kind adventure tours and a network of free hiking trails, Kapalua Resort offers something for everyone:

    Do Kapalua Spa

    The second largest, and only stand-alone, destination spa on Maui. Spanning five acres perched on a hill overlooking serene Kapalua Bay, this spa offers 30,000 square feet (2,787 sq m) of luxury with 9 indoor treatment rooms and 10 outdoor island-style bungalows, some with stone soaking tubs and dual treatment space for couples. With an elegant beach house feel, Kapalua Spa opens up to expansive ocean views. Specialty Hawaiian massage and skin treatments, seaweed and marine algae facials, complete beauty salon services, a movement studio and oceanview fitness facility will full-range Kinesis Circuit training, a saline lap pool and sundeck and separate locker rooms with whirlpool, sauna, steam and ice rain showers for men and women are some of the highlights.

    Buy

    Kapalua Resort features a distinct array of shopping experiences for the resort, adventure, sports and casual shopper:

    Shop Kapalua Logo shop

    This store is dedicated to the world-renowned Kapalua butterfly logo on a variety of golf apparel and lifestyle wear, featuring exclusive fashion labels for men, women and children. Island inspired gifts and home décor merchandise are also featured. Located in the Kapalua Resort Center.

    Maps

    Shop Kapalua Adventures' Outfitters Shop

    In the Kapalua Resort Center, you'll find a boutique of premium outdoor gear, sportswear, hats, shoes and fun accessories for the adventurous-at-heart.

    Maps

    Shop Kapalua Golf and Tennis Garden Pro Shops

    For the finest in golf apparel, golf clubs and equipment, visit The Plantation Course Pro Shop and The Bay Course Pro Shop at the Kapalua Golf Courses. Kapalua Tennis Garden offers tennis apparel and gear, tennis racquets and more in its Tennis Pro Shop next to The Bay Course.

    Maps

    Shop Honolua Store

    In this landmark 'general store' at the heart of the resort, shop for gourmet foods, snacks, soft drinks, juices and groceries along with an impressive selection of beer and wine. You'll also find a charming variety of island and home decor gift items and Kapalua logo souvenirs.

    Shop La Perle

    Located in the Honolua Village Center is a fine jewelry store, featuring exclusive Kapalua butterfly and designer jewelry as well as an exquisite array of Tahitian and South Seas pearls.

    Drink

    Entertainment options and happy hour specials are happening around Kapalua Resort year-round.

    Merriman's Kapalua features island music on weekends; Pineapple Grill offers live jazz on Friday and Saturday nights. Sansei Seafood Restaurant & Sushi Bar is popular for Karoake singing and drink specials on Thursday and Friday nights.

    The Ritz-Carlton features Hawaiian contemporary musicians in the Alaloa Lounge nightly and Pacific-Asian music in The Banyan Tree restaurant Wednesday-Saturday.
