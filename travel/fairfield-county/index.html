Overview

Fairfield County is a county of Connecticut in the New York City metropolitan area, and is home to some of the wealthiest suburbs in the country.

Understand

Fairfield county is one of the highest earning regions of the entire united states, because of this, the lower half of the region is sometimes referred to as the "Gold Coast" by locals. Many residents are commuters to New York City and live in Connecticut because of the lower population density. However, despite being home to some of the highest earners in the United States, some towns, such as Stratford (Connecticut) or Bridgeport (Connecticut) are known to have disproportionately high crime rates for the region. The large income gap between towns is sometimes a source of local tension.

Stay safe

Fairfield County is a relatively safe area of the country, but does have some crime in the bigger cities such as Bridgeport. Just follow common sense rules and you should be fine.
