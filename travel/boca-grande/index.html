Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Oct–Apr. Summer highs reach about 92°F; winter lows dip to about 54°F. Very hot (May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep) — plan outdoor time for mornings. Wettest: Aug and Jun. (climate data from nearby Fort Myers)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 1,330 miles, roughly 23 h without stops. Flying is usually faster.
  • PGD Punta Gorda Airport, Punta Gorda — 20 mi away. Directions
  • RSW Southwest Florida International Airport, Fort Myers — 35 mi away (major hub). Directions

The only road entering Boca Grande enters from the northeast. It goes southeast from Placida Rd across the causeway to the island and continues south as Gasparilla Rd. The intersection with 5th St marks the downtown area.
