Overview

Central Vermont is in center of Vermont and includes the state capital of Montpelier.

Plan your trip

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Sep. Summer highs reach about 78°F; winter lows dip to about 7°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Mar, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Jul and Jun. (climate data from nearby Montpelier)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 940 miles, roughly 16 h without stops. Flying is usually faster.
  • LEB Lebanon Municipal Airport, Lebanon — 33 mi away. Directions
  • RUT Rutland - Southern Vermont Regional Airport, Rutland — 35 mi away. Directions
  • ALB Albany International Airport, Albany — 103 mi away (major hub). Directions

There are Amtrak stations in Waterbury, Montpelier, Randolph, and White River Junction.

Sample 3-day trip

  1. Day 1: Stowe — Vermont Ski and Snowboard Museum, Trapp Family Lodge, Stowe Mountain Resort.
  2. Day 2: Montpelier — Vermont State House, Montpelier City Hall, Savoy Theater.
  3. Day 3: Jeffersonville — Smuggler's Notch Resort, Bryan Memorial Art Gallery, Visions of Vermont Art Gallery.
