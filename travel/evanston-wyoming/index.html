Overview

Evanston is a city in Southwest Wyoming. The town was founded during the construction of the first transcontinental railroad and maintains a strong railroad history. The population was 11,700 people in 2020.

Best things about Evanston

Bear River State Park

324 acre day use park with Visitor Center. Wildlife including bison and elk. Biking, hiking, skiing, river access, and numerous geocaches.

Uinta County Museum

Located in a historic Carnegie building, the museum has collections related to the history of the Evanston area.

Guest ranches

Horseback riding, wagon rides, western cooking classes

Purple Sage Golf Course

Golf course. Cross-country skiing during winter. Restaurant and bar.

Roundhouse and Railyard Tours

Built in 1912. Fully intact four-section roundhouse with operating turntable.

Uinta Lanes

Bowling (call for open times), restaurant and bar.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Jun, Sep–Oct. Summer highs reach about 91°F; winter lows dip to about 21°F. Very hot (Jul) — plan outdoor time for mornings. Cold months (Jan, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: May and Apr. (climate data from nearby Ogden)
