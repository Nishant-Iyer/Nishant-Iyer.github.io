Overview

Republic is a small city in the Rocky Mountain foothills of northeast Washington State. It is the county seat of Ferry County.

3 sights · 3 things to do · 1 places to stay

Best things about Republic

Stonerose Interpretive Center

Near a rich collection of fossils, including the petrified flowers that give the museum its name. At a nearby fossil bed you can collect your own fossils.

Curlew Lake State Park

A relaxing, 123-acre campground park. It is near a fossil dig and an airfield.

Ferry County Historical Museum

Ranald MacDonald's Grave

Ranald MacDonald was the first person to teach English in Japan. He has monuments to him in Japan.

Eagle Track Raceway

Small-town, dirt-track racing.

K-Diamond-K Guest Ranch

Enjoy the scenic horseback riding trails of the area

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Sep. Summer highs reach about 87°F; winter lows dip to about 26°F. Cold months (Jan, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Dec and Jan. (climate data from nearby Spokane)

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 1,920 miles, roughly 33 h without stops. Flying is usually faster.

  • EAT Pangborn Memorial Airport, Wenatchee — 110 mi away. Directions
