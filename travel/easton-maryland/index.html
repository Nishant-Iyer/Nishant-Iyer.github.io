Best things about Easton

Waterfowl Festival

A three-day celebration of the arts to view and buy some of the best wildlife art in the world. Includes demonstrations and competitions. Held the second full weekend in November.

Get in

By car

Driving to Easton is very simple, in large part because of U.S. 50 running through it on its way to Ocean City. From Memorial Day to Labor Day, weekend beach traffic may cause long delays.

Get around

By car

Since U.S. 50 gets backed up during the summer, find routes that avoid the highway as much as possible. That said, you will likely encounter backups at nearby intersections as well. If going downtown, there is a mix of free and metered parking lots. See the parking map on the official town website for the specific parking locations.

On foot

Much of the town is very walkable, especially downtown.

By bicycle

The Easton Rails-to-Trails runs for approximately 2.5 miles and connects the northern and southern portions of the town.
