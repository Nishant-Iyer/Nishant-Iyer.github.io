Understand

St. Cloud, though kind of in the shadow of the larger Kissimmee to the west, has a nice lakefront and some attractions, particularly zoological, for the tourist in the greater Orlando area. Its streets take the name of US states, which belies some of the little less tasteful aspects of the town like that it was a veteran's home for Confederate soldiers or a sundown town.

Do

Do Florida EcoSafaris at Forever Florida (Zipline Safari)

Journey deep into Forever Florida, a 4700 acre eco-ranch and wildlife conservation area, preserved just as nature intended. This beautiful site boasts 9 distinct Florida ecosystems and an endless array of natural flora and fauna including alligators, black bears, nearly two hundred species of birds and the endangered Florida panther. Choose your own fully-guided adventure: Coach Safari, Horseback Safari, soar through the treetops on the Zipline Safari. Reservations required.

📍 4755 N Kenansville Rd, St Cloud☎ +1 407 957-9794
