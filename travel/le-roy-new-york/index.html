Overview

Le Roy is a town in Genesee County, New York. It contains the village of Le Roy and the hamlets of Fort Hill and Lime Rock.

    Understand

    Le Roy is located along Interstate 90 (the New York State Thruway), near the western terminus of Interstate 490 (an auxiliary freeway that heads into the city of Rochester). From I-90, exit on to I-490 (exit #47) and then take the first exit (NY Route 19) to reach Le Roy.
