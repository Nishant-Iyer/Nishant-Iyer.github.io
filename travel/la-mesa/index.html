Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Jan–Dec. Summer highs reach about 86°F; winter lows dip to about 46°F. Wettest: Jan and Feb.

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 2,100 miles, roughly 36 h without stops. Flying is usually faster.
  • SAN San Diego International Airport, San Diego — 10 mi away (major hub). Directions
  • CLD McClellan-Palomar Airport, Carlsbad — 29 mi away. Directions

The local San Diego Trolley light rail system serves La Mesa with two lines: the Orange Line travels through much of La Mesa, heading south through Lemon Grove and on to Downtown San Diego. The Green Line travels through the northern part of La Mesa, going west past SDSU and through Mission Valley to San Diego's Old Town and Downtown. La Mesa has four trolley stations: Spring Street (Orange Line only), La Mesa Blvd (Orange Line only), Grossmont Transit Center (Orange and Green Lines) and Amaya Drive (Orange and Green Lines).
