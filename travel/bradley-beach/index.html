Get around

Since Bradley Beach is such a small town, it is easiest to get around via foot or bicycle.

See

The most visited part of the town is the beach. The beach runs for the length of the town from north to south. Access to the beach is controlled through beach badges, which are available at booths on the beach and can be purchased for a season (May—September) or a day.
