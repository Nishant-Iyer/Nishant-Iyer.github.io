Overview

Missouri River Country is the northeast region of Montana bordering North Dakota to the east and Saskatchewan, Canada to the north.

Plan your trip

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 1,250 miles, roughly 21 h 30 min without stops. Flying is usually faster.
  • GGW Glasgow Valley County Airport Wokal Field, Glasgow — 4 mi away. Directions
  • OLF L M Clayton Airport, Wolf Point — 52 mi away. Directions

Get in

Many of the towns are along U.S. Highway 2 (US-2), what Montanans refer to as the Hi-Line.

By train

The Empire Builder provides daily service to/from Malta, Glasgow and Wolf Point. Train will take you as far as Chicago (Eastbound) or Seattle (Westbound). Westbound train also provides service to Portland via Spokane.

See

Fort Peck Dam southeast of Glasgow was constructed by hydraulic methods and is still the largest hydraulic earth-filled dam in the world.

The world famous Hell Creek Formation runs through this area, known for its dinosaur fossils. You can see these fossils at the Museum of the Rockies in Bozeman.

Stay safe

Winter driving: During the winter, smaller secondary roads are often closed entirely, and even the Interstate can shut down rapidly when the weather turns poor. Verify weather conditions and road status with the Montana Department of Transportation before setting out, and travel with emergency supplies. Unpaved roads are typically impassable for over half the year.
