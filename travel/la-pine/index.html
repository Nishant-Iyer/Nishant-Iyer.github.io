Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Jul–Sep. Summer highs reach about 69°F; winter lows dip to about 19°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr, Nov, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Dec and Jan. (climate data from nearby Crater Lake National Park)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).
