Overview

Cañon City is a city in Colorado. It is the gateway to Royal Gorge, a cavernous canyon of the Arkansas River.

Get in

By air

Pueblo is the closest city with an airport (the Pueblo Memorial Airport), served by Southern Airways Express. Colorado Sprints Airport (COS) (in Colorado Springs) is the closest full-service airport, and is less than 50 miles away. Denver International Airport (DEN) (in Denver) is approximately 125 miles away.

By car

Cañon City is directly linked to Pueblo by US Route 50. From Colorado Springs, go south on Colorado State Highway 115, then west on US Route 50. Cañon City can be driven to from Denver via Interstate 25, State Route 115, and US Route 50.

Get around

Cañon City's main road is Royal Gorge Boulevard, which is part of US Route 50.

Do American Adventure Expeditions

Offering white water rafting trips on the Arkansas River for all ages and skill levels. Enjoy the intense rapids of the Royal Gorge Canyon or the beautiful bighorn sheep in Bighorn Sheep Canyon. The only outpost on the river in the Royal Gorge. Overnight and full day trips available.

Do River Runners Royal Gorge Rafting Center (Rafting Canon City, Colorado)
