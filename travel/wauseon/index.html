Best time to visit & weather

    Understand

    Wauseon is the largest city and county seat of Fulton County. Despite this, with a population of just over 7,000 it still manages to offer a smaller town feel.

    See Cruise Night

    Cruise nights are held every Tuesday from May to August from 6:00 to 8PM. with special Super Cruise nights held once each month. This is the largest cruise in northwest Ohio and southern Michigan.
