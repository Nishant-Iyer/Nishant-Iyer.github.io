Overview

Down East is a region in Maine. It encompasses Hancock and Washington Counties.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Sep. Summer highs reach about 79°F; winter lows dip to about 15°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Oct and Nov. (climate data from nearby Acadia National Park)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 1,230 miles, roughly 21 h without stops. Flying is usually faster.
  • BHB Hancock County-Bar Harbor Airport, Bar Harbor — 38 mi away. Directions
  • BGR Bangor International Airport, Bangor — 42 mi away. Directions
  • PWM Portland International Jetport, Portland — 145 mi away (major hub). Directions

Sample 3-day trip

  1. Day 1: Lubec — West Quoddy Lighthouse and Visitor Center, Roosevelt Campobello International Park, Le Cadeau Originals Gallery.
  2. Day 2: Eastport — Tides Institute and Museum of Art, Old Sow, Peavey Memorial Library.
  3. Day 3: Calais — Saint Croix Island International Historic Site, Whitlock's Mill Light, Dr. Job Holmes Cottage & Museum.

Other destinations

  • Acadia National Park - the first National Park east of the Mississippi. Busy during tourist season but always one of the most beautiful parts of the Maine coast. Approximately 30 minutes south of Ellsworth via Route 3, on Mount Desert Island near Bar Harbor.
