Overview

Southlake is a city in the Dallas-Fort Worth Metroplex region of Texas. It was once chosen as the best place to live within the Dallas/Fort Worth area.

4 sights · 2 things to do · 3 places to stay

Best things about Southlake

Southlake Town Square

A huge high-end shopping center styled as an old town square. Newer additions include a European style plaza, 14-screen movie theatre, and more shops. For a list of shopping and dining options see the square's website.

Southlake Community Band

A group of 70 volunteer musicians; see website for upcoming performances.

Bob Jones Park

Has hiking trails, a fishing pond, athletic fields, and a dog park.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Mar–May, Sep–Nov. Summer highs reach about 96°F; winter lows dip to about 35°F. Very hot (Jun, Jul, Aug) — plan outdoor time for mornings. Wettest: Oct and May. (climate data from nearby Fort Worth)

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 980 miles, roughly 17 h without stops. Flying is usually faster.
  • DFW Dallas Fort Worth International Airport, Dallas-Fort Worth — 7 mi away (major hub). Directions
  • FTW Fort Worth Meacham International Airport, Fort Worth — 15 mi away. Directions

From Dallas, take TX-114 W toward Grapevine, take the exit toward FM-1709/Southlake Blvd. From Fort Worth, take TX-121 N, exit at William D. Tate Ave. From Dallas/Fort Worth Airport, take TX-114 W, exit at Southlake Blvd.

Get in

From Dallas, take TX-114 W toward Grapevine, take the exit toward FM-1709/Southlake Blvd. From Fort Worth, take TX-121 N, exit at William D. Tate Ave. From Dallas/Fort Worth Airport, take TX-114 W, exit at Southlake Blvd.

Get around

To get around Southlake you'll definitely want to have a car. Taxis services to the airport (which is less than 10 minutes away) are available.
