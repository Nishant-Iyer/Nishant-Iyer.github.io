Overview

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Feb–May, Oct–Nov. Summer highs reach about 96°F; winter lows dip to about 36°F. Very hot (Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep) — plan outdoor time for mornings. Wettest: Jan and Feb. (climate data from nearby Merced)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 2,190 miles, roughly 37 h 30 min without stops. Flying is usually faster.
  • MCE Merced Regional Macready Field, Merced — 24 mi away. Directions
  • SCK Stockton Metropolitan Airport, Stockton — 61 mi away. Directions
  • SJC Mineta San Jose International Airport, San Jose — 63 mi away (major hub). Directions

At the junction of State Highways 152 and 165, with Interstate 5 being within 10 miles of the city. Los Banos is 70 miles from Fresno, 85 miles from Monterey, 110 miles from Yosemite National Park, 120 miles from San Francisco, 125 miles from Sacramento, and 265 miles from Los Angeles.

    Go next

    • Santa Nella – Home to Pea Soup Andersen's and a good stopping point for travelers on Interstate 5. About 20 minutes away.
    • Hollister – 152 West to 156 West. Home to motorcycle rallies around July 4. About 50 minutes away with no traffic
    • Fresno – 152 East to 99 South. Home to Fresno State University and the gateway to Yosemite from points south. About 1 hour 15 min away.
    • Gilroy – 152 West. Home to Gilroy Gardens and the Gilroy Garlic Festival. About 1 hour away with no traffic
    • San Francisco – 5 North to 580 West. About 2 hours away with no traffic. Make sure to allow lots of time during peak hours.
    • Los Angeles – 5 South. About 4 to 6 hours away. There is not a lot of services on Interstate 5 until the Grapevine.
