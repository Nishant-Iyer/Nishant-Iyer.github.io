Overview

Emporia is a city in the Flint Hills region of Kansas. It is the county seat of Lyon County and the home of Emporia State University and William Allen White.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Apr–May, Sep–Oct. Summer highs reach about 90°F; winter lows dip to about 20°F. Very hot (Jul) — plan outdoor time for mornings. Cold months (Jan) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: May and Jun. (climate data from nearby Topeka)
