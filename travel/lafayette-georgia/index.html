Overview

LaFayette is a small town in north west Georgia, near Chattanooga, Tennessee.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Mar–Nov. Summer highs reach about 88°F; winter lows dip to about 31°F. Wettest: Jan and Mar. (climate data from nearby Historic High Country)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 620 miles, roughly 11 h without stops. Flying is usually faster.
  • CHA Chattanooga Metropolitan Airport (Lovell Field), Chattanooga — 23 mi away. Directions
  • PDK DeKalb Peachtree Airport, Atlanta — 80 mi away. Directions
  • ATL Hartsfield Jackson Atlanta International Airport, Atlanta — 89 mi away (major hub). Directions

LaFayette is well connected with two major airports,Chattanooga Metropolitan Airport is just 30 miles away which is well connected to major cities and Atlanta is a major port of entry in US which is around 70 miles away.Paid taxi is available form Atlanta and Chattanooga airports. Also there is a bus service available form Chattanooga airport to LaFayette square which is run by Walker County. LaFayette is well connected by road through Highway I-24. If you are living in a nearby state car is the best way for traveling.
