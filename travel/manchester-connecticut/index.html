    Do

    Do Manchester Road Race

    Annual 4.748-mile footrace head through the streets of Manchester every Thanksgiving Day. Normally drawing around 15,000 participants, it is one of the oldest and largest "Turkey Trots" in the nation, as well as a great source of pride for locals.

    ⚠ Last checked Dec 2016 — confirm before you go

    Buy

    Shop Shoppes at Buckland Hills

    Formerly called the Buckland Hills Mall, this shopping center is the only enclosed shopping mall in the Manchester area. Containing about 145 shops and food locations, the variety found in the mall is wide.

    📍 194 Buckland Hills Dr☎ +1 860 644-1450
    MapsWebsite

    Shop The Promenade Shops at Evergreen Walk

    An outdoor mall, Evergreen Walk, is set up to bring up memories of Main Street USA. One thing that helps this shopping area stand out is that it is set up to be animal friendly. Many of the stores have water bowls set out for dogs and the owners can bring their pet shopping with them.

    📍 501 Evergreen Way #503 South Windsor☎ +1 860-432-3398
    MapsWebsite
