Understand

Strictly speaking, Waterport is the tiny hamlet located at the spot where Route 279 crosses the Oak Orchard River at Lake Alice. The surrounding town is called Carlton, but since the post office is in Waterport, mail is addressed to that location, and residents throughout the township consider themselves to live in Waterport.

Unlike other Western New York villages with "-port" in their names, Waterport isn't on the Erie Canal. Instead, it's on the Oak Orchard River (also known as Oak Orchard Creek), just upstream from the dam that creates Lake Alice.

Do

In 2013, Point Breeze was named the country's "Ultimate Fishing Town" by the World Fishing Network—just beating out Cape Hatteras. Sure, it's just an unscientific poll, but the designation is well earned. There's some outstanding fishing to be had, not just at Point Breeze but well upstream along the Oak Orchard River. Brown trout, salmon, and steelhead are among the most common catches.

Eat

Eat Gilligan's Galley

Opened in 2022 in a spot that's seen a series of restaurants come and go over the course of decades, Gilligan's Galley brings a tiki-style atmosphere to Point Breeze. Despite the decor, though, the menu is basic American with burgers, wraps, sandwiches, soups, salads, and appetizers.

Sleep

The hamlet of Waterport itself has no lodging, but there are a couple of places to stay at Point Breeze.

Stay Captain's Cove Resort

This small motel is attached to a popular bait-and-tackle shop and marina at The Bridges, a small hamlet at the spot where Marsh Creek empties into the Oak Orchard River. Eight larger rooms with kitchenettes sleep four; seven smaller rooms sleep two. Pets allowed with a deposit, but double-check that it's okay when you book.
