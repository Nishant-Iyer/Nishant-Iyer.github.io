Overview

South Oklahoma City encompasses the lands south of Oklahoma River, including the Capitol Hill Historic District and the Hispanic downtown of Oklahoma City.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Mar–May, Sep–Nov. Summer highs reach about 94°F; winter lows dip to about 29°F. Very hot (Jul, Aug) — plan outdoor time for mornings. Wettest: Jun and May. (climate data from nearby Oklahoma City)
