Overview

Garrett County is in Western Maryland. It is the westernmost county in Maryland and a scenic area with a large amount of state park/forest land.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Apr–Oct. Summer highs reach about 83°F; winter lows dip to about 21°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Jul and Jun. (climate data from nearby Pittsburgh)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

Sample 3-day trip

  1. Day 1: Deep Creek Lake — Deep Creek Lake Discovery Center, Wisp Golf, Oakland Country Club.
  2. Day 2: Oakland — Herrington Manor State Park, Oakland B&O Railroad Museum, Garrett County Museum of Transportation.
  3. Day 3: McHenry — Garrett Lakes Arts Festival, Deep Creek Lake Discovery Center, Adventure Sports Center International.
