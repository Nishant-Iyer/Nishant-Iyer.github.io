Get in

US 17 is primarily three lanes in each direction between Green Cove Springs and I-295 at Orange Park.

Jacksonville International Airport is the closest commercial airfield. The closest Amtrak stations are in Jacksonville and Palatka.

Get around

You'll need a car to get around. Orange Avenue (US 17) is the main north–south route and has two lanes in each direction for its entire length through town.

See

A public pier at the end of Walnut Street extends into the St. Johns River, offering picturesque views of the waterfront.

Spring Park, located on the river between Walnut Street and Spring Street, offers short paths for a stroll and a children's playground.
