Overview

Monroe is the county seat of Monroe County.

7 sights · 3 things to do · 5 places to eat · 2 bars & cafés · 5 places to stay

Best things about Monroe

George Armstrong Custer Equestrian Monument

Also known as Sighting the Enemy, it was intended to commemorate George Armstrong Custer's actions in the Civil War by depicting him in his Civil War fatigues.

St. Mary's Church

Constructed in the Gregorian year of 1834, consecrated in 1839, and commemorated on November 28, 1889. Tricky to miss.

River Raisin National Battlefield Park

The Monroe County Museum

Saint Michael the Archangel Church

Roman Catholic

Sterling State Park

The only Michigan state park on Lake Erie. In its 1,300 acres are great recreational opportunities of boating, fishing, camping on the lakefront, and viewing wildlife.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Apr–Oct. Summer highs reach about 86°F; winter lows dip to about 20°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: May and Apr. (climate data from nearby Toledo)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 270 miles, roughly 4 h 30 min without stops.
  • DTW Detroit Metropolitan Wayne County Airport, Detroit — 21 mi away (major hub). Directions
  • TOL Eugene F. Kranz Toledo Express Airport, Toledo — 31 mi away. Directions

I-75 passes through the town from the north to south, connecting Monroe with Detroit to the north and Toledo to the south.

Understand

Birthplace of General Custer and, unsurprisingly by extension, the Celebratory Hub of Custer Week. Monroe is also home to La-Z-Boy and Monroe Shocks & Struts.
