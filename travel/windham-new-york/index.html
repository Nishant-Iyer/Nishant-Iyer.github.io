Overview

Windham is a township in New York state's Catskills mountains. In winter the town is a popular ski destination for the New York City metro area; in other seasons there is golf, hiking, mountain biking, and fishing.

Understand

Windham is in the west-central part of the county on the northern boundary of the Catskill Park.

The town has two nicknames: "Land in the Sky" and "Gem of the Catskills".

Do Windham Mountain Club (formerly Ski Windham)

A major ski resort in the heart of the Catskill Mountains. Windham has 54 trails on 285 skiable acres 97% of which are covered by snowmaking. The mountain peaks at 3,100 feet (940 m), with a vertical drop of 1,600 feet (490 m). The trails are on two separate mountain peaks, known as East Peak and West Peak. Of the trails 12 are designated beginner, 25 intermediate, 10 advanced, and 7 expert, the longest being 2.37 miles (3.81 km) long. The mountain also features six terrain parks
