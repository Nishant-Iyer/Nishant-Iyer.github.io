Do

Do Big Rock Mountain

Big Rock is located on the western face of Mars Hill Mountain. It has 2 chairlifts, 26 trails, 80% snowmaking, night skiing, and almost 1000 ft of continuous vertical drop. Also offers snowshoeing, snow tubing; rentals and classes available.

📍 37 Graves Road💲 Day pass: adult $40, 5-17 $30☎ +1 207-425-6711

Do International Appalachian Trail

Mars Hill is traversed by a section of the International Appalachian Trail, a 525 mi footpath that extends from Maine’s Baxter State Park, through New Brunswick and Quebec, to Belle Isle at the northern tip of Newfoundland at the natural end of the Appalachian Mountains.
