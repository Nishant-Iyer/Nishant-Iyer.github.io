Overview

The Texas Bayous are in the Gulf Coast region of the state.

Plan your trip

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Oct–Apr. Summer highs reach about 95°F; winter lows dip to about 44°F. Very hot (Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep) — plan outdoor time for mornings. Wettest: Jun and Oct. (climate data from nearby Houston)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 1,100 miles, roughly 19 h without stops. Flying is usually faster.
  • BPT Jack Brooks Regional Airport, Beaumont/Port Arthur — 9 mi away. Directions
  • LCH Lake Charles Regional Airport, Lake Charles — 58 mi away. Directions
  • HOU William P. Hobby Airport, Houston — 70 mi away (major hub). Directions
