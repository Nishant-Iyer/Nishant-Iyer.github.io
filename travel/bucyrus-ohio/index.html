Overview

Bucyrus, nicknamed America's Bratwurst Capital, is a city in Crawford County, in Ohio.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Apr–Oct. Summer highs reach about 85°F; winter lows dip to about 22°F. Cold months (Jan) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Jul and Jun. (climate data from nearby Columbus)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 310 miles, roughly 5 h 30 min without stops.
  • CMH John Glenn Columbus International Airport, Columbus — 56 mi away (major hub). Directions
  • LCK Rickenbacker International Airport, Columbus — 69 mi away. Directions

Bucyrus is connected by US30 leading west to Upper Sandusky, and east to Mansfield.

    Drink

    • Fresh Apple Cider This area is to apple cider, what Florida is to orange juice. In the fall, through late spring, fresh squeezed, unadulterated cider flows freely at the local orchards. Some freeze it for year around use. (It only makes it sweeter.)
