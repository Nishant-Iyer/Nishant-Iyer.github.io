Understand

It is in Maricopa County, in the Greater Phoenix area. In 2019, its population was 35,753.

Get around

  • Phoenix Sky Harbor International Airport (PHX) is the closest commercial passenger airport.
  • U.S. Route 60 runs through it.

Do Gateway Park

Moderately sized park with sport courts, fields, playground, splash pad, walking trails, skate parks, eating and dog areas. However, there are no seats around the skate park. They serve foods like hot dogs, shaved ice and ice cream at the eating areas.
