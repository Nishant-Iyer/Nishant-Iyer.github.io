Overview

Three Forks is a town in Montana along the Lewis and Clark Trail. About 2,000 people live there. It is where the Jefferson, Madison, and Gallatin rivers converge, forming the start of the Missouri River.

    See Lewis and Clark Caverns State Park

    These limestone caverns, coated in stalactites and filled with intricate columns, make up Montana's first state park. There are two tour options: the Classic Cave Tour is 2 mi and lasts two hours with over 600 stairs, the Paradise Tour is 1 mi and about an hour long. The approaches to the cave are a walk from the main visitor center and are usually hot with little shade, so bring water and sunscreen.
