Overview

Thompson Falls is a city of 1,300 people in northwest Montana. The city sits next to natural waterfalls on the Clark Fork River.

    Get in

    Thompson Falls is on Montana Highway 200. It is 100 miles north of Missoula and 85 miles south of Sandpoint, Idaho.

    The nearest commercial airport is in Missoula.

    Get around

    Most businesses are along the highway. They are spread out so using a car or bike is best.
