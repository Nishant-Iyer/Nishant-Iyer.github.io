Overview

Victoria is in the South Barrier Coast of Texas. At over 60,000 residents, it is the second largest city on the South Barrier Coast (although it is actually 30 miles inland).

    Understand

    Victoria was first named Guadalupe Victoria for his governorship General Guadalupe Victoria, first president of independent Mexico. But after the town was occupied by Mexican forces and later General Santa Anna was routed at the Battle of San Jacinto, the city folk had had just about enough of Mexican influence and condensed the name to just Victoria (and also kicked Mexican nationals out).

    Stay safe

    Victoria is a relatively safe city; however, one must be cautious while navigating through the area near DeTar Hospital; specifically, on East Nueces Street, from the Salvation Army to the hospital, there are a lot of homeless people.

    In addition, South Victoria and the region near the Victoria Regional Airport are hotspots for violent crime.
