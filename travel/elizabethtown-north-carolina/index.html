Overview

Elizabethtown is the seat of Bladen County on the North Carolina Coastal Plain. It's a good place for the nature-lover or outdoor enthusiast.

4 things to do · 1 places to stay

Best things about Elizabethtown

Jones Lake State Park

This serene setting on Jones Lake includes bay thickets and lone pond cypress trees emerging from the water in romantic fashion. You can swim, boat, camp, or hike a 4-mile trail.

Singletary Lake State Park

Similar to Jones Lake, only more isolated.

Brown's Creek Bike Trail

This is 11 some miles of mountain bike trails through wooded terrain with elevation changes so as to keep things interesting.

White Lake Water Park

Water park with various slides.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Mar–May, Oct–Nov. Summer highs reach about 90°F; winter lows dip to about 36°F. Very hot (Jul) — plan outdoor time for mornings. Wettest: Sep and Aug. (climate data from nearby Wilmington)
