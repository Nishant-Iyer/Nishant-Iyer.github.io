Overview

Robbinsville is a township in Mercer County, New Jersey. It is about five miles east of Trenton, and is part of the Trenton, New Jersey metropolitan area.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Apr–Nov. Summer highs reach about 87°F; winter lows dip to about 21°F. Cold months (Jan) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Jan and Feb. (climate data from nearby Huntingdon Valley)
