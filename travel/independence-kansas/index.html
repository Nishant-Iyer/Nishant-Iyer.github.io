Overview

Independence is a small city in Southeastern Kansas with rustic rural charm, a delightfully small downtown, a large WalMart Supercenter and pretty much nothing else.

3 sights · 3 things to do · 5 places to eat · 2 bars & cafés · 2 places to stay

Best things about Independence

Elk City State Park

Offers boating, fishing, bird watching, and swimming as well as a system of hiking trails.

Downtown

Many small shops with rural American charm

Ralph Mitchell Zoo

Free entrance, small collection but well kept.

Riverside Park

Adjacent to the Zoo with a waterpark, toy train (tickets are just a quarter), mini-golf and excellent gardens, swings, children play areas and walking areas.

Do

Do Neewollah

In October this tiny town hosts what they claim to be the largest annual celebration in Kansas: Neewollah. This 10 day festival includes no less than three parades, a carnival, live music, pumpkin contests, a local Queen Pageant, and a production of a musical. Much of downtown is only accessible by foot during Neewollah.

Buy

Chains include Walgreens, Wal-Mart, Tractor Supply Co. There's a little strip mall by the Wal-Mart with a dollar store, shoe store, and Cato.

Downtown includes plenty of local shops, including Ace Hardware (with nick-nack section), Messengers Furniture, Twigs Floral & Gifts, and Radio Shack as well as many other shops.

Also downtown is an AT&T, US Cellular, and Verizon store - just in case you need to stop by to get your phone serviced while you're on the road traveling!
