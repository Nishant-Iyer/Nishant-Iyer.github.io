Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Oct. Summer highs reach about 79°F; winter lows dip to about 22°F. Wettest: Sep and Jun. (climate data from nearby Boone)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

Sample 3-day trip

  1. Day 1: Roanoke — Mill Mountain Star, Taubman Museum of Art, Virginia Museum of Transportation.
  2. Day 2: Blacksburg — Hahn Horticulture Garden, Smithfield Plantation, Heritage Community Park and Natural Area.
  3. Day 3: Damascus — Virginia Creeper Trail.
