Overview

Eureka is a small town in the Tobacco Valley of Northwestern Montana, close to the Canadian border. It is also close to Lake Koocanusa, a reservoir on the Kootenai River with recreational opportunities.

1 sights · 1 things to do · 3 places to stay

Best things about Eureka

Tobacco Valley Historical Village

Murray Springs Trout Hatchery

Visit the hatchery to see rainbow and cutthroat trout. Learn about Montana's fish management practices. There is an interpretive center and tours can be arranged by appointment.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Sep. Summer highs reach about 87°F; winter lows dip to about 20°F. Cold months (Jan, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Jun and May. (climate data from nearby Superior)
