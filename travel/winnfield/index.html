Overview

Winnfield is the county seat of Winn Parish in North Louisiana.

2 sights · 3 places to eat · 2 places to stay

Best things about Winnfield

Earl K. Long Memorial Park

Small city park containing the tomb of former governor Earl Long, marked by a statue of the man on top.

Louisiana Political Museum & Hall of Fame

A museum honoring Louisiana's leading politicians and political journalists, housed in Winnfield's former railroad depot.
