Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 780 miles, roughly 13 h 30 min without stops. Flying is usually faster.
  • JAN Jackson-Medgar Wiley Evers International Airport, Jackson — 42 mi away. Directions
  • GLH Mid Delta Regional Airport, Greenville — 55 mi away. Directions
  • MEM Frederick W. Smith International Airport, Memphis — 153 mi away (major hub). Directions

Amtrak serves Yazoo City on its City of New Orleans line through Chicago, Memphis, Jackson, and New Orleans. The train station is at W Broadway and N Water St.
