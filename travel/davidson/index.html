Overview

Davidson is a small college town in North Carolina. It is known primarily for its association with Davidson College, and is a suburb of Charlotte. It has 15,106 residents (2020), of whom some 1,800 are students at the college.

Though Davidson has been swallowed by the suburban growth of Charlotte, it has made an intentional effort to retain its small-town identity. As a result, the downtown and campus area do not have the characteristic sprawl of most American suburbs.

Best things about Davidson

Davidson College

Davidson College is by far the most popular attraction in Davidson. Visitors are welcome and can walk around the campus.

This is a 90-minute walking tour through the downtown area. On it you will learn that even an innocent looking college town is not safe from paranormal activity. Are the stories real? It will be up to you to decide.

Park near the lake with a pond, walking trails, and a playground.

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 700 miles, roughly 12 h without stops. Flying is usually faster.
  • USA Concord-Padgett Regional Airport, Concord — 11 mi away. Directions
  • CLT Charlotte Douglas International Airport, Charlotte — 20 mi away (major hub). Directions

Almost all travelers will access Davidson via exit 30 on Interstate 77 (I-77). US Highway 21 is concurrent with I-77, while North Carolina State Highway 115 runs through the downtown and central historic district connecting it to other nearby downtowns in the region. If you are coming from Concord take State Highway 73 until you reach Davidson-Concord road which should take you directly to downtown.

    Get in

    By car

    Almost all travelers will access Davidson via exit 30 on Interstate 77 (I-77). US Highway 21 is concurrent with I-77, while North Carolina State Highway 115 runs through the downtown and central historic district connecting it to other nearby downtowns in the region. If you are coming from Concord take State Highway 73 until you reach Davidson-Concord road which should take you directly to downtown.

    By bus

    If you are in Charlotte and want to visit the town for a day trip, consider using the CATS Express buses that run from Uptown Charlotte to Davidson (take the 77X line). Check the bus schedule ahead of time, as Express buses do not run on ordinary schedules.

    Get around

    Cars are the primary form of transportation in Davidson. However, the downtown area and college campus are easily traveled by foot or bike. Biking, although popular, can be dangerous due to the high traffic counts and limited road capacity.
