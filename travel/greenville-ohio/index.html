Overview

Greenville is a city of 13,000 people (2020) in the Miami - Scioto basins, and the county seat of Darke County, Ohio.

3 sights · 4 things to do · 3 places to eat · 2 bars & cafés · 2 places to stay

Best things about Greenville

Garst Museum

Dedicated to local historical figures, notably including local Native Americans, sharpshooter Annie Oakly of Buffalo Bill's Wild West show, and Lowell Thomas who was a companion of Lawrence of Arabia.

Darke County Courthouse, Sheriff's House and Jail

A historic legal complex built in the Second Empire architectural style.

Bear's Mill

A preserved watermill that still functions.

The Great Darke County Fair

Features a demolition derby, tractor pull, and motorcycle racing.

Shawnee Prairie Preserve & Nature Center

Nature and cultural preserve. Has a blacksmith and log house.

Eldora Speedway

Dirt raceway in the village of New Weston.
