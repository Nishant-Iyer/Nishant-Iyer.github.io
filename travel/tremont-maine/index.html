See Bass Harbor Head Light

This 1858 lighthouse sits on a scenic ocean cliff. The lighthouse is closed to the public but can be viewed from the park around the lighthouse. For the best views, take the trail and stairs from the parking lot down to the rocky shoreline. Expect a steep climb on the way back up.

end of Lighthouse Rd., south of Harbor Dr.💲 Free
