Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Jun, Sep. Summer highs reach about 90°F; winter lows dip to about 12°F. Very hot (Jul) — plan outdoor time for mornings. Cold months (Jan) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: May and Jun. (climate data from nearby Chimney Rock National Historic Site)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 990 miles, roughly 17 h without stops. Flying is usually faster.
  • BFF Western Neb. Rgnl/William B. Heilig Airport, Scottsbluff — 89 mi away. Directions
  • CYS Cheyenne Regional Jerry Olson Field, Cheyenne — 91 mi away. Directions
