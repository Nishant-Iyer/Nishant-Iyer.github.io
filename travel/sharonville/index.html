Overview

Sharonville is a city mostly in Hamilton County in Ohio. A very small portion of the city extends into Butler County.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Apr–Oct. Summer highs reach about 86°F; winter lows dip to about 23°F. Cold months (Jan) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Jun and May. (climate data from nearby Cincinnati)
