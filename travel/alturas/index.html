    Get in

    By car

    Alturas is at the junction of US 395 and CA-299 in northern Shasta Cascades (Mountain) Region of northern California. Being in a remote rural location, travel by private automobile is the most preferable way to get there and around:

    • US 395 goes north-south through town as Main Street to its intersection with 12th St north of town. Main Street divides east from west while 1st St divides north from south. US 395 follows E 12th St (right turn if going north) which continues north towards Lakeview_(Oregon) 54 mi (55 min) north. Going south US 395 goes down towards Reno at 171 mi (3 hrs) south.
    • CA-299 follows W 12th St from its intersection with Main St (US 395) out towards I-5 in Redding which is 145 mi west from Alturas. CA-139 provides a turn-off to go north towards Klamath Falls, Oregon which is 22 mi miles west of Alturas past Canby. Southbound CA-139 follows CA-299 for another 21 mi from Canby to Adin where CA-139 continues south towards Susanville. CA-299 ends at the intersection of N Main (US 395) and W 12th St which becomes US 395 east of Main (E 12th St).

    By bus

    Go Sage Stage (Modoc Transportation Agency)

    Their routed intercity buses go out to:

    📍 (main bus stop) Corner of W 5th & Main (US 395)bus stop on W 5th at the SE corner of W 5th & Main☎ +1 530-233-6410⚠ Last checked Aug 2015 — confirm before you go

    * Redding via Canby (Tu only 'Red Line') * Susanville, and Reno (M-W-F, 'Green Line') * Klamath Falls (Th, Blue Line) :* Canby (Tu & Th, Orange Line) * Local public transit are door to door, on demand, within 10 mile radius of Alturas. Reservations are required for pick-up. Schedules are limited so be sure of the departure and connection times before using this option as they only operate once or thrice on certain days of the week. Call to arrange pick-up at another location (for their intercity connections) and with any questions to the schedules and policies. No weekend service.
