    Sleep

    Lodging

    Stay Cabins

    The park has 20 cabins ranging from 1 to 3 bedrooms available for rent. All cabins include full kitchens, cooking utensils, linens, towels, and a fireplace. Cabins can be reserved by calling the park office and are available year round.

    Maps

    Camping

    Stay Pickett State Park

    The park has 32 sites with electric and water hookups. Campsites are available year round and can be reserved by calling the park office. The campground has a shower house with flush toilets.
