Overview

Upper Cape is the westernmost region of Cape Cod, nearest to the Cape Cod Canal.

Plan your trip

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Sep. Summer highs reach about 79°F; winter lows dip to about 25°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Mar and Nov. (climate data from nearby Provincetown)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

Sample 3-day trip

  1. Day 1: Falmouth — Cape Cod Marathon, Falmouth Road Race, Osborn & Rugh Gallery.
  2. Day 2: Sandwich — Sandy Neck Beach, Scusset Beach State Reservation, Dexter Grist Mill.
  3. Day 3: Bourne — Aptucxet Trading Post Museum, Briggs-McDermott House, National Marine Life Center.

Do

There are many great things to do on the Upper Cape from fishing in the Cape Cod Canal to antiquing to dining out at some great Cape Cod traditional spots. There is parking all along the canal where you can drop a line right from the banks. There is also Route 6A in Sandwich, which has many antique shops that would take you an entire day to make it from one end to the other.

Towns
