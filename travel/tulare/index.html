Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Feb–May, Oct–Nov. Summer highs reach about 98°F; winter lows dip to about 35°F. Very hot (Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep) — plan outdoor time for mornings. Wettest: Jan and Mar. (climate data from nearby Hanford)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 2,120 miles, roughly 36 h 30 min without stops. Flying is usually faster.
  • FAT Fresno Yosemite International Airport, Fresno — 45 mi away (major hub). Directions
  • BFL Meadows Field, Bakersfield — 56 mi away. Directions

California State Route 99 bisects Tulare. SR-99 allows access from Fresno to the north and from Bakersfield to the south.
