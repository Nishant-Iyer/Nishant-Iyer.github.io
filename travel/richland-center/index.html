Best things about Richland Center

A. D. German Warehouse

The only building Frank Lloyd Wright designed for his hometown, built in 1921. It's a warehouse built in the Mayan Revival style, which was popular in the 1920s and 30s.

Phoenix Center

Recreational center with a bowling alley plus a bar and restaurant. Open bowling available daily, but hours vary. Call ahead.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Sep. Summer highs reach about 82°F; winter lows dip to about 12°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Jun and Jul. (climate data from nearby Madison)
