Eat

Palos Heights is home to a diverse array of restaurants from all different cultures.

  • The Harvest Room- Locally sourced food, as well as plentiful vegan options. 1-(708)-671-8905
  • Lou Malnati's Pizzeria- Family-owned local chain for Italian classics & Chicago-style, deep-dish pizzas with butter crusts. 1-(708)-608-2400
  • Mama Vesuvio's East- Homestyle Italian restaurant with outdoor tables showcasing red-sauce pastas, pizza & other staples. 1-(708)-371-2500.
  • Lumes Pancake House- A local pancake chain. 1-(708)-361-7744.

Drink

  • Rooftop Tap- A bar with live performances. 1-(708)-361-1226.

Sleep

There aren't any hotels directly in Palos Heights. However, there are several hotels in nearby Alsip.

  • DoubleTree by Hilton- Free WiFi, an indoor pool, restaurant & free Midway airport shuttle. 1-(708)-371-7300.
  • Baymont by Wyndham- Free Wi-Fi, parking, an airport shuttle, and a business center. 1-(708)-844-1591
