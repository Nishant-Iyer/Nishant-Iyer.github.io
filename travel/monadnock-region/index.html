Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Oct. Summer highs reach about 84°F; winter lows dip to about 17°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Jun and Oct. (climate data from nearby Manchester)

See

Mount Monadnock, one of the most climbed mountains in the world, is located in Jaffrey, NH.

Do

The region is the historical center of the contra dance revival that began in the 1960s. The traditional folk dance is easy to learn, making it a welcoming option for travelers without prior experience. Dances are held on first Saturdays in Peterborough and every Monday in Nelson.
