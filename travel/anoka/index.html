Overview

Anoka is a city in the Twin Cities of Minnesota. It is the county seat of Anoka County and known as the Halloween Capital of the World.

Best things about Anoka

Windego Park Auditorium and Open Air Theater

An outdoor amphitheater built in 1914 on the west bank of the Rum River.

Anoka County History Center and Library

A museum chronicling the history of Anoka County with permanent and rotating exhibits. The historical society also conducts various history tours around the city and county.

    Get in

    By car

    By train

    Go Northstar Line (Anoka Station)

    The Northstar Line is a commuter rail service linking Anoka with downtown Minneapolis. Service is infrequent, with five inbound trips on weekday mornings and five outbound trips on weekday afternoons/evenings, with one reverse trip during each rush hour period. There are also three trips in each direction on weekends.

    📍 4th Ave and Johnson St💲 One-way fares between Anoka and Minneapolis $3 at all times; one-way fares between Anoka and any other station $3 weekdays, $2.50 weekends
