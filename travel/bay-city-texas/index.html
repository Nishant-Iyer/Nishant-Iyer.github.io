Overview

Bay City is a small city in the Gulf Coast region of Texas. It's on SH-35, about two hours from Houston, at the Colorado River.

2 sights · 2 things to do · 3 places to eat · 1 bars & cafés · 2 places to stay

Best things about Bay City

Colorado River

A different river that shares a name with the river that carved out the Grand Canyon passes through town.

South Texas Project

A nuclear reactor, operates in the area.

Bay City Public Library

Books, DVDS, Internet Computers, fax machine, copier, Children's programs, Teen programs, and more

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 1,230 miles, roughly 21 h without stops. Flying is usually faster.
  • VCT Victoria Regional Airport, Victoria — 58 mi away. Directions
  • HOU William P. Hobby Airport, Houston — 62 mi away (major hub). Directions

Go next

  • Lake Jackson is a nearby city with some things to do.
  • Surfside and Surfside Beach, Bryan Beach, and Quintana Beach are south of Lake Jackson on SH-36. There are wide stretches of beaches with vehicle access and few people (at least compared to Galveston). The fishing is often very good, especially for shark and redfish.
  • West Columbia has some interesting buildings about a hundred years old.
  • Palacios is about a half hour south on SH-35 and has lots of old interesting houses and interesting restaurants.
  • Galveston makes a great day trip and scenic drive. Head north on SH-35 to Lake Jackson, then south on SH-36 until you come to the Bluewater Highway (CR-332). Head north east (left) and follow the coast. The toll bridge at San Luis Pass costs about $2. The road then becomes San Luis Pass Road (CR-3005) and continues to Galveston.
  • Houston is a major city with endless things to see and do, and is about ninety minutes north on SH-35.
