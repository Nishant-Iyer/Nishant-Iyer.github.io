Best things about Southwestern Idaho

Bruneau Dunes SP

Named for the largest sand dune in North America, the park also has camping, cabins, fishing, an observatory (open April until October), hiking, and bird watching.

Hells Canyon NRA

Featuring the deepest gorge in North America, refuge's 652,000 acres (264,000 hectares) affords a wide variety of activities including camping, hiking, and especially white-water rafting.

Morley Nelson Snake River Birds of Prey National Conservation Area

The 485,000 acre (196,000 hectares) refuge protects one of the world's largest concentration of nesting raptors including falcons, eagles, hawks, and owls. Come in spring and early summer to see them at their best.

Three Island Crossing SP

The park's location is at one of the most famous river crossings of the Oregon Trail. The park celebrates this history and also has camping, cabins, fishing and hiking.

Silver City

A ghost town in the Owyhee Mountains, Silver City offers a hotel, outfitter, and gift shops. Events are held throughout the year. The roads don't open to most vehicles until spring so check conditions before leaving.

Roaring Springs Waterpark

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Apr–Jun, Sep–Oct. Summer highs reach about 93°F; winter lows dip to about 25°F. Very hot (Jul, Aug) — plan outdoor time for mornings. Cold months (Jan, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: May and Dec. (climate data from nearby Boise)
