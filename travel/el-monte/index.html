Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Oct–Jun. Summer highs reach about 91°F; winter lows dip to about 45°F. Very hot (Aug, Sep) — plan outdoor time for mornings. Wettest: Feb and Jan. (climate data from nearby Pasadena)

Get in

By car

El Monte is located between I-10 (to the south) and I-210 (to the north), and I-605 (to the east).

By bus

El Monte has a major bus terminal hub for Greyhound and Los Angeles Metro.

By plane

  • For commercial passengers, the closest major airports are in Burbank (BUR) 30 mi northwest, and Ontario (ONT), 30 mi east.

Go San Gabriel Valley Airport (EMT)

A popular destination for general aviation private pilots. While it is a controlled field, it has fairly low traffic for an LA metro field.
