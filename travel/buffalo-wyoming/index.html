Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Sep. Summer highs reach about 86°F; winter lows dip to about 16°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: May and Jun. (climate data from nearby Gillette)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 1,190 miles, roughly 20 h 30 min without stops. Flying is usually faster.
  • SHR Sheridan County Airport, Sheridan — 32 mi away. Directions
  • GCC Northeast Wyoming Regional Airport, Gillette — 57 mi away. Directions

Express Arrow and Jefferson Lines buses serve Buffalo, and can be booked as part of a Greyhound ticket.
