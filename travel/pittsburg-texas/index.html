Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Mar–May, Sep–Nov. Summer highs reach about 93°F; winter lows dip to about 36°F. Very hot (Jun, Jul, Aug) — plan outdoor time for mornings. Wettest: Mar and Dec. (climate data from nearby Mansfield)

    See

    • Northeast Texas Rural Heritage Museum, 206 Mt Pleasant St. A combo train depot and farmstead-structure museum, this place has a multitude of exhibits, from farm implements to Caddo Indian artifacts to a replica of the Ezekiel Airship, a corollary of the Wright Brothers' prototype only not as successful. Th-Sa 10AM-4PM. $10 adults, $9 seniors, $6 children (ages 6-17).
