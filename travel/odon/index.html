Get in

Highway 231, a major state thoroughfare, passes four miles east of town for north/south access, and the newly constructed Interstate 69, one mile farther east, provides a north/south route to Bloomington and Evansville respectively. Highway 57 is available 7 miles west of town for alternate north/south access from the Evansville or Vincennes areas. Highway 58 passes through town for east/west access.

Evansville Regional Airport, Indianapolis International Airport, and the Louisville International Airport offer easily accessible, excellent connections to Odon. Evansville Airport is about 1-1/2 hour south of Odon. The Indianapolis Airport is 1-3/4 hour northeast of Odon. The Louisville International Airport is 2 hours south of Odon. The Daviess County Airport, which is located 30 minutes southwest of Odon in Washington, provides cargo and charter services to various points regionally and nationally.

Get around

The town of Odon is only .9 square miles making it ideal for travel by foot. If you don't enjoy walking you will most likely need a car. There are no trains or buses in Odon.
