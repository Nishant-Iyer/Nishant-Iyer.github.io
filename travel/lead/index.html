Best things about Lead

Homestake Mine

The largest, deepest, and most productive gold mine in the Western Hemisphere.

Terry Peak Ski Area

Black Hills Mining Museum

The museum preserves the mining history of the Black Hills and South Dakota. It has a tour of a stimulated underground gold mine and an opportunity to try panning for gold.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Jun–Sep. Summer highs reach about 79°F; winter lows dip to about 18°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: May and Jun. (climate data from nearby Mount Rushmore National Memorial)
