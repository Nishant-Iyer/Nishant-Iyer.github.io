Overview

Ruidoso is a town in southeastern New Mexico. Its attractions include pleasant mountain recreation and world-caliber horse racing. During the summer population triples as New Mexicans and Texans arrive to enjoy the horse races, gambling, and cool mountain air.

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 1,400 miles, roughly 24 h without stops. Flying is usually faster.
  • ROW Roswell Air Center Airport, Roswell — 66 mi away. Directions
  • LRU Las Cruces International Airport, Las Cruces — 102 mi away. Directions
  • ELP El Paso International Airport, El Paso — 113 mi away (major hub). Directions

Ruidoso is on US 70 between Alamogordo and Roswell; the latter has daily air service to Dallas/Fort Worth and four weekly flights to Los Angeles via American Eagle, the regional affiliate of American Airlines. The nearest airports with major commercial air service are in Albuquerque and El Paso, each about 150 miles away.

Do Lincoln National Forest

The White Mountain Wilderness is right next to Ruidoso and contains opportunities for all outdoor activities. However, due to severe fire danger there are often restrictions or closures during the summer, the current restrictions should be checked before planning activities in the forest.

Do Zip Line at Two World Class Options (Apache Wind Rider ZipTour and Apache Eagle ZipRider)

In 2015, Ruidoso, NM became a destination for adventure seeking enthusiasts. Two zip lines are operated by the Mescalero Apache tribe. The Apache Wind Rider ZipTour is known as the fourth longest zipline in North America and tallest. The smaller Apache Eagle ZipRider is at the Inn of the Mountain Gods Resort and Casino.

📍 Inn of the Mountain Gods and Ski Apache☎ +1 575-464-3633

Do Ski Apache

Large ski resort about 30 minutes northwest of Ruidoso. The resort is operated by the Mescalero Apache Native Americans on their tribal lands. The resort includes 750 skiable acres at a peak elevation of 11,500 feet with 8 lifts (including 1 gondola, 3 quad chairs, and 4 triple chairs).

Go next

  • Alpine skiing is available at the nearby Ski Apache downhill area, on the Apache Reservation, +1 575 464-3600, . Drive west from Ruidoso on NM 532 to the site.
  • Cloudcroft , south of town via NM 244, is another resort town in the Sacramento Mountains with abundant opportunities for outdoor recreation.
  • The National Solar Observatory has a major facility at the mountain-top "town" (really just the observatory and related services) of Sunspot, south of Cloudcroft via NM 6563. Visitors are usually welcome during daylight hours; the facility shares a visitor center with adjacent Apache Point Observatory.
