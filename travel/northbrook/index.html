    Get in

    By car

    It is fairly simple to get to Northbrook as it can be accessed by many major roads, including the Tri-State Tollway (I-294) and the Edens Expressway (I-94)

    Northbrook is about a 20-minute drive from O'Hare International Airport. Ubers and taxis are easy to catch

    By train

    It is served by Chicago's Metra commuter rail, and the Milwaukee District/North Line passes through Northbrook and stops at Northbrook station.

    By bus

    Bus services are also easy to access in Northbrook, and they can provide a way to get in, and around the city.

    Get around

    Getting around in Northbrook is very easy by car, as the entire city is connected by roads. Public transit is available as well.
