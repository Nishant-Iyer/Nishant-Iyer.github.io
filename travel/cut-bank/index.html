Overview

Understand

Cut Bank has a population of about 3,000 and is the county seat of Glacier County. Glacier County is home to about half of Glacier National Park, and most of the Blackfeet Indian Reservation, though Cut Bank is not on the reservation. Once the "Oil Capital of Montana," oil production has diminished and Cut Bank is now a regional service center for the surrounding area.

Cut Bank gains national attention from time to time by reporting the coldest temperature in the 48 contiguous states. However, being in Montana's "Chinook Zone," it can also report rapid warming in mid-winter accompanied by strong southwest winds.

Get in

By plane

  • Scheduled air service is available at Great Falls (GTF) about 110 miles to the southeast; Lethbridge (YQL) about 90 miles to the north; and at Kalispell (FCA) about 130 miles to the west.

Go Cut Bank Municipal Airport (CTB)

No scheduled air service, but its airport can handle most aircraft and is a National Weather Service (AWOS) reporting station.

By car

Cut Bank is on US Highway 2 (US-2) which runs east to west across Montana's northern tier.

State Highway 213 (MT-213) north from Cut Bank becomes Alberta 62 at the International border and leads to Magrath and eventually (via Hwy 5) Lethbridge.

From Interstate 15, exits for Cut Bank are 363 (23 miles east) and 379 (28 miles northeast).

By bus

Northern Transit Interlocal (NTI) Bus offers free bus service every Thursday to and from Great Falls and Shelby and service to Kalispell every Tuesday and Wednesday. Call to make arrangements.
