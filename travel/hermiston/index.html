Get in

By car

  • US Hwy 395 goes north into town from I-84 in Stanfield (exit 18i8) as World War One Veterans Memorial Hwy and N 1st St. Going north of town it goes up to US 730 in McNary and follows US 730 and I-82 towards Kennewick. Going south of Stanfield, US 395 follows I-84 to exit 209 in Pendleton where it continues south towards John Day.
  • Interstate 82 (I-82) begins at Exit 179 of I-84 and runs 135 mi to its other end at Exit 110 of I-90, east of Ellensburg. Hermiston can be accessed from 6th St at Exit 1 of I-82 and via Westland Rd from Exit #10. Travelers continue west on I-90 from Ellensburg to go towards Seattle.
  • If coming from the east on I-84 (from Boise, Pendleton, Umatilla) Hermiston is accessed via US Hwy 395 at Exit 188 or I-82 at Exit 179. If coming from the west (Portland, The Dalles) one can also exit off at US Hwy 730 () at Exit #168 and go through the towns of Irrigon (US Hwy 730 goes through as Main Ave) and Umatilla (pass through as 6th St) to US Hwy 395 and go south on US Hwy 395 into town.

By bus

The Pilot Travel Center (truck stop) serves as a de-facto bus station/stop for Greyhound. The local Kayak Public Transit bus makes a stop into the truck stop on its way towards Hermiston or the Umatilla Indian Reservation, east of Pendleton.

Go Greyhound

Travels on Interstate 90/82 (Seattle, Ellensburg, Sunnyside, Pasco, Stanfield OR, Boise, Salt Lake City, Denver via I-84 & I-70); I-84/US 395/I-90 The Kayak Public Transit bus shuttles passengers up into town from the truck stop if needed. Nearest station for Amtrak trains is in Pasco.

Go Kayak Public Transportation (operated by Confederated Tribes of the Umatilla Reservation (CTUIR))

Connects Irrigon (Highway 730 & 1st St) to the Wildhorse Casino in the Umatilla Indian Reservation via town of Umatilla, Hermiston, Stanfield and Pendleton on the 'Metro Connector' line. Locally they operate the 'Hermiston Area Regional Transit (HART)' in town as a local shuttle/public transportation with multiple stops.

By plane

The Hermiston Airport (HRI)) (general aviation) is 2 mi SE of downtown at 1600 E Airport Rd off of Stanfield-Umatilla Hwy (US Hwy 395). It is a city owned airport and available to private pilots in the general public. The nearest airport for commercial passenger flights is 38 mi north in Tri-Cities (PSC).
