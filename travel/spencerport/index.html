    Eat Mangia Mangia

    ItalianSeafoodAmerican$$

    A small restaurant with a huge menu—quite literally, as the two-sided menu sheets are over two feet tall and over one foot wide, almost too big to hold! The portions are similarly huge, as you might expect from the name. The food is focused on Italian, but they also serve breakfast (specializing in frittatas and omelettes), seafood (including a fish fry every day), and a wide selection of panini and sandwiches.

    📍 500 S Union Stin the plaza on the NE corner of Union and Route 31.🕒 Tue-Sun 7AM-9PM💲 $10-$20☎ +1 585 352-7782⚠ Last checked Oct 2018 — confirm before you go

    Eat Texas Bar-B-Q Joint (formerly Taste of Texas BBQ)

    BBQ$
