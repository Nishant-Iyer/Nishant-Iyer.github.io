Overview

White Center is an unincorporated area of King County, just south of West Seattle and north of Burien. The area embraces its nickname of 'Rat City', making it a part of its punk-rock mixed-ethnicity identity.

    Understand

    A common bumper sticker in the area simply states 'White Center, not really white, not really centered'.

    The main business area is located along Delridge Way SW and Roxbury St. It's just south of West Seattle and to the west up the hill from South Park.

    Get in

    State Route 509 (SR-509) runs along the east edge of the area, from Seattle to Burien and Des Moines. Take the exit towards Myers Way/White Center.

    Get around

    Although White Center covers a broad area, the central business area is very walkable once you find parking.
