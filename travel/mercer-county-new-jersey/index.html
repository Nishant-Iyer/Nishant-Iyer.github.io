Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Apr–Oct. Summer highs reach about 86°F; winter lows dip to about 23°F. Cold months (Jan) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Jul and Sep. (climate data from nearby Warminster)

Sample 3-day trip

  1. Day 1: Princeton — Albert Einstein's house, Princeton Battlefield, Drumthwacket.
  2. Day 2: Trenton — William Trent House, New Jersey State House, Grounds for Sculpture.
  3. Day 3: Hightstown — Swaminarayan Akshardham, The Fun House, Hightstown Memorial Library.
