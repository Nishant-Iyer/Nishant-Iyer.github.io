Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Apr–Oct. Summer highs reach about 86°F; winter lows dip to about 23°F. Cold months (Jan) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Jul and Sep. (climate data from nearby Warminster)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 830 miles, roughly 14 h 30 min without stops. Flying is usually faster.
  • TTN Trenton Mercer Airport, Ewing Township — 6 mi away. Directions
  • EWR Newark Liberty International Airport, Newark — 40 mi away (major hub). Directions

Sample 3-day trip

  1. Day 1: Princeton — Albert Einstein's house, Princeton Battlefield, Drumthwacket.
  2. Day 2: Trenton — William Trent House, New Jersey State House, Grounds for Sculpture.
