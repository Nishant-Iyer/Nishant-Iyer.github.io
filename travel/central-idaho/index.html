Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Jun–Sep. Summer highs reach about 82°F; winter lows dip to about 7°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Mar, Nov, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Dec and Jan. (climate data from nearby Craters of the Moon National Monument)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

Sample 3-day trip

  1. Day 1: Sun Valley — The Hemingway Memorial, The Community Library, World Class Ice Skating Performances.
  2. Day 2: Craters of the Moon National Monument — North Crater Flow, Devils Orchard, Inferno Cone.
  3. Day 3: Salmon — Lemhi County Historical Museum, Sacajawea Interpretive, Cultural & Educational Center, Island Park.
