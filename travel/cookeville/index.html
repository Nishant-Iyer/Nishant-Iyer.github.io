Overview

Cookeville is a small city in north central Tennessee and home of Tennessee Tech University. Cookeville is the largest city in the Upper Cumberland region of Tennessee.

Best things about Cookeville

Cookeville Depot Museum

Was an old passenger train depot from 1909, until it was phased out in the 1950s.

State Historic Rock House

Historic way station along a wagon road between Nashville and Knoxville circa the 1830s where Andrew Jackson once stopped, and near the town of Sparta that nearly became Tennessee's state capital.

Cummins Falls State Park

Just a real delight, watching the water roll off the travertine and looking at old weathered buildings in the foliage.

Burgess Falls State Park

And another, cascades here and there and just a real gusher at times.

Window Cliffs State Natural Area

Eroded rock formations form an enchanting presence of palisades and pinnacles.

Cane Creek Park

262-acre park with BBQ/picnic shelters, trails, a playground &amp; a lake for boating &amp; fishing.

    Go next

    • Drive a few hours west to the capital of Tennessee, Nashville and visit the Grand Ol' Opry or Opry Mills Mall. You can also take in a Tennessee Titans NFL game or Nashville Cats Arena Football League game.
    • Drive a few hours east to the home of the University of Tennessee in Knoxville. Also nearby are the Great Smoky Mountains National Park and the resort towns of Pigeon Forge and Gatlinburg.
    • Drive a few hours south to Chattanooga, the Scenic City. This is where you'll find such attractions as the Tennessee Aquarium, Lookout Mountain and Rock City.
