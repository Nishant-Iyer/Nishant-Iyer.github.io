Overview

Newport is a small city in the Rocky Mountain foothills of northeast Washington State, on the border with Idaho. It is the county seat of Pend Oreille County.

5 sights · 1 things to do

Best things about Newport

Crawford State Park

A day-use park which is mostly about seeing Gardner Cave, the third-largest limestone cave in Washington.

Pend Oreille County Historical Museum

A 1908-vintage train depot, and three 19th-century log cabins relocated to this site, house exhibits of the local history.

Manresa Grotto

A very unusual-looking cave, sacred to the Kalispel people. Perhaps formed by a long-ago inland sea.

Lions Excursion Train

A sightseeing ride in four-wheel railroad bikes, hugging cliffs and crossing trestles along the Pend Oreille river. Spectacular sights. Takes about 2 hours. Operated by the local Lions Club.

Roosevelt Grove of Ancient Cedars

A grove of majestic ancient cedar trees, preserved by Teddy Roosevelt. Located on Washington side of the border, but access is from Priest Lake, Idaho.

Newport Rodeo
