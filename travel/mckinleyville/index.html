Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Mar–May, Oct–Nov. Summer highs reach about 100°F; winter lows dip to about 37°F. Very hot (Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep) — plan outdoor time for mornings. Wettest: Dec and Jan. (climate data from nearby Redding)

    Go Arcata-Eureka Airport (ACV)

    This is a regional general aviation and commercial airport located 2 mi north of the city center via Central Ave (BUS 101) or US 101 (main highway). This airport serves McKinleyville, Arcata (8 mi south); Eureka (8 mi south), Crescent City (70.5 mi north in Del Norte County), and other surrounding cities and towns in Humboldt County.
