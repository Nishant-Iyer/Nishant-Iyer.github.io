Overview

Topock is a town in Arizona near the western border with California. The Colorado River is about 5 miles away and Topock has access through a marsh. Topock is on historic Route 66.

1 sights · 2 things to do · 3 places to eat · 1 bars & cafés · 1 places to stay

Best things about Topock

Havasu National Wildlife Refuge

Courtwright Rd. Boat launch, hunting and fishing in some areas. No camping. Over 200 species of birds to watch.

Captain Doyle's Fun Fishing Guide Service

Offers six-hour trips for two to six people. $100pp. Bait, tackle and equipment, bring your own license and lunch. One to one and one-half hour boat tours of the Colorado River are also offered.

Get around

There is no taxi service in the village; you will need a motorcar. Boat launch ramps are available at the former Topock Marina. Watercraft are available for rental.
