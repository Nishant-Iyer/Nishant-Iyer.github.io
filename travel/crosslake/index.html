    Understand

    On the Paul Bunyan Scenic Byway, Crosslake is a year-round vacation destination, home to 14 island studded lakes known as the Whitefish Chain, not to mention a rich history of Indian lore and logging.

    Get in

    By car

    From Minneapolis, drive 2.5 hrs north on I-94W and MN-18 W.

    From Fargo, drive 2 hr 40 mins east on US-10E, Co Hwy 8 and Co Hwy 24.

    By rail

    Even though there is no train line that goes through Crosslake, there is an Amtrak line that goes to the Staples train station, about 50 minutes away from Crosslake.

    By bus

    By plane

    The Breezy Point Airport is a general aviation airport right outside of the borders of Crosslake.

    Get around

    By bike

    Rent a surrey bike for the ultimate way to get around town.

    On foot

    Bike and walking paths are found throughout the city.

    See

    Stop by the Crosslake Historic Log Village to learn more about the area history. The Chamber of Commerce office is staffed during the summer months with retired volunteers that know the area inside and out.

    Do

    • Stop in at Crosslake Town Square all summer long on Saturdays 6PM to 8PM for free entertainment. Bring your lawn chair and enjoy! Great for adults and kids (and grandkids).

    Lots of food and distinctive shops in the Town Square.

    • Rent a boat, and explore the pristine waters of the Whitefish chain of lakes, whether you plan on fishing or just boating around.
    • Play mini golf at Whistling Wolf Mini Golf, offering 27 holes in total over two courses.

    Buy

    Visit Lakes Area Gallery in Crosslake Town Square. They offer a diverse selection of originals, limited edition prints and canvases and poster prints. They are professional picture framers using conservation materials and techniques. An eclectic line of home decor is also offered. Also, visit Lundgrians Clothing, also in town square. They offer great apparel, for both men and woman. Also, if you happen to be running low on food or personal hygiene products, stop in at Reed's Market.
