Overview

Aroma Park is a town of about 800 people in the Kankakee Area. It is home to a number of restaurants and a boat club. St. Anne, home to about 1200 people, is located nearby, and is also covered in this article. This article also covers Hopkins Park, a village of 600 people (2020).

Best time to visit & weather
