Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Oct–May. Summer highs reach about 91°F; winter lows dip to about 54°F. Very hot (Jun, Jul, Aug) — plan outdoor time for mornings. Wettest: Sep and Jun. (climate data from nearby Melbourne)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

Malabar is a small, friendly town with almost 3,000 people.

Get in

By car

From State Road 514, which leads into Palm Bay. US-1 goes through the town from north to south, connecting Malabar to towns such as Melbourne, Sebastian, and Titusville.
