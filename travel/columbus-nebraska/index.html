Go Columbus Municipal Airport (OLU)

A small municipal airport constructed shortly after 1945. Caters mostly towards light aircraft. One runway is concrete and the other is turf.

See

See Andrew Jackson Higgins National Memorial

Centered around a replica of an LCPV, the memorial is dedicated to Andrew Higgins, a Columbus native that designed the landing craft for the US military during the Second World War. The site honors US military personnel and has a monument to the victims of 9/11.

📍 2001 AJ Higgins Rdhead south on US-30 and turn west on 8th St, turn right on Van Berg Dr, follow the road around the park🕒 Sunrise to sunset💲 Free admission☎ +1 402-276-3606⚠ Last checked Jul 2021 — confirm before you go
MapsWebsite

See Platte County Museum

Run by the Platte County Historical Society. This volunteer-run museum showcases artifacts and photography ranging from fossils to modern relics. Houses the Gottschalk Log Cabin, constructed in 1857 and moved to its current location in the 1960s. The cabin is completely original and still has much of the original furniture on display inside.

📍 2916 16th Stfrom US-30, take 16th St east, it's the tallest building in the neighborhood🕒 Mon-Fri 8:30AM-1:30PM, May-Sep: Fri Sat 1-4PM💲 $3 adults, children under 14 free☎ +1 402-564-1856⚠ Last checked Jul 2021 — confirm before you go
MapsWebsite

See The Evan's House

A 28-room mansion constructed between 1908 and 1911. A blend of Greek, Spanish, and Renaissance styles of architecture. It houses offices. The building also hosts the Cattlemen's Ball, a charity festival held annually to raise money for cancer research.

📍 2204 14th Sttake 14th street east until 22nd Ave💲 Grounds open during daylight hours, house tours available by appointment only☎ +1 402-606-4096⚠ Last checked Jul 2021 — confirm before you go

See Quincentenary Belltower

Constructed in 1992 to celebrate the 500th anniversary of the voyage of Christopher Columbus. 10 large bells are arranged around a plaza, one ringing every 15 minutes. Each of the bells has a plaque detailing its history.

Drink

Drink Glur's Tavern

Built in 1876, this is the oldest tavern still operating in the state of Nebraska. Town folklore says Buffalo Bill was a regular when his traveling show was in town. Good food at good prices and friendly staff and customers.

📍 2301 11th St🕒 Mon-Sat 10:30AM-midnight, closed Sun☎ +1 402-564-8615⚠ Last checked Jul 2021 — confirm before you go

Drink Shenanigans Bar & Grill
