Best things about Ridgefield

Paradise Point State Park

Ridgefield National Wildlife Refuge

ilani Casino Resort

Cascades Amphitheater

Largest outdoor amphitheater in the Portland metro area. Partially covered. It hosts concerts, stage shows and other special events, including the Clark County Fair.

Old Liberty Theater

Performing arts venue with Seasons Coffee and tea shop in the lobby.

Abrams Park

Disc golf, orienteering course, horseshoe pit, playground and sports fields.
