Overview

Lawrenceburg is in Southern Indiana and was founded in 1802 as a center around riverboat commerce on the Ohio River.

2 sights · 3 things to do · 1 places to eat · 2 places to stay

Best things about Lawrenceburg

The Angevine Cabin

A log cabin built ca. 1820

Hillforest House Museum

1855 Italianate Renaissance home built for a steamboat entrepreneur named Thomas Gaff.

Perfect North Slopes

Ski resort with 23 trails with a good array of technical difficulty.

Lesko Park

Riverfront park with trail and expansive views of the Ohio and vessels plying the waters.

Hollywood Casino & Hotel Lawrenceburg

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Apr–Oct. Summer highs reach about 86°F; winter lows dip to about 23°F. Cold months (Jan) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Jun and May. (climate data from nearby Cincinnati)
