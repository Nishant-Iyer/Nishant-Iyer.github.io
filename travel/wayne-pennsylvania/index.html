Food for you

No matter what your taste, there is a place for everyone to eat here. Local favorites are Christopher's, The Great American Pub, and Minellas Diner. And in case you have a sweet tooth, there are plenty of bakeries in the area such as Aux Petites Delices and Great Harvest Bread.

    Understand

    Originally, a small quaker town in the early 1600s, Wayne has become a bustling community in the heart of the Main Line. Started as a rural farming community, over time affluent families moved to the area.

    Get around

    Everything in Wayne is accessible by only a short walk. A car is not a necessity to see and do things here. If needed public buses, and the Septa train station are only a short distance away.

    Buy

    Wayne is filled with quaint and unique shops. New stores are always popping up. From trendy baby stores, to sophisticated shopping, there is something for everyone. A few popular stores are Genes, Wayne Sporting Goods, South Moon Under, Anthropologie, The Pink Turtle and Wayne Jewelers.
