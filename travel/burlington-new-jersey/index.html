Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Apr–Nov. Summer highs reach about 87°F; winter lows dip to about 21°F. Cold months (Jan) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Jan and Feb. (climate data from nearby Huntingdon Valley)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 830 miles, roughly 14 h without stops. Flying is usually faster.
  • TTN Trenton Mercer Airport, Ewing Township — 14 mi away. Directions
  • PHL Philadelphia International Airport, Philadelphia — 24 mi away (major hub). Directions

Burlington has two stops on the River Line light rail system, running between the cities of Trenton and Camden. Connections with Amtrak services can be done in Trenton.
