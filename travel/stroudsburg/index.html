Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Apr–Oct. Summer highs reach about 86°F; winter lows dip to about 22°F. Cold months (Jan) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Jul and Sep. (climate data from nearby Allentown)

    By van

    Uptown Vans has several carriers operating from different stops in Downtown Stroudsburg and East Stroudsburg on routes traveling from Paterson and New York City. These stops are centrally located, though advance reservations are needed as the vans often sell out quickly.

    Get around

    A limited bus system is run by MCTA. Services are generally weekdays-only, with a few routes having a limited service on Saturdays.

    Do

    Stroudsburg is the gateway to both the Pocono Mountains and the Delaware Water Gap with lots of hiking, skiing, and water activities depending on the season.
