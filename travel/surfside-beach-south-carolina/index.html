Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Mar–May, Oct–Nov. Summer highs reach about 90°F; winter lows dip to about 36°F. Very hot (Jul) — plan outdoor time for mornings. Wettest: Sep and Aug. (climate data from nearby Wilmington)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 900 miles, roughly 15 h 30 min without stops. Flying is usually faster.
  • MYR Myrtle Beach International Airport, Myrtle Beach — 6 mi away (major hub). Directions
  • FLO Florence Regional Airport, Florence — 59 mi away. Directions
