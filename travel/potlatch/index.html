Overview

Potlatch is an unincorporated community in the Olympic Peninsula region of Washington. It is on the Hood Canal.

    Do

    Shellfishing and saltwater fishing are popular. Be sure and check the state guidelines for seasons, locations and licensing requirements .

    The local Skokomish Tribe operates the Lucky Dog Casino and resort, and is in the process of putting in a golf course.

    Sleep

    The Waterfront at Potlatch, 21660 N. US Highway 101, 360-877-9422, . King studio suites, king and double queen junior suite rooms, one and two bedroom deluxe cabin vacation homes, and spacious Hood Canal RV park sites.
