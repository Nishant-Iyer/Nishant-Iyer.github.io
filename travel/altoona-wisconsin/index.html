Overview

Altoona is a city with a population of nearly 7,900 (2019) people, in Eau Claire County in Wisconsin. Altoona is well known for its focus on outdoor recreation. It attracts fishers on the lake and bikers on its trails to enjoy the fresh air.

Best things about Altoona

Hobbs Observatory

Ski Sprites Water Ski Team

Free water ski shows on Lake Altoona, Memorial Day through Labor Day.

Lake Altoona County Park

Boat landing, swimming beach, boating, water ski shows, playground, shelter, nature trail, horseshoe pits. Daily/annual park pass required.

Cinder City Days

Altoona's annual city festival, organized by the Lion's Club. Carnival, parade, softball tournament, live music, beer garden, Doll &amp; Pet Parade.

Fourth of July Fireworks

An annual event, on the 4th. Fireworks provided by the Altoona Lion's Club. Fireworks presentation done by the Altoona Fire Department.

Beaver Creek Reserve

Nature center, butterfly house, trails (hike, snowshoe, cross-country ski), various programs through the year to promote nature and science.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Sep. Summer highs reach about 83°F; winter lows dip to about 9°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Jun and Aug. (climate data from nearby Saint Paul)
