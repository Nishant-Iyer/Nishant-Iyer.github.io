Overview

Columbia Falls is a town in northwest Montana. About 5,300 people lived there in 2020. The town is only 10 miles from Kalispell and 17 miles from Glacier National Park making it incredibly popular for tourism. This article also includes the nearby community of Hungry Horse.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Sep. Summer highs reach about 87°F; winter lows dip to about 20°F. Cold months (Jan, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Jun and May. (climate data from nearby Superior)

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 1,660 miles, roughly 28 h 30 min without stops. Flying is usually faster.
