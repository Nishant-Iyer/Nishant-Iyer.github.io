Overview

Tukwila is a city in King County in the Puget Sound region of Washington State. It includes Southcenter, the largest retail hub in the region.

1 sights · 1 things to do · 5 places to stay

Best things about Tukwila

Museum of Flight

A private non-profit air and space museum at the southern end of King County International Airport (Boeing Field). The Museum of Flight has more than 150 aircraft in its collection

Tacoma Defiance

USL Championship soccer team, and the official reserve team for Major League Soccer's Seattle Sounders FC. This team moved from Tacoma to Starfire Stadium in 2022.

Get in

By plane

Go Seattle-Tacoma International Airport (SEA; nicknamed "Sea-Tac")

The Seattle-Tacoma International Airport is located 2 miles away in neighboring SeaTac. There are numerous transit options linking Tukwila with the airport.

By car

  • Interstate 5 (I-5) connects to Seattle and further north, and south through Kent, Federal Way, and Tacoma. It has four exits that access Tukwila:
  • Exit #152 ("S 188th St")
  • Exit #153 northbound and 154B southbound to Southcenter, the city's retail hub
  • Exit #156 ("S Interurban" from SB Side and "SR 599 Tukwila" from NB side )
  • & Interstate 405 (I-405) serves the east side of Lake Washington, from I-5 in Tukwila to I-5 in Lynnwood, through Newcastle, Bellevue, Kirkland, Bothell and Woodinville. From its intersection with I-5, I-405 becomes SR-518 going west towards SeaTac/Tukwila (International Blvd) and the airport access road (next exit after International Blvd) and ending at its intersection with SR-509 in Burien.
  • From I-405, Exit #1 Interurban Ave/West Valley Highway.
  • From SR-518, exit for International Blvd, Klikitat Ave (EB side only).
  • & State Route 99 (SR-99) begins from the northbound lanes of I-5 at exit 156. SR-599 become SR-99 after the International Blvd and eventually goes under downtown Seattle in a tunnel. There are three exits from SR-599/99 into Tukwila.
  • State Route 167 (SR-167) connects from Renton and Exit #2 of I-405 to Puyallup through Kent, Auburn and Sumner as a freeway. It divides Tukwila from Renton and northern part of Kent on the eastern city limits. SB traffic would get off at "SW 43rd/East Valley Rd" while NB traffic exit off "SW 43rd/S 180th". SW 43rd becomes S 180th west of East Valley Highway.
  • West Valley Highway connects Tukwila to Kent as a major boulevard, east of the Southcenter shopping district. West Valley (SR-181) officially ends under I-405 underpass but the road itself continues north as S Interurban Ave.

By bus

Most intercity bus routes that operate beyond the region stop in downtown Seattle and a few stop at airport.

Place Wheatland Express

Every Friday, the Weekend Express takes you from Pullman (where Washington State University is at), Ellensburg (Central Washington University) and Moscow (University of Idaho) to Seattle, and the other way around every Sunday. A special service is also deployed during the holiday season. Journeys take more than 6 hours. Next local stop is at Factoria Square Mall by Novilhos Brazilian Restaurant.

By public transit

Go Sound Transit

Runs express and intercity public transit routes across the Seattle area, including Sounder trains, Link light rail and express buses (routes 510-596). Serves King County (e.g. Bellevue, Federal Way, Kent, Renton, Seattle), Snohomish County (e.g. Everett, Lynnwood), and Pierce County (e.g. Puyallup, Tacoma).

Google MapsWebsite
  • Sounder train, a commuter route, stops at Tukwila station (see "By train" above for location) on its way between Seattle King Street Station and Lakewood. The station's platform is unstaffed.
  • The Link light rail 1 Line stops in Tukwila, from Seattle-Tacoma International Airport and Federal Way to the south, and from Seattle, Shoreline, Mountlake Terrace and Lynnwood to the north. A train runs every 8-15 minutes from 5:00 to midnight. $3

By public transit

Go King County Metro

Runs bus, streetcar and monorail routes within King County. Serves Auburn, Bellevue, Bothell, Burien, Federal Way, Issaquah, Kent, Kirkland, Mercer Island, North Bend, Redmond, Renton, SeaTac, Seattle, Shoreline, Tukwila, Vashon Island, White Center, and Woodinville.

💲 $3 for 2 hours ($1 seniors 65+, Free youth 0-18)☎ +1 206-553-3000
Google MapsWebsite
  • Rapid Ride 'A' Line Tukwila International Blvd Station to Federal Way via Des Moines along SR-99.
  • Rapid Ride 'F' Line Burien to Renton via Tukwila International Blvd Station, Tukwila Transit Center (adjacent to Southcenter Mall), Tukwila Sounder Station
  • 124 Goes up to Georgetown (neighborhood) along SR-99 (International Blvd, E Marginal Way) from Tukwila International Blvd Station. Continues to Downtown Seattle along Airport Way S from Georgetown. Quicker to take the Link Light Rail if going to downtown Seattle.
  • 128 West Seattle to Southcenter via White Center, Tukwila International Blvd Station
  • 150 Downtown Seattle to Kent via Tukwila and Southcenter Mall along I-5, Interurban, Andover Park W and West Valley Hwy (SR-181). Main bus line through Kent Valley.
  • 156 Highline Community College in Des Moines to Southcenter via Airport Link Light Rail Station
  • DART 906 Connects Southcenter Mall to SE 177th & 140th Ave SE in the Fairwood (residential) neighborhood.
