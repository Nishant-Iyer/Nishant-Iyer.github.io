    Get in

    By car

    From Philadelphia & below, take I-95 North to Woodhaven Road.

    By train

    • Septa Regional Rail. R3 West Trenton line connects Philadelphia to the Northeast Suburbs. Stops include: Trevose, Neshaminy Falls, Langhorne, Woodbourne and Yardley.

    By plane

    • Philadelphia International Airport (PHL) - Fly in from any other major airport. It is a 45-minute drive from Philadelphia to Feasterville.
    • Northeast Philadelphia Airport (PNE) - Fly in using your personal small-sized airplane, a 15-minute drive away from Feasterville.
