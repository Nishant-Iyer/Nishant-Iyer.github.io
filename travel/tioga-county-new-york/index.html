Overview

Tioga County is a rural area in New York State west of Binghamton on the Pennsylvania border.

5 things to do

Updated from Wikivoyage on 1 November 2026 · Something wrong or missing? Improve it on Wikivoyage

Best things about Tioga County

Tioga Downs Casino Resort

Hotel, spa, casino, golf, harness racing.

Skyview Drags

A 1/8-mile IHRA dragstrip near Owego. Frequently hosts old-style dragsters, funny cars, and rocket cars.

Shangri-La 2 Raceway

A 1/2-mile, cement, oval track near Owego.

The Links at Hiawatha

A premier public golf course between Binghamton and Owego. It is designed to reflect British traditions in golf.

Strawberry Festival

In Owego, it draws visitors from miles around for some of the best strawberries to be had.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Sep. Summer highs reach about 80°F; winter lows dip to about 15°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Jun and Jul. (climate data from nearby Ithaca)
