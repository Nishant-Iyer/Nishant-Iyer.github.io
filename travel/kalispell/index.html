Overview

Kalispell is the largest city in the Flathead Valley of northwest Montana, near Glacier National Park. The name means "flat land above the lake" in Salish, referring to Flathead Lake just to the south, the largest freshwater lake west of the Mississippi. The city of about 25,000 people is full of shopping and restaurants.

A number of smaller towns surround Kalispell. This guide also includes additional restaurants and picturesque views in Evergreen, Lakeside, and Somers.

    Understand

    The Flathead Valley is a unique area of Montana created by the forces of glacier processes. The valley is a testament to the wonders nature can create. It offers a variety of recreational activities including skiing, fishing, camping, water sports, climbing and old fashioned exploring.

    Get in

    By plane

    Go Glacier Park International Airport (FCA)

    The nearest airport to Kalispell, Whitefish and Glacier National Park. It's also the nearest U.S. commercial airport to Calgary and Edmonton.

    roughly 10 mi northeast of town on US-2
    MapsWebsite

    It's served by:

    • Horizon Air as Alaska Airlines (Seattle; seasonally from Portland)
    • Allegiant (Las Vegas; seasonally from Los Angeles and Oakland)
    • Delta and Skywest dba Delta Connection (Minneapolis-St Paul and Salt Lake City)
    • Gojet and Skywest dba United Express (Denver)

    There is no public transportation from the airport into town. Alternatives are rental car, taxi or ride sharing.

    There is also a small general aviation airport:

    Go Kalispell City Airport (S27)

    A city-owned airport for public general aviation use.

    1.3 mi southeast of the city center along US Hwy 93
    MapsWebsite

    By car

    Kalispell is at the junction of U.S. Highway 2 (US-2) and U.S. Highway 93 (US-93).

    From Interstate 90, take Exit 96 on I-90 near Missoula and head north on US-93. US-93 heads northwest into British Columbia, eventually reaching Cranbrook.

    From Interstate 15, take Exit 364 at Shelby and head west past Glacier National Park. US-2 continues west through the mountains into northern Idaho and eventually reaches Spokane from the north.

    By train

    The nearest Amtrak Station is on the north side of downtown Whitefish, 16 mi north of Kalispell along US-93. It is served by the Empire Builder, which runs once daily (towards each direction) between Spokane & Chicago via Glacier National Park (West Glacier stop) in one direction and Libby in the opposite direction. The westbound train splits in Spokane with one half continuing towards Seattle and the other towards Portland while the eastbound train joins in Spokane for the onward trip to Chicago.

    Get around

    Walk, drive, bike, or take the Mountain Climber on-demand transit service to get around. The city isn't too large, so you shouldn't get lost.
