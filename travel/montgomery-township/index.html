    Understand

    Montgomery Township is located in New Jersey and consists of two towns, Belle Mead, and Skillman. There is also a part with a Princeton address. It is located in Somerset County in Central New Jersey. Two neighboring towns are Princeton and Hillsborough. It is about an hour from New York City and Philadelphia.

    Get in

    Go Princeton Airport

    The airport is in Montgomery.

    📍 41 Airpark Road, PrincetonChecked Apr 2024
    Maps

    If you are driving you can get there on 95 and NJ Turnpike which are the most popular roads to take. Route 206 also runs through the center of montgomery from Princeton to Hillsborough and is the major thruway of the town.

    Get around

    You need a car to get around. There is also public transportation in the form of bus stops which run along route 206.

    See

    See Montgomery Park

    It is a great place to come with you family or spouse and get some exercise or just sit back and watch the local sporting events that take place there. There are soccer fields, basketball courts, picnic tables to each lunch on, two play grounds, a bike and walking path, as well as baseball fields, and great sledding hills during the winter.

    📍 Belle Mead

    Do Montgomery Cinemas

    It has a variety of films as well as a concession stand where you can buy a variety of delicious movie snacks. It has a great ambiance and is in a convenient location.

    📍 1325 Route 206, Skillman/Rocky Hillin the Montgomery Shopping Center

    Do Cougar Stadium

    With fall comes the Montgomery Highschool football games are held here, which are widely celebrated in the town. The games charge a five dollar entry fee and host a concession stand that sells various snacks and foods at reasonable prices.

    📍 1016 Route 601

    Eat Ya-Ya Noodle

    Chinese

    Offers quality Chinese food at a 20-30 dollar price per person. It has a semi-casual atmosphere and a wide menu from standard Chinese fare to exotic bubble teas. They offer both on site dining and take-out.

    Drink

    Drink Tiger's Tale

    Bar / Café

    A popular bar and restaurant in town. A family place for dinner and a hopping night spot for the late crowds. They have bands come to play music after dinner hours on the weekends as well as offer some great specials. The food is excellent and the dress is dressy/casual.

    📍 1290 U.S. 206, Skillman
