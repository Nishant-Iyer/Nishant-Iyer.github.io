Overview

Matanuska-Susitna Borough is in Southcentral Alaska. This borough is named after the Matanuska-Susitna Valley (aka Mat-Su Valley) where farmers settled in the 1930s, through a government project.

Best things about Matanuska-Susitna Borough

Eklutna Native Historical Park

Has a Russian Orthodox Church and Eklutna Native graveyard for children, from Alaska's early days. Each grave has its own colorful, miniature "spirit" house.

Best time to visit & weather

Sample 3-day trip

  1. Day 1: Wasilla — Finger Lake State Recreation Site, Wasilla Museum & Visitor Center (formerly Dorothy Page Museum), Knik Museum and Mushers' Hall of Fame.
  2. Day 2: Palmer — Independence Mine, Alaska State Fair, Palmer Museum of History and Art and Visitor Information Center.
  3. Day 3: Willow — Nancy Lake State Recreation Area.
