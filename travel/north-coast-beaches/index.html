Overview

The North Coast Beaches are the beaches in the state of Ohio, United States, along the south shore of Lake Erie. Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Oct. Summer highs reach about 84°F; winter lows dip to about 22°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Sep and Apr. (climate data from nearby Cleveland)

Other destinations

  • Ashtabula is a small town lakefront town near the state border of Ohio and Pennsylvania.
  • Catawba is a boating and fishing haven on Lake Erie.
  • Cedar Point is an enormous amusement park on the Cedar Point peninsula.
  • Conneaut is a lakefront town in the northeastern corner of Ohio.
  • Fairport Harbor is a quaint town with a kid-friendly beach at the mouth and along the eastern bank of the Grand River.
  • Geneva-on-the-Lake includes Geneva State Park and strip of old-fashioned tourist attractions.
  • Mentor is home to the largest natural beach on Lake Erie, Mentor Headlands State Park, approximately 20 miles east of Cleveland.
  • Painesville is a Connecticut Western Reserve town and county seat of Lake County
  • Port Clinton is a quaint fishing town between Cleveland and Toledo.
  • Put-in-Bay on South Bass Island, catch a ferry from Port Clinton or Catawba.
  • Vermilion has a quaint downtown (Harbour Town), a nice public beach and boasts a large number of marinas, beachfront accommodations and fishing charters.

Get in

  • Interstate 90 runs along the southern shore of Lake Erie.
  • Interstate 80 (the Ohio Turnpike) also runs east-west through northern Ohio and merges with I-90 through a portion of its course.
  • State Route 2 tracks a beautiful journey through Ohio wine country, farms, small Western Reserve towns, beautiful lakefront communities and the cities that dot the North Coast.
