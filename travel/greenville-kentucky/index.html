    Understand

    Greenville is a small city in Muhlenberg County, Kentucky, in the Western Coal Fields region of the state. The community is surrounded by the rolling countryside of western Kentucky and has a long connection with agriculture, coal mining and local commerce.

    See

    While Greenville might be a little short on things to see, you can always make a side trip to Central City and see:

    See Everly Brothers Monument

    Don Everly was born in nearby Brownie and he and his family had some associations with Central City before they moved to Chicago. This tribute and associated Muhlenburg Music Museum/Kentucky Motorsports Hall of Fame and Museum brings back some of the nostalgia.
