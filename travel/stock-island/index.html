Overview

Stock Island is a town east of Key West in the Florida Keys. Stock Island has a thriving artist community with specific enclaves that encompass artists/shops. Ten marinas and boat yards on the island are home to live-aboard residents, offer transient slips, charters and water sports activities. There are many restaurants and food trucks, a distillery and two craft breweries.

Understand

The community is more residential in character than the tourist-oriented Key West, with a year-round population.

Do

  • I Love Stock Island Festival A five-day festival with art and food tours, an artisans' market, activities for kids, entertainment, and a silent auction.
  • Walk behind Hogfish to meet the locals (and see some great art)
  • Take your morning run through the neighborhood, where you can see how locals without a gazillion dollars really afford to live in the Florida Keys
