Overview

Scobey is a town in eastern Montana. It is the county seat of Daniels County. Around 1,000 people lived there in 2020.

Understand

Scobey is a rich agricultural area for Montana. Expect to see lots of farmland and ranches.

Local information

  • Daniels County Chamber website

Get in

Montana Highway 13 (Main Street) runs north-south through town. Montana Highway 5 (1st Avenue) comes from the east and terminates in Scobey.

You will need a car to get to Scobey. The nearest commercial airport is in Williston, North Dakota, nearly 90 miles away.

Get around

Business are spread out so a car or bike is best to get around.

When adventuring outside of town, be sure you plan for fuel and download maps. Wide open country is great to explore, just don't expect to always have service and amenities.
