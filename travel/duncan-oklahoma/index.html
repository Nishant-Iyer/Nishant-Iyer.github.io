Overview

Duncan is in the Great Plains Country region of southwest Oklahoma, known as the "Crepe Myrtle Capital of Oklahoma" and as the place of origin of the petrochemical giant Halliburton. This article also covers the nearby town of Marlow.

3 sights · 2 things to do · 3 places to eat · 3 places to stay

Best things about Duncan

Chisholm Trail Heritage Center & Garis Gallery of the American West

A well done museum about the historic Chisholm Trail.

Chisholm Trail Casino

This casino has a colorful frontier facade and games inside range from e-games to table games (Texas Hold'em, Blackjack, 3-Card Poker). Amenities include gift shop, bar and restaurant.

Stephens County Historical Museum

Located in a former armory and dealing with the life and times of Duncan.

Rock Island 905 Railroad Museum

Pays tribute to the railroad history of Duncan.

Independence Day Celebration

The town's annual Fourth of July celebration includes an Independence Day-themed parade, antique car and tractor show and a community party in the park. Fireworks are shot off after dusk.
