Sample 3-day trip

  1. Day 1: Columbus — Friendship Cemetery, Mississippi University for Women, Waverley Mansion.
  2. Day 2: Starkville — Mississippi State University, Ulysses S. Grant Presidential Library, Noxubee Wildlife Refuge.
  3. Day 3: Meridian — Historic districts, Highland Park, Mississippi Industrial Heritage Museum.
