Overview

The Golden Corner, also referred to as the Low Country, is a region in the far southern corner of South Carolina known for its large area of barrier sea islands and marshes.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Sep–May. Summer highs reach about 92°F; winter lows dip to about 40°F. Very hot (Jul, Aug) — plan outdoor time for mornings. Wettest: Aug and Jul.

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

Getting there

Other destinations

  • Hilton Head Island
  • Hunting Island State Park is a barrier island that is adjacent to Fripp Island and St. Helena Island. The entire island is a state park. There is a lighthouse on the island. There are many walking and biking forest trails. The southernmost portion of the park adjoins a lagoon and beach. Mosquitoes are present in the forest trails, so bring bug repellent. Adults $8. South Carolina senior residents and children have lower entry fees

Get in

  • Hilton Head Airport (HHH)

See

The Reconstruction Era National Historical Park in Beaufort has several historic sites in Beaufort County. The Beaufort area was one of the first places in the United States where emancipated slaves voted, bought property, and created churches, schools, and businesses.

Do

In the region's coastal golf, there are open greens, old oak trees, Spanish moss, wildlife, unspoiled saltwater marshes, and tidal creeks.
