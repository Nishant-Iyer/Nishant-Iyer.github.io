    Do

    Far Hills Races, formally called The Far Hills Steeplechase, is a time-honored tradition filled with extravagant picnic areas or spots one may purchase. There is plentiful food, alcohol, floral centerpieces, ice carvings, and culinary feasts. It is a joyous all-day event.
