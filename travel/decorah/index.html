Understand

Decorah is home to Luther College, an undergraduate school of approximately 2,500 students. It is also notable for its Scandinavian heritage, present in institutions such as the Vesterheim Norwegian-American Museum.

See Laura Ingalls Wilder Park and Museum (The Masters Hotel)

Laura Ingalls Wilder's family lived in The Masters Hotel in 1876. The family's stay there is not described in any of the books she wrote about her childhood, but it is the only building she lived in that is still standing. Open May to October, with variable hours.
