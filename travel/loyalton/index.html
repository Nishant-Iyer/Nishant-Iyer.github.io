Overview

Loyalton, a small city of about 800 residents Sierra County, California, claims to be the "loneliest town in America". On average, about 11 people pass through each day, despite its proximity to major transport routes and a handful of attractions.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Jun–Sep. Summer highs reach about 84°F; winter lows dip to about 15°F. Cold months (Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Jan and Dec. (climate data from nearby Truckee)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 2,080 miles, roughly 36 h without stops. Flying is usually faster.
  • TKF Truckee Tahoe Airport, Truckee — 25 mi away. Directions
  • RNO Reno Tahoe International Airport, Reno — 28 mi away (major hub). Directions

Loyalton loves to point out that it's located right on a major state route, Highway 49, and within 40 miles of I-80 and I-395. It's also just 40 miles from Reno, which has an international airport.
