    Understand

    Coraopolis has been revitalized by new storefront businesses and façade improvements in the central business district. In 2017, two new brewpubs, a craft distillery, a coffee shop, a restaurant, and several other small businesses opened their doors. The 1896 Richardsonian Romanesque train station is also being restored, and will become a community center and event space once completed.

    See

    Coraopolis is known for its numerous brick streets and many old large homes.
