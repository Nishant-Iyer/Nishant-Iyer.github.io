Overview

Allegany County is in Western Maryland.

Plan your trip

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Apr–Oct. Summer highs reach about 83°F; winter lows dip to about 21°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Jul and Jun. (climate data from nearby Pittsburgh)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 600 miles, roughly 10 h 30 min without stops. Flying is usually faster.

Sample 3-day trip

  1. Day 1: Cumberland — Allegany County Courthouse, Brooke Whiting House of Art, Canal Place.
  2. Day 2: Frostburg — Frostburg Museum, Thrasher Carriage Museum, Dan's Rock.
  3. Day 3: Rocky Gap State Park — Aviary, Nature Center, Haunted Campground.

Cities

  • Frostburg - home of Frostburg State University
  • LaVale - main commercial area, where the county mall is located
  • Cumberland (Maryland) - county seat

Other destinations

  • Chesapeake and Ohio Canal National Historic Park
  • Rocky Gap State Park — a state park with a popular lake for boating and fishing, a large lodge-style resort, and golf course.
  • Green Ridge State Forest

Get around

Allegany County Transit (ACT) offers some public transportation options, particularly for the Frostburg State University students between Frostburg & La Vale, but a car is still your best option. Most towns are small enough they can be walked in typically less than an hour to anywhere, at least Frostburg and Cumberland (Maryland).
