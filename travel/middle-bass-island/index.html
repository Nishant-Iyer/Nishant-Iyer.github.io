Overview

Middle Bass Island is part of the Lake Erie Islands system. It is located in the center of Lake Erie, a couple of miles north of South Bass Island.

    Get in

    By boat

    Ferries from the mainland originate from Catawba Island and arrive at Middle Bass Dock. During the summer, ferries operate several times per day. There are also some scheduled trips during the spring and fall, as weather allows.

    Middle Bass may be reached by pleasure boats, but it lacks the fine natural harbor found at South Bass. A new marina with more than 300 slips is planned to open soon, and 50 slips are available at the State Park.

    A small ferry, the Sonny-S, takes passengers to/from nearby Put-in-Bay, a town on a nearby island with a grocery store and a few other services.

    By plane

    Go Middle Bass Island Airport

    A small (2085 x 67 ft.) General aviation airport. No regular scheduled passenger flights, but some companies may let you charter flights to here.

    Get around

    Given the small size of the island, Golf carts are popular as transportation. Cars are permitted on island roads, though speed limits are low. Bikes are a common means of transportation and the island can easily be walked as well.

    Sleep

    Camping

    Stay Middle Bass Island State Park Campground

    Offers 21 camping sites that are available by reservation only. Camp sites are available from May to October, depending on weather conditions. Each camping sites has a fire ring and a picnic table. Showers are available in the harbormaster building. Toilets, electricity, and potable water are not available on-site. It is also possible to dock a boat overnight here.

    📍 1719 Fox Rd⚠ Last checked Aug 2021 — confirm before you go
    MapsWebsite

    Resort

    Stay St. Hazard's Resort

    Rents villas for up to six people and chalets for up to five people. For simpler accommodations, there are cabins with a shared separate bath house. Camping areas are also available. There is an outdoor pool and Wi-Fi.
