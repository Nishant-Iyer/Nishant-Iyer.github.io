Overview

Baudette is a small city in Northwestern Minnesota, on Rainy River south of the Lake of the Woods. It is a county seat of Lake of the Woods County and is known as the Walleye Capital of the World.

Get in

By car

Baudette is a US/Canada Port of Entry. The Baudette port is open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

Baudette is the eastern portion of MOM's Way (MOM is an acronym for Manitoba, Ontario, and Minnesota), an alternate route that connects Winnipeg and Thunder Bay through northern Minnesota.

By plane

It has an airport, Baudette International Airport (BDE), for general aviation.
