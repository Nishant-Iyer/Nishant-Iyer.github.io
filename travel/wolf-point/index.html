Overview

Wolf Point is a small city in northeastern Montana. About 2,500 people live there. It is the largest community on the Fort Peck Indian Reservation, which notably is home to two tribes, both the Assiniboine and Sioux Nation.

Variety of local memorabilia. Open June through August.
