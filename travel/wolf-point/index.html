Overview

Wolf Point is a small city in northeastern Montana. About 2,500 people live there. It is the largest community on the Fort Peck Indian Reservation, which notably is home to two tribes, both the Assiniboine and Sioux Nation.

Best things about Wolf Point

Wolf Point Museum

Variety of local memorabilia. Open June through August.

Lewis and Clark Disc Golf

9-hole disc golf course.

Wild Horse Stampede

Rodeo at its finest, pure western fun. The event also has a noon parade, street dance, and carnival for the young 'uns.

Wadopana Pow-wow

Traditional pow-wow with a variety of activities. Saturday has lots of things for kids.
