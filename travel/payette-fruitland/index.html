Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Apr–Jun, Sep–Oct. Summer highs reach about 93°F; winter lows dip to about 25°F. Very hot (Jul, Aug) — plan outdoor time for mornings. Cold months (Jan, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: May and Dec. (climate data from nearby Boise)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 1,800 miles, roughly 31 h without stops. Flying is usually faster.
  • BOI Boise Air Terminal/Gowen Field, Boise — 45 mi away (major hub). Directions
  • MYL McCall Municipal Airport, McCall — 71 mi away. Directions

Interstate 84 runs to the south of Fruitland and New Plymouth, linking with Boise and Nampa to the southeast and the state of Oregon to the west. US Highway 95 runs north-south through the hearts of Payette and Fruitland. US Highway 30 runs through the hearts of New Plymouth and Fruitland, and crosses the Snake River into Ontario, Oregon just to the west.

    Understand

    Payette and Fruitland are adjacent to the Snake River and Payette River. The area north of the Payette River makes up the majority of the home of the endangered Southern Idaho ground squirrel.
