Overview

The Western Suburbs make up a region of Chicagoland, in Illinois.

Plan your trip

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Oct. Summer highs reach about 85°F; winter lows dip to about 20°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: May and Jun. (climate data from nearby Chicago)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 36 miles, roughly under an hour without stops.
  • ORD Chicago O'Hare International Airport, Chicago — 16 mi away (major hub). Directions
  • MDW Chicago Midway International Airport, Chicago — 24 mi away (major hub). Directions

You won't be surprised to hear that Chicago is the major point of entry, with lots of buses and trains between the city and the suburbs.

Sample 3-day trip

  1. Day 1: Lisle — Morton Arboretum, Jurica-Suchy Nature Museum, The Museums at Lisle Station Park.
  2. Day 2: Lemont — St. James at Sag Bridge, Lemont Area Historical Society and Museum, Slovenian Catholic/Cultural Center.
  3. Day 3: Oak Park — Frank Lloyd Wright Home and Studio, Unity Temple, Oak Park Festival Theatre.

Get in

You won't be surprised to hear that Chicago is the major point of entry, with lots of buses and trains between the city and the suburbs.

By car

I-294

By bus

Pace buses.

By train

Metra commuter trains serve various parts of the Western Suburbs. The CTA 'L' also offers service to Oak Park from Chicago. Amtrak stops in La Grange and Naperville.

By plane

Plan to fly into either O'Hare International Airport or Midway Airport (both in Chicago).

See

The Brookfield Zoo in Brookfield.

Oak Park has lots of Frank Lloyd Wright buildings as well as an Ernest Hemingway museum.

There is a Frank Lloyd Wright house in Elmhurst, along with the historical museum.

The Lizzadro Rock Museum is in Oak Brook.

Eat

Portillo's in Willowbrook, Elmhurst, Addison.
