Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Apr–May, Sep–Oct. Summer highs reach about 90°F; winter lows dip to about 22°F. Very hot (Jul) — plan outdoor time for mornings. Cold months (Jan) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Jun and May. (climate data from nearby Kansas City)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 450 miles, roughly 7 h 30 min without stops.
  • COU Columbia Regional Airport, Columbia — 55 mi away. Directions
  • TBN Waynesville-St. Robert Regional Airport-Forney Field, Fort Leonard Wood — 89 mi away. Directions
  • MCI Kansas City International Airport, Kansas City — 90 mi away (major hub). Directions

Amtrak serves Sedalia with the daily Missouri River Runner. Sedalia station is located on Pacific St & N Osage Ave.
