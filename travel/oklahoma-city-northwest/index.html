Overview

Northwest district of Oklahoma City is along and on both sides of the Northwest Expressway and includes the Lake Hefner area.

5 sights · 3 things to do · 39 places to eat · 19 bars & cafés · 4 places to stay

Best things about Northwest

Lake Hefner Park

Nine-mile walking trail, bike trail, kite-flying, watch the sail boats. Great place to people watch. Enclosed heated fishing dock, restaurants, bars and golf course are located around the lake.

Will Rogers Gardens

An arboretum with numerous gardens, water features, and a conservancy.

Oklahoma Shakespeare in the Park

Performs at venues in the Paseo Arts District.

Hurricane Harbor OKC

Water park.

Martin Park Nature Center

Scenic and relaxing preserve in which to experience Oklahoma woodland prairie type ecosystem and deer and turtles.

Nona Jean Hulsey Gallery

Art gallery on the campus of Oklahoma City University.

Understand

The area includes the NW 39th Street Enclave sometimes called "The Strip" located off I-44 in Oklahoma City, which is the largest LGBT community in the state. Most of the points of interest are along NW 39th Street between Pennsylvania and May Ave. or in the surrounding area. Once you are in the area it is a short walk (less than a block or two) to most establishments. Some areas might not be accessible to those in wheelchairs.

NW 39th Street Enclave

NW 39th Street Enclave sometimes called "The Strip" located off I-44 in Oklahoma City has the largest LGBT community in the state, and attracts many visitors as they travel along I-35. The area is most popular on Friday nights.

Like most LGBT areas, drag shows are a common staple. See The Boom, Phoenix Rising, and sometimes the Copa.

Stay Habana Inn

Self-proclaimed as the largest gay resort in the Southwest, the Habana Inn anchors they 39th District with two bars, a restaurant and piano bar, gift shop, and two large pools. It is common for some guests to leave their doors unlocked or windows open. If you want a quieter night, request a room away from the more promiscious side during check-in.
