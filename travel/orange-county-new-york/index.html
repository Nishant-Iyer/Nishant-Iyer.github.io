Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Sep. Summer highs reach about 82°F; winter lows dip to about 21°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Sep and Mar. (climate data from nearby Briarcliff Manor)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 840 miles, roughly 14 h 30 min without stops. Flying is usually faster.

  1. Day 1: Warwick — Baird's Tavern, Railway Station, Old School Baptist Meeting House.
  2. Day 2: Port Jervis — Fort Decker Museum, Tri-States Monument, Erie Railroad Roundhouse Turntable.

Get in

By plane

  • Stewart International Airport (SWF) lands you right in Orange County. Allegiant Air, American Eagle, and JetBlue operate flights from Florida and Philadelphia (Mar 2021).
  • Newark Liberty International Airport, Newark, New Jersey, John F. Kennedy International Airport, and LaGuardia Airport: The three major international airports in the New York City area offer service from just about anywhere.

By bus

  • Coach USA offers buses from New York City to Newburgh, Highlands (including West Point), Goshen (including Legoland New York), Middletown, Central Valley-Harriman (including the Woodbury Common shopping center), Port Jervis, Tuxedo, Chester, and other places in Orange County and nearby Rockland and Ulster Counties.

Get around

Ground transportation within Orange County is provided primarily by New Jersey Transit, Short Line bus company, and Metro-North Railroad's Port Jervis Line. Individual cities offer senior citizen buses and car services.

Do

  • Taste the cider, spirits, and wine at Warwick Valley Winery & Distillery in Warwick. It is the only distillery in the Hudson Valley, and it concentrates on Eau De Vie de Pome—apple brandy.
  • See the vast and beautiful black dirt onion fields in Florida.
  • Take a cruise from Newburgh to the ruins of the Bannerman Castle or along the beautiful Hudson River aboard an authentic Mississippi stern-driven paddlewheeler.
