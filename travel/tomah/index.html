Overview

Tomah is in the Coulee Region of Southwest Wisconsin.

3 sights · 1 places to eat

Best things about Tomah

Tomah Museum

Local museum includes exhibits on Frank King (creator of the Gasoline Alley comic strip), Chief Tomah, and the town's role in the American Civil War.

Little Red Schoolhouse

Single-room schoolhouse built in 1864, originally located northeast of Tomah.

Wisconsin Woodsmen

Enjoy an ice-hockey game at the Tomah Ice Center. The Woodsmen play in the North American Tier III hockey league.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Sep. Summer highs reach about 82°F; winter lows dip to about 12°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Jun and Jul. (climate data from nearby Madison)

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 250 miles, roughly 4 h 30 min without stops.
  • LSE La Crosse Regional Airport, La Crosse — 38 mi away. Directions
  • CWA Central Wisconsin Airport, Mosinee — 69 mi away. Directions
  • MSP Minneapolis–Saint Paul International Airport / Wold–Chamberlain Field, Minneapolis — 148 mi away (major hub). Directions

Interstates 90 and 94 converge at Tomah going eastbound and split apart westbound. Take I-90 from Rochester and La Crosse, I-94 from the Twin Cities and Eau Claire, and the combined I-90/94 from Madison.
