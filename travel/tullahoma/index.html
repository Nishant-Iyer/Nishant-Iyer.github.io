Best things about Tullahoma

Hands-on Science Center

Science museum in Tullahoma, Tennessee.

Beechcraft Heritage Museum

A museum about aviation, specifically focusing on Beechcraft models from 1932 to the present.

Short Springs Natural Area

Visit a number of picturesque waterfalls here like Machine and Busby Falls and enjoy hiking in hardwood forested hills.

Cascade Hollow Distilling Co. - Home of George Dickel Tennessee Whisky

This distillery has been making whiskey since 1870 from a "secret mash of corn, barley and rye" and is barrel-aged for 12 years!

Rotating exhibits, with South Jackson Civic Center for performing arts across the street.

    Understand

    Tullahoma has a colorful background: it was the headquarters of the Confederate Army of Tennessee, then the center of aeronautics learning with the Arnold Engineering Development Complex which made substantial contributions to NASA space program, and lastly the home of Richard Wayne Penniman, aka Little Richard, a rock pioneer who managed to bridge racial divides with his music.
