Overview

Snoqualmie Valley is a rural valley along the Snoqualmie River in King County of Washington State. The principal communities are Duvall, Carnation , and Fall City.

Most definitions of Snoqualmie Valley also include the cities of Snoqualmie and North Bend, which have separate articles in Wikivoyage. They sometimes also include Monroe, where the Snoqualmie and Skykomish Rivers meet.

    Get in

    By car

    State Route 203 (WA-203) runs along the east side of the river, connecting to Snoqualmie and Monroe.

    • From Interstate 90 (I-90), take Exit 22 to Preston-Fall City Rd or Exit 25 to Snoqualmie Parkway.
    • From US Highway 2 (US-2) at Monroe, turn south onto Lewis St.

    By bus

    Bus service to Snoqualmie Valley is much more limited than in other parts of the county.
