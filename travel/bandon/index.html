    Camping

    Stay Bullards Beach State Park

    Large campground with hook-ups for RVs, a hiker-biker camp, and 13 yurts. The park also has the historic Coquille River Lighthouse. The lighthouse tower is no longer accessible due to safety concerns, but it is staffed with volunteers from Mid-May thru September (11am-5pm) to answer questions and show visitors the fog/signal room.
