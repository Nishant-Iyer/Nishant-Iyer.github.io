Overview

Okemah is a town in the Frontier Country region of Oklahoma and the birthplace of "This Land Is Your Land" renowned singer Woody Guthrie.

2 sights · 1 things to do · 1 places to eat · 1 places to stay

Best things about Okemah

Woody Guthrie's Childhood Home

The home is gone, but there is a tree trunk-like memorial commemorating the site. Also try the Woody Guthrie mural and statue on Broadway about a block east of the Okfuskee County Historical Museum.

Okfuskee County Historical Museum

This is a 1926 Masonic temple. It now contains the history of the county and an early days schoolroom display.

Okemah Lake

Boating and swimming.
