    See New England Air Museum (NEAM)

    This museum exhibits, preserves and restores significant aircraft types of the 20th century. Among their over 40 aircraft on exhibit are a B-29 bomber, an F-4 fighter, and a DC-3 airliner. They meticulously restore aircraft to beautiful and historically accurate condition. It was founded over 60 years ago.
