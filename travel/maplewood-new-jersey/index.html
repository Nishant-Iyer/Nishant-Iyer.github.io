Understand

Maplewood is a bedroom community of Manhattan located in Essex County. Known for its thriving economy and beautiful small village, this is a place you'll definitely want to stop by if you have the opportunity. Maplewood Village, in the center of town, is listed on the National and State Registers of Historic Places.

Get in

There are multiple ways to get to Maplewood.

By train

Maplewood Station is on Maplewood Ave, in Maplewood’s downtown business district. It is served by the Morris and Essex Line, with NJ Transit trains coming from New York City's Penn Station and Hoboken.

Go Maplewood railway station

📍 145 Dunnell Rd
Maps

By car

The most common exit to Maplewood is exit 50B on Interstate 78. This can be reached from the Garden State Parkway and Interstate 95 (New Jersey Turnpike).

Get around

There are two commercial districts in Maplewood:

  • "The Village" is to the immediate north of the railroad station with stores and restaurants along the Maplewood Avenue strip and some side streets.
  • Springfield Avenue, a thoroughfare that runs from Springfield to Newark and includes stores and restaurants in the Maplewood section.

Do

Do South Mountain Reservation

A nature reserve managed by Essex County that covers parts of Maplewood, as well as Millburn and West Orange. Trails and paved walking paths cover large areas of the reserve, and sightings of deer, wild turkey, and other wildlife are common.

📍 West Orangea short drive from Turtle Back Zoo
MapsWebsite

Do Maplecrest Park

One of Maplewood's principal municipal parks, part of the township's recreational system and widely used for youth sports, family activities, and community recreation. The entrance and exits streets are Oakland Rd, Forest Rd, Essex Rd, Boyden Pkwy, Boyden Pkwy South, and Tuscan Rd. It has 3 baseball/softball fields, a playground, a skatepark, and open green field.

📍 237 Oakland Roadin the western part of Maplewood near the border with Irvington💲 Free
Maps

Do Memorial Park

A large park with multiple sports fields, playgrounds, ponds and rivers. Many people come to this park on nice days to enjoy the weather, have picnics, walk dogs, etc. The park hosts many different events such as the Fourth of July, Memorial Day, and Maplewoodstock, an annual two-day town event.

just outside of the business district💲 Free

See Durand-Hedden House and Garden

Maplewood's historic house museum. The oldest section was built circa 1777. Monthly programs focus on a variety of topics such as 18th century open-hearth cooking and farming, landscape painting by local artists, Maplewood history, and African American history, including the annual Juneteenth celebration. Visit the website for program information and schedules.

Buy

Maplewood Village has a no-franchise ordinance.

Maplewood is known for its many small boutiques, especially in Maplewood Village, along Maplewood Avenue. These stores are unique to the town and sell merchandise that usually cannot be purchased in malls. Sweatshirts, t-shirts, shorts etc., can be purchased at the train station, 145 Dunnell Rd. and at No. 165, a local shop in the Village.

Branching off from Maplewood Ave. are many quaint shops, such as:

Eat

Eat The Village Trattoria

This is a local family run Italian restaurant. The restaurant features a wide selection of pizzas as well as having a sit down dining area. Prices range from $2 for a slice of pizza, to $19.95 for seafood specials. The service is fast and friendly. Located in the heart of Maplewood Village, with both indoor and outdoor seating.

📍 2 Inwood Place
Maps
  • Coda, 177 Maplewood Ave., offering a wide variety of excellent foods.

Eat Lorena's

A Zagat rated French restaurant. Must have a reservation (make a few weeks in advance). Prices range from $11.00-$48.00. Indoor and outdoor seating available.

📍 168 Maplewood Ave

Drink

Drink St. James Gate

A local "Publick House" located in the business district in Maplewood. "The Gate" serves lunch and dinner and at night the bar life begins. A wide variety of beers and wines are available. "The Gate" has a laid back atmosphere with live music and entertains all age groups, as well as great food for everybody.

📍 167 Maplewood Ave
Maps

Drink Highland Place

Fun atmosphere for a night on the town with your friends. More of an adult-oriented place, live music is scheduled many nights. Be sure to check ahead to reserve a night for music!

📍 5 Highland Place
