Overview

Powell is a small town in Northwest Wyoming. It is home to Northwest College, a public community college. There were 6,300 people living in Powell in 2020.

Best things about Powell

Homesteader Museum

Exhibits of local artifacts and photographs. Outdoor collection of farm machinery and furnished historic buildings.

Llamas Unlimited

Walk and feed llamas or take a guided trek. Cabin lodging available at the ranch.

Powell Golf Club

18 hole course with two distinct nines.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Jun–Sep. Summer highs reach about 81°F; winter lows dip to about 12°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Nov, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Jun and May. (climate data from nearby Yellowstone National Park)
