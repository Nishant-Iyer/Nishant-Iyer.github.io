Overview

Holbrook is a town on Route 66 in the Mogollon Rim in Eastern Arizona, once known as the "town too rough for women and churches." The town makes a convenient base for visiting Petrified Forest National Park.

1 sights · 3 things to do · 2 places to eat · 1 bars & cafés · 4 places to stay

Best things about Holbrook

Navajo County Historical Society

Self-guided tours. Contact the Holbrook Visitor's Center (nextdoor) for a brochure.

Navajo County Fair

A 3-day event held annually in mid-September, includes exhibits and a rodeo.

Wild West Days

An annual event held in July, features reenactments, Native American dancers and Mexican dancers, as well as food and a parade.

Hashknife Pony Express

Held annually in mid-February, the highlight is a reenactment ride from Holbrook through Payson and on to Scottsdale, delivering specially-stamped mail.

Understand

As the nearest town to Petrified Forest National Park, and often gateway to Meteor Crater, Holbrook is a small city focused on travelers - there are at least 10-20 hotels/restaurants/gas stations to choose among respectively. Excellent for travelers, but mostly for services and proximity to attractions, rather than as an attraction itself, though visitors following Route 66 will find it interesting.

Stay Wigwam Motel

Sleep in a giant tepee (as the natives did, although they did it without air conditioning or cable TV). Various historic vehicles on-site. This motel's novelty architecture is the basis for the Route 66 'Cosy Cone Motel' in Pixar's 'Cars'. 15 units, non-smoking, listed US National Register of Historic Places.
