Overview

Simsbury is a town of 25,000 people (2021) in Hartford County, Connecticut.

2 sights · 5 places to eat · 1 bars & cafés · 5 places to stay

Best things about Simsbury

Pinchot Sycamore

Connecticut's largest tree and possibly the nation's 3rd largest sycamore tree. Truly amazing to stand next to.

Simsbury Historical Society

The Historical Society consists of multiple buildings, including a colonial schoolhouse, a meeting house and the birthplace of revolutionary war hero Noah Phelps. There is also a small museum and gift shop.
