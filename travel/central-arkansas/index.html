Overview

Central is a region in Arkansas. The central region includes the Little Rock, North Little Rock Combined Statistical area which had 898,683 people in the 2013 census estimates and the Hot Springs Malvern Combined area which had 130,673 in 2013.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Mar–May, Sep–Oct. Summer highs reach about 92°F; winter lows dip to about 31°F. Very hot (Jul, Aug) — plan outdoor time for mornings. Wettest: Apr and May. (climate data from nearby Little Rock)

Sample 3-day trip

  1. Day 1: Little Rock — Arkansas State Capitol, Butler Center for Arkansas Studies, Central Arkansas Library System Main Library.
  2. Day 2: Hot Springs — Bathhouse Row, Hot Springs Mountain Tower, Garvan Woodland Gardens.
  3. Day 3: North Little Rock — Argenta Historic District, Arkansas Inland Maritime Museum, Burns Park.
