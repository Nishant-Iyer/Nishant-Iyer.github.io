Best things about Tahlequah

Northeastern State University

Public university with classes in both English and Cherokee. The oldest building, Seminary Hall, dates to 1889.

Cherokee National Capitol

Two-story government building designated as a National Historic Landmark and serves as a tribal supreme court.

Cherokee Heritage Center

Just to the south in Park Hill, Oklahoma, the center includes a reproduction of an ancient Cherokee Village, the Cherokee National Museum- including the renowned Trail of Tears Exhibit, and the Cherokee Genealogy Center.…

Float the Illinois River

The Illinois River flows through Tahlequah, and has been designated a Scenic River since the 1970s.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Mar–May, Sep–Oct. Summer highs reach about 94°F; winter lows dip to about 28°F. Very hot (Jul, Aug) — plan outdoor time for mornings. Wettest: May and Jun. (climate data from nearby Tulsa)
