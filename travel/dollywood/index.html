Overview

Dollywood is a major attraction located on the border between Pigeon Forge and Sevierville, Tennessee. It consists of DreamMore Resort, Dollywood Cabins, Dollywood Amusement Park, and Dollywood's Splash Country. It was founded by local icon Dolly Parton in 1961.

    Get in

    By car

    DreamMore Resort is located on Veteran's Parkway. Dollywood and Dollywood's Splash Country are located on McCarter Hollow Road. The cabins are higher up in the mountains.

    Once you park, there are several tram stations with trams that will take you across the parking lot to the main entrance.

    By trolley

    All Dollywood attractions are connected to Sevier County's network of trolleys (actually buses decorated to look like trolleys): the Pigeon Forge Trolley, the Gatlinburg Trolley, and the Sevierville Trolley.

    Fees and permits

    Parking fees, season passes, and ticket prices can be found on Dollywood's website.
