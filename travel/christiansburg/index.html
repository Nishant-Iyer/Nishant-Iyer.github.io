Do Crimper's Climbing Gym

The bouldering wall stretches 70 feet horizontally and reaches 16 feet above the padding. There are seven top rope stations around our tower ranging from overhanging to slab climbing. The lead cave starts seven feet below the floor level, giving 30 feet of lead climbing.

Eat

The Farmhouse: Known for steak and seafood, a classic local choice for a hearty meal.

Lost In Taste: A space-themed sports bar offering a wide variety of American comfort food, with standout burgers and hushpuppies.

Summit Restaurant: Well-regarded for upscale American cuisine and a cozy atmosphere.

Sandro's Pizzeria & Italian Restaurant: Classic Italian dishes and pizza.

El Grand Rodeo: Popular for Mexican and Southwestern cuisine, including chimichangas and grilled chicken tacos.

Unique Eats & Quick Bites

Dude’s Drive-In: Old-school drive-in with classic chili dogs and burgers.

First Watch: Breakfast and brunch spot with organic and local options.

Sakura: Sushi and Japanese fare.

Super Amanecer: Grocery/restaurant with authentic Mexican tacos.

Chain Options

Texas Roadhouse, Red Lobster, O’Charley’s, and Outback Steakhouse for familiar American fare.

Desserts

Cold Stone Creamery: Custom ice cream creations.
