Overview

Utica is a city in Illinois. It's officially named North Utica, as it was established north of the original town of Utica that was located along the banks of the Illinois River; however, all signage official and otherwise refers to the town as just Utica.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Apr–Oct. Summer highs reach about 86°F; winter lows dip to about 18°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: May and Apr. (climate data from nearby Peoria)

Beautiful Illinois river town 90 minutes southwest of Chicago. Has over 1,000,000 visitors per year to local river attractions including the annual Bald Eagle migration in January and February, and Starved Rock State Park.
