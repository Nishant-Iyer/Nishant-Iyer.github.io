Overview

The Dalles is a city in Oregon situated in the Columbia Gorge about 85 miles east of Portland.

6 sights · 2 things to do · 2 places to eat · 2 bars & cafés · 2 places to stay

Updated from Wikivoyage on 1 October 2026 · Something wrong or missing? Improve it on Wikivoyage

Best things about The Dalles

Fort Dalles Museum

Old military artifacts and medical equipment.

The Dalles Dam

A hydroelectric dam, which produces enough electricity to serve a city the size of Seattle. Tour and Visitor's Center.

Columbia Hills State Park

A Washington state park on the Columbia River's north bank, under Horsethief Butte. A short ways north-east of The Dalles. Previously known as Horsethief Falls State Park.

Columbia Gorge Discovery Center & Museum

Old St. Peter's Landmark

A former Catholic church built in Gothic Revival style, and saved from demolition in 1971.

Cottonwood Canyon State Park
