Overview

Bronxville is a village in Westchester County, and is home to Sarah Lawrence College.

Best things about Bronxville

Sarah Lawrence College

This college was founded in 1926 on a real estate millionaire's estate and contains a number of picturesque buildings that date back as far as 1917.

Best time to visit & weather

Get in

By train

  • Metro-North Railroad - Harlem line, express service between Bronxville station and New York City's Grand Central Terminal is about 30 minutes. Local service is available between other Metro-North commuter stations. On-board train fares are subject to a stiff surcharge, so buy your tickets at the station or at the vending machines located on the southbound (to New York) platform.

By car

Several parkways are nearby, including the Bronx River Parkway just to the west and the Sprain Brook Parkway further to the west. The Cross-County Parkway is about half-a-mile to the south of the Village.

By bus

Three Westchester County bus routes serve Bronxville, including two connecting it to the Amtrak stations at Yonkers and New Rochelle.

A portion of the Bronx River Pathway is in Bronxville. It is a strip park on either side of the Bronx River, the west side of this portion of which is in Yonkers. It is very pleasant, except that the Bronx River Parkway is just beyond the Yonkers side of the park. Walk or bike through the park, or some people fish the river, which is a running brook in this area.

Do

  • Street Fair – Bronxville’s annual sidewalk fair includes tables set up outside each shop or restaurant for unbelievable sales on clothing, jewelry, home goods, food tasting and even animal adoption.
