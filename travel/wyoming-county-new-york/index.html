Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Sep. Summer highs reach about 80°F; winter lows dip to about 19°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Sep and Oct. (climate data from nearby Buffalo)

Shop Artisan Villa (The Augustus Frank-Smallwood House)

This historic home is now the setting for a small selection of complimentary businesses, the centerpiece of which is Ash-Lin's Elegant Rose Florist and Gift Shop. There's also a salon and a photography studio adjacent. The shop sells a wide array of artisan goods like fashion accessories, jewelry, and home decor.
