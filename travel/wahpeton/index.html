Overview

Wahpeton is a town in the Red River Valley of North Dakota. It has a population of about 8,000 people (2020). It is the home of North Dakota State College of Science.

Best things about Wahpeton

Richland County Hisorical Museum

Chahinkapa Park and Zoo

Open May through September. Zoo features 200 animals, including Siberian tigers, kangaroos, otters, bears, snow leopards, and monkeys. Admission fee $5, $4.50 seniors, and $2 for children under 13

The Big Catfish

This 40-foot long catfish statue is visible from Highway 10 and is next to a dock. It is referred to locally as Wahpper. Locals claim it is the largest catfish sculpture in the world.

Snowmobiling

On the county's 200 miles of groomed trails.
