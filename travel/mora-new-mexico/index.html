Overview

Mora is a town in Northeast New Mexico. Its proximity to the Sangre de Cristo Mountains makes it a useful base of operations for hunting, fishing, hiking, etc.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Oct. Summer highs reach about 86°F; winter lows dip to about 18°F. Wettest: Jul and Aug. (climate data from nearby Santa Fe)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 1,260 miles, roughly 21 h 30 min without stops. Flying is usually faster.
  • TSM Taos Regional Airport, Taos — 38 mi away. Directions
  • SAF Santa Fe Municipal Airport, Santa Fe — 49 mi away. Directions
  • ABQ Albuquerque International Sunport, Albuquerque — 97 mi away (major hub). Directions

The nearest city with major commercial air service is Albuquerque, about 150 miles to the south. To drive to Mora from Albuquerque, follow I-25 to Las Vegas (New Mexico), then turn north on NM 65. This road can be difficult going in the winter.
