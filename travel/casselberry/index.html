    Understand

    Casselberry is one of many suburbs on the northeastern side of Orlando. It is between two important suburbs of the city: Sanford to the north, which is the location of the Sanford/Orlando Airport and a lakefront, and Winter Park to the south, the location of Rollins College.

    Longwood is a suburban community near Casselberry. Like Casselberry, it is primarily residential and those traveling to Longwood will want to stay in one of the larger local suburbs. However Casselberry's few restaurants and parks are within a few miles of Longwood and there is little to separate these two suburbs.

    Get in

    By car

    US-17/92 is the main route through the Longwood/Casselberry area. It connects the towns to the more central parts of the Orlando metro area to the immediate south. I-4 goes just west of the town.

    By train

    There are two Sunrail stations in Casselberry's vicinity, one being for Longwood and the other being for Altamonte Springs. Neither are in great locations for visiting Casselberry itself, though, so you'll need to have a car or know someone with a car to connect from either of these stations.

    Go Altamonte Springs Sunrail Station

    Closest station to Casselberry. A major Lynx bus transit hub, although routes go west, toward Apopka rather than toward Casselberry.
