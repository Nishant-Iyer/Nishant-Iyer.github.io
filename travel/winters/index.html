Best things about Winters

Trestle Train Bridge

Built in 1906 to replace the aging rail and wagon bridge preceding it, this historic train bridge crosses Putah Creek.

Stebbins Cold Canyon Reserve

Hiking at Stebbins Cold Canyon Reserve, a reserve run by UC Davis. 15 minutes west of Winters.

Best time to visit & weather
