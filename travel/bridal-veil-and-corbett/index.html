Overview

Bridal Veil and Corbett are communities in Multnomah County, Oregon, at the west end of the Columbia River Gorge, about 25 miles east of Portland.

8 sights · 1 things to do · 2 places to stay

Best things about Bridal Veil and Corbett

Bridal Veil Falls

Horse Tail Falls

176-foot high waterfall near the HCRH. Trail adjacent is steep.

Multnomah Falls

At 620 feet, it is the tallest waterfall in Oregon. Surrounded by hiking trails and bridges.

Vista House

Rooster Rock State Park

Monolith on the Columbia River. Called iwash (penis) by the Chinook. Has an adjacent nude beach. Picnic and disc golf facilities.

Wahkeena Falls

242-foot high waterfall.

Getting there

    Interstate 84, exit 22 for Corbett and 28 for Bridal Veil. Alternately use the Historic Columbia River Highway; most of the destinations are best reached from it.

      Stay Ainsworth State Park

      Seasonal campground with 40 full hookup sites and walk-in tent sites with nearby parking. A hiker-biker camping area does not accept reservations. It is near Interstate 84 and rail line- online reviews disagree how noisy this is in the campground. The park sells firewood, and has a dump station ($10) open to non-campers.

      🕒 Open for camping and day use Mar 21-Oct 31Check-in 4PM · out 1PM☎ +1 503-793-9885 (park info)Checked Sep 2026

      Additional accommodations can be found in Cascade Locks, Troutdale and near the Portland Airport.
