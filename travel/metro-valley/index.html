Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Jun–Sep. Summer highs reach about 70°F; winter lows dip to about 14°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Mar, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Dec and Jan. (climate data from nearby Snowshoe)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

Sample 3-day trip

  1. Day 1: Huntington — Marshall University, The Keith Albee Theatre, Heritage Station.
  2. Day 2: Charleston — West Virginia State Capitol, The Clay Center for the Arts and Sciences, West Virginia State Museum.
  3. Day 3: Logan — Hatfield Cemetery, Chief Logan State Park, West Virginia Mine Wars Museum.

Do

  • Dunbar is a small suburb of Charleston that features the Thanksgiving tradition of the Commode Bowl: a friendly football game that has brought together the town for over 75 years. The winner of the Hillside Rams versus the Riverside Rats receives a plunger. Make sure to arrive early to see the parade that features toilet-festooned cars.
