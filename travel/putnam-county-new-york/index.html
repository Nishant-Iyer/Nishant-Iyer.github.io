    Cities

    • Cold Spring — quaint Hudson River village is a popular day trip from New York City; antique stores, cafés, and views of the mountains
    • Brewster — small suburban area that was once dominated by farmlands; is known for holding many historical landmarks of the Putnam County area.

    See

    See Manitoga/The Russell Wright Design Center

    584 Route 9D, Garrison. The home site of the famous designer. Wright transformed an old quarry back to a carefully designed "natural" landscape. Four miles of walking trails. House and landscape tours M-F at 11AM, Sa and Su at 11AM and 1:30PM. Advance reservations required for weekend tours, group tours by appointment only. Hours may change without notice due to special events or restoration work, so you may want to call ahead.

    Do

    • Constitution Island near Cold Spring is part of West Point. It is most famous for the Great Chain that was placed across the Hudson during the Revolutionary War and the Warner family who lived on the Island during the 19th century.

    Do Thunder Ridge

    Come ski or snowboard down the Berkshire Mountains of Patterson, NY. If you don't know how Thunder Ridge also provides lessons if you would like to learn how to ski or snowboard. For those who have no interest there is also intense tubing/ sledding. In the off season Thunder Ridge offers hayrides and haunted houses for the fall and when you are in a pugnacious mood, Liberty paintball is available as well. Special occasions such as a weddings and other catered events are hosted on the base of the mountain at The Lodge.

    Eat

    Eat Aversano's Restaurant and Bar

    ItalianPizzaSouthern / Soul

    Classic southern Italian cooking has drawn people in on the weekends making reservations something to heavily consider. If you feel like eating out but are not in a formal dining sort of mood? Aversano's is also partly a pizzeria where you can come in and grab a slice wearing sweats but are still served with the same Italian hospitality and respect. While you are in make sure to say hi to John and Paul, the owners of the place, they are sure to remember you the next time you come!

    📍 1620 Route 22, BrewsterAt Route 312 intersection.☎ +1 845-279-2233
    Video reviews: Instagram reels · TikTok · YouTube
    MapsWebsite

    Eat Fiesta Mexicana

    Mexican

    Fiesta as people like to call it consists of classic Mexican-style food, Pinatas, a small live band that plays at each table (requests are encouraged) and their famous guacamole that is made right in front of you all make it a very popular place on the weekends. The bar makes it noisy sometimes but the band usually is able to play over them.

    Stay Heidi's Inn

    Although near a busy road Heidi's Inn still provides a sort of "getaway" spot. Most rooms have full kitchens but if you prefer to go out, there are restaurants within a one mile radius of the inn.
