    Get in

    By plane

    La Habra is within 15-20 miles of Los Angeles International Airport (LAX), Long Beach Airport, Orange County International Airport, and Ontario International Airport.

    By car

    No freeways run through La Habra, but many arterial streets do. To get to La Habra from the west, take Whittier Blvd or Imperial Highway. From the north, use Fullerton Road or Hacienda Blvd. From the east, use La Habra Blvd or Imperial Highway. From the south, use Harbor Blvd, Euclid Street or Beach Blvd.

    By bus

    La Habra is served by OCTA.
