    Get in

    By car

    Cave Junction is located on U.S. Route 199 (Redwood Highway) about 30 miles (48 km) southwest of Grants Pass. To get to the Oregon Caves, turn onto Oregon 46 and follow it 20 miles (32 km) to the monument.

    By bus

    The Junction Inn bus stop on Redwood Highway at Caves Highway is the main stop for buses serving Cave Junction.

    Go SouthWest POINT

    Inter-city bus service serving southwestern Oregon and Del Norte County, California. The bus runs from Brookings and Crescent City to the west and Grants Pass, Medford, Ashland, and Klamath Falls to the east.

    Stay Laughing Alpaca Campground and RV Park

    Campground on the Illinois River with full hook-ups for RVs, and tent camping. It includes WiFi, cable TV, club house and billiards room, but does not appear to have any actual alpacas- laughing or otherwise. You can bring your dog, though. Swim or boat in the calm Illinois River at your own risk.
