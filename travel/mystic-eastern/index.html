Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Oct. Summer highs reach about 85°F; winter lows dip to about 19°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Oct and Sep. (climate data from nearby Hartford)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 970 miles, roughly 17 h without stops. Flying is usually faster.
  • WST Westerly State Airport, Westerly — 26 mi away. Directions
  • BDL Bradley International Airport, Hartford — 36 mi away (major hub). Directions

Sample 3-day trip

  1. Day 1: Mystic — Mystic Aquarium and Institute for Exploration, Mystic Seaport - The Museum of America and the Sea, Denison Pequotsepos Nature Center.
  2. Day 2: Woodstock — East Woodstock Cemetery, North Woodstock Cemetery, Bradford Marcy Cemetery.
  3. Day 3: Preston — Amos Lake, Hallville Mill Historic District, Long Society Meetinghouse.
