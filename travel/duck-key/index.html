    Stay Hawks Cay Resort

    Midway down the Florida Keys is the secluded island of Duck Key, a 40-acre retreat offering lodging accommodations with world class fishing, the renowned Offshore Sailing School, a Dolphin Encounter program, beaches around a saltwater lagoon, swimming pools, dining at a variety of restaurants, kids and teen clubs, and a full-service spa.
