Overview

Greenwood Village is a city of about 15,000 in the Denver Metropolitan Area of the U.S. state of Colorado. The city lies southeast of Denver in Arapahoe County.

    Understand

    Originally settled in the 1860s, Greenwood Village quickly became a farming community. Today, the growing area is known for the opportunities it offers with top area employers such as FASCore, Cigna, Comcast, and Great West Live & Annuity Insurance as well as opportunities in the business hub of adjacent Denver Tech Center.
