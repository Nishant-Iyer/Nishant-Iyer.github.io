Get around

West Bend is a pedestrian friendly community. The Milwaukee River meanders through on its way to Lake Michigan. The community has developed a bike trail, river walk, and a vibrant downtown. It is a popular destination on warm Thursday nights to hear music and Saturday mornings for the Farmer's Market.

By shared-ride taxi

Taxis are scheduled by telephone, at least 30 minutes ahead of time for West Bend and 24 hours for Washington County.
