Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Jul–Aug. Summer highs reach about 55°F; winter lows dip to about -3°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr, Oct, Nov, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Oct and Jul. (climate data from nearby Mount Washington State Park)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 1,030 miles, roughly 18 h without stops. Flying is usually faster.
  • PWM Portland International Jetport, Portland — 67 mi away (major hub). Directions
  • AUG Augusta State Airport, Augusta — 68 mi away. Directions

Gorham is at the Junction of Routes 2 and 16. Take Rt. 2 east from Lancaster or west from Bethel, Maine. It also can be reached by taking Route 16 north from North Conway.
