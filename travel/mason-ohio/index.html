    Get in

    By car

    From Interstate I-71 northbound, take Ohio Exit 24 (Western Row Road/Kings Island). At the end of the exit ramp, turn left (West) on Western Row Road/Tylersville Road. Stay right on the three lane road to stay on Tylersville Road. At the intersection of Tylersville Road with Mason-Montgomer Road, turn right (north). One mile north, Mason-Montgomery Road intersects with Main Street (State Route 42). You are now in downtown Mason, Ohio.

    From Interstate I-71 southbound, take the Kings Mills Road exit. Kings Mills Road west. At a T-intersection, Route 741 turns north, Kings Mills turns south. Kings Mills Road curves through a residential neighborhood before turning back to the west. Kings Mills Ts into Main Street (State Route 42). Turn left (west) on Main Street about 3 blocks to the intersection of Main Street with Mason-Montgomery Road. You are in downtown Mason, Ohio.

    From Interstate I-75, take the Tylersville Road exit. Drive east to the intersection of Mason-Montgomery Road with Tylersville Road. Turn left (north) onto Mason-Montgomery Road. One mile north, Mason-Montgomery Road intersects with Main Street (State Route 42). You are now in downtown Mason, Ohio.

    By bus

    SORTA, Southwest Ohio Regional Transit Authority, provides bus service from downtown Cincinnati to a parking lot at Western Row Road and Columbia Road and along Bus Route 71/72 (I-71/Columbia Road/Montgomery Road/Fields Ertel Road).

    By plane

    Mason served by three major airports:

    • John Glenn Columbus International Airport, Columbus (CMH).
    • Cincinnati-Northern Kentucky International Airport (CVG) [ http://www.cvgairport.com/].
    • James M Cox Dayton International Airport, Dayton (DAY).

    Each airport can be accessed from Mason with at least some portion of interstate driving.

    Estimated driving time from Mason to the airports are:

    • Mason to CVG: 45 minutes; I-71 South to I-75 South to I-275 West to Airport Exit.
    • Mason to CMH: 90 minutes; I-71 North to I-670 East to Airport Exit, Exit #9.
    • Mason to DAY: 60 minutes; Tylersville Road to I-75 North, through downtown Dayton, to I-70 West to Airport Exit.

    Do

    Do Kings Island

    Kings Island is one of North America's most visited amusement parks. The park is divided into three different areas - the main park with adult attractions, a children's park, and a water park. Admission to the park grants access to all areas. Kings Island is also the home to record holding coasters like the Beast, the world's longest wooden roller coaster.

    📍 6300 Kings Island Dr, Kings Island☎ +1 513-754-5700Checked Feb 2026
    MapsWebsite

    Do The Beach Waterpark (The Beach at Adventure Landing)

    At The Beach Waterpark, parents and kids get soaked at more than 40 water rides and attractions. Go airborne on the five-story drop of The Cliff, ride the foaming river of Volcanic Panic, and propel your body at 65-miles per hour on The Banzai. With volleyball and basketball courts, teen and reggae dance nights, and the award-winning Dive-In Movies, a visit will truly be a day at The Beach!

    📍 2590 Waterpark Dr☎ +1-513-398-7946
    MapsWebsite

    Do Great Wolf Lodge (Great Wolf Lodge - Mason’s Water Park)

    With over 400 lodge-themed suites you can get soaked at one of America's largest indoor water parks, measuring 78,000 sq. ft., with eleven waterslides, six pools, an interactive treehouse waterfort, and 410,000 gallons of heated water - all year round.

    📍 2501 Great Wolf Dr
    MapsWebsite

    Do Western & Southern Financial Group Masters & Women's Open

    The best and brightest in tennis visit Mason every year for this premier tour stop. The Women's event is held in July and features players the likes of Serena Williams. The Men's event is held in August and features players the likes of Roger Federer and Rafael Nadal.

    MapsWebsite

    The best men's and women's pro beach volleyball players battle it out in the AVP's season-ending event in late August. Mason will host the tournament through 2011.
