Overview

Fallon is a city in Central Nevada. Fallon is a city in one of Nevada's few agricultural areas. The farms are irrigated using water extracted from the Carson River near the river's terminus into the Carson Sink.

Churchill County Museum and Archives

A collection of exhibits on local environment and culture. Offers tours of the nearby Hidden Cave archaeological site on the second and fourth Saturday each month.

Gambling

If you win...

Chances are that, if you win it big in Fallon and you are not a U.S. citizen your winnings will be subject to a 30% withholding tax from the IRS. That $10,000 slot winning can dwindle quite quickly if that is taken off the top. Not to worry though you can reclaim your gambling winnings tax through a 1042-S form. You should get this from the casino so don't lose it...it is your starting ticket to getting your gambling winnings back.

Fallon has a handful of casinos, all owned by Nugget.
