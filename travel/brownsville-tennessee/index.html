Best things about Brownsville

Fort Pillow State Historic Park

Site of the Civil War Battle of Fort Pillow, where some 300 African American Union soldiers were brutally massacred by Nathan Bedford Forrest's men upon his orders after surrendering.

Alex Haley Museum and Interpretive Center

Boyhood home of the author of the intergenerational African American epic Roots.

West Tennessee Delta Heritage Center and Tina Turner Museum

This museum offers a vignette on the Western Tennessee delta region with exhibits on things like cotton, blues artists, turtles and other endemic species, and Tina Turner (grew up in this community of Brownsville).

Mindfield Cemetery

An abstract metal artwork creation that looks a bit like Watts Towers in LA.

Tipton County Museum

Regional museum, military artifacts, mounted animals, etc.

Hatchie National Wildlife Refuge Visitor Center

Swamp and marshland area where you can explore walking trails, view wildlife and lily pads, and look at dioramas.

Understand

This community is one of many in the delta region that grew up around wealthy cotton-planting ventures requiring heavy slave labor and the subsequent mistreatment of African Americans thru disenfranchisement, including lynching, even after that way of life was upended. But now it is often correlated with Tina Turner who grew up here, and bar-b-que cooking.
