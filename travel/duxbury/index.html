Overview

Duxbury is a city in Massachusetts. It is one of the wealthier communities in the South Shore region.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Sep. Summer highs reach about 82°F; winter lows dip to about 18°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Mar and Oct. (climate data from nearby Braintree)

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 1,060 miles, roughly 18 h 30 min without stops. Flying is usually faster.
  • BOS Boston Logan International Airport, Boston — 28 mi away (major hub). Directions
  • EWB New Bedford Regional Airport, New Bedford — 29 mi away. Directions

From Boston take I-93 south to Route 3. Route 3 passes through Duxbury with exits onto Route 14 and Route 3A.
