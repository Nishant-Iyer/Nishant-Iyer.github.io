Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Jun–Aug. Summer highs reach about 71°F; winter lows dip to about -11°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Mar, Oct, Nov, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Jul and Jun.
