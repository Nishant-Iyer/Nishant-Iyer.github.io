Overview

Oakhurst is a settlement of 2,800 people (2010) in the foothills of the Sierra Nevada mountain range of California. It is 14 mi south of the entrance to Yosemite National Park.

Get in

Place Yosemite Area Regional Transit System (YARTS)

The YARTS shuttle travels on Highway 41 between Fresno and Wawona and Yosemite Valley, with a stop in Oakhurst at the Best Western. Ticket prices include park entry, and can be purchased from the shuttle driver.

Stay safe

Dial 911 for all emergencies

Law enforcement in Oakhurst is provided by the Madera County Sheriff's Department and the California Highway Patrol.

  • Oakhurst Sheriff's Substation. 39884 Road 425B. +1 559 658-2555.
  • CHP Oakhurst Area Office. 35500 Highway 41, Coarsegold. +1 559 683 6565.

Fire and Emergency Medical Service is provided by the Madera County Fire Department from Oakhurst Station #12. 49015 Civic Circle. +1 559 683-4808.
