Overview

Miesville is a city in Dakota County, in the Twin Cities region of Minnesota. It is southeast of south Saint Paul.

Plan your trip

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Sep. Summer highs reach about 83°F; winter lows dip to about 9°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Jun and Aug. (climate data from nearby Saint Paul)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).
