Overview

Thornburg is in Spotsylvania County, Virginia. As of July 2024, Thornburg is a developing area still with fun amenities within the area.

Best things about Thornburg

Mudd Tavern historical marker

Before it was known as Thornburg, much of the area was referred to as Mudd Tavern and Thornsburg. Famous for being the death site of American Civil War, Confederate lieutenant general Thomas "Stonewall" Jackson.
