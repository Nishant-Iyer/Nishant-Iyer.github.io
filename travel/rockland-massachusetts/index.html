Overview

Rockland is a town in the South Shore region of Massachusetts. Its population was about 18,000 in the 2020 census.

Abington is a town with a population of roughly 17,000 (2020).

Hanover is a town with a population of roughly 15,000 (2020).

Hanson is a town with a population of roughly 11,000 (2020).

Best things about Rockland

South Shore Natural Science Center

See Eco Zone, a multidimensional, interactive exhibit depicting the ecosystem of southeastern Massachusetts. Live animals, the outdoor trails, environmental education programs, lectures and special events.

The Company Theatre

Theatre with stage productions and "celebrity showcases" throughout the year.

Boston Bowl

A fun family activity open during all seasons. Candlepin and 10-pin bowling, an arcade, concession stand, bar. Has a sister location in Dorchester.

Emilson YMCA

Full exercise room equipped with weights and cardio machines. Full size lap pool and swimming pool with slide, basketball court, game room, as well as indoor and outdoor tennis courts.

Rockland Ice Rink

Public skating, hockey hour, ice rentals, skating lessons, and home to various hockey teams in the area.

Starland Sports and Fun Park

Get around

In order to get around these small towns, it is quicker and more convenient to drive from point to point. If you're looking for a way to exercise or be outdoors, there is a small 2.5 mi rail trail mostly in Rockland. It doesn't go anywhere or connect to anything, but it is scenic.
