Overview

Ashland is a town of 18,000 in Massachusetts approximately 25 miles west of Boston. Ashland was the original starting point of the Boston Marathon and birthplace of the electric clock.

Holliston is a town with a population of 15,000 (2020).

Hopkinton is a town with a population of 19,000 (2020). It is notable as the current starting place of the Boston Marathon.

Sherborn is a town with a population of 4,000 (2020).

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 1,010 miles, roughly 17 h 30 min without stops. Flying is usually faster.
  • BED Laurence G Hanscom Field, Bedford — 17 mi away. Directions
  • ORH Worcester Regional Airport, Worcester — 21 mi away. Directions
  • BOS Boston Logan International Airport, Boston — 24 mi away (major hub). Directions

From Route 495: Take exit 21A (West). Follow through Hopkinton center where the road merges with Route 135. Ashland center is about 3.5 miles from the exit. From Route 9: Go south on Route 126 to Framingham center (about 2 miles). Turn west on Route 135. Ashland center is about 4 miles from Framingham center. The towns are also reachable by Route 16 for points east and west, and Route 126 for points north and south.
