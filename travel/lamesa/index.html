Overview

Lamesa is a city in the Llano Estacado region of Texas. It is heavy on the agriculture sector and gives off an authentic feel of provincial Texan life.

1 sights · 3 places to eat · 4 places to stay

Best things about Lamesa

Dal Paso Museum

Located inside a restored historic hotel, this museum has works of local artists as well as exhibits on local history, pioneer life, and Indian artifacts.
