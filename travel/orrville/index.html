Overview

Orrville is a city in Wayne County in Northeast Ohio. Orrville is the corporate headquarters for The J.M. Smucker Company and its fruit spread producing facility, which Smucker's claims is the largest in the world.

Best things about Orrville

The J. M. Smucker Company, Inc.

.The J.M. Smucker Co., creator of Smucker's Jams, Peanut Butters, and other foodstuff is in Orrville. Founded in 1897 it started out by producing jams made from trees Johnny Appleseed planted.

Wayne College

A branch of the University of Akron.

Wayne County Speedway

The Orrville Historical Society

Holds occasional history related seminars and displays.

The Orrville Railroad Heritage Society

Runs seasonal Rail Tours that are hosted by the "Wheeling &amp; Lake Erie Railway" and the "Ohio Central Railroad".

    The population was 8400 in 2019, and the city has an area of 5.3 mi2.

    Orrville was laid out in 1852, and named after Judge Smith Orr, proprietor. A post office called Orrville has been in operation since 1852
