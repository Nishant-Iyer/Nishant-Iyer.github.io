Overview

Mays Landing (also referred to as Hamilton Township) is a small town of about 6,200 (2023) and is the county seat of Atlantic County, New Jersey. It is a lovely town and a please place to spend the day. It has charm in its historic section with fine restaurants, local shops, marinas and a great view of the river from Gaskill Park.

    By car

    See

    Locals will recommend a quaint cove nearby where one can sit in the sun on a little beach by Lake Lenape.

    Do

    Boating, camping, and exploring the vast areas of the surrounding Atlantic County Park System are some of the local area activities. There is a roller skating rink and fields for football, baseball, hockey and other sports in the area.

    Buy

    • Hamilton Mall, 4403 Black Horse Pike, 609 646 8326. Anchored by such department stores as Macy’s, this mall also offers over one hundred forty specialty stores and shops, a variety of restaurants, and a food court. Located close to the mall are large retail outlets such as Target and Best Buy.
