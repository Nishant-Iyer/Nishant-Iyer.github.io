Overview

Granbury is a city in Hood County, near the Dallas/Fort Worth area of Texas.

5 sights · 3 things to do · 5 places to eat · 6 places to stay

Best things about Granbury

Acton State Historic Site

It's where Davy Crockett's second wife, Elizabeth Crockett, is buried.

Historic square

Which is made up of several buildings which are registered historic landmarks.

Granbury Opera House

Hood County Jail and Museum

An old western jail built in 1885, with original cell block and hanging tower, exhibits, artifacts and memorabilia. It is also said to be haunted.

Granbury State Historical Cemetery

Visit the graves of General Hiram Granbury (which the town of Granbury is named after), Jesse James (supposedly buried here), and Ashley Crockett (son of Davy Crockett).

Brazos Drive-In Theatre

An authentic 1950s drive-in, open March 1st- December 1st.

Get in

Granbury is located approximately 30 miles Southwest of Fort Worth, Texas on US Highway 377. Take the Granbury 377 business exit off of 377, which will take you straight into the historic square.

Get around

A car is the best way to get around, but the Granbury stretch of US 377 can be quite clogged around noon and rush hour.

  • Brazos Carriage Company, on the Square on Bridge Street, +1 817 723-2322. Offers horse-drawn carriage tours of Granbury's historic district.
