Overview

South Atlanta is a district in Atlanta that contains the Grant Park, Peoplestown, Lakewood Heights, and Mechanicsville neighborhoods, and is near the world's busiest airport.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Mar–May, Sep–Nov. Summer highs reach about 90°F; winter lows dip to about 36°F. Very hot (Jul) — plan outdoor time for mornings. Wettest: Jul and Mar. (climate data from nearby Atlanta)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 720 miles, roughly 12 h 30 min without stops. Flying is usually faster.
  • ATL Hartsfield Jackson Atlanta International Airport, Atlanta — 6 mi away (major hub). Directions
  • PDK DeKalb Peachtree Airport, Atlanta — 12 mi away. Directions

MARTA East/West Line stops at King Center Station. Interstate 75, Interstate 20, and Freedom Parkway provide access by car.
