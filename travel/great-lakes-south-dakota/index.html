Overview

The Great Lakes region of South Dakota is in the central part of the state in the United States of America. It follows the course of the Missouri River from North Dakota South to Nebraska.

Sample 3-day trip

  1. Day 1: Pierre — South Dakota State Capitol, South Dakota Cultural Heritage Center, Oahe Dam.
  2. Day 2: Mobridge — Sitting Bull Monument, Klein Museum, Walley Up Statue.
  3. Day 3: Chamberlain and Oacoma — Dignity of Earth and Sky, South Dakota Hall of Fame, Atka Lakota Museum and Cultural Center.
