Overview

The Piedmont Triad (or simply the Triad) is an urban region in central North Carolina, halfway between the Research Triangle and Charlotte. The three cities that make up the Triad are Greensboro, Winston-Salem, and High Point.

Plan your trip

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Mar–Jun, Sep–Nov. Summer highs reach about 90°F; winter lows dip to about 32°F. Very hot (Jul) — plan outdoor time for mornings. Wettest: Aug and Mar. (climate data from nearby Piedmont)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 710 miles, roughly 12 h without stops. Flying is usually faster.
  • GSO Piedmont Triad International Airport, Greensboro — 11 mi away (major hub). Directions
  • USA Concord-Padgett Regional Airport, Concord — 56 mi away. Directions

The Triad is served by Greyhound buses from other parts of North Carolina and beyond. Beyond that, PART has one line that goes from Chapel Hill to Burlington and Greensboro.

Cities

  • Greensboro – biggest city in the region
  • Winston-Salem – historic city with an old Moravian settlement
  • High Point – "furniture capital of the world"

Other destinations

  • Hanging Rock State Park
  • Pilot Mountain – a state park featuring a distinctive mountain

Get in

By plane

Go Piedmont Triad International Airport (GSO)

Located near Greensboro, PTI has around 75 daily flights to most major hubs on the east coast.

Google MapsWebsite

By car

By bus

The Triad is served by Greyhound buses from other parts of North Carolina and beyond. Beyond that, PART has one line that goes from Chapel Hill to Burlington and Greensboro.

By train

Amtrak's Carolinian and Piedmont trains stop in Burlington, Greensboro, and High Point; this is a convenient and pleasant way to get to these cities from Charlotte or the Research Triangle. The Crescent also stops in Greensboro and High Point on its way from New York City to New Orleans.

Get around

PART (Piedmont Authority for Regional Transit) buses serve various cities and towns in the region. The system's big hub is the Coble Transportation Center.
