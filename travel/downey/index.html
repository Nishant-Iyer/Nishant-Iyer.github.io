Best things about Downey

World's Oldest McDonald's

This is the oldest standing McDonald's fast food store. A portion of the store is a museum. The original 60-foot-high sign is also still standing. Both are listed on the U.S. National Register of Historic Places.

Downey History Center

Close to You and Only Just Begun

Karen and Richard Carpenter used to own these two apartment buildings across from each other.

Columbia Memorial Space Center

Space museum with a Lego robotics lab.

Downey Theatre

Downey Symphony

The symphony presents three concerts each season and also performs a few wind instrument concerts in nearby Furman Park.
