Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Jul–Aug. Summer highs reach about 62°F; winter lows dip to about 21°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Mar, Nov, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Sep and Oct. (climate data from nearby Seward)
