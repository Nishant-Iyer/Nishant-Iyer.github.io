Overview

Steuben County is in the Southern Tier region of New York, although the northern part, including Hammondsport, Wayland, and Cohocton, is in the Finger Lakes region.

Plan your trip

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Sep. Summer highs reach about 80°F; winter lows dip to about 15°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Jun and Jul. (climate data from nearby Ithaca)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 640 miles, roughly 11 h without stops. Flying is usually faster.
  • ELM Elmira Corning Regional Airport, Elmira/Corning — 26 mi away. Directions
  • ITH Ithaca Tompkins Regional Airport, Ithaca — 49 mi away. Directions
  • ROC Frederick Douglass Greater Rochester International Airport, Rochester — 61 mi away (major hub). Directions
