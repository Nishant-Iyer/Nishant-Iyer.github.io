Best things about Clinton

Bickelhaupt Arboretum

A 14 acre botanical garden operated by the Clinton Community College with shrubs, conifers, herbs, hostas, daylillies, and that kind of thing.

Sawmill Museum

This museum explores Clinton's legacy as a lumbering and transportation center given its proximity to rail lines and waterways.

Eagle Point Park

An expansive park that overlooks the Mississippi River with trails, stone bridge, and even a castle.

Clinton Area Showboat Theater

Theatrical shows and musicals (probably folksy) aboard a beached paddlewheeler.

Wild Rose Casino & Hotel Clinton

They've got the buffet and gaming floor with the pervasive milieu of secondhand smoke hanging in the air.

    Understand

    Clinton is in fact the easternmost city in Iowa, and just south of the Mississippi River's widest section, the area above Lock and Dam No. 13. Clinton has its origins as a railroad and lumbering town, and in the 1880s-1890s boasted the most millionaires in the country.

    If you need a heads up on details, you can stop by the Clinton, Iowa Visitors Information Kiosk at 400 Riverview Dr daily from 7AM-8PM.
