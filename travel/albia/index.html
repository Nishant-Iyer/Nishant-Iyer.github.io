    Maps

    See

    • Take a stroll around the historic town square with the county courthouse in the center.

    Do

    • Dress up and enjoy the Victorian Stroll, the first weekend each December. Period costumes and displays.
    • Rodders Car Show, takes place on the square late June admire some of the finest automobiles from the area.
    • Restoration Days, takes place on the fourth Saturday of August. Activities include a parade, art show, vintage car displays, class reunions, book sale and more.

    Buy

    • There are interesting stores around the square.
