Overview

East Atlanta is the trendiest area in Atlanta. It includes Inman Park, the popular dining and nightlife neighborhoods of Little Five Points, Virginia-Highland, and Poncey-Highland. The Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) and one of the top U.S. research universities, Emory University, are in this area.

    Neighborhoods entirely within the City of Atlanta include:

    Place Inman Park

    includes two national historic districts: the Inman Park Historic District, and the Inman Park-Moreland Historic District.

    MapsWebsite

    Place Little Five Points

    is an Inman Park neighborhood business district of Atlanta that caters to the alternative crowd and has become a hangout for hipsters, punks, goth high school students, and some individuals who defy description. It's also a great location for vintage-everything (clothing, record, book, etc.) shopping. The name Little Five Points refers to an intersection of five streets. This is not to be confused with Five Points in Downtown Atlanta.

    MapsWebsite

    Place Virginia-Highland

    is an area east of Piedmont Park in the center of town known for its pubs and restaurants. Pedestrian-friendly sidewalks are busy until late at night. The name is derived from the intersection of Virginia Avenue and Highland Avenue.

    MapsWebsite

    Get in

    MARTA Rail East-West Blue Line stops in King Memorial (E2), Inman Park/Reynoldstown (E3), Edgewood/Candler Park (E4), and East Lake Station (E5). Parking is available at all these stations except King Memorial. Interstate 20 is also close.

    To park in Virginia-Highland on a Friday or Saturday night:

    • Near the corner of Virginia and Highland, find the parking lot entrance across from the fire station at the corner of N Highland and Los Angeles Ave. Parking there is free.
    • For parking farther south on N. Highland (near Hand in Hand), there are $5 lots near the CVS. Watch out for resident-only parking on the side streets; tow trucks really do tow cars who park in the resident-only areas.

    See Jimmy Carter Presidential Library & Museum

    One of ten presidential libraries administered by the National Archives & Records Administration and the only one in the Southeast, includes photographs, historical memorabilia from his presidency, an exact replica of the Oval Office, a permanent exhibit of significant events during Carter’s life and political career and the permanent home of the former president’s Nobel Peace Price awarded in late 2002.

    Stay Hotel Clermont

    Marking the revitalization of a legendary Southern landmark, Hotel Clermont reopened in the colorful and central Poncey-Highland neighborhood in June 2018 as a 94-room boutique hotel drenched in local history and culture. Three suites, 76 standard rooms and 15 bunk rooms - which house four guests per room and exist as an ode to the hotel’s storied revolving door of notable, musical guests - make up the hotel’s dynamic accommodation offerings, designed to appeal to all types of travelers.
