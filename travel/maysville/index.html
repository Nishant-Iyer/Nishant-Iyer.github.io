Overview

Maysville, in Kentucky's Northern Ohio River Region, is an old historic town located on the banks of the Ohio River.

6 sights · 1 things to do · 1 places to eat · 2 places to stay

Best things about Maysville

Simon Kenton Memorial Bridge

Carries U.S. Route 62 over the Ohio River.

Russell Theatre

Murals

National Underground Railroad Museum

Focuses on the underground railroad activity here during the 1850s, located in the Bierbower estate where runaway slaves were formerly housed on their path to freedom.

Kentucky Gateway Museum Center

Explores area history, such as European American arrivals and African American heritage.

Washington Historic District

Site of first county seat in Mason County, this suburb of Maysville has a unique historic district with such attractions as Harriet Beecher Stowe Museums, Mefford's Fort, and the Old Church Museum

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Apr–Oct. Summer highs reach about 86°F; winter lows dip to about 23°F. Cold months (Jan) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Jun and May. (climate data from nearby Cincinnati)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 370 miles, roughly 6 h 30 min without stops.
  • LUK Cincinnati Municipal Airport Lunken Field, Cincinnati — 47 mi away. Directions
  • CVG Cincinnati Northern Kentucky International Airport, Cincinnati / Covington — 56 mi away (major hub). Directions

Amtrak serves Maysville via its thrice-weekly Cardinal train, running between Chicago, Indianapolis, Cincinnati and Washington, D.C., New York. Westbound trains arrive around midnight while eastbound ones arrive early morning around 5 AM. However, note that delays are quite common. Maysville station is located at the intersection of West Front Street and Rosemary Clooney Street, west of downtown.
