Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Apr–Nov. Summer highs reach about 87°F; winter lows dip to about 21°F. Cold months (Jan) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Jan and Feb. (climate data from nearby Huntingdon Valley)

Getting there

  • From you: to see whether to drive or fly, and how long it takes.
  • TTN Trenton Mercer Airport, Ewing Township — 12 mi away. Directions
  • PHL Philadelphia International Airport, Philadelphia — 26 mi away (major hub). Directions

Bristol is approximately 20 minutes north of central Philadelphia via Interstate 95. The Pennsylvania Turnpike, I-276, has an exit in Bristol. Coming from New Jersey, Bristol is serviced by the Burlington-Bristol Bridge or the turnpike bridge, which connects the New Jersey Turnpike to the Pennsylvania Turnpike.
