Overview

Blue Ridge is a small resort town in the Georgia's Northeast High Country. It is located in the foothills of the Appalachian Trail.

Getting there

  • TYS McGhee Tyson Airport, Knoxville/Maryville — 68 mi away (major hub). Directions

Blue Ridge is 90 miles north of Atlanta and is easily reached by taking I-75N to I-575 (exit 268). I-575 will become Hwy 515/76 as you drive north and will take you directly into Blue Ridge.

Understand

Blue Ridge is a very popular vacation destination, especially in the fall when leaf color is at its peak, but the pristine mountain lake draws a crowd during the summer months as well.

Get in

Blue Ridge is 90 miles north of Atlanta and is easily reached by taking I-75N to I-575 (exit 268). I-575 will become Hwy 515/76 as you drive north and will take you directly into Blue Ridge.

Get around

By foot

Due to the relatively compact and tourist-oriented nature of this town, walking is quite easy, although as is true for most of the state, pedestrians do need to be careful for cars.

Drink Grumpy Old Men Brewing

Sells beer to enjoy on the premises and also sells beer to go. Inside and outside seating is available. The brewery does not serve food, but outside food is allowed in the brewery. Also, the brewery is attached to Jeff's Hot Dogs, which sells gourmet hotdogs and soft pretzels, which can be brought into the brewery. There is live music on Fridays and Saturdays, and there is karaoke on Thursdays. Dog-friendly.

Stay Red Apple Vacation Rental Cabin in Blue Ridge

Red Apple Cabin is perched on Prince Mountain within the Cohutta National forest and adventure area. 2,100 feet of elevation lends to cool mountain breezes and crisp clean air as the wind brushes through the leaves. Every room comes with a view of the mountain range and forest. Warm gas fireplace plus central heat and air conditioning. Wake up to silence; 2.2 acres of natural beauty with no other cabins in sight. Upstairs master bedroom, spacious garden bathroom, equipped with Jacuzzi tub and separate shower, spectacular views on your private porch. Guest bedroom on the main floor with another oversize full bath and full size laundry services. Lower basement level provides a third bedroom.

📍 587 Lower Prince Mountain RdFrom the 515, turn on Lucius Road just south of the Cherrylog Community entrance sign and head west past the train tracks. Follow to the end as it intersects with Boardtown Road. Turn right on Boardtown Road and then make your next left onto Bushyhead Rd. Drive past the white little Church of Christ. From Bushyhead Road, follow as it turns to gravel and then look for Caches Valley Lane to the right, just at the crossing for Fightown Creek. Take a sharp right on Caches Valley Lane. Follow past Prince Mountain Road and then left onto Lower Prince Mountain Road where their red mailbox is.
