Overview

Dillon is a city in Southwest Montana. In 2020, about 3,800 people lived there. Dillon is home to University of Montana Western.

Understand

Dillon began as a railroad town. It served as the transportation hub for the many nearby towns with gold and silver mines.

University of Montana Western is a smaller college, so there is not the degree of student-life and activity as other college towns.

Get in

The town is off Interstate 15 (I-15).

The nearest airports are Butte and Idaho Falls.

Get around

The central business area can be easily traveled on foot. Most sightseeing activities will require driving.

The city offers a free City Bus M-F during the day, you can request a ride by calling.

Do

The Beaverhead and Big Hole rivers offer world class trout fishing. The Labor Day PRCA rodeo includes a parade and concerts and is named "Montana's Biggest Weekend".
