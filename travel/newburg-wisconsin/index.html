Get in

This is a friendly city between Port Washington and West Bend, at the junction of Highway 33, County Road Y and County Road MY.

Get around

This is an extremely small town. It is quite easy to park in the municipal lot, next to the fire department, and walk along the Milwaukee River, or grab a sandwich at Sandy's Parkview Inn.

See

Newburg is on the Milwaukee River, and its heart is Firemen's Park. It has baseball/softball diamonds, fishing spots, and river runs on the West and North sides of the park.
