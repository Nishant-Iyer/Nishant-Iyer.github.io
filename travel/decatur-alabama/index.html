Old State Bank

Built in 1833, it's the oldest bank building in Alabama and one of only four to survive when the Union set fire to the town.

Point Mallard Park

Known primarily for its water park along the Tennessee River, it also features an indoor ice rink, golf course, and camping. Special celebrations occur on Memorial Day and Independence Day.

Wheeler National Wildlife Refuge

Cook Museum of Natural Science

Opened in 2018, this science museum particularly showcases some interesting flora and fauna exhibits.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Mar–May, Sep–Nov. Summer highs reach about 92°F; winter lows dip to about 33°F. Very hot (Jul, Aug) — plan outdoor time for mornings. Wettest: Dec and Mar. (climate data from nearby Huntsville)
