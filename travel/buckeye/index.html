Best things about Buckeye

Buckeye Valley Archeological & Historical Museum

Eastmen Gin

Heritage Park includes several acres of agriculture representing Buckeye's history; the restored Eastman Gin and a working mini-gin capable of producing 1 lb bales of cotton.

Raney House

The "Raney" house was built by Nels Benson and called the Benson House for many years. Now abandoned.

Skyline Regional Park

Pristine low desert scenery peppered with saguaro and barrel cacti and outcroppings that make for excellent hiking, especially at sunrise and sunset.

Helzapoppin' PRCA Rodeo

Held the third weekend in March, the Helzapoppin' PRCA Rodeo is the only sanctioned PRCA rodeo in the southwest valley. These are the pros, hoping to head on to the NFR in Las Vegas in December!
