Overview

Ashford is a town in the South Sound of Washington State that serves as a gateway to Mount Rainier National Park.

2 places to stay

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Jun–Sep. Summer highs reach about 76°F; winter lows dip to about 25°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Nov and Dec. (climate data from nearby Mount Rainier National Park)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

Get in

State Route 706 (WA-706) is the only road through the town. The rural highway connects to the rest of western Washington via WA-7, and through Mount Rainier to Longmire, Paradise Valley Road, and Stevens Canyon Road.

By car

From Tacoma, take State Route 7 (WA-7) south. At Elbe, keep straight onto WA-706, which leads into the MRNP Nisqually entrance.

Stay Historic Mineral Lake Lodge

1905 three story log lodge on pristine Mineral Lake near the Southwest entrance to Mt. Rainier National Park. Registered on the National Register for Historic Properties. Magnificent view of Mt. Rainier. Open for lodging year round, just five minutes off the beaten path to Mt. Rainier on beautiful Mineral Lake.
