Overview

Willow Creek is a small village in the Copper River Valley in Southcentral Alaska near the Wrangell-St. Elias National Park and Preserve.

Plan your trip

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Jun–Jul. Summer highs reach about 64°F; winter lows dip to about 19°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Mar, Nov, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Sep and Oct. (climate data from nearby Valdez)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

Willow Creek is on the Richardson Highway (State Highway 4) about midway between Copper Center and Tonsina. It is close to the intersection with the Old Edgerton Highway leading to Kenny Lake, which should not be confused with the Edgerton Highway (State Highway 10) that is the direct route to Kenny Lake, but intersects with the Richardson Highway further south, close to Tonsina.
