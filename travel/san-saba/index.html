Overview

San Saba is a small town of about 3,000 residents (2020) in the German Hill Country of Texas. It is the birthplace of actor Tommy Lee Jones of Lonesome Dove and No Country For Old Men fame.

Best things about San Saba

San Saba County Museum

Two 100-year old log cabins filled with articles from early ranch and farm life.

Mill Pond Park

A relaxing city park with spillway and aqueducts taking form.

San Saba River Nature Park

Natural area encompassing the San Saba River with trails.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Oct–Apr. Summer highs reach about 98°F; winter lows dip to about 42°F. Very hot (Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep) — plan outdoor time for mornings. Wettest: May and Oct. (climate data from nearby Austin)

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 1,170 miles, roughly 20 h without stops. Flying is usually faster.
  • GRK Killeen Regional Airport / Robert Gray Army Airfield, Fort Cavazos — 53 mi away. Directions
  • ACT Waco Regional Airport, Waco — 92 mi away. Directions
  • AUS Austin Bergstrom International Airport, Austin — 93 mi away (major hub). Directions

San Saba is about 100 miles northwest of Austin. To get there by car, drive north on US Highway 183 for about 85 miles to the town of Lometa, then turn west on US Highway 190 for the last 15 miles into San Saba.
