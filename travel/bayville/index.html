Overview

Bayville is an incorporated village in Nassau County, New York.

Plan your trip

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Oct. Summer highs reach about 84°F; winter lows dip to about 22°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Oct and Jun. (climate data from nearby Pleasantville)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 890 miles, roughly 15 h 30 min without stops. Flying is usually faster.
  • HPN Westchester County Airport, White Plains — 14 mi away. Directions
  • LGA LaGuardia Airport, New York — 19 mi away (major hub). Directions

Bayville can be accessed by two main roads. One is by Bayville Avenue, the main street that runs through the length of the village. The other is by Ludlam Avenue, which becomes West Shore Road after passing over the Bayville Bridge on your way out of Bayville.
