Best things about Garden City

Lee Richardson Zoo

Hang out with your favorite North American prairie animal friends or animal friends in general at this zoo with free admission and even a self-driving tour.

Sandsage Bison Range

Wildlife area with over 100 bison. For your safety, you cannot enter areas with bison unescorted, so call ahead to arrange a tour.

Beef Empire Days

Beef Empire Days occur around the first week of June. Activities include a steak cookout, tennis tournament, horse shoe tournament, 3 on 3 basketball tournament, softball tournament, and a parade.

Get in

Amtrak's daily Southwest Chief Chicago-Los Angeles line stops in Garden City. The westbound train arrives at 6:45AM and the eastbound train arrives at 11:35PM.

Garden City is served by Bee Line Express. The station is at the Finney County Transit building (next to the Valero).

By plane

Go Garden City Regional Airport (GCK)

American Airlines has nonstop service from Dallas/Fort Worth International Airport

Google MapsWebsite

Get around

There is only one cab company in town.

By bus

Finney County Transit operates the City Link Bus Phone: +1 620 272-3626. Buses run between 6AM and 7PM, Monday through Friday. The fare is $1. A monthly pass is available for $30 from the transit office at 1008 N. Eleventh Street.
