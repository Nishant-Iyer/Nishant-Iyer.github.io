Eat

Eat Rockaway Pronto Pup

Claims to have invented the corndog in the 1930s, and to have the world's first riding mechanical corndog. Besides 3 sizes of beef corndogs, they have veggie dogs as well as battered zucchini, dill pickle, and a three cheese variety, which are all vegetarian. They also have a seasonal corndog flavor, soft-serve ice cream, and the inevitable hats and T-shirts.
