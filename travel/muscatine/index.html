Overview

Muscatine is a city sitting alongside the Mississippi River in Eastern Iowa.

2 sights · 4 things to do · 3 places to stay

Best things about Muscatine

Muscatine Art Center

Pieces by artists such as Grant Wood (born in Anamosa, IA, about one hour from Muscatine), Matisse, Degas and Renoir. Several number of pieces revolve around the Mississippi River and cities along its banks.

Sino-US Friendship House

The small museum sits in a house where China's now-President Xi Jinping stayed during his visit to Iowa in 1985, when he was a county-level official leading an agricultural delegation around the state.

Muscatine Aquatic Center

Water park features 5 slides.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Apr–Oct. Summer highs reach about 86°F; winter lows dip to about 15°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Jun and May. (climate data from nearby Davenport)

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 220 miles, roughly 4 h without stops.
  • MLI Quad City International Airport, Moline — 28 mi away. Directions
  • BRL Southeast Iowa Regional Airport, Burlington — 44 mi away. Directions
  • DSM Des Moines International Airport, Des Moines — 135 mi away (major hub). Directions

If coming from the northwest or north, take Interstate 80 to exit 271. Then take U.S. Highway 6 West/S.R. 38 South. Muscatine is about 15 miles (24 km) south of I-80. From the east or northeast, take I-280 around Davenport and exit at U.S. Highway 61 South (exit 6). If coming from the southeast, take I-280 around Moline and exit at S.R. Get in

By car

If coming from the northwest or north, take Interstate 80 to exit 271. Then take U.S. Highway 6 West/S.R. 38 South. Muscatine is about 15 miles (24 km) south of I-80. From the east or northeast, take I-280 around Davenport and exit at U.S. Highway 61 South (exit 6). If coming from the southeast, take I-280 around Moline and exit at S.R. Highway 92 West (exit 11A).

Hailing from the south and on the Iowa side of the Mississippi River, take U.S. Highway 61 North/S.R. 92 East. If on the Illinois side of the river but inconvenient to get on I-74 North, S.R. 92 West will take you directly downtown after crossing over the river.
