Overview

Batavia is in Clermont County, Ohio. This article includes both the village of Batavia, as well as Batavia Township which surrounds it.

3 sights · 2 things to do · 1 places to stay

Best things about Batavia

Clermont County Courthouse

A neoclassical courthouse building.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Apr–Oct. Summer highs reach about 86°F; winter lows dip to about 23°F. Cold months (Jan) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Jun and May. (climate data from nearby Cincinnati)

How is the food here?

There are few restaurants within Batavia. The best option for travelers would be to follow U.S. Route 50 to Milford or Miami Township where there are plenty of restaurants.
