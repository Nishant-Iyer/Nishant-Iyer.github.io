Overview

Lynchburg is a small community in Tennessee. It is contained within Moore County, with which it forms a consolidated government.

Lynchburg Historic District

Several dozen buildings built as early as 1850 in downtown Lynchburg make up the Lynchburg Historic District which is recognized on the National Register of Historic Places.

Moore County Courthouse and Jail

Built in the late 1800s, the courthouse and old jail (which now serves as a museum) are listed on the National Register of Historic Places.

    Get in

    By plane

    • Smyrna Airport, +1-615-459-2651, is a reliever airport for BNA about 60 mi from Lynchburg, a 1-hour and 15-min drive.
    • Huntsville International Airport (HSV), +1-256-772-9395, is a commercial airport about 60 mi from Lynchburg, a 1½-hour drive.
    • Nashville International Airport (BNA), +1-615-275-1675, is a commercial airport about 70 mi from Lynchburg, a 1½-hour drive.
    • John C. Tune Airport, +1-615-350-6551, is a general aviation airport about 80 mi from Lynchburg, a 1½-hour drive.
    • Chattanooga Airport (CHA), +1-423-855-2200, is a commercial airport about 101 mi from Lynchburg, a 2-hour drive.

    By car

    Lynchburg is located between two interstate highways, I-65 (north–south) and I-24 (northwest–southeast). Lynchburg can be reached from I-24 via Tennessee State Route 55 (Lynchburg Highway) through Manchester and Tullahoma or from I-65 via U.S. Route 64 (Pulaski Highway) through Fayetteville to Tennessee State Route 55.

    Get around

    As no public transportation is available, getting around by car or on foot is your best bet. Free parking is readily available at local businesses. The distillery is along the main highway going through town, so walking there is not advised, but you can explore the square and surrounding buildings on foot.
