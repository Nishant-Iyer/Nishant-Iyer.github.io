Overview

Bluffton is a fast-growing coastal city in the Low Country of South Carolina located 7 miles (12 km) west of the major southeastern United States resort town of Hilton Head Island.

    Understand

    From its birth in the late 1700s to today, Bluffton hasn’t lost its identity. It's a beautiful coastal town with unspoiled marshes, stunning sunsets, and warm and friendly locals. Due to committed homeowners, local business owners and elected officials, Bluffton is a place that many tourists now call home.

    Get in

    Bluffton is accessible by air, road and boat. By air you can fly in to Savannah or Hilton Head Island. Both airports are small and easy to get around.

    Get around

    As there are very few public transit options in Bluffton, a car is the best way to get around.

    The Bluffton Breeze service offers weekday bus service to certain destinations in Bluffton.

    Old town Bluffton has a small walkable district and nearby parking.
