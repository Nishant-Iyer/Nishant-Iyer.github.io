Get around

By seasonal tram

  • Kaaterskill Trolley Co. runs the very congested route of Tannersville, Haines Falls, Palenville, and throughout Kaaterskill Clove down to Palenville. Given that New York State Route 23-A is quite narrow and frequented, legal parking is non-existent during the warm months along the road.

See North-South Lake

An excellent all-around introduction to the Catskills for either day or overnight. Fish, boat and swim in the lakes, or hike the extensive trail system to the ruins of the Catskill Mountain House and the views it made famous.

See Kaaterskill Falls

One of the highest waterfalls of New York state, there is a viewing platform by parking on the Laurel House Trail. Next to the platform there's a small swimming hole off Spruce Creek. It gets very busy in the summer and weekends and parking is limited. Below the viewing platform, several trails are available to the often crowded base of the falls.
