Overview

Ocoee is in the United States in the state of Tennessee. Ocoee's claim to fame is the fact that they were a venue for the 1996 Olympics hosted by Atlanta, GA.

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 600 miles, roughly 10 h 30 min without stops. Flying is usually faster.
  • CHA Chattanooga Metropolitan Airport (Lovell Field), Chattanooga — 28 mi away. Directions
  • TYS McGhee Tyson Airport, Knoxville/Maryville — 63 mi away (major hub). Directions

Ocoee is reached primarily by road. U.S. Route 64 follows the Ocoee River through the area and connects it with Cleveland and Ducktown. Interstate 75 provides access from Chattanooga and other parts of Tennessee.

Get in

By car

Ocoee is reached primarily by road. U.S. Route 64 follows the Ocoee River through the area and connects it with Cleveland and Ducktown. Interstate 75 provides access from Chattanooga and other parts of Tennessee.

By plane

The nearest major airport is Chattanooga Metropolitan Airport, approximately an hour's drive from the Ocoee area.
