Best things about Independence

Independence Mental Health Institute

An old state mental asylum constructed in 1873, built under the Kirkbride Plan. The hospital is still in operation. The "Days of Yore" museum is open to the public.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Oct. Summer highs reach about 85°F; winter lows dip to about 11°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Jun and May. (climate data from nearby Waterloo)

  • DSM Des Moines International Airport, Des Moines — 111 mi away (major hub). Directions

US Highway 20 runs along the south side of town, linking Independence with Dubuque to the east and Waterloo-Cedar Falls to the west. Other routes include Iowa Highway 939, which parallels US 20, and Iowa Highway 150, which runs north-south and links to Interstate 380 about 15 miles south to town, providing access to Cedar Rapids and Iowa City.
