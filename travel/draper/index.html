    Get in

    By Plane

    • Salt Lake City International Airport (SLC) is about 30 minutes north of Draper, and serves as Delta Air Lines' mid-country hub, with over 120 daily departures for Delta alone. It has direct international flights to various cities in Mexico and Canada, and to Paris.

    By Train

    Place TRAX Light Rail

    UTA TRAX offers an inexpensive $2.50 ride from the airport to Draper via the Green and Blue Lines.

    MapsWebsite

    Place FrontRunner Commuter Rail

    FrontRunner runs between Ogden and Provo with a stop in Draper.

    MapsWebsite

    Get around

    By Bus

    Utah Transit Authority operates a few routes in Draper Utah.

    Useful routes:

    • F514, the 300 West Flex, runs from the Draper FrontRunner station north to the Living Planet Aquarium, South Jordan FrontRunner station and the Sandy Civic Center Trax Station. The F514 does not run on weekends.
    • 126, the South Valley Regional, travels South from Daybreak Parkway station into Riverton, then travels East along 12600 South to Draper FrontRunner, then travels along 12300 South through the Draper Peaks Shopping Center, terminating at the Draper Town Center TRAX station. The 126 does not operate on weekends.
    • 871, the Tech Corridor Rail Connector, starts at Sandy Civic Station, then travels south along State Street before turning onto Kimballs Lane to provide access to Draper Peaks. After Draper Peaks it then travels south on I-15 to The Outlets at Traverse Mountain, subsequently terminating at Lehi FrontRunner.

    By Microtransit

    UTA also operates an On-Demand zone that encompasses the entire city and surrounding South Valley destinations. The price of a trip is equivalent to a one-way bus or one-way light rail ticket. Rides can be requested via the UTA On-Demand App.
