How is the food here?

For a small village with only a few hundred year round residents, Jeffersonville is home to a surprisingly large number of restaurants. This is due to the presence of Smuggler's Notch Resort, one of the largest ski resorts in the Northeast. If you visit outside of ski season, keep in mind that some of these restaurants may be closed.

Get in

By car

Jeffersonville is located at the intersection of VT 15 and VT 108. In winter, it can only be accessed via VT 15. It is roughly 40 minutes from Burlington.

Get around

The village of Jeffersonville can be easily navigated on foot due to its small size. Getting to businesses along VT 108 or at Smuggler's Notch Resort will probably require a car.

See

Most of Jeffersonville's appeal is in its surroundings. The village is situated next to a river and is surrounded by hills and mountains.
