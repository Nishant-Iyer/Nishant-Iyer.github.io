Overview

Bath is a city in Maine on the Kennebec River. It has always been a center for shipbuilding and still builds ships at Bath Iron Works.

4 sights · 1 things to do · 1 places to eat · 5 places to stay

Updated from Wikivoyage on 1 November 2026 · Something wrong or missing? Improve it on Wikivoyage

Best things about Bath

Maine Maritime Museum and Shipyard

Gallery exhibits, historic shipyard site, adult and children's educational programs, special events and narrated excursions along the rivers and coast.

The Chocolate Church Arts Center

Wonderful 1846 Gothic style church is home for offerings of art exhibits, dramatic and musical productions, youth theatre, comedy, jazz, classical and folk artists and more.

Studio Theatre of Bath

Wayne Robbins Woodcarving

Visit the studio of Wayne Robbins, woodcarver of marine mammals and sea birds. Call for appointment.

Bath Country Club Golf Course

18 holes, 6,298 yards, rating: 68.1, slope 126. Carts, clubhouse, range, pro shop, bar grill.
