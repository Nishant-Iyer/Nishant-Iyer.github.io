Overview

Lehi is a city in the Wasatch Range in Utah, United States.

Lehi is a city of 50,000 that is known for Lehi Roller Mills and Thanksgiving Point.

    Get in

    The I-15 freeway runs directly through the middle of Lehi. Exit 279 takes you to Main Street; head west to get to downtown. Exit 284 will take you to Thanksgiving Point.

    FrontRunner. A commuter rail line that runs between Ogden, the Salt Lake City Intermodal Hub, and Provo, passes through Lehi. Service runs every hour on weekdays (with more frequent service during rush hours) and every hour or two on Saturdays. One-way fares range from $2.50-10.30 depending on how far you travel.

    See Thanksgiving Point

    Thanksgiving Point is home to a number of attractions, particularly geared at families, offers varied classes and is often the site of festivals and activities, Check the calendar for special events such as the Scottish Festival (June) and the Great Salt Lake Truck Show (August).

    See Ashton Gardens

    Large, beautiful sculpted grounds hidden in a bowl in the valley. The gardens also include the Children's Discovery Garden with a Noah's Ark play fountain and a small maze. Inside the main entrance building is a gift shop and the Trellis Café (11AM-3PM, $5-10).
