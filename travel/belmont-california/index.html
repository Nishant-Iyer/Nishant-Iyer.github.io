Overview

Belmont is on the Peninsula in the San Francisco Bay Area of California.

2 sights · 2 things to do · 1 places to eat · 3 places to stay

Best things about Belmont

Ralston Hall Mansion

Built in 1868, the Ralston Hall Mansion is a National and California Historic Landmark inside the Notre Dame de Namur University. It is closed because of structural integrity issues resulting from an earthquake.

Len Turner Memorial Vista Point

Near the intersection of Ralston Avenue and Belmont Canyon Road, you can get a great view the San Francisco Bay Area if no fog is present.

Movement Belmont

Place to go if you want to try indoor rock climbing. They also have a fitness gym and yoga lessons.

Water Dog Lake Park & Open Space

Nice little park with a lake in the middle, with nice hiking and biking trails on the hillside. Allows dogs and mountain biking.
