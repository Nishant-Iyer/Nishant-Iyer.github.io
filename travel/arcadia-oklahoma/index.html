Overview

Arcadia is a very small town in Greater Oklahoma City on Route 66 inside the city of Edmond. Edmond entirely surrounds the city of Arcadia.

    Eat Pops

    Breakfast / BrunchDessert / Ice cream

    See the 66-foot-tall soda pop bottle and stop in the gas station, restaurant, gift shop. They sell 700 different kinds of ice-cold sodas and beverages, and they'll turn any bottle into an ice cream float in the restaurant. Lunch and dinner is served every day (10:30AM–9PM; $10–15), and breakfast on the weekends (7AM–10:30AM; $8–12).
