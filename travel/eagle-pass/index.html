Overview

Eagle Pass is a city on the South Texas Plains. It is just across the river from the slightly larger city of Piedras Negras in the Mexican state of Coahuila.

2 sights · 2 things to do · 5 places to eat · 1 bars & cafés · 1 places to stay

Best things about Eagle Pass

Fort Duncan

Several building of this 1847 fort are preserved in a municipal park. In the post headquarters building is an interesting Texas history museum.

Get in

  • US 277, 40 miles west of Carrizo Springs or 60 miles south of Del Rio
  • From San Antonio, the easiest route is south on I-35, then exit onto U.S. Highway 57 at Moore.

Stay safe

Eagle Pass is a frequent border crossing point for both legal and illegal migrants. The legal migrants are rarely a concern but the illegal ones have caused quite a bit of turmoil in Eagle Pass as of late. Local services are under massive strain due to illegal border crossings.

Go next

  • Coahuila, Mexico - Visit Piedras Negras, just across the river, or Guerrero, a Spanish colonial village with important mission ruins another 28 miles down the road.
