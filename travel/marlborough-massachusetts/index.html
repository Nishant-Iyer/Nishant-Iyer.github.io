Do

Do Ghiloni Park/Country Club

One can spend a beautiful day in the Marlborough Country Club (referred to as "Ghiloni Park" by local residents). There are soccer fields, a softball field, basketball courts, and a walking track. You can also play Golf if you have membership. The track is popular for joggers and bicyclers. In the summer, an ice cream truck comes daily to satisfy people's cravings for ice cream in the park.
