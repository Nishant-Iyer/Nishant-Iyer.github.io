Overview

Laguna Hills is a city in Orange County in Southern California. Very much a bedroom community of upper middle to high class residential homes, the community is safe and well maintained.

    Understand

    The city is surrounded by Laguna Woods to the North, Mission Viejo to the east and Laguna Niguel to the west. It is approximately 15 min from the Pacific Ocean and Dana Point.

    Get in

    Laguna Hills is about 50 miles south of Los Angeles on Interstate 5. Exits going south include El Toro Road and Alicia Parkway.

    By plane

    John Wayne Airport is the closest airport.

    Do

    In addition to typical sports such as basketball, tennis, and football, ice skating is available in Aliso Viejo a 5-minute drive away from the center of town. Beach and ocean sports are located at Laguna Beach and Dana Point.

    Buy

    Many shopping malls can be found in Laguna Hills.

    Sleep

    Hotels of various levels abound around the community with high end luxury properties located at Dana Point.
