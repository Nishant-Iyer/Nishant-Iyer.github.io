Do Blue Ridge Tunnel Trail

Walk or cycle through the Blue Ridge Tunnel - a retired rail tunnel built in the 1850s, almost a mile long, that was an engineering marvel in its day. Use the Eastern Trailhead if you want a gentler access route from the parking lot to the tunnel entrance; the Western Trailhead offers a steeper and more challenging path to get in. Going from one trailhead to the other and back again is about 4.5 miles. Going from the Eastern Trailhead to the far end of the tunnel and back again is just under 3 miles. Bring a headlamp or flashlight - there are no lights in the tunnel! Consider bringing a jacket; the tunnel temperature is usually around 55-65F even when the weather outside is much warmer.

Events

  • Fall Foliage Festival Early October.
  • Commemoration the Civil War Battle of Waynesboro. This 1865 battle between Generals Jubal Early and Philip Sheridan helped defeat Old South.
  • Summer Extravaganza Weekend after the 4th of July. Features live music, food, carnival rides, crafts, art, magic shows, cloggers, and more. Runs Saturday morning to Sunday evening, with a fireworks display ending the weekend at sundown on Sunday. Takes place in Ridgeview Park, a public park located near the center of the city

Do riverfest

This free annual event takes place river-side at Constitution Park South. Activities include Reptile World shows, presentations by the Wildlife Center of Virginia, Stream Safari exploration, Fish and Fun Rodeo, canoe rides, kids arts & crafts, interactive exhibits, and more. The day ends with the Great South River Duck Race!
