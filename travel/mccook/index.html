George W. Norris House

An historic house museum, formerly owned by George W. Norris (1862–1944), a Nebraska politician who championed the New Deal of the 1930s and the Rural Electrification Act.

    Get in

    By car

    US Highways 6, 34, and 83.

    By train

    McCook is served once daily in each direction by Amtrak and its California Zephyr service between Chicago and Los Angeles. McCook station is at 101 Norris Ave, at the southern end of the city centre.

    By plane

    McCook Ben Nelson Regional Airport has subsidised Essential Air Service on Boutique Airways to Denver.
