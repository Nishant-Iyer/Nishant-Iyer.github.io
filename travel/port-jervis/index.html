Overview

Port Jervis is a village with about 8,800 inhabitants in Orange County, New York.

4 sights · 2 things to do · 3 places to eat · 2 bars & cafés · 1 places to stay

Best things about Port Jervis

Fort Decker Museum

This stone house built in 1793 was part of a much larger fort at the site. It now functions as a museum, run by the Minisink Valley Historic Society.

Tri-States Monument

Granite monument that marks the tripoint of the state boundaries of New Jersey, New York, and Pennsylvania

Erie Railroad Roundhouse Turntable

The largest functioning rail turntable, originally a part of the Erie railroad.

Port Jervis Free Library

A historic Carnegie library built in 1903.

Deerpark Reformed Church

Organized in 1737, this is one oldest congregation in the area. The current building dates from 1838.

John Kucher Memorial Promenade

A promenade with many amenities such as a fountain, benches, and bike racks. It is named after John Kucher, a local business owner who was killed in a robbery in 2014.

Understand

From late spring to early fall, many thousands of travelers and tourists pass through Port Jervis on their way to enjoying rafting, kayaking, canoeing and other activities in the Delaware Water Gap National Recreation Area and the Upper Delaware Scenic and Recreational River and the surrounding area.

Get in

By car

Highway I-84 passes by just south of the city.

By rail

Metro-North operates a commuter rail service from Hoboken, on the eponymous Port Jervis Line.
