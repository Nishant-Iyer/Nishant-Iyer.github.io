Overview

Calexico is a city in Imperial County in California, located just across the U.S.-Mexican border from Mexicali.

2 places to stay

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Nov–Apr. Summer highs reach about 107°F; winter lows dip to about 41°F. Very hot (May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep, Oct) — plan outdoor time for mornings. Wettest: Jan and Feb. (climate data from nearby Salton Sea)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 2,010 miles, roughly 34 h 30 min without stops. Flying is usually faster.
  • IPL Imperial County Airport, Imperial — 12 mi away. Directions
  • YUM Yuma International Airport / Marine Corps Air Station Yuma, Yuma — 52 mi away. Directions
  • SAN San Diego International Airport, San Diego — 98 mi away (major hub). Directions

Nearest airport for commercial flights by Mokulele Airlines is at Imperial Airport (IPL) located 15 mi north in El Centro. The next nearest airport for commercial flights is in San Diego International Airport (SAN) where travelers can rent a car or take a Greyhound bus to Calexico. From Mexico the nearest airport for commercial flights is in Mexicali (MXL)

By bus

Go Greyhound/Flixbus

Calexico is served by Greyhound from Los Angeles (around 6 hours via Imperial; El Centro, Indio/Palm Springs, San Bernardino); Phoenix (via Yuma, Gila Bend); and San Diego (around 4 hours)

Go Imperial Valley Transit (IV Transit)

Operates buses to El Centro via Herber (Rt #1N/1S); Brawley (Rt 32D); Inter-Calexico Service between US Ports of Entry (EXP), and Imperial Valley College (IPVC) (#21N/21S). From Brawley the #31D bus goes to Calexico. Several eastbound routes stop at the E 3rd & Pauline stop. But, not all buses may stop there so check the website or printed schedules and maps as to where they stop at and when. Bus transit center (220 E 3rd St) may still be under construction.

📍 (bus stop) E 3rd and Pauline☎ +1 760 482-2900

Do

  • Most visitors to Calexico arrive on foot from the sister city Mexicali. From downtown Calexico it's a short walk to the border crossing and several tourist areas on the other side. U.S. citizens do not require a passport, only proof of citizenship, for short trips to the border area of Mexico, but you will need a passport, passport card, or enhanced driver's license to reenter the U.S.
