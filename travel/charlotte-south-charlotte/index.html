Overview

South Charlotte is a conglomeration of neighborhoods in the major city of Charlotte in the U.S. state of North Carolina. The district includes the neighborhoods of Ashley Park, Starmount, Carmel and Cedarwood. It is also home to numerous ethnic restaurants and the Quail Hollow Golf Club, which hosts numerous PGA golf tournaments.

It is bordered by South End, SouthPark, and Cotswold to the north; Ballantyne and Matthews to the south; NC-Highway 160 to the west.
