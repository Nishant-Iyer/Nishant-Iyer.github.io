Best things about Derry

Taylor Mill Historic Site

A sawmill in current operation.

Robert Frost Farm

The farm of notable American poet Robert Frost is now a museum just outside the town's center. Frost lived there and many of his notable poems were written there.

Derry Rail Trail (Bike Path)

The southern portion of the Granite State Rail Trail runs directly through Derry, with multiple easy parking areas in town.

Get in

By air

Manchester - Boston Regional Airport (MHT), Brown Av. Route 101 exit 2, Phone: +1 603 624-6556. A convenient way to get to the city and much of southern New Hampshire. Sometimes used as a low-cost, low hassle alternative to Logan International Airport in Boston.

By bus

Vermont Transit, 119 Canal St. Manchester, Toll free: +1 800-552-8737. Service from Montréal, Vermont, Maine and New Hampshire.

Concord Trailways, 119 Canal St. Manchester, Toll free: +1 800-639-3317. Service from Boston to Manchester. During rush hour, there is also commuter service to nearby Londonderry.

By car

The town is off exit 4 of Interstate 93.

Get around

By car

The main mode of transportation in Derry is by car, with notable attractions being well outside of downtown.

By bicycle

With multiple bicycle paths within Derry, most of the town can be accessed easily and safely by bicycle.
