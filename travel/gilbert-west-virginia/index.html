Overview

Gilbert is a town in Mingo County, in the New River and Greenbrier Valleys of West Virginia, with a population at the 2020 census of 450. The town motto is "you should see the hills from here!"

    Understand

    On the banks of the Guyandotte River, Gilbert is along US-52 roughly halfway between Welch and Williamson. Visited by thousands of tourists each year, scenic Gilbert offers a number of outdoor recreation opportunities in its vicinity, yet remains a destination off the well-beaten tourist path.

    Although it's within Mingo County, Gilbert and its surroundings are closer to the city of Logan in Logan County than to the Mingo County seat of Williamson. Operated by the US Army Corps of Engineers, R.D. Bailey Lake is several miles east of Gilbert on the Guyandotte River and is locally popular for camping and boating. The Hatfield-McCoy ATV trail system has several facilities in the Gilbert area, making the town a popular stop for ATV riders.
