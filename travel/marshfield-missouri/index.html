Best things about Marshfield

Hubble Space Telescope Statue

a 1/4 scale model of the telescope, recognising that Edwin Hubble was born in Marshfield.

Webster County Historical Museum

Museum in the former Carnegie Library. Exhibitions on local history, tomato canning, and Astronomer Edwin P. Hubble.

Hidden Waters Nature Park

11.3 acres park with walking trails and the Callaway Cabin which was built in 1853.

Rotary Park

17 acres park with playground
