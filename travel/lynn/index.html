    Get in

    Given Lynn's proximity to Boston's Logan airport, please refer to the Get In and Get Around sections of that article for most of the details.

    By train

    Use the Newburyport/Rockport Line departing from North Station in Boston to arrive in Lynn. This stop is in the town center, and you'll find many shops and restaurants within walking distance.

    Go Lynn Station

    Lynn Station has been closed and is being rebuilt. A temporary platform will be built at some point in 2023 to restore train service. Shuttle buses will be provided until that point. Parking lot demolished. Bike racks demolished. MBTA zone 2.

    📍 325 Broad St💲 $7 to BostonChecked Oct 2022
    MapsWebsite

    By bus

    Various MBTA bus routes travel to and throughout Lynn.

    From the south or east, you can use buses 441, 442, or 455 to get to Lynn from Wonderland station. From Saugus you can use buses 426 and 429 to get to Lynn. From Peabody, use buses 435 and 436. From Salem, bus 450 and 455 can get to Lynn.

    By boat

    The MBTA operates a seasonal ferry service from Boston to Lynn.

    By car

    You will need to use Route 107 or Route 1A to get to Lynn. You can also use Route 129 from the northwest.

    Get around

    By car

    Routes 1A, 129, 129A, and 107 go throughout Lynn

    By bus

    MBTA buses 441 and 442 travel along Route 1A of Lynn. Buses 424, 450, and 455 travel along Route 107 of Lynn. Bus 426 travels along Route 129. Most bus routes go to the Lynn commuter rail station, including bus 455 and bus 429.
