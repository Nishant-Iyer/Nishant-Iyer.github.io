Overview

Garland, which is sometimes known as Garland City, is a small town of about 240 people in Arkansas, about 22 miles east of Texarkana.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Mar–May, Sep–Nov. Summer highs reach about 93°F; winter lows dip to about 35°F. Very hot (Jul, Aug) — plan outdoor time for mornings. Wettest: Dec and Jan. (climate data from nearby Arcadia)
