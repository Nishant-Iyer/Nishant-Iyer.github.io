Overview

Kentucky Derby Region is Kentucky's most famous and most visited region, home to the state's biggest city, Louisville, and of course, the world famous Kentucky Derby.

Plan your trip

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Apr–Oct. Summer highs reach about 89°F; winter lows dip to about 28°F. Wettest: May and Apr. (climate data from nearby Louisville)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 360 miles, roughly 6 h without stops.
  • SDF Louisville Muhammad Ali International Airport, Louisville — 24 mi away (major hub). Directions
  • OWB Owensboro Daviess County Airport, Owensboro — 68 mi away. Directions

Sample 3-day trip

  1. Day 1: Louisville — McAlpine Locks and Dam, Jefferson Memorial Forest, Zachary Taylor National Cemetery.
  2. Day 2: Bardstown — Maker's Mark Distillery, My Old Kentucky Home State Park, Wickland, Home of Three Governors.
  3. Day 3: Shelbyville — American Saddlebred Horse Farm Tours, Science Hill, University of Louisville Golf Club.
