Overview

American Fork is in the Wasatch Range, a region in north central Utah. It is on the north border of Utah Lake, about 30 miles south of Salt Lake City.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Sep. Summer highs reach about 90°F; winter lows dip to about 21°F. Very hot (Jul) — plan outdoor time for mornings. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: May and Oct. (climate data from nearby Timpanogos Cave National Monument)

    Understand

    Oftentimes, citizens in American Fork are made fun of for saying, "American Fark" or "mount'n" (without emphasizing the t), but most of them will get frustrated when you mention this, because it doesn't actually reflect most people in the city.

    Do

    • Timpanogos Cave National Monument, UT-92 - take I-15 exit 284 and follow that east 10 miles. Moderately difficult hike to a large cave (45-60 minute tour).

    Sleep

    Stay 5th East Hall

    Exotic themed B&B rooms, large bathtubs for 2 people, fireplaces. You'll find themed hotel suites with waterfalls, real sand, a king sized round bed, etc.
