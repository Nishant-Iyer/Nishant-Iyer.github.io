Overview

Magnolia is a village of 1,000 people (2018) in Stark County and Carroll County, Ohio.

1 sights · 2 things to do · 2 places to eat · 1 bars & cafés

Best things about Magnolia

St. Mary's of Morges

Roman Catholic Church built in 1853. Now listed on National Register of Historic Places.

Whitacre Greer Equestrian Park

Six miles of trails for equestrian use only between Magnolia and Waynesburg.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Apr–Oct. Summer highs reach about 84°F; winter lows dip to about 20°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Jun and May. (climate data from nearby Akron)
