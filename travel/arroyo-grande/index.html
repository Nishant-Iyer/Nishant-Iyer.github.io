Overview

Arroyo Grande (population 17,000) is in San Luis Obispo County in the Central Coast region of California. It is part of the Five Cities Metropolitan Area which also includes Oceano to the south-west, Grover Beach to the west, and Pismo Beach and Shell Beach to the north-west.

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 2,230 miles, roughly 38 h 30 min without stops. Flying is usually faster.
  • SBP San Luis County Regional Airport, San Luis Obispo — 9 mi away. Directions
  • SMX Santa Maria Public Airport Captain G Allan Hancock Field, Santa Maria — 17 mi away. Directions
  • FAT Fresno Yosemite International Airport, Fresno — 124 mi away (major hub). Directions
