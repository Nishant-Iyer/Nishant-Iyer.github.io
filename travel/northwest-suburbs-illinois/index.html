Overview

The Northwest Suburbs are in Chicagoland, primarily in northwestern Cook, southern Lake and northern DuPage County, centered along Metra’s North Central Service, though encompassing areas well beyond the rail line. The Northwestern Suburbs constitute the largest portion of Chicagoland outside of the city.

For further-out suburbs, see Fox River Valley.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Oct. Summer highs reach about 85°F; winter lows dip to about 20°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: May and Jun. (climate data from nearby Chicago)

  • MDW Chicago Midway International Airport, Chicago — 17 mi away (major hub). Directions

Understand

Like much of Chicagoland, the Northwest Suburbs originated from the population boom following World War II. With the influx of new residents, easy access to Chicago, and a boom around O'Hare International Airport, the area grew quickly and has some of the largest communities in the suburbs. Visitors will quickly note that many of these suburbs contain a mixture of 1950s-style homes and modern mansions, sometimes alongside one another.

Get in

With its proximity to O'Hare International Airport, much of the Northwest Suburbs area is accessible via both I-90 and I-94. Other major routes include US 20 and IL 53.

Go Chicago Executive Airport (PWK, formerly Palwaukee Municipal Airport)

Chicago Executive Airport is a general aviation airport. It is the fourth busiest airport in Illinois, despite only operating charter flights.

Get around

  • I-90 runs east-west through the southern part of the region, while I-94 runs north-south through the eastern part of the region.
  • The Metra North Central Service runs north-south, but it doesn’t run on weekends or holidays.

Go next

Visitors to the Northwest Suburbs can go in a variety of directions to many places, such as:
