    Understand

    Bainbridge is located north of Lake Seminole on the Flint River in southwest Georgia. Fishing, camping, sightseeing and waterskiing are popular with locals and visitors alike. Bainbridge hosts many bass fishing tournaments due to its size and proximity to the lake. Several Wildlife Management Areas provide abundant hunting activities.
