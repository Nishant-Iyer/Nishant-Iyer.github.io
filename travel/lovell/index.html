    Understand

    Lovell is the largest town in Big Horn County with a population of about 2,250 (2020). It is the gateway to the Bighorn Canyon National Recreation Area South District.

    Get in

    Lovell is located along US Route 14A, US Route 310, and Wyoming Highway 789.

    See

    See EJZ Bridge over Shoshone River

    The EJZ Bridge over Shoshone River is a Warren pony truss bridge located near Lovell, which carries Big Horn County Road CN9-111 (Cowley-Lovell Road) across the Shoshone River. The bridge was added to the National Register of Historic Places in 1985.

    See Pryor Mountain Wild Mustang Center

    The Pryor Mountain Wild Mustang Center is dedicated to preserving the future of the wild horses of the Pryor Mountains. Visitors can learn about the color, conformation, and behavior of the Pryor Mountain Wild Horses as well as the history of the Pryor Mountain Wild Horse Range. Visitors can learn how and where to best view the wild horses as the Center keeps track of the current location and status of individual horses.
