Overview

Trenton is a city in northwest Missouri and the county seat of Grundy County. It is located in the rural Green Hills region of northern Missouri and serves as a local center for the surrounding communities.

      Understand

      Trenton is a small city with a relaxed, rural atmosphere. The city and surrounding area offer parks, local businesses, historic sites, and access to the countryside of northern Missouri.

      Trenton is also a convenient base for exploring Grundy County and nearby communities in the Green Hills region.

      Get in

      Trenton is accessible by road via U.S. Route 65 and Missouri Route 6. These highways connect Trenton with other communities in northern Missouri.

      Get around

      A car is the most practical way to get around Trenton. The city's central areas can also be explored on foot.

      See

      • Trenton Downtown - The city's central business district, with local shops, restaurants, and historic buildings.
      • Grundy County Courthouse - The county courthouse in downtown Trenton.
      • Trenton City Park - A public park with space for recreation and outdoor activities.
      • Swan Lake National Wildlife Refuge - A nearby wildlife refuge known for wetlands, forests, and birdwatching.

      Do

      • Explore downtown - Walk around Trenton's central business district and visit its local shops and businesses.
      • Visit the parks - Spend some time outdoors at Trenton's parks and recreational areas.
      • Explore the surrounding countryside - Drive through the rural landscapes and small communities around Grundy County.
      • Visit nearby Swan Lake National Wildlife Refuge - Explore the refuge's trails, wetlands, and wildlife-viewing areas.

      Buy

      Trenton has several local stores and businesses where visitors can purchase everyday goods, groceries, clothing, and other supplies. Most shops are concentrated around the downtown and main commercial areas.

      Eat

      Trenton has several restaurants and locally owned eateries offering American food, pizza, sandwiches, and other casual meals. Most restaurants are located along the city's main roads and around downtown.

      Drink

      Trenton has several places to get coffee, soft drinks, and other non-alcoholic beverages. Coffee shops and casual restaurants are the main options for visitors looking for a drink.

      Sleep

      Trenton has a small selection of places to stay, including motels and other accommodations. Additional lodging options can be found in nearby communities.

      Go next

      • Chillicothe - A nearby city to the southeast with historic sites, shopping, and restaurants.
      • Cameron - A city to the southwest with parks, shops, and places to eat.
      • Maryville - A larger regional center to the northwest with Northwest Missouri State University.
      • St. Joseph - A larger city to the west known for its historic downtown and museums.
      • Kansas City - A major metropolitan area farther southwest with museums, sports, shopping, and entertainment.
