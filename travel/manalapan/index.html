Understand

The name "Manalapan" comes from the Lenape word for land of good bread or good land to settle upon.

Manalapan Township is primarily a suburban area and was originally formed by an Act of the New Jersey Legislature on March 9, 1848, The area was created from existing portions of Freehold Township.

Englishtown was formed as a borough from portions of Manalapan on January 4, 1888, based on the results of a referendum held the previous day.

Yorketown is an unincorporated area located within Manalapan Township.

The Battle of Monmouth was fought in Monmouth Battlefield State Park, which is in Freehold Township and Manalapan.

By train

While there is no designated station for this particular township, the nearest train station, by distance, is Aberdeen-Matawan, which runs along the North Jersey Coast Line towards New York City/Hoboken and Bay Head. The station situates on the intersection of Main Street & Atlantic Avenue in Matawan, New Jersey, approximately 10 miles/23 minutes away from the northern township border between Marlboro and Manalapan.

By car

U.S Highway 9, New Jersey Turnpike, Route 33, Garden State Parkway, Route 527

  • U.S. Route 9 is in the northeast
  • State Route 33 passes through near the southern part of the township.
  • County routes 522 and 527 traverse the municipality.
  • County route 537 follows the southeast border of the township.

Other major roads provide access to the area.

  • Garden State Parkway is in bordering Old Bridge
  • Interstate 195 is in bordering Millstone Township.
  • New Jersey Turnpike Interstate 95 is in East Windsor Township (Exit 8) and bordering Monroe Township (Exit 8A).

By bus

New Jersey Transit drop-off location Union Hill Road or Gordon’s Corner Rd
