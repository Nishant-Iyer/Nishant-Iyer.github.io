Overview

Millersburg is a city of 3,200 people (2019) in Wooster area Ohio that is located about midway between Cleveland and Columbus. Millersburg is the county seat of Holmes County

    Understand

    The Amish comprise nearly half of the county's population of 44,000, making this region the largest Amish settlement in the world. The area also has many Mennonites in the population. When travelling to and driving in this area, be alert for the horse-drawn Amish buggies.

    Buy

    The area around Millersburg is loaded with cottage industry and craftsmen who offer their wares along the road.

    Shop Three Feathers Pewter

    Since 1984 Three Feathers Pewter has been creating functional tableware in the Early American style, including plates, bowls, goblets, spoons, candlestick holders as well as an extensive line of jewelry of traditional symbols and shapes, plus various styles of tree ornaments and buttons for sewing, and accurate reenactment of various historical periods (American Civil War, French and Indian War, Celtic Garb, etc). Customers here experience the pewter in person, view the workshop from the gallery while speaking with craftsmen. Three Feathers Pewter both retails and wholesales pewter items.
