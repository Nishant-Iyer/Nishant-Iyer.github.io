Understand

New Carlisle was established in 1810 and certified as an Ohio city in 1973. The downtown area is a historic area, and was built back in 1810.

Get around

The downtown area is best navigated on foot. As there are no hotels in central New Carlisle, a car is recommended to get to a hotel. There are no buses.
