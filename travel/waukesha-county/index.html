Overview

Waukesha County is a county in Southeast Wisconsin and home to Wisconsin's seventh largest city, Waukesha.

Plan your trip

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Sep. Summer highs reach about 82°F; winter lows dip to about 17°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Jun and Apr. (climate data from nearby Milwaukee)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 100 miles, roughly 2 h without stops.
  • MKE General Mitchell International Airport, Milwaukee — 18 mi away (major hub). Directions
  • MSN Dane County Regional Truax Field, Madison — 56 mi away. Directions

Sample 3-day trip

  1. Day 1: Delafield — Hawks Inn Historical Museum and Visitors Center Tours, Lake Nagawicka, Glacial Drumlin State Trail.
  2. Day 2: Waukesha — Chicago and North Western Depot, Carroll University, Waukesha County Historical Society Museum.
  3. Day 3: Brookfield — Dousman Stagecoach Inn Museum, Bluemound Bowl, Sharon Lynne Wilson Center for the Arts.

Get around

Car is the primary method of transportation.

By bus

  • Waukesha Metro has fixed-route service in the City and County of Waukesha. There is less coverage farther away from downtown Waukesha. Not all routes operate on Sunday.
  • Waukesha County Transit has commuter services focused on downtown Milwaukee, with services from Oconomowoc, Nashotah, Hardland, Pewaukee, Delafield, Waukesha, and Brookfield.
