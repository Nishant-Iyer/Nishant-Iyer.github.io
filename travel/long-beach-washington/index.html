Overview

Long Beach is a beachfront city in southwestern Washington State. It is the largest city on the Long Beach Peninsula, claimed to be the longest continuous beach in the United States.

This article also includes the community of Seaview, immediately to the south.

Washington State International Kite Festival

Third weekend of August. The largest kite festival in North America.

Cranberry Museum

Small museum displaying farming equipment and the history of cranberry farming in the region, next to a demonstration cranberry bog.

Funland Family Fun Center

Arcade games, including virtual reality, and laser tag.

    Get in

    Washington State Route 103 (SR-103) is the main street through town.

    U.S. Highway 101 (US-101) connects to SR-103 less than 1 mi south of town, linking the peninsula to Astoria and the Pacific Coast.

    Go next

    Further up the peninsula are the small towns of Ocean Park and Oysterville.

    To the south is the fishing town of Ilwaco and Cape Disappointment State Park.

    The nearest substantially larger city is Astoria, across the Columbia River in Oregon.
