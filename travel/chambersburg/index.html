Best things about Chambersburg

Old Jail

Built in 1818, the jail was a stop on the Underground Railroad and remained after Chambersburg was burned by the Confederates in 1864. Tours anytime during the day.

Chambersburg Heritage Center

County history museum, featuring exhibits on local history and a children's activity room.

Penn National Golf Club

It is a complete golf course which received a four star rating from Golf Digest magazine. 3-day packages for $300.

Rhodes Grove Camp

Year-round Christian camp surrounded by farmland; camping, retreats and various other activities including swimming, horseback riding, canoeing, water sliding.

Capital Theater

Various performances during the year.

Nellie Fox Bowl & Sport Shop

Twenty lanes, party packages, laser bowling.

Understand

This town is located on the Lincoln Highway and an hour away southwest of Harrisburg. For a small town, Chambersburg offers a wide range of activities for people of all ages. Since the town is 15 minutes away from Shippensburg University, many students spend most of their weekends in Chambersburg. This town is also the home of Wilson College. It is a town that has been around since 1730 and has been growing ever since.

Get in

By car

  • When traveling from Philadelphia, take I-76 W toward Harrisburg.
  • On 76 W, take exit 226 for US 11 toward I 81/Chambersburg/Carlisle.
  • Continue on US 11 and take the exit to I-81 S toward Chambersburg and get off at exit 16.
