Best things about Willoughby

Squire's Castle

The ruins of a manor house.

Kirtland Temple

The first temple of the Latter Day Saint movement, dedicated by Joseph Smith in 1836. The temple is now operated by the Community of Christ which offers tours of the building.

Lake County Captains

A local minor league baseball team.

North Chagrin Reservation

The Fine Arts Association
