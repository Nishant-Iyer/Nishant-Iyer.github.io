Overview

Rosemont is a city in Cook County in the state of Illinois, just outside of Chicago. It does enormous business as the primary hotel supplier for O'Hare International Airport, but has a couple of the region's best-known concert venues as well.

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 18 miles, roughly under an hour without stops.
  • ORD Chicago O'Hare International Airport, Chicago — 2 mi away (major hub). Directions
  • MDW Chicago Midway International Airport, Chicago — 15 mi away (major hub). Directions

The CTA Blue Line has a stop in Rosemont, on River Road between I-190 and I-90 right before O'Hare. And Metra's North Central Line stops at Rosemont Station on Balmoral Avenue just west of I-294, with service between Chicago and Antioch.

Get around

PACE buses serve the area, including the free Rosemont Entertainment Circulator (route 811), and hotels can be expected to run shuttles to the airport. For anything else, though, you'll likely want to have a car.

Do Polish National Catholic Cemetery

A quaint cemetery set along the Des Plaines River, it possesses a charming array of old monuments and markers. It is also the only Chicagoland cemetery with its own picnic area, preserving the old world custom of picnicking at the graves of deceased loved ones.

📍 9200 West Higgins Rd.

Do AMC Dine-In Rosemont 12

This huge movie theater, over 100,000 square feet, is more than just a theater. For those 21 and up, the VIP Theatre has free popcorn, bigger seats, and cocktails. There's a full-service restaurant on site (you can eat while watching a movie), a playroom, and child care for kids age 3-10.

Events

Do Midwest FurFest

One of the world's two largest furry conventions (along with Anthrocon), held in Illinois since 2000, and in Rosemont since 2010. Over 16,000 attended in 2024, many of them costumed mascots and fursuiters. The event is held in late November or early December each year; admission is charged and prices vary depending on if you pay in advance or at the door, or if you choose a higher tier with more perks.

💲 Basic entry $80 if purchased in advance, $90 at the doorChecked Jan 2024
MapsWebsite
  • Anime Central. One of the largest anime conventions in the USA (and definitely largest in the state), ACen books all five hotels out near the Hyatt Regency during the second or third weekend in May. Admission starts at $60 for the whole weekend.

Drink

Each 'upscale' hotel in Rosemont includes a hotel bar (and in the Hyatt Regency, a semi-Starbucks), but these can be pricy.

You can also drink and eat at the Rivers Casino's buffets. Its creation and build created much controversy only a few years ago.
