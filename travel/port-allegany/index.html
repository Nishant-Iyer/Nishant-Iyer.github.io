Overview

Port Allegany is a borough located in the mountains of northwest Pennsylvania. It is located on the border of McKean and Potter Counties. Route 6 is the Main Street of Port Allegany. The atmosphere is of a small town Americana with a populace of just over 2,100.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Sep. Summer highs reach about 81°F; winter lows dip to about 14°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Jun and Jul. (climate data from nearby Titusville)
