Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Mar–May, Sep–Nov. Summer highs reach about 92°F; winter lows dip to about 33°F. Very hot (Jul, Aug) — plan outdoor time for mornings. Wettest: Apr and Mar. (climate data from nearby Memphis)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

Get in

By plane

Go Tupelo Regional Airport (TUP)

Served only by Contour Airlines to Nashville International Airport (BNA).

Google MapsWebsite
  • Golden Triangle Regional Airport (GTR) is 1 hour and 10 minutes south of Tupelo. It flies to Atlanta.
  • Memphis International Airport (MEM) is 1 hour and 40 minutes northwest. It was a key Delta Air Lines hub and is also served by six other airlines.
  • Birmingham–Shuttlesworth International Airport (BHM) in Birmingham, Alabama. This airport is about 2.5 hours southeast.

By car

Tupelo is on US Route 78, between Memphis and Birmingham.

Get around

Tupelo requires a car to see freely. A tour bus will keep you on the main tourist trail.
