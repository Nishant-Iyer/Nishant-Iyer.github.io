Best things about Uvalde

Fort Inge

This 1849 US Cavalry post is located in a large park with hiking trails.

Janey Slaughter Briscoe Opera House

Restored 1891 Grand Opera House.

John Nance Garner House

Home of former U.S. Vice President John Nance Garner and a National Historic Landmark.
