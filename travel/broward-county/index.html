Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Nov–Apr. Summer highs reach about 90°F; winter lows dip to about 61°F. Very hot (Jul, Aug) — plan outdoor time for mornings. Wettest: Jun and Sep. (climate data from nearby Fort Lauderdale)

Regions

Broward County can be generally defined by two distinct regions: the densely-populated east coast metropolitan area, which abuts the Atlantic Ocean and immediately inland; and the essentially uninhabitable western portion making up part of the Everglades.

By bus

Place Broward County Transit (BCT)

The county bus system. BCT provides local bus services in Broward County and connections with the bus systems in other parts of the metropolitan area: Metrobus in Miami-Dade County and Palm Tran in Palm Beach County. Buses are available, but often slow and inconvenient. There is a push to increase bus service.

Place LauderGO! Community Shuttle

A free shuttle serving the downtown, beach, and convention center areas. It also provides a link from downtown to the Broward Blvd Tri-Rail station.
