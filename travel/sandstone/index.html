Overview

Sandstone is a town in Northeastern Minnesota.

3 sights · 3 things to do · 1 places to eat · 1 places to stay

Best things about Sandstone

Banning State Park

State park located along the Kettle River to the north and east of Sandstone. The park contains the remains of a historic sandstone quarry.

Sandstone History and Art Center

Small museum with displays on the Great Hinckley Fire of 1894, sandstone quarries, river dams, local Native Americans, and more.

Robinson Park

Site of a former sandstone quarry on the west bank of the Kettle River.

Midwest Country Music Theater

Venue hosting traditional country music performances.

Sandstone Ice Festival

The ice festival takes place annually for one weekend in December, and features ice climbing, skinny skiing, snow shoeing, and winter camping.

Kettle River Paddle Festival

The Paddle Festival takes place annually in May, and features canoeing, kayaking, and rafting along the Kettle River.
