Overview

The Southern Coast is part of Maine. It is coextensive with York County.

Plan your trip

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Sep. Summer highs reach about 80°F; winter lows dip to about 16°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Oct and Dec. (climate data from nearby Seacoast)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 1,060 miles, roughly 18 h 30 min without stops. Flying is usually faster.
  • PWM Portland International Jetport, Portland — 22 mi away (major hub). Directions
  • PSM Portsmouth International Airport at Pease, Portsmouth — 27 mi away. Directions

Sample 3-day trip

  1. Day 1: Ogunquit — Ogunquit Playhouse, Ogunquit Museum of American Art, Ogunquit Beach.
  2. Day 2: York — Boon Island Lighthouse, The Museum Buildings of Old York, Jefferds' Tavern.
  3. Day 3: Kennebunkport — Seashore Trolley Museum, The Franciscan Monastery, The Kennebunkport History Center.
