Overview

Maggie Valley is a tourist destination in the Smoky Mountains of North Carolina. It is semi-popular (although not as much as it once was) due to its close proximity to the Great Smoky Mountains National Park. It is a popular destination for skiing as the Cataloochee Ski Area is located nearby.

    Understand

    Maggie Valley was originally incorporated in 1904. The town was named after a young girl named Maggie Mae Setzer who was the daughter of Jack Setzer. Jack Setzer incorporated the town as he is the one who gave it a post office.

    The town became a tourist destination after the Ghost Town in the Sky Amusement Park opened in 1961. Ghost Town has since closed in 2009 and has not reopened due to many factors. Still, the many tourist attractions and natural beauty of the area draw many.

    Get in

    By plane

    The nearest airport to Maggie Valley is Asheville Regional Airport.

    By car

    Cars are the most common way to get to Maggie Valley. Traffic heading west on I-40 from Asheville should take exit 27 for the US-74/Great Smoky Mountains Expressway then take exit 103 for US-19 toward Maggie Valley and Cherokee and continue straight. I-40 traffic from Tennessee should take exit 20 for US-276 which will take you directly to US-19.

    Get around

    By car

    Getting around Maggie Valley by car is fairly simple, as most of the town resides along US-19. Almost all tourist attractions are on this road.

    By foot

    Sidewalks are present in the town's most popular areas and easily walk-able.
