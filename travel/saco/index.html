Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Sep. Summer highs reach about 80°F; winter lows dip to about 16°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Oct and Dec. (climate data from nearby Seacoast)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

Getting there

  • From you: to see whether to drive or fly, and how long it takes.
  • PWM Portland International Jetport, Portland — 12 mi away (major hub). Directions
  • PSM Portsmouth International Airport at Pease, Portsmouth — 35 mi away. Directions

There are also buses from Portland 6 times a day Monday through Saturday and 5 times on Sunday operated by Biddeford Saco Old Orchard Beach Transit. The fare from Portland to Saco is $2 with the trip taking just over an hour.
