Overview

White Sulphur Springs is in the Smith River Valley of Central Montana, and is the county seat of Meagher County. It is known for its namesake hot springs.

Best things about White Sulphur Springs

Castle Museum

'The Castle' is an imposing mansion built in 1892 by Bryon Roger Sherman, and is now the home of the Meagher County Museum.

Showdown Ski Area

Family friendly ski area in the Belt Mountains. Restaurant, coffee bar, and saloon.

Spa Hot Springs Motel & Clinic

Three naturally heated mineral pools (2 outdoor, 1 indoor) which are cleaned and refilled nightly.

Red Ants Pants Music Festival

Annual outdoor music festival.
