Overview

McHenry is a city in Garrett County. McHenry is known for Deep Creek Lake, a picturesque lake with many large vacation homes. People tend to visit McHenry for skiing during the winter and to get away from the heat in the summer.

See Garrett Lakes Arts Festival

The Garrett Lakes Arts Festival's mission is to enrich our community with high-quality performing arts and arts education. Garrett Lakes Arts Festival is the largest presenter of performing arts and arts education in beautiful Garrett County, Maryland, and serves the entire tri-state region (Maryland, Pennsylvania, and West Virginia. Our performance season runs during the spring and summer, with most performances taking place at the Garrett College auditorium.
