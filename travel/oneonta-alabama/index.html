Overview

Oneonta is a small town in the mountains of Alabama, home to the Covered Bridge Festival. For once it seems the North and South have some commonality, as this Oneonta bears the name after the one in New York.

Best things about Oneonta

Easley Covered Bridge

A single lane covered bridge built in 1927.

Swann Covered Bridge

Another single lane bridge, built a little later in 1933, that stretches some 324 feet.

Horton Mill Covered Bridge

A really quite striking covered bridge from 1934 with a length of 220 feet.

Nectar Falls

Like a little water terrace with the after effects of an abandoned covered bridge nearby.

Palisades Park

Some rather enthralling cliffs and viewpoints here, plus a historic Quilter's Cottage.

Covered Bridge Festival

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Mar–May, Sep–Nov. Summer highs reach about 92°F; winter lows dip to about 33°F. Very hot (Jul, Aug) — plan outdoor time for mornings. Wettest: Dec and Mar. (climate data from nearby Mountains)
