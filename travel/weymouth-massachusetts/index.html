Best things about Weymouth

Abigail Adams House

July-Labor Day, Tu-Su. Birthplace of Abigail Smith Adams in 1744. She was the daughter of a local clerygyman, wife of President John Adams, and the mother of President John Quincy Adams.

Town Hall

Built in 1928, it is a replica of the old Massachusetts State House.

Webb Memorial Park

River Street. A part of Boston Harbor Islands, a national park area, this peninsula reserve has views of Boston and the Harbor Islands, trails for walking, shores for fishing and places for picnicking.

    Get in

    • Logan International Airport in Boston serves the area.

    Get around
