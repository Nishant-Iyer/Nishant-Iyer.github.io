Overview

Manchester is a town in Massachusetts with a population of about 5,000 (2020). It is also known as Manchester-by-the-Sea.

4 sights · 1 things to do · 8 places to eat · 1 bars & cafés · 1 places to stay

Best things about Manchester

Singing Beach

Beach Street. A popular beach named for its Singing Sand.

Masconomo Park

Has mini baseball field, gazebo, and toddler playground. The park is lined with benches along the bay with boat docks.

White Beach and Black Cove

Boston is in the distance from the view of White Beach, with soft sand. Black Cove is rockier, but has a sandbar at low tide.

Tuck's Point

Smaller property hidden away with a gazebo, beach, lawn, docks, and the Manchester Yacht Club.

The Ocean Lawn

Walk the trail through the woods to find a view of the ocean, able to see Singing Beach around the cliff. Park closes after dusk.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Sep. Summer highs reach about 80°F; winter lows dip to about 18°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Oct and Dec. (climate data from nearby Beverly)

Get in

MBTA's Commuter rail provides service from North Station on its Rockport branch. Tickets are $6.75 one way.

About 45 minutes from Boston and 15 minutes from the Danvers Liberty Tree Mall.

From route 128, take exit 16 to Pine Street or exit 15 to School Street. Route 127 also enters Manchester from Beverly, and continues through Gloucester and Rockport.
