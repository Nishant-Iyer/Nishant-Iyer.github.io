Overview

Clifton Park is a city in the Hudson Valley in the Capital District region of New York. It is in Saratoga County between Albany and Saratoga Springs in upstate New York. Home of one of the largest school districts in the state, it has plenty of attractions, and close access to Albany and Saratoga.

Best things about Clifton Park

Mohawk Towpath

An official New York Scenic Bypass, you can walk or drive along the Mohawk River for some fresh air. Just follow the signs.

Visher Ferry Nature and Historic Preserve

These 41 acres of land was part of the original Erie Canal. Bring a friend or a dog and explore the trails at any time of the year by foot, ski, or snowshoe.

Stony Creek Reservoir

A prime place for fishing or relaxing by the water.

Dwaas Kill Nature Preserve

250 acres of technology free environment to explore.

Colin's Park

Located across from the main campus of Shenendehowa School District, this park is a popular place to bring the kids for a picnic in the afternoon and play on the swing set.

Longkill Park

Located in the northern part of Clifton Park, this is a popular park for high school kids to play a game of pick up, whether it be football, Frisbee, kickball, or basketball.

Get in

By plane

Albany International Airport

By car

Located on I-87, it is 10 minutes north of Albany and 15 minutes south of Saratoga Springs.

By train

There are Amtrak stations are in Saratoga and Rensalear. Service runs between Montreal and New York City.

By bus

Greyhound runs through the town with a stop at Exit 8 Park and Ride.

Do

Do Clifton Common

This has a little bit of everything. An outdoor concert venue with free concerts every Sunday, 6 softball/baseball fields, a multitude of soccer fields, basketball courts, a full playground and extra room for whatever else you want to play. Also the home of the ice arena and tournaments for a variety of sports.

📍 7 Clifton Common Blvd

The capital region's premier ice arena is the home to figure skating, hockey, and free skating. Open year round, go check out the hours to see when you can drop by for some fun.
