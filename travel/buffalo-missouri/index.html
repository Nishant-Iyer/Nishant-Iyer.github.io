    Understand

    Buffalo is part of the Springfield, Missouri Metropolitan Statistical Area. The population was 3,312 in the 2020 census. It is the county seat of Dallas County.

    Get in

    By plane

    • Buffalo Municipal Airport
    • Springfield-Branson Airport

    By car

    • U.S. Route 65 and Missouri Routes 32 and 73

    By bus

    • Greyhound in Springfield has regular service running up and down I44. Buses typically transfer in Tulsa for onward service to Dallas & Austin.

    Get around

    By taxi

    • Yellow Taxi is the primary taxi service in Springfield though others do exist.
    • User serves Springfield and surrounding areas.
