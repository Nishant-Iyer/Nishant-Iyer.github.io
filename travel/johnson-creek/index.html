    Get in

    By plane

    • Dane County Regional Airport in Madison is 33 miles away and the closest airport with commercial flights.
    • It may be cheaper to fly into Milwaukee Mitchell International Airport or Chicago O'Hare International Airport and drive the rest of the way.
    • The closest airport for general aviation is Dodge County Airport in Juneau (Wisconsin), which is 27 miles away.

    By car

    I-94 goes through Johnson Creek. State route WI-26 also runs through the city. It is 45 minutes from Madison on I-94 from the west, and 45 minutes from Milwaukee and 2 hr 15 min from Chicago on I-94 from the east. It is 2 hours from Green Bay on I-41 and WI-26.

    By bus

    • Badger Bus stops in Johnson Creek on its line from Madison to Milwaukee. The route comes 4-6 times per day. Tickets to or from Johnson Creek must be purchased online and in advance, as the bus does not stop if no passengers are getting on or off.
