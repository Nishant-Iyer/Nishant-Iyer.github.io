    Understand

    Jupiter is a town located in northern Palm Beach County, Florida, near the Martin County border. According to the 2020 census, the town had a total population of 61,047.

    Get in

    By plane

    Go President Donald J. Trump International Airport (Formerly Palm Beach International Airport (PBI)))

    This is a small, yet highly adequate airport. While both the Miami and the Fort Lauderdale airports tend to be pretty busy, DJT is easy to get in and out of, and is about five minutes from downtown West Palm Beach. Travelers flying into South Florida or the Bahamas should strongly consider DJT. The airport serves both air carriers (airlines) and general aviation aircraft. The airport has a 24-hour FAA control tower and a U.S. Customs & Immigration port of entry facility. The general aviation traffic at DJT has a choice of three fixed-base operators.

    By car

    & Interstate 95 and SR 706: The easiest way to reach Jupiter is by car. Interstate 95 goes through the western outskirts of Jupiter. Taking Exit 87B or 87A will take you onto State Road (SR) 706, which becomes Indiantown Rd, where you can then go eastwards to enter Jupiter. Continuing east will take you through miles of suburban development, before crossing Indiantown Bridge and reaching coastal Jupiter, where you can go north or south to access the beach and the many resorts along it.

    and US Highway 1 and SR A1A: US-1 enters Jupiter close to the coast, and it intersects Indiantown Road. A1A runs parallel to US-1 and also intersects Indiantown before merging into US-1 further north.

    Old Dixie Highway: The northern end of Old Dixie Highway goes through the middle of Jupiter before merging onto US-1. Other than taking US-1, it's a reliable way to get into Jupiter.

    By train

    The nearest train station is in Magnolia Park, the current northernmost terminus for Tri-Rail. It's recommended that you have someone waiting at the station to pick you up, or have a taxi ready, as the station is about an hour away from Jupiter. To reach Jupiter by public transportation (via Palm Tran), you can take Route 33 north to The Gardens Mall, then switch to Route 10 to continue heading north to Jupiter. You could also take Route 31 north to the West Palm Beach VA Medical Center, then switch to Route 3 north to the Gardens Mall, and Route 10 to Jupiter.

    There are plans by both Tri-Rail and Brightline to expand northward and eventually have a station of some kind in Jupiter. Tri-Rail's planned station would be placed between Toney Penna Drive and Jupiter Lakes Boulevard, with it acting as the terminus of the new "Green Line".

    Get around

    By car

    The easiest way to get around Jupiter and South Florida is by car. The town is set up on grid system, and is fairly easy to navigate. Downtown is roughly 20 minutes north east of President Donald J. Trump International Airport. Palm Beach County is served by Interstate I-95 and U.S. Highway 1 and US 441. It is also served by Florida's Turnpike.

    See Loggerhead Marinelife Center

    A nonprofit sea turtle research, rehabilitation, education, and conservation facility with interactive exhibits, viewing of sea turtles in their care, and guided tours. The facility also helps operate the nearby pier and is right next to a park and nature trail. Every year, a festival known as TurtleFest is hosted in the park behind the facility. It is a free-admission event that contains interactive educational exhibits as well as music, art, shopping, and games.
