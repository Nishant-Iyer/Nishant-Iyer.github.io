    Understand

    Harrodsburg, the first permanent English settlement west of the Allegheny Mountains, was the site of a fort built by James Harrod in 1775.

    In 1805, Shaker Village of Pleasant Hill, or Shakertown, was founded when 44 converts to the Shaker religion signed a covenant agreeing to mutual support and the common ownership of property. Dissolved in 1910, it is now preserved as a restored historic landmark.

    See

    See Shaker Village of Pleasant Hill (Shakertown)

    Shaker Village is America's largest restored Shaker community, with 34 restored buildings and 3,000 acres of preserved farmland. Tours, exhibitions, and activities cover various topics in the historic buildings, working farm, and nature preserve. With a farm-to-table restaurant and lodging in the historic group homes and cottages (both listed below), this can be a unique place to stay, or the focus of a few days' worth of activities.

    Do

    Do Dixie Belle

    The 115-passenger riverboat located at Shaker Village cruises the scenic Kentucky River palisades. A one hour narrated cruise passes by the oldest exposed rock in Kentucky, and goes under High Bridge built in 1877. The riverboat is accessible to persons with physical disabilities.

    📍 Leaves from Shaker Landing, 3501 Lexington Rd🕒 Daily Apr 28-Oct 28. Departures at 2PM and 4PM, arrive at Shaker Landing thirty minutes prior to departure💲 $10 (13 & above), $5 (6-12), free for children 5 & under
    MapsWebsite

    Do Herrington Lake

    Herrington Lake was formed in 1925 by the damming of the Dix River to create hydroelectric power for Kentucky Utilities. Herrington Lake is the deepest lake in Kentucky with a maximum depth of 249 feet and 325 miles of shoreline.

    📍 located in Mercer, Garrard and Boyle counties
