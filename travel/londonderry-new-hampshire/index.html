    Get in

    By car

    Londonderry is accessible from exit's 4 and 5 on Interstate 93. State Route 102 is the only notable east to west route running from Route 101 near Epping to Route 111 in Hudson.

    By bus

    The Boston Express bus runs from stops at Exit 4 and Exit 5 in Londonderry to Boston's South Station and Logan International Airport.

    By plane

    Substantial portions of the Manchester-Boston Regional Airport (MHT) are in Londonderry, but there is no public transit from the airport to the rest of the town. Boston's Logan International Airport (BOS) is less than an hour south by car in light traffic.

    Get around

    There is essentially no public transit within the town, and as destinations are spaced out, people almost always get around by driving.
