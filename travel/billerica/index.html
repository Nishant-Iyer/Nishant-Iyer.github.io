Overview

Billerica is a city of 42,000 people (2020) in Massachusetts that is on the border between Boston’s inner suburbs to the south and the Merrimack Valley region to the north.

2 sights · 4 things to do · 5 places to eat · 2 places to stay

Best things about Billerica

The Billerica Historical Museum

Formerly the Howe School, which was the major school in Billerica before the building of the Billerica Memorial High School, it has since been renovated into a museum documenting the history of Billerica

Nutting Lake Conservation Land

Beautiful to visit in both spring and summer, this land is home to Micozzi Beach, which includes an on-duty lifeguard and boat ramp. Great spot for ice-fishing in the winter.

Yankee Doodle Homecoming

A big town parade and fair. Arts and crafts tables, food, and games following the parade. Witness a recreation of the tar and feathering of Thomas Ditson, along with a model colonial camp and war reenactments.

Grigg's farm

Get in

By car

Take Interstate 95 to Rt. 3 (via exit 32).

Billerica is located 20 miles (32 km) north-northwest of Boston along the Northwest Expressway (Massachusetts) portion of U.S. Route 3. The town is also less than 3 miles (5 km) from the Massachusetts Route 128/Interstate 95 High-Technology belt to the south and less than 2 miles (3 km) from the Interstate 495 (Massachusetts) outer belt highway to the north.

By train

Take the MBTA train - T line from North Station in Boston to the North Billerica stop, which is the second to last stop for this line. Or first stop coming from Lowell, MA.

  • MBTA (Massachusetts Bay Transit Authority), +1 617-222-3200. Services across Massachusetts.
  • LRTA (Lowell Regional Transit Authority), +1 978-459-0164. Bus routes across the Lowell area and neighboring towns, including Billerica.
