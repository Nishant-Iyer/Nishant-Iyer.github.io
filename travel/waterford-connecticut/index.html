Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Oct. Summer highs reach about 83°F; winter lows dip to about 22°F. Cold months (Jan) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Oct and Mar. (climate data from nearby Narragansett)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 980 miles, roughly 17 h without stops. Flying is usually faster.
  • WST Westerly State Airport, Westerly — 18 mi away. Directions
  • HVN Tweed New Haven Airport, New Haven — 39 mi away. Directions
  • PVD Rhode Island T. F. Green International Airport, Providence/Warwick — 45 mi away (major hub). Directions

Waterford is accessible via I-95, 1-395. A Greyhound and Amtrak station are located in nearby New London.
