Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Mar–Jun, Sep–Nov. Summer highs reach about 90°F; winter lows dip to about 32°F. Very hot (Jul) — plan outdoor time for mornings. Wettest: Aug and Mar. (climate data from nearby Piedmont)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 670 miles, roughly 11 h 30 min without stops. Flying is usually faster.
  • GSO Piedmont Triad International Airport, Greensboro — 26 mi away (major hub). Directions
  • USA Concord-Padgett Regional Airport, Concord — 64 mi away. Directions

King is served by PART Route 6, with direct service from Mount Airy, Pilot Mountain, and Winston Salem and connections from other parts of the Triad.
