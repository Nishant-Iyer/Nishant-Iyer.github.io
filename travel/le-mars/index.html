Overview

Le Mars is a city in Western Iowa.

2 sights · 2 things to do · 3 places to stay

Best things about Le Mars

Blue Bunny Ice Cream Parlor and Museum

Exhibits on the history of the Wells company, owners of Blue Bunny. Located in the 1875 George E Pew building in downtown Le Mars.

Le Mars Arts Center

Rotating art exhibits, plus a large permanent collection of barn quilts.

Plymouth County Fair

One of Iowa's largest county fairs.

Postal Playhouse

Community theater located in Le Mars' former post office.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Oct. Summer highs reach about 85°F; winter lows dip to about 10°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Jun and May. (climate data from nearby Sioux City)
