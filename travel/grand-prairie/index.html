Best things about Grand Prairie

Cedar Hill State Park

A popular state park that offers fishing, camping, hiking and biking trails.

Texas Trust CU Theatre

Catch a live performance at the 6,350 seat hall for concerts, shows and events.

Lone Star Park at Grand Prairie

Thoroughbred horse racing. The track features a 7-level, glass-enclosed, climate-controlled Grandstand building with penthouse suites, terraced track-side dining room, box seats and outdoor seating. The Post Time Pavilio…

Joe Pool Lake

A Corp of Engineers Lake with boating and fishing opportunities. Along the shores enjoy camping and hiking.

Uptown Theater

Restored in 2008, this 408-seat theater hosts performing and visual arts, as well as music, dance, drama and film.

Dr. H.V. Copeland Home - Grand Prairie Museum

This home has a State Landmark as well as a Grand Prairie Landmark. It was the historic home of Dr. Horace Victor Copeland (1875-1969).

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Feb–May, Oct–Nov. Summer highs reach about 97°F; winter lows dip to about 38°F. Very hot (Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep) — plan outdoor time for mornings. Wettest: Oct and May. (climate data from nearby Dallas)

    A skateboarders' paradise. Lessons, skate camps, concerts, and lock-ins. It also has a full skate shop with shoes, clothing and skateboards. Open year round.

    📍 1000 Lone Star Pkwy☎ +1 972-262-4GPX

    Do Texas AirHogs

    Opened in 2008, this minor league ballpark features a Kids Zone, restaurants, sports bar, cigar bar and swimming pool. It seats 4,000 and offers 13 luxury suites.

    📍 1600 Lone Star Pkwy☎ +1 972-504-9383

    Do Lone Star Park at Grand Prairie

    Thoroughbred horse racing. The track features a 7-level, glass-enclosed, climate-controlled Grandstand building with penthouse suites, terraced track-side dining room, box seats and outdoor seating. The Post Time Pavilion boasts more than 150 television screens, a sports bar and restaurant. See live horse racing via simulcast from top tracks around the world.

    📍 1000 Lone Star Pkwy🕒 $5 on live race days. Concerts included until 7:30PM, then are $20/person☎ +1 972-263-RACE
