Understand

Dana Point is named for Richard Henry Dana, who chronicled the area in his book Two Years Before the Mast. At that time, its harbor served as a port for trading hides raised in the San Juan Capistrano area. One unusual thing is that many of its streets are named for colored lanterns, harkening back to a system of identifying a ship's wares.

Get in

I-5 is the primary north-south route through Orange County, and passes through the southeastern portion of the city. State Route 1 begins in Dana Point and continues northwest through the city to other coastal Orange County cities. The PCH interchange from I-5 is the most centrally located exit to Dana Point.

Get around

The coastal town offers multiple flexible transit options.

  • Walking and cycling: The Dana Point Harbor Island paths, Baby Beach flats, and the central Lantern District feature clean, dedicated pedestrian walking lines and bicycle rental lanes that are exceptionally scenic to navigate.
  • The Dana Point Trolley: A spectacular, highly popular open-air shuttle bus service operating completely free of charge during the summer peak tourist season Memorial Day through Labour Day. The trolley tracks continuous loops linking the major luxury resorts, the harbor front, and downtown plazas.
  • Ride-hailing apps: Mobile app-based private car bookings operate heavily across the entire city, providing a safe, reliable, and air-conditioned travel alternative.

See

See The Ocean Institute

Founded in 1977, the Ocean Institute is a non-profit organization dedicated to ocean preservation through education, setting the standard for educational excellence and serving as a leading resource for universities, museums, teachers and all others seeking to increase their knowledge of the ocean. Visitors can enjoy ocean-themed exhibits, marine life touch tanks and aquaria with hundreds of marine creatures. Institute staff and volunteers are on hand to share the secrets of our seas. Unique maritime and ocean-themed items are available in the Chambers Gallery every day, 9AM – 6PM. For an added charge, visitors can enjoy marine mammal cruises on board the Institute’s R/V Sea Explorer or sail on board the tall ship Spirit of Dana Point.

Buy

Shopping in Dana Point heavily celebrates upscale coastal lifestyles, premium surfboards, and maritime souvenirs.

  • Hobie Surf Shop: Located prominently in the Lantern District along Pacific Coast Highway. This is a historic, legendary surf institution where you can purchase premium, hand-shaped surfboards, high-grade wetsuits, local California beach apparel, and custom surf memorabilia.
  • The Harbor Walk shops: Small boutique retail shops lining the marina promenade sell beautiful nautical home decor, local marine art photography, straw sub hats, and unique handcrafted seashell jewelry.
