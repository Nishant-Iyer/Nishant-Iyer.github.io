Get in

By car

Port Washington is a quick hop off of I-43 between Milwaukee and Sheboygan. It is on Lake Michigan and it has a great marina to pull your boat into.

From the north

Coming from Sheboygan take I-43 South, exit Highway 32 South near the Allen-Edmonds Shoe Store.

From the west

From the West Bend/Newburg Area, take Highway 33 East till you get to the lake.

From the south

Coming from Milwaukee, take I-43 North, exit Highway 32 North just after the Grafton Exit. Highway 32 merges with Highway 33 to take you to the downtown area.

By boat

Go Port Washington Marina

Marina season is 1 Apr–1 Nov. Daily reservations can be made online.

Get around

This is a small Wisconsin town. It is nice to park on the street and walk around the downtown. You can also stroll out to the lighthouse on a pleasant day, but it is kinda of slippery. If you park your boat, the marina is in the downtown. The whole place is very main street USA.
