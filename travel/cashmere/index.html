Overview

Cashmere is a small town in the North Central Cascades of Washington State. It is home to Liberty Orchards, producers of Aplets & Cotlets.

Peshastin Pinnacles State Park

The park features sandstone slabs and spires in a desert environment with views of the surrounding orchards, Enchantment Range, and Wenatchee Valley. Park activities include rock climbing, hiking, birdwatching, and wildl…

Cashmere Museum and Pioneer Village

Aplets & Cotlets Candy Kitchen

Best time to visit & weather
