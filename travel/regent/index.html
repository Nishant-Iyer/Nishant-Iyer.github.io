Overview

Regent is a farming community of about 250 people in Western North Dakota, founded in 1910. The surrounding area is a popular fishing and hunting destination.

    Get in

    Regent is accessible via the Enchanted Highway (100th Ave and 102nd Ave), 30 miles south of Interstate 94 exit #72 from Gladstone.

    It can also be reached by State Highway 21, between New England (North Dakota) and Mott.

    Get around

    In this wide open country, the most popular local transportation is the pickup truck. Local guides are available for hunters and fishers.

    See

    See Enchanted Highway

    This 32-mile rural road is adorned with large-scale metal sculptures inspired by the local area. Local artist Gary Greff began building these sculptures in 1989 and is still working on more. Audio guide available via app or phone. Note the 12 mile gap after the first two sculptures.

    Do

    Fishing

    On Larson Lake, Indian Creek Dam, Castle Rock Dam, Blickensderfer Dam, Mott Watershed Dam and Cannonball River for pike, walleye, perch, bluegill, trout, largemouth and smallmouth bass.

    • North Dakota Game and Fish Department

    Hunting

    For Ringneck Phaesant, Sharptail Grouse, Hungarian Partridge. Also: Antelope, Coyote, Fox, Jack Rabbit, Whitetail and Mule Deer, Mink, Beaver, Muskrat, Raccoon, and Skunk.
