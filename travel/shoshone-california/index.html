Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Mar–May, Oct–Nov. Summer highs reach about 101°F; winter lows dip to about 27°F. Very hot (Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep) — plan outdoor time for mornings. Wettest: Feb and Jan. (climate data from nearby Pahrump)
