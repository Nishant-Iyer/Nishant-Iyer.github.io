Understand

Weedsport is a small town in Cayuga County, and is right off of Exit 40 on the New York State Thruway. Because of its location, travellers often stop to fill up their tanks or get a bite to eat before heading out again on the road. Weedsport's location on Exit 40 also makes it a hub to visit Auburn to the south, or Owasco and Cayuga Lakes, two of the Finger Lakes.
