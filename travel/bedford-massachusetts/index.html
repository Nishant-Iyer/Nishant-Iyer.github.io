Overview

Bedford is a town in Massachusetts with a population of 14,000 (2020).

Carlisle is a town with a population of 5,000 (2020). A low population density gives the town a rural feel, and it's known for outdoor activities including hiking and biking.

    Get in

    By car

    Driving is the easiest way to get to Bedford and Carlisle. If coming from the Boston area, take MA-2 and Lowell Rd, or from I-95, and take the exit for MA-225 west.

    By bus

    The MBTA operates the 62 bus to Bedford Monday through Saturday, service every 50-75 minutes.

    By bicycle

    Bedford and Carlisle are popular destinations for cyclists. Many enter on Concord Rd or Bedford Rd. The wildly popular Minuteman Bikeway begins in Cambridge and terminates near Bedford Center. Several options like the Bruce Freeman Rail Trail branch out from there.

    Get around

    Given the infrequent nature of the 62 bus, driving and biking are the easiest ways to get around.

    Do

    Town owned conservation lands can be found throughout Carlisle and all areas have hiking trails.

    Do Great Brook Farm State Park

    Hike or bike 20 miles of trails around operating farm fields and through the forest. There is a boat launch onto a small pond for canoes and kayaks. Come in the winter for cross-country skiing when there is sufficient snow cover. During the spring, summer, and fall the farm offers tours of the dairy barn and there is also an ice cream stand.
