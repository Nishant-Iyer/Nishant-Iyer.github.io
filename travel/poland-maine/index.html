Overview

Poland is a town in Androscoggin County in Maine that is home to Range Ponds State Park, which has hiking trails and a pristine freshwater beach. Poland is a historic resort area, and includes the village of East Poland.

Range Ponds State Park

Poland Spring Bottling Museum & Spring House

Poland Spring Golf Course

Par 71, slope 117, 6,116 yards. First resort course ever built (1895-1912), but still a good course. Collared shirts, no jeans.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Jul–Aug. Summer highs reach about 55°F; winter lows dip to about -3°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr, Oct, Nov, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Oct and Jul. (climate data from nearby Mount Washington State Park)
