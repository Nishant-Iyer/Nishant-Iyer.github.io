Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Sep. Summer highs reach about 78°F; winter lows dip to about 16°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Jun and Aug. (climate data from nearby Binghamton)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 760 miles, roughly 13 h without stops. Flying is usually faster.
  • BGM Greater Binghamton/Edwin A Link field, Binghamton — 26 mi away. Directions
  • ITH Ithaca Tompkins Regional Airport, Ithaca — 52 mi away. Directions
  • SYR Syracuse Hancock International Airport, Syracuse — 65 mi away (major hub). Directions
