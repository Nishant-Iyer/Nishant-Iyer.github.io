Overview

Davenport is a rural city of 1,800 people (2020) in the Columbia River Plateau of Washington. It serves as the seat of and a hub for business, medical and educational services in Lincoln County.

2 sights · 1 things to do · 1 bars & cafés · 1 places to stay

Best things about Davenport

Fort Spokane

Set in the Lake Roosevelt National Recreation Area at the confluence of the Spokane and Columbia rivers, this site was a US army fort, then an Indian boarding school and a hospital.

Lincoln County Historical Museum

Artifacts from Lincoln County's history.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Sep. Summer highs reach about 87°F; winter lows dip to about 26°F. Cold months (Jan, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Dec and Jan. (climate data from nearby Spokane)
