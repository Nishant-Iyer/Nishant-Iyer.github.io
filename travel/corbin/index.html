Understand

Corbin (population 7,900 in 2020) is a small community on US 25, the main north-south highway in the area before Interstate 75 opened in the 1950s.

Get in

By car, take Interstate 75 to US Route 25W.

Get around

Taxi services include Venture Cabs (+1 606-526-1211) and Tri-County Cabs (+1 606-622-0472).

See

See Harland Sanders Café and Museum

Former 1930s roadside café, once famous for country ham, steaks and fried chicken, heartily recommended by Duncan Hines Adventures in Fine Dining (1939). When Interstate 75 bypassed US 25 in 1956, killing local business, the Colonel took to the road to promote a franchise. The café is now a museum, the associated filling station and motel are gone. A modern KFC franchise is adjacent.

📍 688 U.S. Route 25W, North Corbin🕒 10AM-10PM☎ +1 606 528-2163
