Best things about Monticello

Indiana Beach Boardwalk Resort

The only major amusement park in northern Indiana, Indiana Beach has quite a few rides, including the Steel Hawg, which for a while held the world record for steepest drop in a roller coaster.

Twin Lakes

Two lakes on the Tippecanoe River formed by hydroelectric dams, Lakes Shafer and Freeman are great for boating and fishing.

Camp Buffalo

The local Boy Scout camp is open for use by Scout-unaffiliated groups as well, featuring over 300 acres of natural beauty.

Lake Shore Drive-In

There's not a lot of drive-in movie theaters anymore, but this one is so good, it makes you wonder why there's not more still around.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Oct. Summer highs reach about 82°F; winter lows dip to about 18°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: May and Jun. (climate data from nearby Indiana Dunes National Park)

Get in

  • US 24 goes east-west through the city, connecting directly to Peoria, Illinois and Fort Wayne, Indiana
  • US 421 comes up from the south and leaves on the west side of town, connecting Monticello to Indianapolis, Indiana to the southeast.

Get around

It's easy enough to get around downtown by walking, but having a car is a good idea.
