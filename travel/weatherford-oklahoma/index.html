    Historic 1929 Route 66 fuel station with owner's residence upstairs. Lucille Hamons, the "Mother of the Mother Road", ran the station from 1941-2000. The station, which closed upon her death, was restored but was not reopened. A restaurant in Weatherford operates as an approximate replica of the station.
