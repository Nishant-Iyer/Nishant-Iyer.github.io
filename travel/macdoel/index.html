Overview

Macdoel is a town in Siskiyou County in California. To get there from Weed, turn on US-97 N until you reach it. It is nearby Meiss Lake, and has an elementary school. Potato manufacturing is big here.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Oct. Summer highs reach about 86°F; winter lows dip to about 22°F. Wettest: Feb and Mar. (climate data from nearby Lava Beds National Monument)

See

Meiss Lake

This lake is good for birding (there is a nice birding trail overlooking it). It also has a waterfowl hunting ranch. It used to be a very large lake, but now it is only a remnant.
