Overview

Corsicana is a city in the Blackland Prairie region of Texas. Many people come to the town for their famous holiday fruitcakes.

2 sights · 1 things to do · 3 places to eat · 1 bars & cafés · 4 places to stay

Best things about Corsicana

Corsicana Opry

Pioneer Village

Reconstructed early 1800s pioneer village, plus a few other area history exhibits.

Cook Planetarium/Pearce Museum

This is the 2nd largest planetarium in Texas. They have regular shows here. The Pearce Museum is a Civil War museum and a western art museum.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Feb–May, Oct–Nov. Summer highs reach about 97°F; winter lows dip to about 38°F. Very hot (Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep) — plan outdoor time for mornings. Wettest: Oct and May. (climate data from nearby Dallas)
