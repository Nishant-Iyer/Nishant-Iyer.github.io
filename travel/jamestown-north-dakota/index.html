    Understand

    Jamestown, "The Buffalo City", was originally named for the James river. It was first settled in 1872.

    Peggy Lee, the jazz singer, Louis L'Amour, the western novelist, and Darin Erstad, the professional baseball player for the 2002 World Champion Anaheim Angels, are natives of Jamestown.

    See Frontier Village

    Jamestown is home to "The World’s Largest Buffalo", named Dakota Thunder, a 60-ton cement sculpture that stands 26 feet (8 m) tall and stretches 46 feet (14 m) long. It was built in 1959. A number of old buildings were moved in around it to create the Frontier Village, which today attracts over 100,000 people annually.
