Get in

By car

  • Escanaba is commonly reached by highway. State Highway M-35 is a scenic drive along Lake Michigan to Menominee and the Wisconsin border. Highways US-2 and US-41 share a combined stretch of road that lead North into the communities of Gladstone, Rapid River, and the Hiawatha National Forest.

By bus

  • Indian Trails bus service serves many stops in Michigan, Wisconsin, Illinois, and Indiana. In Escanaba, buses arrive daily at the Delta Area Transit Authority (DATA) terminal.

By plane

Go Delta County Airport (ESC)

has daily Delta Airlines flights and is also the local FedEx air freight terminal.

Maps

Get around

  • Many locations in Escanaba can be reached on foot or bicycle as the city is not very large.
  • Citywide and countywide public transportation is provided by the Delta Area Transit Authority (DATA). DATA does not run traditional bus routes, instead operating its fleet of seventeen busses on a dispatcher-coordinated on-demand service.
  • There are three taxi options for travelers: Escanaba Taxi co., JN Taxi, and Delta Taxi Service.

Do

At the end of Ludington Street is the beautiful Ludington Park. Playgrounds are plentiful as poppies and the beach is always welcoming. Be sure to come around the Fourth of July for an excellent lakeside fireworks display.
