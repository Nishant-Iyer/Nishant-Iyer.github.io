Understand

Bowling Green is on the northern boundary of Hardee County. It had an estimated population of 2,930 residents as of 2010. Bowling Green has a scattering of businesses and is mainly a very rural residential community. Ranching and citrus growing are the main business activities of the area in which Bowling Green is located.
