Overview

Alpine is a town in Southwest Wyoming, 30 miles south of Jackson, at the confluences of three beautiful rivers: the Salt River, Greys River, and Snake River. The three rivers meet in Alpine and flow into Palisades Lake.

    Get in

    Alpine is at the junction of Hwy 26 and Hwy 89.

    The nearest airport is in Jackson.

    Sale Lake Express buses serve Alpine Junction.

    Do

    Alpine Trails and Pathways maintains paths for winter and summer activities. Winter offers groomed trails for cross-country skiing, skate skiing and fat biking. During summer there are 30 hiking areas within 30 miles of town.

    Sleep

    Stay Flying Saddle Resort

    This resort offers spectacular mountain views, a steakhouse serving hand-cut Black Angus beef, an all-you-can-eat salad bar, or a western bar complete with saddle bar stools. The resort offers a lodge, 34 rooms along the Snake River, hot tub and tennis court. Guests can snowmobile from the resort into the 150 miles of groomed trails of the Grey's River Trail System during the winter snow season.
