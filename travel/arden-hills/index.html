    Understand

    Arden Hills is a small suburban city surrounded by New Brighton and Roseville. It is the home of the Knights from Irondale High school and the Mustangs from Moundsview High School. Just a short distance away from St. Paul and Minneapolis it is conveniently close to many of the Minnesota sights.

    Get in

    Interstate 35W, Interstate 694, and US-10.

    Get around

    In Arden Hills there is a cab company. Some adjacent suburbs have bus service to Minneapolis and St. Paul.

    See
