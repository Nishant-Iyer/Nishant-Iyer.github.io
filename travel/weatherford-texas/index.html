Overview

Weatherford is a city in the Cross Timbers region of Texas. It is the county seat of Parker County.

4 sights · 3 things to do · 5 places to stay

Best things about Weatherford

National Vietnam War Museum

Memorial and meditation gardens, and a visitors center with a small gallery are open during the construction of this new museum that aims to foster understanding of this war.

Chandor Gardens

English gardens and Chinese style architecture are a part of this estate.

Parker County Courthouse

Parker County has a very ostentatious-looking courthouse, in Second Empire style.

Doss Heritage and Culture Center

A Texas history museum, well, particularly, life as they saw it here in Parker County. Think windmills and pistols.

Splash Kingdom Hudson Oaks

Holland Lake Park

This is a pretty elaborate park with a large pond, hiking trails, and even some log cabins to explore.

    Do Parker County Peach Festival

    The Parker County Peach Festival is a premiere Texas one day event. A variety of times are available including fresh produce of locally grown peaches. Some of the favorites at the festival are the homemade ice cream, peach cobbler, and the number of local vendors selling their wares. There traditionally has been a bicycle event that conincides with this one. Contact Weatherford Chamber of Commerce for the most up to date details prior to making reservations to come.
