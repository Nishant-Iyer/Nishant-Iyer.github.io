Do Bartholomew's Cobble

This land used to be the property of Col. John Ashley, an American revolutionary and slaveholder, in the 18th century. His house (Ashley House) can also be visited for free, with tours given for $5 for adults and free for children.

📍 105 Weatogue Road, Ashley Falls🕒 Open year-round, daily, sunrise to sunset. Natural History Museum and Visitor Center (public restroom) open year-round, daily, 9AM – 4:30PM (closed Sundays and Mondays, December to March).💲 Adult $5, Child (6-12) $1. Group rates offered for special programs☎ +1 413 229-8600
