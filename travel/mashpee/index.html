Overview

Mashpee is in Massachusetts on Cape Cod. It includes the village of New Seabury. Purchased in 1660 to be a reservation for the Wampanoag tribe and later revoked and incorporated as a town. Until the 1960s, Wampanoags made up the majority of the population.

Best things about Mashpee

Wampanoag Indian Museum

The world's only museum devoted exclusively to Wampanoag history.

Lowell Holly Reservation

This 135-acre preserve has large stands of holly and Mashpee-Wakeby pond is one of the best freshwater fishing sites on the Cape.

South Cape Beach State Park

Restrooms. Excellent beach.

Waquoit Bay National Estuarine Research Reserve

Wonderful 2200-acre reserve perfect for hiking. Also runs public events such as Evenings on the Bluff and the Watershed Block Party.

Wakeby Pond, Ryder Conservation Area

Lifeguards, food, and rest rooms. The Cape's largest freshwater body of water.

Cape Cod Children's Museum

Pirate ship, indoor planetarium, puppet theatre, hands-on exhibits and special programs.
