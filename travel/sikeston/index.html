Eat Lambert's Cafe

"Home of Throwed Rolls". This restaurant is well worth the stop. Country style cooking with a meat and 2 sides style menu. During the meal, "Pass Arounds" will show up at your table. These are extra sides that you can accept or decline. And as advertised, the rolls are "throwed" to your table.
