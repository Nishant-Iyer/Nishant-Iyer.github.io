Overview

Lindley is a small town in Stueben County, New York. It is just outside of the county's largest city, Corning.

Plan your trip

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Sep. Summer highs reach about 80°F; winter lows dip to about 15°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Jun and Jul. (climate data from nearby Ithaca)

Understand

It is known for its rural character, rolling hills, and the Tioga River, which flows through it.

Get in

By car

Lindley is located just off of I-99/US-15.

Get around

There is no public transportation in Lindley, and the area is spread over a large distance with some steppe inclines. Private cars should be used.

See

The Appalachian Mountain Range runs through Lindley, and provides for gorgeous views, especially during the fall.

Do

  • Tioga River: There is a small gravel boat launch and beach at the intersection of Indian Hills Road and Maynard Road. Next to the beach is a small swimming hole and a rock outcrop to jump from. There is also plentiful fishing, including creek chubs, smallmouth and largemouth bass, and carp.

Go next
