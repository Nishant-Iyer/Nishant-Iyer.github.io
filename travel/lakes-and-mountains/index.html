Overview

Lakes and Mountains is a Maine region that encompasses the counties of Androscoggin, Franklin and Oxford, plus the northern portion of Cumberland County.

Plan your trip

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Jul–Aug. Summer highs reach about 55°F; winter lows dip to about -3°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr, Oct, Nov, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Oct and Jul. (climate data from nearby Mount Washington State Park)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 1,080 miles, roughly 18 h 30 min without stops. Flying is usually faster.
  • AUG Augusta State Airport, Augusta — 43 mi away. Directions

Towns

  • Farmington — a quaint town with many buildings on the National Register of Historic Places
  • Frye Island — a seasonal resort town on an island in Sebago Lake
  • Kingfield — the principal gateway to Sugarloaf, a major ski resort
  • Lewiston—Auburn — twin cities with a strong French-Canadian heritage
  • New Gloucester — home to the last active Shaker village in the U.S.
  • Poland and Poland Spring — a historic resort area that is home to Range Ponds State Park
  • Rangeley — the center of the Rangeley Lakes Region resort area
  • Standish — a recreational area and suburb of Portland
  • Weld — gateway to Mount Blue State Park
