Overview

Pickerington is a city in Fairfield and Franklin Counties, Southeast Ohio. It is a suburb of Columbus.

2 sights · 4 things to do · 2 places to eat · 2 places to stay

Best things about Pickerington

The Motorcycle Hall of Fame Museum

Olde Carnegie Library Museum

Includes native American weapons and 1800s memorabilia.

Picktown Palooza

Marcus Theatres Pickerington UltraScreen

Friday Flicks

In the summer, movies are shown every Friday in Sycamore Park Amphitheater, projected on the side of a barn. Movies begin at dark, and admission is free. Bring a blanket or lawn chair.

Pickerington Ponds

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Apr–Oct. Summer highs reach about 85°F; winter lows dip to about 22°F. Cold months (Jan) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Jul and Jun. (climate data from nearby Columbus)
