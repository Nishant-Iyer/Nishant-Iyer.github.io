Get around

Ellijay has a quaint downtown area with various speciality and antique shops and restaurants but the shopping centers and all the chain restaurants are on the highway.

See

  • Amicalola Falls State Park and Lodge, 418 Amicalola Falls State Park Rd, Dawsonville, a state park centered around a cascade about 20 min southeast of Ellijay on Hwy 52 East.

Do

  • Buy some apples at apple orchards in Apple Alley along Hwy 52 East
  • Go to the Georgia Apple Festival in October
  • Ride the nearby Blue Ridge Scenic Railway
  • Pan for gold in nearby Dahlonega
  • Shop in Downtown Ellijay
  • Raft, canoe, kayak or tube the Cartacay River
  • Go to Carter's Lake
  • Hike in the Ridgeway Recreation Area west of town, Walnut Mountain east of town, or Bear Creek Trail northwest of town
  • Visit the southern terminus of the Appalachian Trail at
