    Get in

    Both Oakland International Airport (OAK) and San Francisco International Airport (SFO) have Bay Area Rapid Transit (BART) stations. Take the Richmond Line to the North Berkeley or El Cerrito Plaza Station, the closest stations to Albany. AC Transit buses serve Albany directly. If traveling by car, take the Albany exit (Buchanan Street) from Interstate 80 or Interstate 580.

    Because the cities and towns on the eastern shore of the San Francisco Bay all butt up against each other and function as one large metro area, it's likely that you will also visit Albany's neighboring East Bay towns, such as Berkeley, Emeryville, El Cerrito, and Richmond, so much of the information about getting to those places also applies to getting to Albany.

    See

    See Albany Bulb

    Now part of the Eastshore State Park system, this reclaimed landfill is home to trails with views of San Francisco and the bay, sea birds, as well as tons of crazy urban junk art. There's a sandy beach, and dogs are allowed off-leash. In spring, colorful wildflowers grow amid the broken blocks of concrete. Might not be safe after dark. Park at or ride bikes to west end of Buchanan St. Free.
