Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Jun–Oct. Summer highs reach about 67°F; winter lows dip to about 39°F. Wettest: Dec and Jan. (climate data from nearby Coos Bay)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

Sample 3-day trip

  1. Day 1: Lincoln City — Drift Creek Falls, Chinook Winds Casino, Siletz Bay National Wildlife Refuge.
  2. Day 2: Depoe Bay — Boiler Bay State Scenic Viewpoint, Whale Watching Center, Fogarty Creek State Recreation Area.
  3. Day 3: Newport — Oregon Coast Aquarium, Hatfield Marine Science Visitor's Center, Yaquina Head Lighthouse/Outstanding Natural Area.
