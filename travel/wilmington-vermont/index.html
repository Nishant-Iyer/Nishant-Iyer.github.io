Best things about Wilmington

Mount Snow Skiing and Riding

Located only 29 miles from Interstate 91, Mount Snow is Vermont's closest big mountain to Northeast metropolitan areas.

Vermont Snowmobiling

Explore picturesque scenic trails and beautiful vistas in the Green Mountain National Forest.

Vermont Sleigh Rides

The sleigh ride operation has three sleighs and can accommodate up to 50 people at a time. Each sleigh can hold 15-18 people.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Oct. Summer highs reach about 84°F; winter lows dip to about 16°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Jul and Jun. (climate data from nearby Albany)
