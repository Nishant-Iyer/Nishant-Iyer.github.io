Overview

Hamilton is a city of about 5,000 people in the Bitterroot Valley of Northwestern Montana. It is the central trade area for the many farms, ranches, and orchards in the valley, and a base for exploring the steep Bitterroot Range.

This article also covers other nearby attractions and small towns across the Bitterroot Valley.

    Understand

    Hamilton is the county seat of Ravalli County. It was founded by Butte copper king Marcus Daly in the late 19th century. It was named for J.W. Hamilton, who provided the right-of-way to the railroad. Daly is said to have wanted to begin business in the then county seat of Grantsdale, but was denied the opportunity. He supposedly founded Hamilton out of his own pocket as a reaction to being rebuffed at Grantsdale.

    Get in

    Hamilton is on US-93. 50 miles to the north is Missoula. 50 miles to the south is Lost Trail Pass taking you into Idaho. The few towns on this stretch of US-93 are quite small.

    The nearest airport is Missoula International Airport (MSO).

    Get around

    Many of the shops and restaurants are spread out along US-93, making a car or bicycle best for getting around.

    Do Skalkaho Falls
