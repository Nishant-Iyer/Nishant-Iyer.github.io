Overview

Lander is a city in central Wyoming, USA, a wonderful vacation destination. Lander has a population of approximately 7,500 people.

Best things about Lander

Sinks Canyon State Park

An underground river through a limestone cavern. It goes into the Sinks and comes out at a pool called the Rise.

Fremont County Pioneer Museum

Complex with historic structures, log cabins and Native American dances during summer months.

South Pass City State Historic Site

Historic site on trail; snacks/drinks only, no fuel or gear. May hold packages—confirm via southpasscity.org/cdt. Most hikers resupply in Lander or Atlantic City instead.

South Pass City Historic Site

A former gold mining ghost town on the Oregon Trail, abandoned in the 1950s.

Lander Brewfest

Advertised as "The Best Little Brewfest in the West".

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Jun–Aug. Summer highs reach about 79°F; winter lows dip to about 6°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Mar, Nov, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: May and Dec. (climate data from nearby Jackson)
