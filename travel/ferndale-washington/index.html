    Get in

    By car

    Ferndale is right along Interstate 5. There are two main exits you can take to get into Ferndale.

    • Exit 262 goes to the city center
    • Exit 263 gets you to Portal Way, which connects to 2nd Ave.

    If you'd prefer to avoid the interstate, you can get to Ferndale from Bellingham via Axton Rd which is left off Washington State Route 539 (Guide Meridian) or via Northwest Drive off of West Bakerview Rd in Bellingham (near Bellis Fair). From Blaine/Canada you can take Portal Way.

    By plane

    The closest commercial airport is Bellingham International Airport (BLI).

    The nearest hub airport is Vancouver International Airport (YVR), about an hour drive north. If you don't want to cross the border, Seattle-Tacoma International Airport (SEA) is only about an hour and a half away by car. There are also airport shuttles that leave from the Ferndale Bus Stop.

    Get around

    • Bus routes serving Ferndale:

    Go Whatcom Transportation Authority (WTA)

    Operates public transit bus service in Whatcom County including Bellingham, Blaine, Ferndale, Lynden, and Sumas. Also operates bus route 80X traveling north from Burlington and Mount Vernon in Skagit County to Bellingham.

    ☎ +1 360-676-7433Checked Sep 2022
    MapsWebsite
    • Route 27 from Meridian Station in Bellingham is the main route serving Ferndale. Stops at the Ferndale Station, mainly follows Main Street until it turns onto Church Road, and does a loop back to Main Street via Vista Drive before returning to the Ferndale Station and then back into Bellingham.
    • Route 75 stops at Ferndale Station, on its way between Blaine and Bellingham. Goes directly to Bellingham Station rather than Meridian, useful if you're trying to get to Ferndale from downtown Bellingham. Operates Monday to Saturday.
