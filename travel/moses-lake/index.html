Best things about Moses Lake

Potholes State Park

A park with freshwater shoreline on Potholes Reservoir (also known as O'Sullivan Reservoir). It is not all that close to the Pothole Lakes.

Moses Lake Museum & Art Center

History of Moses Lake and the Columbia Basin, Native American artifacts and fine art gallery.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Sep. Summer highs reach about 87°F; winter lows dip to about 19°F. Cold months (Jan, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Dec and Jan. (climate data from nearby Leavenworth)

Get in

Interstate 90 (I-90) is along the south edge of town, about 177 mi east of Seattle and 100 mi west of Spokane.

  • Exit #176 (W. Broadway)
  • Exit #179 (SR-17 'Othello, Moses Lake, Ephrata')

SR-17 goes through town, connecting to Ephrata 20 mi to the northwest and Othello 26 mi to the south.

By bus

Greyhound has four departures daily to Seattle from a stop at the southeastern edge of town.

Earnies Fuel Stop on 1810 Kittelson Rd NE, on SR-17 & Kittelson Rd (Exit #179 of I-90) in the outskirts of town serves as a de facto bus station for:

By plane

The nearest airports for commercial flights with multiple airlines are Spokane (GEG) 109 mi east via I-90 and Seattle-Tacoma (SEA) 181 mi west via I-90 & I-405.

Go Grant County International Airport (MWH)

For travelers this airport is only used for general aviation and VIP flights. The airport is mainly used for cargo flights and aviation training and testing by Boeing, US Air Force/Air National Guard, Big Bend Community College, private flight training schools and their contractors and support companies. Map marker is at airport office/terminal building while the other companies and Big Bend Community College campus are in different locations surrounding the airport grounds.
