Best things about Mille Lacs

Mille Lacs Indian Museum

A museum covering the history of the Mille Lacs Band of Ojibwe.

Garrison Concourse Wayside Park

Scenic overlook on Mille Lacs Lake. There is a giant walleye sculpture in the middle of the park.

Mille Lacs Lake Historical Society Museum

Small museum covering the farming, fur trading, logging, and railroad building that took place in the Mille Lacs area.

Grand Casino Mille Lacs

Casino resort run by the Mille Lacs Band of Ojibwe.

Mille Lacs Band of Ojibwe Traditional Powwow

Friday-Saturday-Sunday of the 3rd weekend of August.

Understand

The name Mille Lacs is French for "one thousand lakes". Mille Lacs Lake is the second-largest inland lake in Minnesota, covering 207 square miles.
