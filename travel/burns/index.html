By bus

Reid's Country Store at 230 Hines Blvd (Hwy 20), in the west side of town, serves as a de-facto bus station for:

Place Grant County People Mover

Goes towards Prairie City through John Day, Mt Vernon and Seneca. Additional stops at near hotels and shopping in Hines (629 N Hwy 20, Hines) and Burns Hospital (W Washington St & Fairview). The bus comes into town only on the 1st, 3rd and 5th Thursdays per month. Check schedules CLOSELY.
