Overview

Texas' Gulf Coast stretches for hundreds of miles, from the Louisiana border to Brownsville on the Rio Grande. The beaches are excellent, and oil-rich cities like Houston make for fun nightlife, too.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Oct–May. Summer highs reach about 95°F; winter lows dip to about 48°F. Very hot (Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep) — plan outdoor time for mornings. Wettest: Sep and Jun. (climate data from nearby Corpus Christi)

Regions

Rio Grande Valley

From the Mexican border at Brownsville up to the Mansfield Channel.

South Barrier Coast

From the Mansfield Channel in the south near Raymondville past the Corpus Christi area and up to Port Lavaca.

North Barrier Coast

Heavily-developed coastal areas from Point Comfort in the south to Galveston Bay east of Bay City. Includes the sprawling Houston metropolitan area.

Bayous

Marshy land from Galveston Bay east to the Louisiana border.
