Understand

Massapequa is home to more than 20,000 residents. Inside Massapequa is a smaller village known as Massapequa Park, but many of the locals don't consider the two to be any different. It has been home to many famous and infamous celebrities including Jerry Seinfeld, The Baldwin Brothers, The Baldinger Brothers, Elliot Easton, Charlie Kaufman, and Joey Buttafuoco.

Get in

By train

Massapequa has a station on the Babylon Line of the Long Island Rail Road, which connects Babylon with New York City.

By car

The main roads in and out of here are Sunrise Highway and Merrick Road. If traveling through Long Island it is recommended that one takes the Southern State Parkway or Sunrise Highway to arrive.

By bus

Nassau Inter-County Express operates a number of bus connections to Massapequa.

By plane

The nearest airport is Republic Airport; however, JFK is recommended.

Get around

It is advisable to have a car in order to get around, but a bike could do the trick if you don't mind riding on roads.

Eat

Eat All American

All American is the burger joint to go to on Long Island. Especially if you're already in Massapequa. The place looks exactly the same as it started back in the 1963 (without the rollerskating waitress'). Lines are almost always out the door but you can place your order and eat it all in about 20 minutes on a good day. Many people travel from all over to eat at All American and the locals swear by it.

📍 4286 Merrick Road🕒 Mon-Sat 10AM-10PM, Sun 11AM–9:30PM

Eat Il Classico

Wonderful Italian food with an old charm to it. The waiters are very knowledgeable of the food they serve and also great with wine suggestions. The place is never too packed.

📍 4857 Merrick Rd. Massapequa Pk☎ +1 516-798-8496
Google MapsWebsite

Eat Krisch's Restaurant & Ice Cream Parlour

Step back in time into the 1950s, Krisch's has all the touches of a 50s hamburger joint and the style too. The ice cream is a must and the food isn't half bad either. They also have an amazing fudge and candy selection as well. No need to sit-down, but it's half the experience! They have a fun challenge called the Kitchen Sink, finish it all and you get a T-Shirt.

📍 11 Central Ave💲 $☎ +1 516 797-3149

Drink

Drink The Good Life

Tons of craft beers to choose from, both on tap and bottled. One of the more lively drinking scenes in Massapequa. Plenty of standing room and the crowd ranges from all ages. Food is pretty good too.
