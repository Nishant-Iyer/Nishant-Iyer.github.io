Overview

Norwalk is a city in Los Angeles County in Southern California. It is 17 miles southeast of Downtown Los Angeles.

2 sights · 1 things to do · 2 places to eat · 4 places to stay

Best things about Norwalk

Hargitt House Museum

Built in 1891 by the D.D. Johnston family, the Hargitt House Museum was built in the architectural style of Victorian Eastlake. It was donated to the people of Norwalk by Charles ("Chun") Hargitt and Ida Hargitt.

Sproul Museum

Golf N Stuff

Putt-putt, go-karts, rides, arcade games, and so on.
