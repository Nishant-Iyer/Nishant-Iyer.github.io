Overview

Apopka is a city in Orange County, Florida. Apopka is referred to as the "Indoor Foliage Capital of the World" due to the many greenhouse nurseries there.

6 sights · 2 things to do · 2 places to eat · 2 places to stay

Best things about Apopka

Apopka Seaboard Air Line Railway Depot

In 1918 the station was built as a part of Florida's extensive railroad network. It was added to the National Register of Historic Places in 1993.

Carroll Building

Since operated as a bakery, the building was once a history museum. It was added to the National Register of Historic Places in 1993.

Mitchill-Tibbetts House

Added to the National Register in 1991, early residents included R. Mitchell and Union Army General George Tibbetts.

Museum of the Apopkans

Regional history museum.

Ryan & Company Lumber Yard

One of multiple Apopka buildings added to the register in 1993.

Waite-Davis House

Added to the National Register of Historic Places in 1990.
