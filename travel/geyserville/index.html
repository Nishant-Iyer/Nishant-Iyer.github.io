Overview

Geyserville is a quaint one stop sign town of fewer than 900 people in 2010. It has a myriad of wineries to taste at, off the beaten track roads to bike on, rivers to raft on, scenic views and good places to eat.

Do Francis Ford Coppola Winery

Yes, that Francis Ford Coppola. In addition to wine-tasting and a restaurant with gorgeous views from the outside dining area, there is a collection of movie memorabilia, the car from the movie Tucker, a day-use pool with private cabanas (reservations absolutely required), and bocce courts.

📍 300 Via Archimedes🕒 Tasting room: 11AM to 6PM daily; restaurant (Rustic): 11AM to 9PM☎ +1 707 857-1400

Drink Bannister Wines

New to downtown Geyserville as of 2022, this tasting room offers a number of pinot noirs, as well as unusual varietals. Various tasting options, including tastings paired with food. Reservations recommended.

📍 21035 Geyserville Ave.🕒 Thu-Mon noon-7PM☎ +1 707 387-0124

Eat Catelli's

Italian, with the menu highlighting ingredients from local and organic farmers as well as over 100 local wines. Outdoor patio (dogs allowed during the day, but not in the evening).

📍 21047 Geyserville Ave🕒 Tue-Thu 11:30AM-8:30PM, Fri 11:30AM-9PM, Sat noon-9PM, Sun noon-8:30PM☎ +1 707 857-3471

Eat Diavola Pizzeria & Salumeria

Set in a historic landmark more than 100 years old, the restaurant's pizza is wood-fired, with a thin, crispy crust (also available gluten-free). It also serves good pasta, panini, and craft beer, and has covered trellises that make for a lovely summer spot for eating.
