Overview

Sunnyside is a city of about 16,000 in the Yakima Valley, on the Columbia River Plateau of Washington State.

Sunnyside Lighted Farm Implement Parade

The parade includes combines, boom trucks, sprayers, swathers, grape pickers, and all types of tractors decorated with colorful lights. First held in 1989, it is the longest-running parade of its kind in the country.

See

See Jerry Taylor Veterans Plaza

The plaza was dedicated in 1977, named for former Sunnyside mayor and World War II veteran Jerry Taylor. As of 2021, the memorial consisted of nineteen blocks of granite spanning the length of a city block. The memorial is planned to contain 42 blocks when done and will be the largest such memorial in the Pacific Northwest.
