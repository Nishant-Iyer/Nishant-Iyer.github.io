Best things about Conway

VW Bug Ranch

Five Volkswagen Beetles buried nose down in the ground. Similar to the Cadillac Ranch in nearby Amarillo and the upended tractor equipment at Canyon.

Route 66 remnants

A number of dilapidated ruins of a former gas stations, café, and motor lodge provide some interesting photographic opportunities.
