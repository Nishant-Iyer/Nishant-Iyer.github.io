Overview

Bay City is a city in Central Michigan. A former lumbering/mill town that was incorporated in 1865, it was at one time the home of the celebrity Madonna, which is commemorated in a mural downtown.

Do

The Riverwalk located along the Saginaw river. Features artwork, gardens, softball fields and a children's playground.

Do Center Avenue Homes

Center Avenue consists of a long string of classic and historic homes built by some of the elite lumber barons who helped settle the area. Each home is different and seems to have been constructed to "out-do" its neighbor. Look for tower rooms, sprawling three and four story homes with wings off the main structure.

📍 Eastsidepart of Hwy 25💲 Free
