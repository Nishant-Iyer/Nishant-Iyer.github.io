See

See Holy Family Shrine

Completed in 2002, this Catholic church quickly became famous for its architectural design and commanding location. The chapel walls are constructed almost entirely out of glass, allowing unobstructed view of the farmland and prairie surrounding the building. The rest of the site is constructed in a flowing, modernist style. Please keep in mind that this is a working church. No drones, no commercial photography, and appropriate dress is expected. Services are held Sunday at 10AM.

Do Schramm Park State Recreation Area

This park has 4 hiking trails with plenty of nature viewing. The education center features an aquarium and touch tank, along with virtual mountain bikes for an additional cost of $10 for adults, $7 for children. Remnants of the state's first fish hatchery are now a museum and can be explored as well. No overnight camping. Day use only.
