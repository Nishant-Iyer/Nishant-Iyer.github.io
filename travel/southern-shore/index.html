Cities

  • Cape May — Victorian town at the southern tip of New Jersey.
  • Millville
  • Ocean City — a popular family resort on the Southern Shore.
  • Vineland
  • Wildwood — the ultimate vacation spot for families, with great dining, nightlife, free beaches and plenty of fun for the whole family.
