Overview

Southern Shore is one of the coastal regions of New Jersey.

Plan your trip

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Apr–Oct. Summer highs reach about 87°F; winter lows dip to about 27°F. Wettest: Aug and Mar. (climate data from nearby Magnolia)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 850 miles, roughly 14 h 30 min without stops. Flying is usually faster.
  • ACY Atlantic City International Airport, Atlantic City — 24 mi away. Directions
  • DOV Dover Civil Air Terminal/Dover Air Force Base, Dover — 31 mi away. Directions
  • PHL Philadelphia International Airport, Philadelphia — 49 mi away (major hub). Directions

Atlantic City International Airport is the closest "large" airport, but nearby Philadelphia International Airport is significantly larger, offering many more flights.
