Overview

Sapulpa is in the Green Country region of Oklahoma, a conservative little town that sounds like Sebulba from Star Wars if you say it fast.

Best things about Sapulpa

The Buffalo Statue

Celebrating Oklahoma's 100th Year of Statehood.

Sapulpa Historical Museum

Little odds and ends from Sapulpa's past, like one of those washing machines with feet that walks when it gets overloaded. Across Water St is the vintage Waite Phillips-Barnsdall Filling Station.

Heart of Route 66 Auto Museum

Nostalgic museum with lots of classic cars and old-time gasoline pumps.

Rock Creek Bridge

An authentic segment of Route 66.

Pretty Water Lake

A pretty lake with a 1 mile walking trail.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Mar–May, Sep–Oct. Summer highs reach about 94°F; winter lows dip to about 28°F. Very hot (Jul, Aug) — plan outdoor time for mornings. Wettest: May and Jun. (climate data from nearby Tulsa)

Buy

Shop Frankoma Pottery Factory Outlet

Once Oklahoma's most famous pottery company and one of a few in the country with its own clay on the site of the Kiln, Frankoma closed its 9549 Frankoma Road factory in 2010. There is a strong aftermarket for the pieces as collectibles. An attempt was made in 2012 to revive the brand on a very limited scale; products were available locally or ordered on-line from Houston TX.
