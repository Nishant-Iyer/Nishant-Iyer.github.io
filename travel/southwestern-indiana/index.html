Sample 3-day trip

  1. Day 1: Evansville — Angel Mounds State Historic Site, Mesker Park Zoo, Evansville Museum of Arts, History, and Science.
  2. Day 2: Jasper — Dubois County Courthouse, Huntingburg Antique Show, St. Patrick's Day Celebration.
  3. Day 3: Vincennes — Grouseland, St. Francis Xavier Cathedral and Library, Lincoln Memorial Bridge.
