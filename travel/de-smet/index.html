Overview

De Smet, South Dakota—"The Little Town on the Prairie"—is the home of author Laura Ingalls Wilder. Visitors arrive each summer to visit the Ingalls Homestead, the Laura Ingalls Wilder Memorial Society, and the Laura Ingalls Wilder Pageant.

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 650 miles, roughly 11 h without stops. Flying is usually faster.
  • ATY Watertown Regional Airport, Watertown — 41 mi away. Directions
  • FSD Sioux Falls Regional Airport, Sioux Falls — 68 mi away. Directions
  • MSP Minneapolis–Saint Paul International Airport / Wold–Chamberlain Field, Minneapolis — 216 mi away (major hub). Directions

Although there is a small airport to the north of town, it has no scheduled air service. The only way to arrive is by road.
