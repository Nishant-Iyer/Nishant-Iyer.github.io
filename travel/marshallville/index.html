Overview

Marshallville is a village of about 800 (2020) in Wayne County in Northeast Ohio.

Marshallville Historical Days

Marshallville Antique Tractor Pullers

Antique tractor pulling event held on the last Saturday of each summer month. See the Facebook page for more details.

Heartland Trail Trailhead

Multi-use paved biking and walking trail. As of 2024, the trail extends 2 miles south of Marshallville. Future construction in 2024-26 will extend the trail further southwest to Orrville, and northeast to Clinton.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Apr–Oct. Summer highs reach about 84°F; winter lows dip to about 20°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Jun and May. (climate data from nearby Akron)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 380 miles, roughly 6 h 30 min without stops.
  • CAK Akron Canton Regional Airport, Akron — 15 mi away. Directions
  • CLE Cleveland Hopkins International Airport, Cleveland — 36 mi away (major hub). Directions

Route 94 runs north to south through town; interchanging at 585 and continuing into Wadsworth, North Royalton, Parma, and Cleveland to the north, and interchanging in Dalton and continuing into Amish country to the south.
