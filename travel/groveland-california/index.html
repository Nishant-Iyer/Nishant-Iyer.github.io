    Get in

    Place Yosemite Area Regional Transit System (YARTS)

    The YARTS shuttle travels on Highway 120 from Sonora to Yosemite Valley, with stops in Groveland at Mary Laveroni Park and the Yosemite Pines RV Park. Ticket prices include park entry, and can be purchased from the shuttle driver.

    🕒 Shuttle operates from mid-May through late-September⚠ Last checked May 2015 — confirm before you go

    Eat

    Eat Priest Station Cafe

    BurgersAmericanBreakfast / BrunchVegan options

    Restaurant in the tiny gold rush town of Priest, serving standard American food: burgers, brunch, sandwiches, etc. The small selection of outdoor equipment for sale hints that the restaurant's main business is as a roadside stop on the way to and from Yosemite. The mist machines outside are a nice touch for hot summer days, and good vegan options are available.

    📍 16756 Old Priest Grade, Big Oak Flat🕒 Mon–Wed 8AM–3PM, Thu–Sun 8AM–8PM☎ +1 209 962-1888Checked Aug 2023
