Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Oct. Summer highs reach about 82°F; winter lows dip to about 18°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: May and Jun. (climate data from nearby Indiana Dunes National Park)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 60 miles, roughly 1 h without stops.

Sample 3-day trip

  1. Day 1: La Porte — LaPorte County Courthouse, Marion Ridgeway Polygonal Barn, Downtown LaPorte Historic District.
  2. Day 2: Indiana Dunes National Park — Century of Progress Homes, Mt Baldy, West Beach.

Do

Do Pierogi Fest

The street is only open to foot traffic during the festival. There are food booths set up along the streets. The main attraction is walking up and down the street tasting food from the many different food vendors, listening to live bands playing, visiting the beer garden, or riding some carnival rides. - Late July.

Eat

Lake Perch is a local favorite that a traveler isn't going to find outside the Great Lakes regions. That being said, several locations in Northwest Indiana serve quality lake perch fresh from the Great Lakes.

Eat Teibel's Family Restaurant

Excellent lake perch and fried chicken. Loved by locals.

📍 1775 U.S. 41, Schererville
MapsWebsite

Eat Johnson's Fish & Shrimp

One of the best kept secrets in Northwest Indiana, this gem serves up excellent gulf shrimp and lake perch.

📍 2619 Central Avenue, Lake Station☎ +1 219 962-1956
Maps

Eat The Beach Café

Ownership of this restaurant has changed a couple of times, but the new owners are still offering the variety of seafood including the well known boned and buttered lake perch.

📍 903 N. Shelby, Gary☎ +1 219 938-1100
Maps

Eat The New Country Lounge

They also offer excellent lake perch, but the real gem of this restaurant is their shrimp dejonghe, "Jumbo white gulf shrimp broiled in a special butter crumb and garlic sauce".

📍 3700 Montgomery St, Hobart☎ +1 219 942-6699
