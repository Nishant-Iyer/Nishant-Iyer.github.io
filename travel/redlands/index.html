Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Oct–Jun. Summer highs reach about 94°F; winter lows dip to about 39°F. Very hot (Jul, Aug, Sep) — plan outdoor time for mornings. Wettest: Jan and Feb.

    Understand

    Climate

    Summers in Redlands can be pressingly hot, with average temperatures easily reaching 100°F. Temperatures over the rest of the year is much more pleasant, averaging around 80°F during spring and fall and 65°F during the winter months.

    Get in

    Ontario International Airport is the closest major airport, but it only serves a few international locations. Located west of Redlands, this airport serves as the regional point of entry. For international passengers, Los Angeles International Airport (LAX) is the optimum point of entry, and being the world's third busiest airport, it serves 159 non-stop destinations, making it easier to travel to and from. If taking the I-10 freeway, you can reach LAX in about 1 hour and 30 minutes. San Bernardino International Airport has only limited passenger service.

    If you are traveling by car, Redlands is located alongside Interstate 10, east of the 215 Interstate interchange. Route 210 terminates when it turns south and joins Interstate 10 in Redlands.

    A commuter rail line from San Bernadino will open in 2022. There will be three stations in Redlands.
