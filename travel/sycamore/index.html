Overview

Sycamore is a city in the Kishwaukee Valley, located in Northern Illinois with a population of about 17,188.

Best things about Sycamore

Farmers Market

Support our local Farmers and discover quality fare. Every Sunday 10am - 2pm at the corner of Somonauk and Elm Streets in downtown Sycamore.

Summer on State: Back to the Future

Free outdoor screening of Back to the Future. Real De Lorean cars will be there! Bring your lawn chairs and blankets and curl up under the stars. Summer on State movies are brought to you by the Sycamore Park District.

Treasures From the Attic

City-wide Estate, Yard and Sidewalk Sales. Pick up a map of Yard Sales from the downtown sidewalk sales. List your house's sale for free by calling 895-3456. Treasures From the Attic is brought to you by OC Imageworks

Cruise Night

free concert by Vintage Vinyl, food, beverages, 50s costume contest, remote control car races and a swing dancing competition. Cruise Night is brought to you by Auto Meter.
