Overview

Forsyth is a town in southeastern Montana. In 2020, about 1,600 people lived here. It is located on the southern bank of the Yellowstone River.

Get in

Forsyth is on Interstate 94 (I-94) with two exits, #93 and #95. It is also on U.S. Highway 12 (US-12).

There are no passenger trains servicing Forsyth. There is bus service by Rimrock Stages.

The nearest large airport is Billings Logan International Airport (BIL). There is also a small airport in Miles City.

Get around

There is no taxi service for Forsyth. You will need a vehicle to get around.

The town is split by the railroad tracks with about ⅓ on the street where the interstate exits are located. Three streets have railroad crossings to reach the rest of the town: Hwy 12, 10th Ave (near the Town Pump), and Slaughterhouse Rd (directly off the east I-94 exit).

See

The Downtown Historic District features several buildings constructed before 1930. Several local residences are on the National Register of Historic Places as well. The city website has a walking tour brochure.
