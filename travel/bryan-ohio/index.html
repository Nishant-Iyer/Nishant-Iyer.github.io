Best things about Bryan

Spangler Candy Museum

The Spangler Candy Company offers guided trolley tours through their candy factory, which manufactures lollipops. The gift shop sells single flavor bags of Dum Dums.

    Understand

    Bryan was founded in 1840, and became a small manufacturing city by 1940. Bryan is sometimes known as the Fountain city.

    Despite its small size, Bryan is the home of both Dum Dum lollipops, and the Etch a Sketch.
