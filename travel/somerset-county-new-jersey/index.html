Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Oct. Summer highs reach about 86°F; winter lows dip to about 29°F. Cold months (Jan) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Aug and Jul. (climate data from nearby New York City)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 830 miles, roughly 14 h 30 min without stops. Flying is usually faster.
  • TTN Trenton Mercer Airport, Ewing Township — 22 mi away. Directions
  • EWR Newark Liberty International Airport, Newark — 25 mi away (major hub). Directions

Sample 3-day trip

  1. Day 1: Belle Mead — Mill Pond Bridge, Historic River Road Crossing, Gulick/Ditmar House.
  2. Day 2: Montgomery Township — Montgomery Park, Montgomery Cinemas, Cougar Stadium.
  3. Day 3: Bernardsville — Somerset Hills Golf Club, Roxiticus Golf Club.
