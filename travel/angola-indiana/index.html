Overview

Angola is a town of 9,300 people (2020) and the county seat of Steuben County in Northern Indiana. It is the home of Trine University.

    Get in

    • US 20 goes east-west through the center of town
    • I-69 goes near the west side of town, and there are off-ramps at US 20 and N 200 W
    • Indiana 127 and Indiana 827 come in from the north
    • Old US 27 comes in from the south

    Get around

    Walking or riding a bike are the most common ways to get around downtown, although many large chains are closer to I-69, so driving might be the better way to go in that way.

    See

    See Civil War Monument

    Located in the Circle (roundabout of US 20 and Indiana SR 127 in the center of town) stands Steuben County's most popular monument, dedicated to people from Steuben County who fought in the Civil War. It has a plaque and small statue for each of the four branches of the military around the center pillar with a larger statue of Columbia on top.
