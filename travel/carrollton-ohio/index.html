Overview

Carrollton is a village in Carroll County, Ohio. It is the county seat.

Best things about Carrollton

Carroll County Courthouse

1884 second empire-style building listed on the National Register of Historic Places.

McCook House Museum

Museum in 1837 house built by a family famous for their exploits during the American Civil War.

Algonquin Mill Complex

Bluebird Farm

A restaurant, gift shop, and toy museum.

Carroll County Fair

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Apr–Oct. Summer highs reach about 84°F; winter lows dip to about 20°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Jun and May. (climate data from nearby Akron)
