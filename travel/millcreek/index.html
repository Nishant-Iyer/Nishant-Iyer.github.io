Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Apr–Jun, Sep–Oct. Summer highs reach about 94°F; winter lows dip to about 24°F. Very hot (Jul, Aug) — plan outdoor time for mornings. Cold months (Jan, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Apr and Mar. (climate data from nearby Salt Lake City)

    Understand

    Millcreek became a city in 2016. Millcreek spans far east to west due south of Salt Lake City and is home to many suburban neighborhoods.

    Get around

    Public transport is very lackluster in Millcreek, so a rental car is almost certainly needed.
