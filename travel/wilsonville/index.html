    Get in

    By car

    • Interstate 5 Wilsonville is accessed from exits #282/282A ("Milley Rd & Portland-Hubbard Hwy") through #286 ("Elligsen Rd") along I-5. There are three exits from I-5 into town. The Wilsonville Town Center (closest thing to a 'downtown') can be accessed from Exit #283 ("SW Wilsonville Rd") from both directions of I-5.
    • Interstate 205 begins and ends at exit #288 of I-5 between Tualatin and Wilsonville.
    • Wilsonville Rd. offers a winding trip through agricultural land and forested areas directly to Wilsonville and I-5 (Exit 238) from Newberg.

    By train

    • WES Train Commuter rail from Beaverton Transit Center to Wilsonville via Tigard & Tualatin. Passengers transfer to the MAX light rail in Beaverton to continue towards Portland. Nearest connections to Amtrak trains, Greyhound, Oregon Point, Tri-Met buses and trains; and other intercity bus lines are in Portland; and to the Cascade Point Bus (between Portland and Eugene) and other local Tri-Met buses are in Tualatin. The local train station is at the SMART Central Station on the other side of the freeway along SW Barber St.

    By bus

    • South Metropolitan Area Regional Transit (SMART) operates local public transportation in/around Wilsonville and to Salem (1x); Tualatin (2x) and to Canby (3x) from the SMART Central Station.
