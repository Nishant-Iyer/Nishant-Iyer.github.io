Best things about East Hartford

Wickham Park

280 acres park of gardens, sports facilities, woodlands and ponds run by a non-profit foundation.

Garvan-Carroll Historic District

A residential district of homes built between 1890 and the 1920s. The buildings are mostly wooden houses, which you can view from the street.

Pratt & Whitney Stadium at Rentschler Field

Stadium for football and soccer, where the University of Connecticut Huskies football team play.

Historical Society of East Hartford

Runs four old houses as museums, which are open occasionally. The museums are open on Sa or Su in July and August, check website for details.

Understand

East Hartford is a city with a history that is demonstrated by the presence of some charming buildings that are listed on the National Register of Historic Places in the United States of America. However, history has not passed the city by, as it hosts the headquarters of Pratt & Whitney aerospace corporation, a major local employer, and Pratt & Whitney Stadium, where the University of Connecticut Huskies football team plays.
