Overview

Moapa Valley is in Southern Nevada. It is somewhat agricultural and sleepy in nature below dry escarpments and barren southern Nevada peaks.

    Understand

    Moapa Valley is the result of the merger of two former communities in the Muddy River Valley, Logandale and Overton. There's another municipality called Moapa on the north side of I-15 that's more or less a part of the community too. This area was settled by Mormons after the Anasazi and Paiutes had lived here long before.

    Do Valley Of Fire State Park

    Here you can marvel at some rather majestic rock formations weathered by the forces of nature, including sites like Mouse's Tank, Elephant Rock, Atlatl Rock, and the White Domes. The visitor center and web page can orient you to the intricacies therein.

    Do Warm Springs Natural Area (Moapa Valley National Wildlife Refuge)

    This area preserves a natural oasis with thermal springs, where endangered dace fish live, and palm trees that you can explore with a 2/3-mile interpretive trail. Across Warm Springs Road is the Moapa Valley National Wildlife Refuge.
