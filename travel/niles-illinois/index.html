    Shop Golf Mill Shopping Center

    The prototypical suburban indoor shopping center, anchored by Kohl's, Target, and JC Penney. The mall, opened in 1960, features a strange tower designed to look like a golf ball (putting a spin on the mall's uncreative name—it's at the corner of Golf and Milwaukee, after all). As these kinds of malls are going the way of the dodo, this could be a tourist destination one day.

    📍 239 Golf Mill Center⚠ Last checked Jun 2019 — confirm before you go
