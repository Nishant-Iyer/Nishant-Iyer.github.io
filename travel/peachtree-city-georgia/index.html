Best things about Peachtree City

Lake Kedron

Allows fishing with a license.

Commemorative Air Force Museum

Maintains historic aircraft, and models mostly related to World War II.

Atlanta Air Show

Annual airshow showcasing a variety of aircraft.

Glenloch Recreation Complex

Public Park including a swimming pool and sports pitches.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Mar–May, Sep–Nov. Summer highs reach about 90°F; winter lows dip to about 36°F. Very hot (Jul) — plan outdoor time for mornings. Wettest: Jul and Mar. (climate data from nearby Atlanta)
