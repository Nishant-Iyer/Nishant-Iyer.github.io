Overview

Moundsville, West Virginia, is a city in Ohio Valley. It is the county seat of Marshall County.

8 sights · 2 things to do · 2 places to stay

Best things about Moundsville

Grave Creek Mound

Archeology Complex, Jefferson Ave. One of the largest and most famous conical burial mounds built by the Adena Indians, built between 250 to 150 BC. It is measured 69 feet high and 295 feet in diameter at its base.

Delf Nora Museum

Explains aspects of Adena Native American culture.

Fostoria Glass Museum

Dedicated to the products of Fostoria Glass, which moved to Moundsville in 1891 until it was forced to close in 1986.

West Virginia Penitentiary Tours
