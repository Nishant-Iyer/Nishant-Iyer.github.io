Overview

Fairfield and nearby Benton are small towns in Maine which are suburbs of Waterville.

1 sights · 1 things to do

Best things about Fairfield

L.C. Bates Museum

Science museum with plenty of taxidermy.

East Benton Fiddler's Contest

In its heydays of the 1970s and '80s, as many as 3,500 people came to revel in the park and listen to bluegrass. While the long-running festival isn't as large today, it's still a great family-friendly event.
