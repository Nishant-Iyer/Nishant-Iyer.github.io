Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 2,260 miles, roughly 39 h without stops. Flying is usually faster.
  • STS Charles M. Schulz Sonoma County Airport, Santa Rosa — 37 mi away. Directions
  • SMF Sacramento International Airport, Sacramento — 75 mi away (major hub). Directions

To get to Lakeport from San Francisco, take U.S. 101 north to Hopland, then head east on State 175. It is about 2.5 hours drive at normal speeds, up to 4 hours with heavy traffic (mostly in Marin County and Santa Rosa areas).
