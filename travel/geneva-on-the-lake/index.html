Best things about Geneva-on-the-Lake

Geneva State Park

Offers a Lake Erie beach, fishing, archery range, and other outdoor activities.

Jennie Munger Gregory Museum

Preserved farmhouse built in 1823.

Adventure Zone Family Fun Center

Small amusement park featuring go karts, bumper boats, a zip line, and other activities.

Thunder on the Strip

Annual bike rally held in September.

Lake Erie Canopy Tours

Offers zip line tours over Geneva State Park.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Oct. Summer highs reach about 84°F; winter lows dip to about 22°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Sep and Apr. (climate data from nearby Cleveland)
