Overview

Van Buren is a village in Hancock County in Northwest Ohio. Located next to I-75, it is primarily visited as either a brief stop for travelers on the interstate, or as a destination for those visiting the large state park located there.

Best things about Van Buren

Village Square

The historic village square is easily walkable, and has some quaint architecture.

Van Buren State Park

A large public park surrounding a lake with hiking and horse trails, as well as camping sites. Boating and fishing is allowed on the lake with a licence. Bow hunting is permitted with a licence.

Black Knights

Van Buren Schools has a variety of sports teams that play as the Black Knights. Sports include Football, Baseball, Soccer, and Basketball.

Allen Township Sports Plex

Has open baseball diamonds and soccer fields for recreation.

Van Buren Lions Club Apple Butter Festival

A festival of apple butter, foodstuff from food trucks, crafts, and music.

Understand

Van Buren was founded in 1833 and was named after the then vice president Martin Van Buren, who would later go on to become the 8th President.

By bike

Go Slippery Elm Trail

A trail that goes from North Baltimore to Bowling Green. The trailhead in North Baltimore is 5 mi north from Van Buren on flat country roads.
