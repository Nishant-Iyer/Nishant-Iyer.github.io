Overview

Dodgeville is a city in the Southern Wisconsin River Valley of Wisconsin. It is the county seat of Iowa County.

5 sights · 2 places to eat · 1 places to stay

Best things about Dodgeville

Dodge Mining Camp Cabin

A cabin built for lead miners in 1827, and the oldest standing structure in Iowa County.

Iowa County Courthouse

The courthouse was constructed in 1859 and is the oldest continuously active courthouse in Wisconsin.

Iowa County Historical Society Museum

A small history museum with artifacts from Iowa County's past. They also manage the Floyd School and the Dodge Mining Camp Cabin, listed above.

Floyd School

One-room schoolhouse built in 1886 that was in use until 1961. The school was originally located on Floyd Road (about halfway between Dodgeville and Spring Green) and was moved to the historical society grounds in 2008.

Slag Furnace

An outdoor stone furnace built in 1876 and operated only until 1890, used to re-melt slag (a by-product of iron smelting) to extract more iron.
