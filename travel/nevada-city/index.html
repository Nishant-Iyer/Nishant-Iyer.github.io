Overview

Nevada City is a city in Nevada County in California's Gold Country. It's known for its well-preserved 19th century downtown and countercultural affiliations.

2 sights · 3 things to do · 1 places to eat · 3 bars & cafés · 2 places to stay

Best things about Nevada City

Malakoff Diggins State Historic Park

State park featuring gold rush-era buildings with rock faces carved out by hydraulic mining. Also has access to camping and fishing in Blair lake.

Nevada County Narrow Gauge Railway Museum

Museum dedicated to preserving local history of narrow gauge used in the region. Features guided tours and a preservation shop for trains.

Scotts Flat Lake

Beautiful high elevation lake near Nevada City provides camping, day use, boating, and hiking recreation.

Firehouse #1 Museum

Historic museum featuring treasures from the area, including artifacts from the Donner Party. Limited hours.

Nevada City Film Festival

World renowned film festival since 2001 considered to be the “Sundance of the Sierra” for its focus on independent film.

    Get in

    By car

    From Sacramento take I-80 East to Auburn and then take 49 from Auburn to Grass Valley. From Truckee take I-80 west and take the highway 20 exit towards Nevada City and Grass Valley.

    By plane

    Go Sacramento International Airport (SMF)

    The closest international airport to Nevada City is Sacramento International (SMF). This is about an hour and 15 minutes away by car and allows transfer to both bus and rail connections to Nevada City.

    ⚠ Last checked Oct 2021 — confirm before you go
    MapsWebsite

    By train

    Go Amtrak

    Provides regular service to Auburn Amtrak station, 27 miles south of Nevada City. From the station, you would need to take Nevada County Connects bus 5 to central Grass Valley (about an hour bus ride) and then transfer to Nevada County Connects bus 1 to Nevada City. Be sure to check the bus schedule as buses are infrequent and run only limited times per day.

    ⚠ Last checked Oct 2021 — confirm before you go
    MapsWebsite

    By bus

    Place Local buses

    If you have time and the timing work out, you can get to Nevada City from Sacramento by using local buses. From Downtown Sacramento, you can take Sacramento Light Rail to the Watt/I-80 station. Here, you can take Placer Transit Route 10 to Auburn Amtrak Station and then from there, you can take Nevada County Connects bus 5 to central Grass Valley and then transfer to Nevada County Connects bus 1.
