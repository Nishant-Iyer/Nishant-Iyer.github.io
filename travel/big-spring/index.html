Best things about Big Spring

Big Spring State Park

Hangar 25 Air Museum

Big Spring Bombadier School during WWII, and then later incorporated as part of Webb Air Force Base, the hangar now holds a museum which showcases military history in the area.

Heritage Museum of Big Spring

History museum focusing on early pioneer life, railroad history, and oil production in the area.

Potton House

Restored Victorian home built in 1901 which showcases turn of the century home furnishings.

By car
