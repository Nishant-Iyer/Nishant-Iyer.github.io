    Get in

    Since the town of Dorris is small and remote, by car is the best way to go. Here are directions from Redding:

    Take I-5 north to Weed. Take US-97 to the NE until you reach Dorris. If you reach the Oregon border, you have gone too far.

    Get around

    You can probably walk or drive a car throughout Dorris. There isn't any public transportation, however.

    See

    If you are into roadside attractions, Dorris has the third-tallest flagpole in the US. There isn't much else, though.
