Overview

Tilton is a small city in the state of New Hampshire in New England that offers numerous hotels, places to eat, and gas stations. Primarily a bedroom community and service center it sits at the junction of both north-south and east-west highways and is the gateway to the NH Lakes Region.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Oct. Summer highs reach about 84°F; winter lows dip to about 17°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Jun and Oct. (climate data from nearby Manchester)

Tilton is located off of exit 20 on I-93 Styles Bridges Highway. Exit 20 gives easy access to the main shopping area on the east side of the town. Exit 19 can also be used (Northfield) though this is a northbound exit only. Exit 19 will give you easier access to the town center and attractions that lie to the west of I-93.
