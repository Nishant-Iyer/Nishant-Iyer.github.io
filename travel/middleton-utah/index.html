Overview

Middleton is a small Southern Utah community halfway between St. George and Washington. This article also covers Green Springs, a small community next to Middleton between Saint George and Washington.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Mar–May, Oct–Nov. Summer highs reach about 100°F; winter lows dip to about 29°F. Very hot (Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep) — plan outdoor time for mornings. Wettest: Feb and Jan. (climate data from nearby Zion National Park)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 1,730 miles, roughly 29 h 30 min without stops. Flying is usually faster.
  • SGU St George Regional Airport, St George — 6 mi away. Directions
  • CDC Cedar City Regional Airport, Cedar City — 47 mi away. Directions
  • LAS Harry Reid International Airport, Las Vegas — 115 mi away (major hub). Directions
