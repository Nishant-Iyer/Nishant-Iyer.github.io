Overview

Norwich is a city of 3,600 people (2020) in eastern Vermont across the Connecticut River from Hanover, New Hampshire. It is commuter town for nearby Hanover, New Hampshire, across the Connecticut River, and is home to some of the state of Vermont's wealthiest residents. The town contains the Norwich Mid-Century Modern Historic District.

Best things about Norwich

Montshire Museum of Science

Norwich Historical Society

Historic 1807 Lewis House, with an extensive collection of artifacts, photos, and archives relating to the town’s history. Also hosts annual exhibits and special events throughout the year.

Hike

The Appalachian Trail passes right through downtown Norwich. There are also a number of shorter trails in the town. A good resource for short hikes in the area is the Upper Valley Trails Alliance.

Visit an old cemetery

There are a number of old cemeteries in town, some of which contain graves dating back to the Revolutionary War period.

English Country Dancing

Join in an afternoon of English Country Dancing. All dances will be taught, no partner is needed, and all are welcome. Please wear clean, non-marking, soft-soled shoes (or socks) only. Contact for dates and times.

King Arthur Flour

Visit the King Arthur Flour store, café, or baking school. Shop, eat ($10–15 for breakfast or lunch), or take a baking class.

    Get in

    By car

    • From Boston (about 2.5 hours)

    I-93 north to I-89 N at Concord, NH. Exit I-89 at I-91 N in White River Junction, VT. Take Exit 13 at Norwich. Turning left at the exit takes you to downtown Norwich.

    • From Burlington, Vermont (about 1.5 hours)

    Take I-89 S to I-91 N in White River Junction, VT. Continue as above.

    • From New York (about 5 hours), southern New England and points south

    Take I-91 N to Exit 13. Continue as above.

    By plane

    • Cape Air flies to Lebanon Regional Airport (LEB) from Boston.
    • Other alternatives are Manchester (New Hampshire) and Burlington (Vermont), both about 1.5 hours away by car; or Logan International in Boston or Bradley International in Windsor Locks, both about 2.5 hours away.

    Shop Dan & Whit's General Store

    Open every day. Nexus from which all distances are measured. A true general store with a treasure trove of gadgets and tools spanning the early farming days of the area to the present. If you need something that is rare or out of season, they will have it in the back. Slip behind the deli counter to find gardening and sugaring supplies, sleds, hardware and paint. "If we don't have it, you don't need it."
