Overview

Grosse Pointe is an area in Wayne County in the state of Michigan. It includes the cities of Grosse Pointe, Grosse Pointe Park, Grosse Pointe Farms, Grosse Pointe Shores, and Grosse Pointe Woods. This article also covers the city of Harper Woods.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Oct. Summer highs reach about 84°F; winter lows dip to about 19°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: May and Jul. (climate data from nearby Detroit)

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 300 miles, roughly 5 h without stops.
  • DTW Detroit Metropolitan Wayne County Airport, Detroit — 26 mi away (major hub). Directions
  • FNT Bishop International Airport, Flint — 58 mi away. Directions

The nearest freeway is Interstate 94, which runs through Detroit and Harper Woods.
