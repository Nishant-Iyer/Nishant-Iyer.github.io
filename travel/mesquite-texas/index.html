Overview

Mesquite, also known as the Rodeo Capital of Texas, and prior hangout of outlaw train robber Sam Bass, is an eastern suburb of Dallas.

4 sights · 2 things to do · 1 places to eat · 1 places to stay

Updated from Wikivoyage on 1 November 2026 · Something wrong or missing? Improve it on Wikivoyage

Best things about Mesquite

Opal Lawrence Historical Park

AMC Dine-In Mesquite 30

Oversized movieplex, with IMAX theater.

Florence Ranch Homestead

Old farmstead circa 1871-1872, a Mesquite landmark.

Samuell Farm

Open but developed farmland with pond and woods and a strange tractor contraption where people can explore and play.

Mesquite Championship Rodeo

Rodeo performances, pony rides, a petting zoo, and more every Friday and Saturday evening from April to September.

Celebration Station

Kiddie place with amusement rides and arcade.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Feb–May, Oct–Nov. Summer highs reach about 97°F; winter lows dip to about 38°F. Very hot (Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep) — plan outdoor time for mornings. Wettest: Oct and May. (climate data from nearby Dallas)
