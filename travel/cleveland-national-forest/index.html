    Get in

    By foot

    The Pacific Crest Trail (PCT) is a well-known trail that extends along the West coast of the United States, from Mexico to Canada. It passes through California, Oregon, and Washington State.

    Fees and permits

    People planning on parking and walking through the forest are required to display an Adventure Pass. These can be obtained from businesses throughout the forest. $5 for a day pass and $30 for a year pass. You are not required to buy a pass if you are stopping temporarily, such as to use a restroom or to eat lunch.
