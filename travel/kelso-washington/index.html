    Get in

    By car

    • Interstate 5 (I-5) goes through Kelso:
    • Exit #39 (Allen St/Kelso) leads to most food, gasoline and lodging
    • Exit #40 (N Kelso Ave) accesses a residential district north of town.
    • Allen St: Goes through town from I-5 as Allen St to 5th Ave N (WB) & 4th Ave N (EB). It becomes the Ocean Beach Highway (SR-4) west of its intersection with Washington Way, west of town, connecting to the coast (Long Beach & Astoria) along the Washington side of the Columbia River.
    • Washington Way Local boulevard that connects West Kelso to Longview.

    Buy

    Shop Three Rivers Mall

    Three Rivers Mall hosts Regal Cinemas, JCPenney, Planet Fitness, Kelso Public Library, Sportsman Warehouse and numerous other shops & restaurants inside the mall and in several free standing establishments surrounding the mall (Target, Safeway, Pier 1 Imports, GuestHouse Kelso/Lonview (see 'sleep' in below), among others). An episode of the Food Network show Food Court Wars was filmed in the mall. The episode titled Gaufre Gourmet vs. Pressed originally aired on March 30, 2014.

    📍 351 Three Rivers DrSW of Allen and its junction with I-5 (Exit #39).🕒 Daily 11AM-8PM⚠ Last checked Sep 2020 — confirm before you go
    MapsWebsite

    Shop Strip Mall on 200-236 S Kelso Dr

    This smaller mall hosts Wilco Farm Store, Dollar Tree, Subway, a beauty supply shop, etc. in the mall itself, fronting a large parking lot. There are other free standing establishments closer to S Kelso Dr such as McDonald's, Super 8 motel, etc. in front of or next to this mall.

    📍 200-236 S Kelso DrSE of Allen and its junction with I-5 (Exit #39), along S Kelso Dr, opposite side of the freeway from Three Rivers Mall.🕒 Hours vary by tenant/storeChecked Sep 2026
    Maps

    Shop Triangle Center

    Southwest of the intersection of Ocean Beach Hwy (SR-4) & Washington Way (in a large triangular shaped lot). Triangle Center hosts Bed Bath & Beyond; Michaels, Maurices, Petco, Ross Dress for Less, Ulta, WinCo Foods and numerous other shops, banks & restaurants in the mall and in the immediate surrounding areas not part of the mall (Taco Bell, Sizzlers, Stuffy's, among others) along Ocean Beach Hwy & Washington Way. Unlike a traditional mall where everything is under one big roof, in a single building, this mall consists of several smaller buildings spread further out from each other in a huge parking lot.

    📍 1015 Ocean Beach Hwy🕒 Hours vary by tenant/store⚠ Last checked Sep 2020 — confirm before you go
