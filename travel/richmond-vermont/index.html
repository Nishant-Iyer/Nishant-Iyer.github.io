Overview

Richmond is on the eastern edge of the Champlain Valley, at the foot of the Green Mountains. This article also covers the nearby towns of Huntington and Bolton.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Sep. Summer highs reach about 78°F; winter lows dip to about 7°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Mar, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Jul and Jun. (climate data from nearby Montpelier)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 920 miles, roughly 16 h without stops. Flying is usually faster.
  • BTV Patrick Leahy Burlington International Airport, Burlington — 9 mi away. Directions
  • PBG Plattsburgh International Airport, Plattsburgh — 29 mi away. Directions
  • ALB Albany International Airport, Albany — 121 mi away (major hub). Directions

Richmond is 2 miles (3.2 km) south of Interstate 89. Take Exit 11, State Highway 117, from I-89 and go south-east.
