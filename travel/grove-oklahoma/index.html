Overview

Grove is in the Green Country region of Oklahoma. With Grand Lake o' The Cherokees at its doorstep, it's a retiree's and fisherman's haven.

4 sights · 3 things to do · 2 places to stay

Updated from Wikivoyage on 1 November 2026 · Something wrong or missing? Improve it on Wikivoyage

Best things about Grove

Lendonwood Gardens

8 acre garden with azaleas, dogwoods, hostas, evergreens. Also a Japanese garden.

Grand Lake O The Cherokees

This lake is kind of the town's livelihood (or lifeblood) and is much loved for boating and fishing.

Har-Ber Village Museum

Like one of those reconstructed pioneer village type of things with demonstrations and workshops and a nature trail.

Cayuga Mission Church

A church that goes back to 1893 that was quarried from local stone.

Honey Creek Area at Grand Lake State Park

Access point to Grand Lake of the Cherokees with swimming pool, boat landing, docks, swimming, deer, and campfires.

Grand Lake Casino

Indian Gaming

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Mar–May, Sep–Oct. Summer highs reach about 94°F; winter lows dip to about 28°F. Very hot (Jul, Aug) — plan outdoor time for mornings. Wettest: May and Jun. (climate data from nearby Tulsa)
