Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Oct–Jun. Summer highs reach about 91°F; winter lows dip to about 45°F. Very hot (Aug, Sep) — plan outdoor time for mornings. Wettest: Feb and Jan. (climate data from nearby Pasadena)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

Get around

Covina is served by two bus systems, two major cab companies, and the Metrolink rail transit system. The two major bus systems are Foothill Transit and Metro. The major cab companies include:

  • Covina Taxi at +1 626-428-1646
  • Blue Cab Taxi Express at +1 626-967-7250
