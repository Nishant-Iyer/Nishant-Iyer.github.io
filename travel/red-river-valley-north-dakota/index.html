Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Sep. Summer highs reach about 82°F; winter lows dip to about -6°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Mar, Nov, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Jun and Jul. (climate data from nearby New Rockford)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 810 miles, roughly 14 h without stops. Flying is usually faster.
  • DVL Devils Lake Regional Airport, Devils Lake — 33 mi away. Directions
  • GFK Grand Forks International Airport, Grand Forks — 49 mi away. Directions

Sample 3-day trip

  1. Day 1: Fargo — North Dakota State University, Plains Art Museum, Fargo Air Museum.
  2. Day 2: Grand Forks — Ralph Engelstad Arena, Sports, Theater.
  3. Day 3: Wahpeton — Richland County Hisorical Museum, Chahinkapa Park and Zoo, The Big Catfish.
