Overview

Mansfield is a borough in Tioga County in the state of Pennsylvania.

Plan your trip

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Sep. Summer highs reach about 80°F; winter lows dip to about 15°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Jun and Jul. (climate data from nearby Ithaca)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).
