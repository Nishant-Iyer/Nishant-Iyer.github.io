Getting there

  • From you: to see whether to drive or fly, and how long it takes.
  • SLE Salem-Willamette Valley Airport/McNary Field, Salem — 51 mi away. Directions
  • PDX Portland International Airport, Portland — 71 mi away (major hub). Directions

Pacific City is located north of Lincoln City on the Oregon coast. The easiest way to get there is to take highway 101 which leads thru Pacific City. You can take a number of highways west to get to 101, depending on where you are located. Highways 22, 99 and 18 are the best choices for getting there from Salem or Portland.

    Eat

    Eat Pelican Pub & Brewery

    Breakfast / Brunch

    Offers a wide variety of meals for breakfast, lunch and dinner. The prices are reasonable considering the large portions that are served. They have an excellent selection of brews including ales and stouts. It is located right on the beach with a view of Haystack Rock.

    Stay Inn at Cape Kiwanda

    Offers ocean view rooms and comfortable lodging and sleeping arrangements. It is a nice way to get away for the weekend and relax.

    Stay Shorepine Vacation Rentals

    Offers beach homes with fully equipped kitchens, grills, deck furniture, bicycles, washers and dryers. Homes accommodate 4 - 10 people and are supplied with linens and towels, detergent for laundry, and toiletries. There is private beach access through their own boardwalk. The clubhouse has a complete fitness room, pool table, free WIFI access, leather couches and a large flat-screen TV.
