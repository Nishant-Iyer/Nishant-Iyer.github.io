Overview

Harbor Beach is a city in Huron County, part of the Thumb region in Central Michigan. The population is 1,664 as of the 2014 US Census estimates.

    Understand

    Harbor Beach is a small town sitting on Lake Huron in Huron County. Major highways in the town include M-25 and M-142, which intersect practically less than a mile from the shores of the Great Lake. M-25 allows one to reach northern Huron County and further south to Port Huron. M-142 provides easy access to the larger city of Bad Axe, though its current population sits at just over 3,000 residents.

    Get in

    Get around

    The main roads of Harbor Beach are Michigan State Roads 25 (Huron Ave.) and 142 (State St./Sand Beach Rd.).
