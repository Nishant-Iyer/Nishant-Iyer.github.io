Overview

Lewisboro is a town of about 12,000 residents in northern Westchester County, about 40 miles north of New York City in the state of New York.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Oct. Summer highs reach about 84°F; winter lows dip to about 22°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Oct and Jun. (climate data from nearby Pleasantville)
