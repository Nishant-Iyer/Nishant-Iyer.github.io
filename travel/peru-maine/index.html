Best things about Peru

Worthley Pond

Worthley Pond is a moderate size pond with camps surrounding it. Activities include swimming, canoeing, fishing, and boating. Due to the size of the pond, there may be restrictions as to the size of boats allowed.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Jul–Aug. Summer highs reach about 55°F; winter lows dip to about -3°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr, Oct, Nov, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Oct and Jul. (climate data from nearby Mount Washington State Park)
