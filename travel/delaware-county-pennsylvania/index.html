Get in

By plane

  • Philadelphia International Airport (PHL) is located in Tincum Township, Delaware County. The airport is the primary international hub for American Airlines, and is also served by most major airlines. Philadelphia International has non-stop service from Canada, the Caribbean, Europe, Israel and Mexico.

By car

Most of Delaware County has access to Interstate 476 (locally known as the "Blue Route"). Other major highways running through Delaware County include Interstate 95 and Interstate 76 (The Schuykill Expressway).
