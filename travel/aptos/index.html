    Do Seacliff State Beach

    This beach can get crowded in the summer, but has some nice walks, a little visitors' center, and a striking centerpiece: the Palo Alto, a concrete ship with a strange history. It rots away in the water but is fascinating in a melancholy way. Read about it in the center if it's open when you're there (call ahead due to state budget cuts).

    📍 State Park DrExit at State Park Drive from Hwy 1 in Aptos.💲 Parking fee, unless you are creative and careful regarding street parking☎ +1 831 685-6442
    MapsWebsite
