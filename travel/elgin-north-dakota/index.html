Overview

Elgin is a town in Western North Dakota. It has a population of about 659 people. Its elevations is 2,351 feet.

2 sights · 6 things to do · 2 places to eat · 1 bars & cafés · 1 places to stay

Best things about Elgin

Elgin Museum

Open during summer months by appointment. Railroad depot museum with an original caboose.

Medicine Rock State Historic Site

This site marks an ancient, sacred American Indian gathering place that was used for dance ceremonies before a buffalo hunt. Look for the circular ring of dark vegetation, site of the pow wows

Elgin Golf Course

Lake Tschida

Fishing for catfish, walleye, perch, northern pike, crappie, bass, and saugeye.

Tail Race

Fishing for catfish, walleye, northern pike, and bass

Sheep Creek Dam

Fishing for trout, walleye, blue gills, and bass

Understand

Elgin is much larger than Carson, 14 miles to the east, which is the County Seat. It describes itself as "A land of rolling hills, bountiful wildlife, and beautiful sunsets".

Get in

  • By car: from exit 108 off I-94, 32 miles south on state highway 49.
  • By air: Elgin Municipal Airport with a 3,000 foot runway.
