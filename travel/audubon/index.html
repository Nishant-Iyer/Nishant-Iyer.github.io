Best things about Audubon

Albert the Bull

The world's largest bull statue. The red and white Albert gazes at drivers as they "mooooove" north on Highway 71. Worth a stop and a photo op.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Apr–Oct. Summer highs reach about 88°F; winter lows dip to about 15°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: May and Aug. (climate data from nearby Omaha)
