Overview

Vermilion is a city in Ohio. It is in both Erie County and Lorain County. Located on the banks of the Vermilion River and Ohio's North Shore.

Best things about Vermilion

Harbor Town 1837 Historic District

Vermilion History Museum

The museum illustrates the manner in which 19th- and 20th-century letter press printing and newspaper publishing were accomplished. The building served as a print shop and a weekly newspaper from 1905 to 1964.

Mystic Belle

(off US 6). Paddle-wheel tour of historic and scenic Vermilion River. Jun-Sep: Tu-Su 2PM, 4PM and on weekends only, one-half hour before sunset.

Vermilion Family YMCA

Romp's Dairy Dock

Schoeple Garden

Schoeple Garden, near the Vermilion River, is composed of 70 acres of beautiful botanical gardens and natural woodland. Guided tours are available.

    Understand

    The Vermilion Harbor dates to about 1840, today it is still a favorite port with over 8,000 pleasure craft at the public docks and marinas.

    Get in

    Vermilion is located on US-6 on the southern shores of Lake Erie. It is easily accessible via the Ohio Turnpike (I-80/90) and St. Rt. 2.

    By boat

    Vermilion is accessible by private boat.

    Boat launch ramps:

    • Vermilion Municipal Docks Launch Ramp W. River Rd., +1 440-967-7087 Ramp fee is $6, operating Apr-Nov
    • Bulan's Boatyard Launch Ramp 710 Vermilion Rd., +1 440-967-5785 Ramp fee is $5, rest rooms and winter storage available
    • Maple Grove Mairna Launch Ramp 1120 Vermilion Rd., +1 440-967-4525

    Marinas:

    • Bulan's Boatyard, 710 Vermilion Rd. +1 440-967-5785
    • Romp's Water Port & Dairy Dock, 5055 Liberty Ave. +1 440-967-4342
    • Valley Harbor Marina, 1295 River Rd. +1 440-967-5225

    By car

    Vermilion is easily accessible by car.

    By plane

    There is no public airport nearby.

    Get around

    The business district is walkable. One can also rent a golf cart to get around.
