See

One place you won't be able to visit, but you might want to tell folks back home that you were near, is what the US Geological Survey calls "Alcohol and Drug Abuse Lake". In Columbia, it is a 92-acre (37 hectares) body of water inside a state-run drug rehab facility, which prefers to call it the Morris Village Pond. The pond is enclosed by a fence, part of which runs along Wood Circle.

Do

  • Late September: If you are in town, you must visit the Okra Strut in Irmo: a gathering in the woods that celebrates the classic West African-originated soul food dish. You will find music, colorful costumes, and as much fried okra as you can eat. Watch the parade, see Miss Okra Strut represent the community, and maybe even run a 5K to burn off a calorie or two from carnival food.
