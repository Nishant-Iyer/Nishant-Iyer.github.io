Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Jun, Sep–Oct. Summer highs reach about 91°F; winter lows dip to about 21°F. Very hot (Jul) — plan outdoor time for mornings. Cold months (Jan, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: May and Apr. (climate data from nearby Ogden)

Understand

Garden City is the largest town in Rich County with a population of just over 500. It sits on the shore of Bear Lake.

Get in

Drive north on I-15 from Salt Lake City. Take exit 362 (Brigham City), and follow US 89/91 to Logan. Continue on US 89 to Garden City. It is about 2 and half hours away from Salt Lake City.

Get around

A car will be needed to get around most of the time. There are bike rentals and many miles of trails.
