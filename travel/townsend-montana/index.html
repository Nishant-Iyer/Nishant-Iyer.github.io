Overview

Townsend is a town in Montana. It is the county seat of Broadwater County and about 1,700 people live there. Townsend is at the southern tip of Canyon Ferry Reservoir, a year-round popular spot for boaters and fisherman.

    Townsend is at the intersection of US-12 and US-287, 34 miles southeast of Helena.
