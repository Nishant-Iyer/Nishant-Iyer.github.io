Overview

Ellensburg is a city of 21,000 people (2019) in the North Central Cascades of Washington State. It is home to Central Washington University, and also to the Ellensburg Rodeo.

Best things about Ellensburg

Central Washington University

The ten thousand undergraduates of CWU very nearly outnumber all the other residents of Ellensburg.

Olmstead Place State Park

This 217 acre state park has farm houses, barns and other outbuildings functioning as a museum of sorts depicting pioneer farm life in the 19th century.

Gallery One Visual Arts Center

Gallery One Visual Arts Center is dedicated to the creation, exhibition and appreciation of visual arts in Central Washington.

Ellensburg Rodeo

The Ellensburg Rodeo has been called one of America’s top 25 rodeos. Within the Northwest, it is second in size only to the Calgary Stampede. Established in 1923, it regularly attracts more than 600 contestants

    Get in

    Ellensburg is at the junction of Interstates 90 and 82. Road is the only practical way in, there is no air or rail service.

    • Interstate 90 (I-90) is along the southwest edge of town. It is 110 mi (1 hr 38 min) east of Seattle and 170 mi (2 hr 44 min) west of Spokane.
    • Exit #106 W. University Way & Thorp Hwy. US-97 northbound is the first left (north) from the east side of the freeway.
    • Exit #109 S Canyon Rd.
    • Interstate 82 (I-82) begins southeast of Ellensburg at Exit 110 from I-90. I-82 goes southeast through Yakima 36 mi, Toppenish, Sunnyside, Tri-Cities 113 mi and down into Hermiston, Oregon 142 mi to Interstate 84.
    • US 97 goes north across Blewett Pass to Wenatchee, Omak 160 mi (3 hr) and another 48 mi (45 min) to Osoyoos, BC at the Canadian border. Going southbound, US-97 follows I-82 to Union Gap just south of Yakima.

    Drink Whipsaw Brewing

    Bar / Café$

    Whipsaw Brewing is one of the newer breweries in Ellensburg and has a great lineup of beers. There is indoor and outdoor seating with a few games about in the beer garden. A food truck is there pretty much every day. There are mostly common styles of beer brewed here, with maybe a dozen on tap.
