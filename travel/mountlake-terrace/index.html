Overview

Mountlake Terrace is a city in the Puget Sound region of Washington State, and is one of many Seattle's northern suburbs along Interstate 5.

Get in

By car

Interstate 5 passes through Mountlake Terrace, traveling north and south.

By light rail

Link light rail, operated by Sound Transit, stops at

Go Mountlake Terrace Station

📍 6001 236th St SWChecked Dec 2025
MapsWebsite

Adult fares are a flat $3.00, regardless of distance; children 18 and under ride for free. A train runs every 8-15 minutes from 5:00 to midnight. All trains come from Lynnwood City Center to the north, and from Shoreline, Northgate, University of Washington, Capitol Hill and Downtown to the south after splitting at International District/Chinatown:

  • The 1 Line from Downtown Federal Way to the south, via Seattle-Tacoma International Airport, Tukwila and South Seattle.
  • The 2 Line from Downtown Redmond to the east, via Bellevue and Mercer Island.

By bus

Place Sound Transit

Has regional public transit routes in King County (hubs in Bellevue and Seattle), Snohomish County (hubs in Everett and Lynnwood), and Pierce County. Most of these buses connect at the Mountlake Terrace Transit Center.
