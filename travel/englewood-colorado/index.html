Overview

Englewood is a city of 35,000 people (2019) in the Denver Area of Colorado. Englewood is a full-service city with its own, independent park, library, and public works systems, but little to draw visitors.

As part of the Denver Metro Area, Englewood is served primarily by the Denver points of entry (airport, rail, bus, etc.). Getting to Englewood (proper) requires traveling US 85 (Santa Fe Blvd.), Broadway Road, or, US 285 (Hampden Avenue). Note that many areas to the southeast use Englewood as their city designation due to postal assignments. Englewood (proper) centers around the intersection of US 285 and Broadway Road.

    Get in

    As part of the Denver Metro Area, Englewood is served primarily by the Denver points of entry (airport, rail, bus, etc.). Getting to Englewood (proper) requires traveling US 85 (Santa Fe Blvd.), Broadway Road, or, US 285 (Hampden Avenue). Note that many areas to the southeast use Englewood as their city designation due to postal assignments. Englewood (proper) centers around the intersection of US 285 and Broadway Road.

    Englewood is served by many RTD routes, however the primary routes are Light Rail Lines C and D at Englewood Station and Oxford Station, as well as Route 0.
