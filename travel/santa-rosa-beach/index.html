Overview

Santa Rosa Beach is in the Emerald Coast region of Florida. The settlement, once known as Hogtown, is on the shores of Hogtown Bayou of the Choctawhatchee Bay.

6 sights · 2 things to do · 1 places to stay

Best things about Santa Rosa Beach

Eden Gardens State Park

A 115 acre Florida State Park and historic site in Point Washington, south of Freeport, off US-98 on County Route 395.

Topsail Hill Preserve State Park

A 1,640-acre park with three miles of white sand beaches, along with lakes, and wetlands. The endangered Choctawhatchee beach mouse can be found here.

Deer Lake State Park

There are beaches and picnicking areas in the park, but it also provides opportunities for birding, fishing, and wildlife viewing.
