Understand

Moultrie is a city of 15,000 people in southwestern Georgia. It is the county seat of Colquitt County and self-described "Antique Capital of South Georgia." It rather surprisingly even has a medical school satellite campus (PCOM) where one could get a DO degree.

Get in

It's about 20 miles due west of I-75, and US Route 319 provides a direct link to both it and Tallahassee to the south.

Do Reed Bingham State Park

At this park, you can hike the 3.5 mile Coastal Plains Nature Trail as it traverses a bald cypress swamp, sandhill area, and pitcher plant bog with boardwalks and Spanish moss-draped trees. There are also canoe, kayak, and bicycle rentals and even a miniature golf course.
