Overview

Elk Grove is a city of 176,000 people (2020) in Sacramento County in California. Elk Grove has many wineries, wine cellars, and vineyards.

4 sights · 3 things to do · 4 places to eat · 2 bars & cafés · 2 places to stay

Best things about Elk Grove

Elk Grove Historical Society

Small museum with some history about Elk Grove that holds events for some holidays, such as Halloween and Christmas. Good place to take children to.

Grave of Elitha Cumi Donner Wilder

A small historical California landmark in remembrance of the survivor of the Donner Party.

Site of the First County Free Library Branch in California

Another historical landmark of the state of California.

Community Rose Garden

If it's on your way, this is a nice garden to stop and relax in. It's inside of a park that also has places to walk.

Pinot's Palette

An art studio chain where you can sign up for classes and paint, along with enjoying alcohol. The bartenders make pretty good recommendations.

Exit Strategy Games

An escape room; one of two locations, the other in Mammoth Lakes. A good activity for families or for parties.
