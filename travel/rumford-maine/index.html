Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Jul–Aug. Summer highs reach about 55°F; winter lows dip to about -3°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr, Oct, Nov, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Oct and Jul. (climate data from nearby Mount Washington State Park)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 1,070 miles, roughly 18 h 30 min without stops. Flying is usually faster.
  • AUG Augusta State Airport, Augusta — 41 mi away. Directions
  • PWM Portland International Jetport, Portland — 64 mi away (major hub). Directions

Rumford is located on Route 2 between Bethel and Farmington. It is also along the Scenic Route 17 between Jay and Rangeley. Visitors from the south can find Rumford at the Western end of Route 108.

Day 1

  • Morning Shopper's Island, Strathglass Building, Rumford Historical Society
  • Lunch Rumford House of Pizza
  • Afternoon Edmund Sixtus Muskie Memorial, Strathglass Park
  • Dinner Mountain Valley Variety
  • Evening Hotel Rumford

Day 2

  • Morning Rumford Municipal Building, Mechanics Institute Building, Paul Bunyan Statue
  • Afternoon Indian Sillhouettes, Rumford Museum
  • Evening Eagle's Club

Day 3

  • Morning Hotel Harris, Vietnam Memorial, Pennacook (Rumford) Falls
  • Afternoon Rumford Library, Rumford Settlement Marker
  • Evening American Legion Hall
