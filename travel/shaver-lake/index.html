Overview

Shaver Lake is a vacation community less than an hour east of Fresno on the western slopes of the central Sierra Nevada Mountain Range, between Yosemite National Park and Sequoia National Park.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Mar–May, Oct–Nov. Summer highs reach about 98°F; winter lows dip to about 40°F. Very hot (Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep) — plan outdoor time for mornings. Wettest: Jan and Feb. (climate data from nearby Fresno)
