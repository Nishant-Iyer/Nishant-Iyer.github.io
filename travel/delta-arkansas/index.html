Overview

The Arkansas Delta is the eastern section of Arkansas along the Mississippi River Delta and includes Jonesboro and West Memphis.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Mar–May, Sep–Nov. Summer highs reach about 92°F; winter lows dip to about 33°F. Very hot (Jul, Aug) — plan outdoor time for mornings. Wettest: Apr and Mar. (climate data from nearby Memphis)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 650 miles, roughly 11 h without stops. Flying is usually faster.
  • MEM Frederick W. Smith International Airport, Memphis — 43 mi away (major hub). Directions
  • GLH Mid Delta Regional Airport, Greenville — 79 mi away. Directions

Jonesboro Municipal Airport (JBR) or Memphis International Airport (MEM) across the River in Tennessee.

See

See Jacksonport State Park

The site of a thriving river port at the confluence of the White and Black River that saw many a paddleboat and also the surrender of the Confederate Army of Northern Arkansas to Union forces in 1865, this former county seat features a courthouse museum and visitor center, along with just a good snapshot of the past.

off of Hwy 69 northwest of Newport💲 Free☎ +1 870 523-2143

Do Village Creek State Park

A chance to explore Crowley's Ridge with wooded scenery, wildlife specimens like deer and armadillos, rooting for grubs and such, and a segment of the Trail of Tears to walk upon. Plus camping and a chance to be eaten alive by mosquitoes.
