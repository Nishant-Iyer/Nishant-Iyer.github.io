Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Feb–May, Oct–Nov. Summer highs reach about 97°F; winter lows dip to about 38°F. Very hot (Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep) — plan outdoor time for mornings. Wettest: Oct and May. (climate data from nearby Dallas)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 980 miles, roughly 17 h without stops. Flying is usually faster.
  • TYR Tyler Pounds Regional Airport, Tyler — 28 mi away. Directions
  • GGG East Texas Regional Airport, Longview — 68 mi away. Directions
  • DAL Dallas Love Field, Dallas — 73 mi away (major hub). Directions

To get here from Dallas, take US 175 South. From Houston, take I-45 North to Corsicana, then Texas State Highway 31 East. From Austin, take I-35 North to Waco, then Texas State Highway 31 East. From Tyler take US 31 West.
