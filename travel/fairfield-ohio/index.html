    Groceries

    Shop Jungle Jim's International Market

    A grocery store that's effectively a small theme park. Jungle Jim's is best known for their large selection of international foods, including African, Middle Eastern, European, Eastern European, Hispanic, Indian/South Asian, and Asian. They have thousands of different types of wine and beer, more than 1,000 different types of cheese, 1,500 different types of hot sauce, etc. Robotic characters are installed in some themed aisles, and a non-operational monorail, and other features really make this feel like a 1980s theme park. So huge that you could easily spend several hours wandering up and down the aisles. Also offers a cooking school.

    📍 5440 Dixie HwyFairfield🕒 8AM–10PM daily☎ +1 513 674-6000Checked Jul 2023
