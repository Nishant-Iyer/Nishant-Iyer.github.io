Overview

Hankinson is a small, rural town in the Red River Valley of North Dakota. It has a population of about 1,058 people.

3 sights · 1 things to do · 1 places to eat · 4 places to stay

Best things about Hankinson

Harkinson Railroad Museum

Harkinson City Park

Tennis courts, ice skating with warming house, softball, outdoor stage, playground, basketball courts, horseshoe pits, picnic shelters, RV hookups, grills.

Lake Elsie Park

Picnic area, swimming, sandy beach, boat dock and trailer parking, fishing and boating.

Dakota Magic Casino

Open year-round.
