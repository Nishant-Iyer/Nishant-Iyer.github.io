Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Oct–Apr. Summer highs reach about 93°F; winter lows dip to about 52°F. Very hot (May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep) — plan outdoor time for mornings. Wettest: Jun and Jul. (climate data from nearby Winter Haven)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 1,230 miles, roughly 21 h without stops. Flying is usually faster.
  • LAL Lakeland Linder International Airport, Lakeland — 25 mi away. Directions
  • MCO Orlando International Airport, Orlando — 29 mi away (major hub). Directions

Two expressways go to Haines City. US-27 goes north-south across the west side of the city, ultimately connecting with I-4 near Walt Disney World. US-17/92 also passes through the city from east-west; it enters from the northeast on 17th Street and enters from the west on Hinson Avenue.
