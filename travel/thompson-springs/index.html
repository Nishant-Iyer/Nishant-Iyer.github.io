Best things about Thompson Springs

Sego Canyon Petroglyphs

Ghost Town of Sego

Former coal-mining town that was completely abandoned in the 1950s. A boarding house, company store, and some foundations and dugouts are all that remain.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Apr–May, Sep–Oct. Summer highs reach about 99°F; winter lows dip to about 22°F. Very hot (Jun, Jul, Aug) — plan outdoor time for mornings. Wettest: Oct and Aug. (climate data from nearby Arches National Park)
