Overview

Cedarville is a college village in Southwest Ohio, near Dayton.

2 sights · 2 things to do · 3 places to eat · 1 places to stay

Best things about Cedarville

Cedarville University

A private Christian university that was founded in 1887. Offers campus tours.

Harper Mausoleum

A mausoleum from 1915 noted for being stylized after ancient Egyptian architecture. Features twin sphinx.

Cedarville Opera House

A historic opera house built in 1888. Hosts a variety of performing arts shows.

Cedarville Yellow Jackets

The collegiate athletics teams of Cedarville University.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Apr–Oct. Summer highs reach about 86°F; winter lows dip to about 22°F. Cold months (Jan) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Apr and May. (climate data from nearby Dayton)

Understand

Founded in 1816 as Milford, the name was changed to Cedarville in 1843 for the cedar trees growing near the town center.
