Overview

Northfield is a city in The suburbs of Chicago Illinois It is about 20 miles North of the city of Chicago. The Population is around 5500 people. The city is primarily a residential village, with a small section of commercial real-estate.

    Get in

    • By Car I-94/US-41 (local Interstate, known to locals as the Edens expressway), Five minutes from the interstate, with connections to other local suburbs.
    • By Plane O’Hare International Airport (Closer to the Northern Suburbs, Including Northfield, most major airlines fly there) or Midway International Airport (closer for the city of Chicago, cheaper airlines fly there, still access to Northfield).
    • By taxi From both airports or from anywhere in the city.
    • By train From both airports and the Chicago (Metra, and the “L”) is Chicago’s subway except it's above ground)
    • By bus Pace/Metra, From trains, airports, and other local suburbs.

    Get around

    • Pace bus - local public transportation
    • Bike - many new bike trails through Forest Preserves
    • Car - very few taxis throughout the roads

    Do Local Farmer's Market

    Starting in late May through early September local farmers come to sell everything from fresh produce to milk and cheese. Other vendors sell baked goods and popcorn. A good place to meet and greet with the local people in the town to find out what is happening during the week. Weekends only. Located across from New Trier High School(in the field).

    Eat

    Eat Northfield Restaurant

    Breakfast / Brunch

    A local restaurant were all the locals go to have breakfast. They offer everything from your basic scrambled eggs and toast to a creative take on a contemporary breakfast. The employees are all so friendly and the food is so much like your mom makes it you won't even believe you're not at home. Good home-made cooking. The location very close to interstate and main area of town.

    📍 391 Central Ave☎ +1 847 446-3808
