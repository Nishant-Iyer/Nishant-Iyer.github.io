    Get in

    Wall Township is best reached by car. It is at the eastern end of I-195 and the southern end of NJ-18. It can also be reached by the Garden State Parkway at exit 98.

    NJ Transit bus route 830 from Asbury Park has a stop at the Target in Wall Township.

    Get around

    There is no public transit for getting around the township and most major roads don't have sidewalks, so a car is the only good way to get around.
