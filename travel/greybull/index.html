See The Museum of Flight & Aerial Firefighting

The curious traveler can't miss investigating the row of planes behind the rest stop in Greybull. This leads to a single-wide trailer where inside are airplane parts and equipment on display and of course someone to collect the entrance fee. After a video and a step outside a short trail both awes and disappoints. The retired civilian and military planes were converted for firefighting and while some are still flight worthy others are slowly rotting away.
