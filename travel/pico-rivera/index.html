    Do

    Do Pico Rivera Sports Arena

    The Pico Rivera Sports Arena was built next to Bicentennial Park in 1979 in dedication to Pico Rivera residents who served the United States during every war up to the Vietnam War. Sitting around 6,000, it is said to be the largest Mexican-style rodeo ring "Rancho de Charro" in the country. It hosts about 25 shows annually, featuring rodeos, wrestling, and boxing.

    📍 11003 E. Rooks Rd

    Eat

    Eat A-Mi Hacienda

    Mexican

    The price range is moderate and has valet parking, no fee to park if you're there for the restaurant but it's about $5 if you're there for the club. The mood is very casual and fun. The food is good Mexican cuisine. They have a live DJ and dancing. If you're looking for a good time late at night without the kids, this is the place to go. If you're looking to take your family here, going during the daylight hours is suggested.

    📍 9613 Whittier Blvd☎ +1 562 699-2500⚠ Last checked Aug 2016 — confirm before you go
    Video reviews: Instagram reels · TikTok · YouTube
    Maps

    Eat Dal Rae

    If you're looking for something classier and in around the same moderate price range, then Dal Rae is your place. Perfect for a romantic date or a special occasion without spending too much. It's quality without the cost. Opened back in 1958, it has kept its atmosphere along with its piano and dance floor. Dal Rae also has a charming patio area where lunch and dinner may also be served.

    📍 9023 E. Washington Blvd☎ +1 562 949-2444
