Overview

Aroostook is the northernmost region in Maine. It is coextensive with Aroostook County.

Plan your trip

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 1,210 miles, roughly 21 h without stops. Flying is usually faster.
  • PQI Presque Isle International Airport, Presque Isle — 26 mi away. Directions
  • BGR Bangor International Airport, Bangor — 128 mi away. Directions
  • PWM Portland International Jetport, Portland — 224 mi away (major hub). Directions

Sample 3-day trip

  1. Day 1: Caribou — The Nylander Museum, Caribou Performing Arts Center, Goughan's Berry Farm.
  2. Day 2: Fort Kent — University of Maine, Ployes Festival, Scarecrow Festival.
  3. Day 3: Frenchville — Frenchville Historical Society, The Green Water Tank, The Station House.

Get in

By plane

  • Northern Maine Regional Airport at Presque Isle (PQI), 650 Airport Drive, Presque Isle, +1 207-764-2550. Served by PenAir.
  • Northern Aroostook Regional Airport (WFK), near Frenchville, has no scheduled flights.

Stay safe

Aroostook County is one of the safest places in the U.S. People feel safe enough to leave their car running while they go in the store. And many people who live in rural houses leave their doors unlocked.
