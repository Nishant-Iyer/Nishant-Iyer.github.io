    Get around

    By car

    The easiest way to get around Greenwood is by car.

    By bus

    • IndyGo +1 317 635-3344. IndyGo services all over Indianapolis and surrounding areas.

    By taxi

    Most taxis and cabs service only around Indianapolis. They become pricey due to the 20 - 30 minutes from Indianapolis to Greenwood.

    • Downtown Greenwood Downtown Greenwood still holds a very small town feeling with a few shops and services.

    Do

    Parks

    • Craig Park 10 E Smith Valley Rd. Park with walkways.

    Festivals and events

    • Freedom Festival It offers a parade, the BBQ, food & business vendors, Old Towne Merchants Association Street Fair, live entertainment, concerts, kids' fairs and fireworks. It is held in late June/early July.
    • Farmer's Market Every Saturday morning, there is a local farmers' market. There is great variety including fresh produce, honey, gourds, herbs, hanging baskets, cut flowers, and bedding plants. It is found behind the Greenwood Public Library - south of Main Street off of Madison or Meridian Avenue.
    • Our Lady of Greenwood Festival It is held during the summer, varying between June and July, at Our Lady of Greenwood School. It is a small festival with rides and games. Or, in a sense, it is like a small town carnival.
