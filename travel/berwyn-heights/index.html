    Do

    Enjoy two playgrounds ( and ), one sports park, Lake Artemesia trails, and Indian Creek Trail. Lake Artemesia is especially good for bringing a lunch to eat on a park bench or on the grass next to the lake. Greenbelt Park is a massive park with trees, wildlife, walking trails, and camping sites.

    Buy

    Along Greenbelt Road, along the northern border of the town, is Beltway Plaza Mall.
