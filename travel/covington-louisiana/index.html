Overview

Covington is a city in the Florida Parishes of Louisiana. The seat of St. Tammany Parish, it's about an hour's drive north of New Orleans.

Best things about Covington

H J Smith & Sons General Store

Old style general store not only has an interesting variety of goods for sale, they also have a large room housing a free museum.

    Get in

    Driving from New Orleans, take I-10 west to Causeway Boulevard in Metairie; then take Causeway north, keep going across the lake, then follow Highway 190 and the signs straight to Covington.

    From elsewhere, from I-12 to either Highway 21 or Highway 190 and go north, and you'll be in the heart of Covington.

    Get around

    Mostly by car; the small old town section is pleasant for walking.

    See

    The old town section on and around Columbia Street. The south end of Columbia has "Columbia Street Landing", a park at the riverfront.
