Best things about Hugo

Endangered Ark Foundation

The non-profit Endangered Ark Foundation protects Asian elephants, especially those who've retired from circus performing, of which they have a large herd. They offer tours to help fund their protection efforts.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Feb–May, Oct–Nov. Summer highs reach about 97°F; winter lows dip to about 38°F. Very hot (Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep) — plan outdoor time for mornings. Wettest: Oct and May. (climate data from nearby Dallas)
