Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Oct. Summer highs reach about 79°F; winter lows dip to about 22°F. Wettest: Sep and Jun. (climate data from nearby Boone)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 590 miles, roughly 10 h 30 min without stops. Flying is usually faster.
  • BKW Raleigh County Memorial Airport, Beaver — 58 mi away. Directions
  • ROA Roanoke–Blacksburg Regional Airport, Roanoke — 66 mi away. Directions
  • GSO Piedmont Triad International Airport, Greensboro — 87 mi away (major hub). Directions

Wytheville is located at the intersection of two interstate highways. I-77 and I-81 provide access from the neighboring states of North Carolina, Tennessee, and West Virginia. I-81 also connects Wytheville to the larger metro areas of Northern Virginia.
