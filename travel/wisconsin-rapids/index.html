Overview

Wisconsin Rapids is a town of 19,000 people (2020) in North Central Wisconsin that is known for its papermaking history, and its cranberry industry.

2 sights · 1 things to do · 1 places to stay

Best things about Wisconsin Rapids

Alexander House

An old colonial home on the west bank of the Wisconsin River. The house serves as both an art gallery with rotating exhibits and a local history museum with exhibits on papermaking and the lumber industry.

South Wood County Historical Museum

Local history museum. Exhibits include a display on the work of Grim Natwick, an early animator for the Walt Disney Company.

Performing Arts Center of Wisconsin Rapids

833-seat proscenium theater hosting various events.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Sep. Summer highs reach about 82°F; winter lows dip to about 12°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Jun and Jul. (climate data from nearby Madison)
