Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Feb–May, Sep–Nov. Summer highs reach about 92°F; winter lows dip to about 37°F. Very hot (Jul, Aug) — plan outdoor time for mornings. Wettest: Aug and Jul.

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

Do

Despite what it may seem, the Plantation Midlands region has some substantial recreation areas, like the Okefenokee Swamp for sure as well as General Coffee and Seminole State Parks

  • Seminole State Park (south of Donalsonville) - Recreation area along the Lake Seminole segment of the Chattahoochee River, with boardwalks through swamps, camping, and wildlife
