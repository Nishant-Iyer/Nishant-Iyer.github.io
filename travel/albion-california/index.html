Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 2,310 miles, roughly 40 h without stops. Flying is usually faster.
  • STS Charles M. Schulz Sonoma County Airport, Santa Rosa — 71 mi away. Directions
  • RDD Redding Municipal Airport, Redding — 118 mi away. Directions

Get in

  • MTA (Mendocino Transit Authority) offers limited bus service from Mendocino and Fort Bragg, with connecting service to Ukiah, Santa Rosa, and other parts of Mendocino and Sonoma counties.
