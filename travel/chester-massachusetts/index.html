Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Oct. Summer highs reach about 85°F; winter lows dip to about 19°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Oct and Sep. (climate data from nearby Hartford)

    Get in

    By plane

    • Bradley International is in the town of Windsor Locks, Connecticut, half way in-between Hartford and Springfield. It has flights coming in from around the world. It's about an hour away
    • Westover Metropolitan Airport offers very light jet service to parts of New England, New York State, and Canada. It is about 40 minutes away.

    Do

    Chester-Blanford State Forest Rte. 20, (413) 354-6347 (summer), (413) 269-6002 (winter) . Hiking, camping. An easy uphill-grade 1 mile hike will bring you to the 60-foot cascading waterfall known as Sanderson Brook Falls. A more challenging hike on the H. Newman Marsh Memorial Trail brings you to an observation tower with views of the Westfield River valley.
