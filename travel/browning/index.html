Overview

Browning is a town in Northwestern Montana. It is the headquarters for the Blackfeet Indian Reservation. The townspeople are proud of their heritage and active in the recognition and rights of Native Americans.

2 sights · 3 things to do · 2 places to eat · 1 places to stay

Best things about Browning

Museum of the Plains Indian

Variety of exhibits such as historic clothing, horse gear, weapons, and household implements. Many of the northern plains tribes are represented.

Blackfeet Nation Bison Reserve

Tribe's bison herd, visible from the road.

Glacier Peaks Hotel and Casino

Slot machines, poker, and bingo. Must be 18 to play any casino game in Montana.

Blackfeet Trail

70 mile self-guided tour through the reservation. 15 historical markers with information about important sites along the route.

Northern American Indian Days

One of the largest gatherings of United States and Canadian tribes.

Understand

The Blackfeet Indian Reservation is home to the Blackfeet Nation. The reservation is huge, encompassing an area of over 3,000 square miles. It borders Glacier National Park to the west and the Canadian province Alberta to the north. While the town has 1,000 people, the Blackfeet Nation has 16,500 enrolled members.
