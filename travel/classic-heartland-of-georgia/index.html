Overview

Georgia's Classic Heartland is at the center of the U.S. state.

Plan your trip

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Feb–May, Oct–Nov. Summer highs reach about 94°F; winter lows dip to about 36°F. Very hot (Jun, Jul, Aug) — plan outdoor time for mornings. Wettest: Jul and Dec.

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 800 miles, roughly 13 h 30 min without stops. Flying is usually faster.
  • MCN Middle Georgia Regional Airport, Macon — 23 mi away. Directions
  • ATL Hartsfield Jackson Atlanta International Airport, Atlanta — 67 mi away (major hub). Directions

Sample 3-day trip

  1. Day 1: Augusta — Augusta Canal Authority, The Boyhood Home of President Woodrow Wilson, Sacred Heart Cultural Center.
  2. Day 2: Macon — Ocmulgee Mounds National Historical Park, Rose Hill Cemetery, Museum of Arts and Sciences.
  3. Day 3: Athens — Double Barreled Cannon, The Tree That Owns Itself, Georgia Museum of Natural History.

Some highlights of the Classic Heartland of Georgia include the Callaway Gardens and Little White House Historic Site that was Franklin Delano Roosevelt's retreat near Warm Springs, the Museum of Aviation near Warner Robins, the Old Governor's Mansion in Milledgeville, the National Civil War Naval Museum in Columbus, and the Jimmy Carter and Andersonville National Historic Sites, the latter being a testament to some of the darkest privations this country has endured.

  • Rock Eagle Effigy Mounds (and adjacent Rock Hawk Effigy Mound, north of Eatonton)
