Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Apr–Oct. Summer highs reach about 85°F; winter lows dip to about 22°F. Cold months (Jan) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Jul and Jun. (climate data from nearby Columbus)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 400 miles, roughly 7 h without stops.
  • HTS Tri-State Airport / Milton J. Ferguson Field, Huntington — 47 mi away. Directions
  • LCK Rickenbacker International Airport, Columbus — 55 mi away. Directions
  • CMH John Glenn Columbus International Airport, Columbus — 67 mi away (major hub). Directions

By car, Jackson is located just off of OH-32. Accessible from the north (Columbus area) by taking US-23 south through Circleville and Chillicothe , then taking US-35 towards Jackson. From the west (Cincinnati area) take OH-32 east.
