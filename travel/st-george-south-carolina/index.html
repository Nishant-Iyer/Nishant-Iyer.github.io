Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Feb–May, Oct–Nov. Summer highs reach about 93°F; winter lows dip to about 37°F. Very hot (Jun, Jul, Aug) — plan outdoor time for mornings. Wettest: Jul and Jun. (climate data from nearby Santee Cooper Country)
