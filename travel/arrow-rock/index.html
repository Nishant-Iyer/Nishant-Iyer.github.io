Overview

Arrow Rock is a village in Missouri. The entire town is a National Historic Landmark for its relationship with the Santa Fe Trail, Lewis and Clark, Boonslick region, and George Caleb Bingham.

Get in

By car

  • I-70 (Exits 89 & 98) and Missouri Route 41 are the main roads to town.

Get around

The best way around is on foot. There are no gas stations in the Blackwater or Arrow Rock area. The closet cities with fuel stations are Boonville and Marshall. The closest ATM's are in Blackwater itself.
