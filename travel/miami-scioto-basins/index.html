Overview

Scioto basins is a largely rural area of southwestern Ohio that includes the City of Dayton. Visitors come for its many state parks, and for the cultural attractions of Dayton.

Understand

Climate

;Winter : Winters can vary from mild to very cold. Snowfall isn't excessive, however, it's possible and certainly not unheard of that snow can fall at a rate that will cause havoc with traffic. ;Spring : Springtime is often cool, with cold and warm days sprinkled throughout the period. As Dayton lies in the upper tip of "Tornado Valley", storms during this period may produce tornadoes, so it's important ;Summer : Summers are characteristically hot and humid. While the thermometer may read one number in the summer, it will often feel hotter due to the humidity. ;Autumn : Autumn in Dayton and Southwest Ohio is usually cool, but temperatures can spike as high as the 90°F's with a sucker punch of humidity. Storms seem to increase during this period too, which, due to the fluctuation of temperatures can produce extremely dangerous storms with the potential of producing tornadoes late into the year.

Get around

By bus

SORTA (Southwestern Ohio Regional Transit Authority) operates the Metro bus system, which serves Cincinnati, Hamilton County, Clermont County, and Warren County.

See

  • Hopewell Culture National Historical Park in Chillicothe. A group of 23 earthen mounds built by the Hopewell culture, 200 BC to AD 500.
  • Sugarloaf Mountain Amphitheatre - Tecumseh! in Chillicothe. Huge outdoor stages.

Museums

If you're interested in visiting museums, Dayton offers the Dayton Art Institute, the Dayton International Peace Museum, the Hoover Historical Center, the National Museum of the U.S. Air Force, and the Wright Cycle Company Complex. In Chillicothe, you can find the Adena Mansion and Gardens, the Chillicothe Railroad Museum, the Hopewell Culture National Historical Park, the James M. Thomas Telephone Museum, the Dr. John D. Harris Dental Museum, and the Lucy Hayes Heritage Center.
