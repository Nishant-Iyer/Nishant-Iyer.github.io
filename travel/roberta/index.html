Understand

Roberta is a peaceful, quiet city that started out as a railroad town that connected from Macon.

Get in

By car

You can enter Roberta via highway 80, which extends from the eastern coast of Georgia to Texas, originally running all the way to California. There are also many small roads that enter it.

By bus

There are no major bus services available in Roberta, although Crawford County Transit Authority, 700 Spruce St, +1 478 825-5995, serves all of Crawford County and its surrounding areas.

By plane

The Roberta Industrial Park Airpark Accepts small airplanes to land and takeoff.

Get around

By foot

Getting around by foot is perhaps the best way to get around Roberta, as the downtown area is reasonably compact.
