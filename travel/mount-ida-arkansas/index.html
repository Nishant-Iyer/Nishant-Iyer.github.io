    Understand

    Mount Ida is a little town located in Central Arkansas. It is well known for Quartz Crystal mining and is claimed to be the Quartz Crystal Capital of the World. This activity brings thousands of tourists to the small town every year.

    Do

    Do Wegner Crystal Mines

    Wegner Crystal Mines is a place for the whole family to come and dig for quartz crystals. They have several different activity options so that you can pick the best one for you. Besides the opportunity to go out to the mine and dig you can see lots of different mineral specimens from all over the world in a 10,000 square foot warehouse.

    📍 82 Wegner Ranch Road6 miles South of Mount Ida off Highway 27🕒 Open from 8AM-4:30PM💲 The prices range from 5$ - 15$☎ +1 870 867-2309
