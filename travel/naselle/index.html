Overview

Naselle is a town of about 400 residents in southwest Washington State on the southwest corner of the Willapa Hills and just inland of the southeast corner of Willapa Bay, some 10 mi east of Long Beach. Naselle sits on the Naselle River and is known for year-round sturgeon fishing.
