    Eat The Hamburger Wagon

    German / EuropeanBurgers

    Famous burgers from this Miamisburg classic. It was built in 1913 to help provide relief from the Great Dayton Flood. It's been in business ever since. Good German food as well.

    📍 12 E Central AveChecked Jan 2022

    Eat Marion's Piazza

    Pizza

    This unique and delicious style of pizza, intensely popular with the locals, has been a part of Dayton traditions for over 40 years, and is a must try for anyone visiting the area.
