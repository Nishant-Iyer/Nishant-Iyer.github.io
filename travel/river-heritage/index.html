Overview

River Heritage is the southern region of Alabama, excluding the Gulf Coast region in the south west. It includes the Wiregrass region of southeastern Alabama.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Feb–May, Oct–Nov. Summer highs reach about 94°F; winter lows dip to about 36°F. Very hot (Jun, Jul, Aug) — plan outdoor time for mornings. Wettest: Mar and Jul.

Sample 3-day trip

  1. Day 1: Montgomery — Civil Rights Memorial, Dexter Avenue King Memorial Baptist Church, Alabama State Capitol.
  2. Day 2: Auburn — Auburn University, Donald E. Davis Arboretum, Jordan-Hare Stadium.
  3. Day 3: Dothan — US Army Aviation Museum, Dothan Opera House, Wiregrass Museum of Art.
