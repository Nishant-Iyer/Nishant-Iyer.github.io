Overview

Little River is a city in the Grand Strand region of South Carolina. It has about 12,000 residents.

2 sights · 3 things to do · 6 places to eat · 5 places to stay

Best things about Little River

Vereen Memorial Gardens

Enjoy some walks on boardwalks through swamps and saltwater marshes.

Sea Racer Dolphin Tours

Boat tours for watching dolphins. Tours last about 90 minutes.

The Big "M" Casino Ship II

Little River is quite the destination for offshore gambling excursions, and this one features a brunch buffet, at least when Covid-19 wasn't around. There's a sun deck for those who just want to enjoy the ride.

Eagle Nest Golf Club

Myrtle Beach FC

Doug Shaw Memorial Stadium, on 33rd Avenue. A professional soccer team playing in the National Premier Soccer League. The regular season runs from May to July and tickets are free for kids aged 10 and under.

Get in

By car

Little River is located near the South Carolina-North Carolina state line, near the easternmost point in South Carolina. To get there from Myrtle Beach, South Carolina, take U.S. Route 17 north. From Wilmington, North Carolina, take U.S. Route 17 south.

By plane

Being a small town, Little River does not have its own commercial airport. The nearest airports with scheduled commercial service are in Myrtle Beach (28 miles; 44 km) and Wilmington (54 miles; 87 km).

Get around

A car is generally required to get around. Taxis are available though.
