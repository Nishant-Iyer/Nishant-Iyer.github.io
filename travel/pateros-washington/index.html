Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Sep. Summer highs reach about 87°F; winter lows dip to about 19°F. Cold months (Jan, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Dec and Jan. (climate data from nearby Leavenworth)

Do

Do Alta Lake State Park

A park of 181 acres at the northern end of Alta Lake, beneath towering stone cliffs that rise 1000 ft above the valley floor, and carry on up to the top of Old Goat Mountain. The park offers swimming, boating, fishing, sailboarding, camping and 2 mi of hiking trails.

📍 191A Alta Lake Rd2 mi southwest of Pateros🕒 Apr-Oct💲 Discover Pass required for parking ($10/day, $45/year)☎ +1 509 923-2473Checked Oct 2025

Eat SweetRiver Bakery

In addition to fresh breads and pastries, this bakery offers sandwiches, soups, hand-tossed pizza and calzones. During the summer, live music is featured every weekend.
