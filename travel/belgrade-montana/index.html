Overview

Belgrade is a town in Montana with a population of 10,500 in 2020. Only 10 miles west of Bozeman, many travellers may not consider Belgrade as its own destination. Yet it has the same mountain scenery and closeness to premium skiing and Yellowstone National Park.

Best things about Belgrade

Gallatin River

The Gallatin River offers a tremendous amount of water sport opportunities, including rafting at varying skill levels. The river is also known for its scenic beauty and serenity.

Get in

Belgrade is on Interstate 90 (I-90).

By plane

Bozeman Yellowstone Airport is located in Belgrade, despite the name. There's taxis and hotel shuttles to Belgrade and Bozeman.

Get around

There are two major roads in Belgrade - Jackrabbit Lane and Main St. Most locations can be reached from these two roads.
