Understand

Whitehouse is a small village of about 4,000 that was established in 1856.

Get in

By car

Whitehouse Ohio lies on State Route 64.

By bike

Go Wabash Cannonball Trail

A bike trail that passes through the town. The path is split into two forks that meet at the edge of Toledo, with Whitehouse lying on the shorter south fork. By Transferring to the north Fork, Montpelier and Wauseon are accessible.

MapsWebsite

Get around

Whitehouse has many more roundabouts and cyclists then surrounding communities, so it's important to be alert to these different driving conditions.
