Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Sep. Summer highs reach about 82°F; winter lows dip to about 19°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Jul and Jun. (climate data from nearby Rochester)

    Sleep

    • Days Inn - Convenient to I-86/NY-17 (The Southern Tier Expressway). Basic with very limited amenities. Continental breakfast included. 3 miles from Downtown, and 1 mile from restaurants.
    • Econo Lodge - Basic with some amenities. Popular restaurant on site. 1 mile from I-86/NY-17 (The Southern Tier Expressway) and 1.5 miles from Downtown.
    • Best Western Maple City Inn - Located in Downtown, three miles from the Southern Tier Expressway. Has an indoor pool.
