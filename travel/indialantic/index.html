Overview

Indialantic is a one square mile town across the Melbourne Causeway from Melbourne, in Florida. It has an excellent beach and fine shopping.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Oct–May. Summer highs reach about 91°F; winter lows dip to about 54°F. Very hot (Jun, Jul, Aug) — plan outdoor time for mornings. Wettest: Sep and Jun. (climate data from nearby Melbourne)
