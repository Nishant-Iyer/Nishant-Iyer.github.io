Best things about Douglas

General Coffee State Park

Park showcasing a vintage farmstead and cypress swamp environment.

WWII Flight Training Museum

A museum covering the topic of World War II flight training and air combat, since Douglas was the site of the Raymond-Richardson Aviation School for that purpose.

Museum Heritage Station

A small museum about Douglas and Coffee County's tobacco industry.

Understand

Douglas, named after the popular sovereignty advocate (as in, states had the right to choose in matters such as slavery) and senator Stephen A. Douglas, who went up against Lincoln for the presidency in 1860 but ultimately lost. Tobacco is a major commodity in the Douglas area.
