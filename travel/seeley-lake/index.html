Overview

Seeley Lake is a small town in Northwestern Montana. About 1,600 people live there. Seeley Lake is popular for outdoor recreation, being situated in a valley, by the lakes and rivers, forested mountain area, and near the Bob Marshall and the Mission Mountain Wilderness areas.

The nearest airport is in Missoula.

Get around

The town is small enough to walk.

See

The lake sits right next to the town. It's about 1,000 acres and is a popular fishing spot. It is a part of the 'Chain of Lakes' running north and south. All are easy to access from Highway 83.

Do

You can enjoy fly fishing, golf, whitewater rafting, horseback riding, hiking, cross-country skiing, and snowmobiling.

Do Blackhawk Disc Golf Course

18 hole course with the 'Benevolent Nine' to begin with and the 'Malevolent Nine' at the end. Maintained by the high school student council. First hole is behind the school at the NE corner of the gym. Or park at the top of the hill and start at hole 12.
