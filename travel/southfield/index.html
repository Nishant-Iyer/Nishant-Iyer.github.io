Overview

Southfield is a suburban city in Oakland County, in the Metro Detroit region of Michigan. It is one of Metro Detroit's major suburban commercial and business centers. Inside of Southfield is the enclave city of Lathrup Village.

Best things about Southfield

Burgh Historical Park

Small city park with restored turn-of-the-20th-century buildings on the grounds. The park also features a small gazebo, which is the sight on an annual concert series during summer.
