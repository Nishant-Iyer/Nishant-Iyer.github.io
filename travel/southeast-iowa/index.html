Understand

This small region includes the counties of Louisa, Des Moines (which is not the same as the city Des Moines in Central Iowa), Lee, Henry, Van Buren, and Jefferson. All together, about 125,000 people live in this region.

Do

Do Keokuk Family Geode Festival

A geode hunting event for rock hounds of all ages. You'll meet up in town and then drive to pre-designated sites to search for geodes. Wear old boots or shoes that can tramp through fields, mud, and streams. Bring a bucket, simple gardening tools, food, water, and whatever you need to be comfortable while hiking and digging for a few hours at a time. Pre-registration opens a few months before the event.

📍 Central meeting point: 3476 Main St, Keokuk🕒 Annual event in late September💲 $25 adults, $5 kids to participate, plus $25 per bucket of geodes collectedChecked Jun 2025
