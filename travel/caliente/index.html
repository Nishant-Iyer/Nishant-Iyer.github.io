Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Apr–May, Sep–Oct. Summer highs reach about 96°F; winter lows dip to about 18°F. Very hot (Jun, Jul, Aug) — plan outdoor time for mornings. Wettest: Mar and Feb.

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).
