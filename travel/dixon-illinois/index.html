Overview

Dixon is called "The Petunia City" but is probably best known as the boyhood home of former US President Ronald Reagan.

3 sights · 1 places to stay

Best things about Dixon

Ronald Reagan Boyhood Home

The Reagan family's home from 1920-1923, this white house has a gift shop and memorial garden for the late president.

Lowell Park

Northwest Territory Historic Center

This museum is located in the school building where Reagan attended 7th and 8th grades.
