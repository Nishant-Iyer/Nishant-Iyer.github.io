Understand

A former spa town which then fell into disrepair, Sharon Springs has had a revival as a getaway destination for people from New York City.

Get in

By car.

Get around

Although the main area of the village can be navigated on foot, a car will be necessary for any sort of excursion further afield.

  • Howe Caverns

See Howe Caverns

The bigger and more famous of the two show caves in the hamlet of Howes Cave, The eponymous cave at Howe Caverns features two tour offerings: a 90-minute traditional tour, which includes a boat ride over an underground lake, 1.25 mi walking underground and numerous speleothems to observe, and a 2.5-hour "adventure tour" in the more underdeveloped part of the cave. The cave also includes a geological museum and a motel.

📍 255 Discovery Drive Howes Cave🕒 Tours 10AM-5PM💲 Traditional Tour: adult $29.50, child $19.50☎ +1 518 296-8900

See Secret Caverns

The smaller, quirkier and lower-key counterpart to Howe Caverns, Secret Caverns is just 1.4 mi away from its erstwhile rival. The tour is about 45 minutes and is full of good (and bad) puns, cave facts and ends in a 100 ft waterfall. Be sure to check out the psychedelic art on the billboards advertising the cave on your way and inside the visitor's lodge.
