    Understand

    Maryville is a beautiful and historic city located at the foothills of the Great Smoky Mountains. It was distinctively abolitionist and pro-Union during the Civil War on account of the presence of the Society of Friends. Maryville has many beautiful parks and historic houses and an unbelievable view of the Great Smoky Mountains.

    Get in

    The nearest commercial airport is McGhee Tyson Airport (TYS) which lies a few miles north in adjacent Alcoa.

    Coming from the north, the Pellissippi Parkway connects West Knoxville with Maryville and US 129/Alcoa Hwy (Airport Hwy) connects it with downtown "Knoxville. There are also many other smaller highways that connect the two cities.

    See

    • Sam Houston Schoolhouse, 3650 Old Sam Houston School Rd. Where Sam Houston taught school from 1811-12 before embarking on adventures that would lead him to become father of the Texas Republic.
    • Great Smoky Mountains National Park

    Do

    • Springbrook Pool, 636 Vose Rd, Alcoa. City pool.
    • Walks along Bicentennial Park, a downtown greenbelt with a lagoon.
    • The Palace Theatre, 113 W Broadway Ave. Vintage cinema.
