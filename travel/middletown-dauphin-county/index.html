Overview

Middletown is a town of 9,500 people (2020) in the Harrisburg area of Pennsylvania. The town is old, and diverse historic architectural styles abound. Middletown has everything from log houses, some whose construction is obscured by modern siding, to Victorian mansions.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Apr–Jun, Sep–Oct. Summer highs reach about 90°F; winter lows dip to about 30°F. Very hot (Jul) — plan outdoor time for mornings. Wettest: Jul and Sep. (climate data from nearby Baltimore)

    By train

    Go Middletown Station

    By car

    • Pennsylvania Routes 230 (Main St./West Harrisburg Pike) and 441 (Union St.) pass through.

    By bus

    • Capital Area Transit (CAT) bus route 7 from downtown Harrisburg and the airport (M-Sa).

    Get around

    Capital Area Transit (CAT) bus route 7 runs through part of downtown. However, the town is very easily explored on foot or by car.
