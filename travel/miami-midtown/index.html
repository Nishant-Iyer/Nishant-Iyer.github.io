Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Nov–Apr. Summer highs reach about 91°F; winter lows dip to about 61°F. Very hot (Jul, Aug) — plan outdoor time for mornings. Wettest: Jun and Sep. (climate data from nearby Miami)

Getting there

  • From you: to see whether to drive or fly, and how long it takes.
  • MIA Miami International Airport, Miami — 6 mi away (major hub). Directions
  • OPF Miami-Opa Locka Executive Airport, Miami — 9 mi away. Directions

The MetroMover as well as many bus lines stop at the Omni Terminal. MetroMover goes to Downtown Miami, and the buses go to destinations such as downtown and Miami/South Beach. A main thoroughfare for buses is Biscayne Boulevard. From Miami Airport, you can take the J (or 150) to Biscayne Boulevard and walk, or take a connecting bus, depending on your destination.

    Eat Sabor a Peru

    Latin AmericanSeafood

    This place may appear to be a small hole in the wall restaurant but it is extremely well maintained and upkept and usually has a short wait for a table. They also offer take out and delivery. This venue is very popular. If you haven't tried Peruvian food before you must order the Ceviche (Fish/Seafood) and the Lomo Saltado (Beef).
