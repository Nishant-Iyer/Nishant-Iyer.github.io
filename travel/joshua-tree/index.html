Understand

Joshua Tree is a village in the Morongo Basin, on California State Route 62 between Yucca Valley to the west and Twentynine Palms to the east.

Joshua Tree is home to Joshua Tree National Park, with the western entrance to the park located to the south.

Aside from the park, Joshua Tree (also known simply as "JT") is an artists and musicians community, with a farmers market and art galleries.

Get in

By car

Most visitors will arrive by car on California State Route 62, which runs east-west through the Morongo Basin, west from the Palm Springs area and Yucca Valley, and east from Twentynine Palms and the Arizona state line.

By bus

There is a limited bus network, run by the Morongo Basin Transit Authority (MBTA). There's an hourly service to Twentynine Palms and to Yucca Valley, where it is possible to connect with Palm Springs.

By plane

The closest passenger airport is:

  • Palm Springs International Airport (PSP), 40 mi west of Joshua Tree Village.

Further afield are:

  • Ontario International Airport (ONT), about 90 mi west.
  • Los Angeles International Airport (LAX), 140 mi west.
  • San Diego International Airport (SAN), 150 mi southwest.
