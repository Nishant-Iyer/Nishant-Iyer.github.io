Overview

Delafield is a city in Southeast Wisconsin. It's a launching point for fishing and boating on the lakes, golfing, and miles of hiking and biking trails.

    Understand

    Delafield, Wisconsin is among the tranquil water and rolling hills of Waukesha County’s “Lake Country," between Milwaukee and Madison. Experience theatre under the stars, art walks, and music festivals are just a few ways to enjoy Delafield. Of course, there's a fine selection of dining and one-of-a-kind shopping too! Smiles await you in Delafield.

    The heart of Delafield is its historic downtown with one-of-a-kind boutiques and restaurants. A short drive down Milwaukee Street, you’ll come upon the bustling commercial center, with a mix of local and chain restaurants and shopping.
