Overview

Cape Girardeau is a city named after a French soldier/trader in southeastern Missouri. Watch out for murals and flood walls in this college town.

2 sights · 1 bars & cafés · 7 places to stay

Best things about Cape Girardeau

Mississippi River Tales Mural

a mural containing 24 panels illustrating the history of the area, the mural covers nearly 18,000 square feet on the downtown floodwall.

    • Bill Emerson Memorial Bridge, MO-34. Cable stayed bridge across the Mississippi that is a Cape Girardeau landmark.
    • Southeast Missouri State University, 1 University Plaza. Emphasis on arts.
    • The Crisp Museum, 518 S Fountain St. Rotating art and history and archeology exhibits. Free classes occasionally. Tel: +1 573-651-2260. M-F 9AM-5PM, Sa-Su 1PM-4PM, First Fridays open late.
    • Cape River Heritage Museum, 538 Independence St. Small history museum about Cape Girardeau. Tel:+1 573-334-0405 or (800) 777-0068. Th-F Noon-4PM, Sa 11AM-4PM.

    Do

    • Century Casino Cape Girardeau, 777 Main St. Slots and entertainment and a nice view of the river.
    • The Bedell Theater of Southeast Missouri State University
    • The Holland School of Visual and Performing Arts of Southeast Missouri State
    • Cape Girardeau Bike Trail
    • Cape Girardeau County Park Conservation Center
    • Cape Rock Park, E Cape Rock Dr. Fine views of Mississippi from this bluff. Hiking and biking.
    • Riverfront Park Downtown Cape Girardeau, East Morgan St. Alongside the river.

    Buy

    Most standard American big box stores, including Walmart, Target, and Lowe's, have a presence in the town. There is also a mall with standard fast-food establishments nearby.

    Eat

    • Minglewood Brewery, 121 Broadway, They have fantastic pizzas and brew their own beer on site. Neat place in historic downtown Cape Girardeau.
    • Dexter Queen, 1109 W Business U.S. 60, Dexter. Bbq.
    • Broussard's Cajun Cuisine, 114 Main St. Cajun seafood and steak.

    Drink

    • Rude Dog Pub, 123 North Main St.
    • Spectrum Record Lounge, 113 Broadway St.
    • Independence Place, 5 S Henderson Ave. Night club.

    Drink D'ladium's

    Bar / Café

    Arguably the best bar in Cape Girardeau. This is the place college students meet locals and alumni. Great atmosphere and has everything one looks for in a bar.
