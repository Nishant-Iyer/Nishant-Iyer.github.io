Overview

Catron County is a rural county in Southwest New Mexico. Pie Town is covered in a separate article.

5 sights · 1 things to do · 1 places to eat · 2 places to stay

Best things about Catron County

Alma

A ghost town with a well-tended cemetery. It was the site of the Alma massacre in 1880, and on that event's centenary a monument to it was erected in the Gila Wilderness.

Mogollon

A mining ghost town that is on the National Register of Historic Places.

Lightning Field

An artistic installation of 400 stainless-steel poles near Quemado, created by Walter de Maria.

Whitewater Baldy

The highest point in Southwestern New Mexico, at 10895 ft.

Elfego Baca Memorial

Commemorates the Frisco Shootout that occurred on December 1, 1884. Supposedly Baca single-handedly held off eighty attacking cowboys.

Catwalk National Recreational Trail

A one-mile trail following the path of an old water pipe used in mining.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Apr–May, Sep–Oct. Summer highs reach about 93°F; winter lows dip to about 21°F. Very hot (Jun, Jul, Aug) — plan outdoor time for mornings. Wettest: Jul and Aug. (climate data from nearby Petrified Forest National Park)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 1,530 miles, roughly 26 h 30 min without stops. Flying is usually faster.
  • SVC Grant County Airport, Silver City — 90 mi away. Directions
  • SOW Show Low Regional Airport, Show Low — 94 mi away. Directions
  • ABQ Albuquerque International Sunport, Albuquerque — 129 mi away (major hub). Directions
