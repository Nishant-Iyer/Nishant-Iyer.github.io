Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Sep. Summer highs reach about 84°F; winter lows dip to about 14°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Oct and Dec. (climate data from nearby Nashua)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 990 miles, roughly 17 h without stops. Flying is usually faster.
  • ORH Worcester Regional Airport, Worcester — 16 mi away. Directions
  • BED Laurence G Hanscom Field, Bedford — 27 mi away. Directions
  • BOS Boston Logan International Airport, Boston — 42 mi away (major hub). Directions

Sample 3-day trip

  1. Day 1: Clinton — Museum of Russian Icons, Wachusett Reservoir, Tower Hill Botanical Gardens.
  2. Day 2: Gardner — Dunn State Park, Lake Wampanoag Wildlife Sanctuary, West Gardner Square Historic District.
  3. Day 3: Harvard — Fruitlands Museum, Biking and Trails, Apple Blossom Festival.
