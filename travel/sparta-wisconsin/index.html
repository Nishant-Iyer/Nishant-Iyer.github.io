Best things about Sparta

Ben Bikin'

Ben Bikin' is a statue of a unicyclist. A contest was held during its construction to name the statue. Jeff Ascher provided the winning entry.

Deke Slayton Memorial Space and Bike Museum

Dedicated to the career of astronaut Donald Kent "Deke" Slayton, the History of Bicycling, Wisconsin Astronauts, and Aviation in Monroe County. Local History museum downstairs, Deke Slayton and Bicycle museum upstairs.

FAST Fiberglass Mold Graveyard

A large field with hundreds of massive fiberglass molds of various characters, animals, and objects. Walking paths take you between piles of discarded shells of Burger Boys, Ice Cream Cones, and Elephants.

Sparta-Elroy State Trail

a 32-mile (51 km) rail trail between Elroy and Sparta.
