Overview

DuBois is a city and largest community in Clearfield County in the state of Pennsylvania. The community lies within the Pennsylvania Wilds.

Plan your trip

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Sep. Summer highs reach about 83°F; winter lows dip to about 18°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Jul and Jun. (climate data from nearby Franklin)
