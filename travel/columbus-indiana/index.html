Overview

Columbus is a city in Southern Indiana and the seat of Bartholomew county.

Best things about Columbus

Bartholomew County Courthouse

A historic courthouse building built in the second empire style. It features large clock tower.

Fire Station Number 4

An early post modernist fire station, most clearly recognized by its tower displaying the number "4".

The Republic Newspaper Office

A former office for the local newspaper, it uses glass walls extensively. It is now used by Indiana University for classes.

Indiana University Columbus

An extension campus located near the airport.

McEwen-Samuels-Marr House

A two-story brick house in the Italianate style.

Miller House

Famous for its conversation pit.

    Get in

    By car

    Columbus lies adjacent to Interstate 65. The easiest exit to take, whether coming from the north or south is exit 68 at State Road 46 (also called Jonathan Moore Pike).

    By plane

    Columbus has a municipal airport, but it is only used for private and the occasional charter flight. The nearest commercial airport is Indianapolis International Airport (IND). It is about a 60-minute drive from downtown Columbus. Louisville International Airport (SDF) is about a 75-minute drive.

    Go Columbus Municipal Airport (CLU)

    A large General aviation airport. A former World War II and Cold War era air base, its history is documented in an onsite museum. The airport also has the "Hanger Five" restaurant in the terminal, serving American breakfast and lunch.

    📍 4770 Ray Boll Blvd☎ +1 812-376-2519⚠ Last checked Nov 2019 — confirm before you go
    MapsWebsite

    By bus

    There is a daily bus that runs from Columbus to the bus station in downtown Indianapolis. It can be boarded at the "transportation depot" next to the county jail.
