Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Apr–May, Sep–Oct. Summer highs reach about 90°F; winter lows dip to about 20°F. Very hot (Jul) — plan outdoor time for mornings. Cold months (Jan) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: May and Jun. (climate data from nearby Topeka)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 560 miles, roughly 9 h 30 min without stops.
  • MCI Kansas City International Airport, Kansas City — 44 mi away (major hub). Directions
  • MHK Manhattan Regional Airport, Manhattan — 67 mi away. Directions

Sample 3-day trip

  1. Day 1: Lawrence — The University of Kansas, University of Kansas Natural History Museum, Allen Fieldhouse.
  2. Day 2: Topeka — Brown v. Board of Education National Historic Site, Combat Air Museum, Kansas State Capitol.
  3. Day 3: Overland Park — Overland Park Arboretum and Botanical Gardens, Kansas International Film Festival (KIFF), Deanna Rose Children's Farmstead.
