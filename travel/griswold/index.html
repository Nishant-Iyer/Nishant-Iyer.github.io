Overview

Griswold is a town in the Mystic-Eastern region of Connecticut. It is a small, historic town with multiple places on the National Register of Historic Places.

6 sights · 1 bars & cafés · 3 places to stay

National Register of Historic Places house.

Best time to visit & weather
