See Milam County Railroad Museum (Old Town Cameron)

A railroad museum the work of John and Frances Johnson who worked for over 35 years to create a model of Cameron, Texas as it looked in the 1940s. It is a finely detailed model of life in a small town in Central Texas. It also recollects the railroad history of Cameron highlighting the Southern Pacific and Santa Fe Railroads.
