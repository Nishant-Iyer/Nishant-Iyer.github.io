Eat

Eat Hard Eight BBQ

Hard Eight BBQ provides a truly unique experience in authentic mesquite smoked Texas-style BBQ. Visitors come from all over Texas and afar to enjoy their award winning cuisine. The "Old World" method of slow cooking over open pits was adopted from the German settlers that came to the Texas Hill Country over a century ago. This method truly stands out as aromatic flavor unlike any you have tasted before.
