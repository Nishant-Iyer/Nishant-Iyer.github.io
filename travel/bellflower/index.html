    See

    Tours are available Monday- Friday from 9AM-4PM. Please call Bellfower Historical Center in advance to schedule your tour. Booking a tour a few months prior to your visit is recommended due to the high demand of this historical site viewing.

    • Los Angeles County Fire Museum, 16400 Bellflower Blvd. W-Sa 9AM-2PM.

    Do

    Visit the neighborhood Starbucks on Rosecrans and Bellflower. Buy a cup of coffee and drink it while you chat away with the friendly Starbucks staff. Visit Downtown Bellflower where you can enjoy walks down Laurel Street with your children.

    Buy

    The Bellfower City Store is open from Monday to Friday, 11AM to 2PM. This is where you can buy souvenirs from "Bellfower: The Friendly City"

    Stay safe

    For all emergencies in Bellflower, dial 911

    Medical emergencies

    • Los Angeles County Fire Department services Bellflower. Both of the stations in Bellflower have Paramedics on duty.
    • Station 23-9548 E Flower Street
    • Station 98 – 9814 Maplewood Avenue

    Law enforcement

    Police services in Bellflower are provided by the Los Angeles County Sheriff's Department.

    • From 8AM-8PM: Bellflower Substation – 16615 Bellflower Boulevard, +1 562 925-0124.
    • From 8PM–8AM: Lakewood Regional Station – 5130 N. Clark Avenue, Lakewood, +1 562 866-9061.

    Go next

    • Disneyland – 15 miles east of Bellflower, about 22 minutes driving time.
    • Long Beach – A few miles south of Bellflower
