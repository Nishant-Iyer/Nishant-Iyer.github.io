    Get in

    By car

    • Interstate 5 bisects Red Bluff offering access from Redding to the north and Sacramento to the south. Red Bluff is accessed from Exits #647-653.
    • goes north/south through town as Main St. It goes 106 mi east to Susanville and 142 mi west to Fortuna
    • The Golden State Freeway goes down towards north of Sacramento through Chico and Yuba City.

    By bus

    Go Greyhound

    Greyhound travels primarily on Interstate 5 between Portland and Sacramento via Salem, Corvallis, Eugene, Roseburg, Grants Pass, Medford, Weed, Redding, Chico, Oroville, and/or Marysville. Passengers transfer buses in Portland, Sacramento, Eugene-Springfield, Medford or Los Angeles to reach additional destinations. Some of the buses are direct between Redding and Sacramento. Check schedules. Greyhound no longer serve Red Bluff. The nearest Greyhound stop/station is in Redding.

    📍 1530 Yuba St, ReddingBus will board at Bus Bay #8 inside the Raba Passenger Terminal. Bay #8 is adjacent to the train tracks.☎ +1 530 241-2070⚠ Last checked Dec 2016 — confirm before you go
    MapsWebsite

    Place Susanville Indian Rancheria Public Transportation Program (SIR)

    Connects Susanville to Chester, Red Bluff and Redding. Passengers connect in Susanville to Lassen (County) Rural Bus to continue to other towns in Lassen County or to another SIR or Sage Stage bus to continue towards Reno from Susanville. Schedules are limited so be sure of the departure and connection times before using this option especially if going to Reno.

    📍 (bus stop) Red Bluff Transit Center at Rio and Walnut St☎ +1 530 257-1128⚠ Last checked Aug 2017 — confirm before you go
    MapsWebsite

    By train

    Go Amtrak Thruway Motor Coach Rt #3

    Connects Redding (via Red Bluff, Chico, Oroville, Marysville, Davis, etc) to the Amtrak Gold Runner and Capitol Corridor trains in Sacramento and only the Gold Runner in Stockton. Tickets on this bus are only available for connections to Amtrak trains. Thruway buses stop at the Red Bluff Transit Center at on Rio & Walnut St in downtown Red Bluff. Amtrak trains do not stop into town.

    ⚠ Last checked Aug 2017 — confirm before you go
    MapsWebsite

    Get around

    By bus

    Go Tehama Rural Area Express (TRAX)

    TRAX has two routes in Red Bluff. In addition to the two routes that service the city, two other routes that lead to other cities in the county originate in Red Bluff. TRAX buses may be flagged down at any point along the published routes where it is safe for the bus to stop. (Map marker at the Red Bluff Transit Center on Rio & Walnut St).
