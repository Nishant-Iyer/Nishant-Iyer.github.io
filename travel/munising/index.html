Overview

Munising is a small city in the Upper Peninsula of Michigan, on the southern shore of Lake Superior.

2 sights · 1 things to do · 9 places to eat · 1 bars & cafés · 14 places to stay

Best things about Munising

Art in the Alley

Art installation from mid-May through mid-October of reproduced works of art from Alger County residents. The alley is one-half block south of Munising Avenue between Elm Avenue and Maple Street.
