Overview

Mount Vernon is the county seat of Knox County, Ohio. It has a small, cute downtown, and is home to Mount Vernon Nazarene University and the manufacturer Ariel.

    By car

    The easiest way into Mount Vernon is by car. Mount Vernon is connected by US36.

    By bus

    Place Knox Area Transit (KAT)

    Door-to-door service in and out of Knox County (call for rates), as well as $1 shuttles between Mount Vernon and Gambier (hourly M-F 8AM-10PM, Sa 2-10PM) and within Mount Vernon (see below).

    📍 (office) 25 Columbus Road, Mt. Vernon🕒 Mon–Fri 6AM–7PM, Sat 6AM–5PM☎ +1 740 392-7433
    MapsWebsite

    Go Go Bus (Operated by Barons Bus)

    Offers regular service between Columbus, Newark OH, Martinsburg, Harrison Township, Gambier, Mt Vernon, Loudonville, Shreve and Wooster.

    📍 (bus stop) Colonial City Lanes (Bowling Alley) 110 Mt Vernon AveBus stop at front parking lot. Bowling alley does not sell tickets or offer parking for bus riders.
    MapsWebsite

    By bike

    The Kokosing Gap Trail connects to Gambier and is part of the Ohio to Erie Trail from Cincinnati to Cleveland via Columbus.

    Get around

    By bus

    In addition to service to and from Mount Vernon, Knox Area Transit offers shuttle service within the city on four lines: Blue (North), Red (East), Orange (South), and Green (West). Shuttles cost $1 and run every half hour M-Sa 8AM-4PM. Transfers are available to the shuttles to Gambier.
