Get around

St. Helena is a small town, but the wineries are spread out. Driving is recommended but biking can also be fun. Please do not Drink and Drive. Even though you may not feel like you are drinking, it only takes a few wine tastings to be over the legal limit. Be safe and designate a driver or hire a taxi or limo.
