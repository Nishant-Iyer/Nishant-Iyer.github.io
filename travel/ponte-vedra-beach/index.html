Overview

Ponte Vedra Beach is a seaside community on the First Coast. An area recognized as a premier golfing destination, the Sawgrass development is an epicenter for the sport. The PGA Tour and The Players Championship call it home. The Association of Tennis Professional also call the resort home.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Oct–May. Summer highs reach about 92°F; winter lows dip to about 42°F. Very hot (Jun, Jul, Aug) — plan outdoor time for mornings. Wettest: Jun and Sep. (climate data from nearby Jacksonville)
