Overview

Clark County is the most populous county in the state of Nevada. It is home to Las Vegas valley, as well as Red Rock Canyon, Laughlin, Lake Mead and Primm.

Getting there

    Nevada's main airports are Harry Reid International Airport (LAS) in Las Vegas for commercial flights and Henderson Executive Airport (HSH) in Henderson for VIP flights and general aviation use.
