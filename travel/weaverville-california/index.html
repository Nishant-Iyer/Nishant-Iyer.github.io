Overview

Weaverville is in Trinity County, which is an inland part of California's North Coast region. It sits at 2188 feet of elevation.

    Pizza

    There’s only a couple of options in town and they are virtually right next to each other (only separated by the foundation of the old Roundtable Pizza that burnt down years ago):

    The Water Bar at 100 Nugget Ln. makes excellent pizza on naan bread with fresh toppings piled on, served with a boba milk tea, Italian soda or a smoothie all made with their superb tasting alkaline Ph balanced water (hence the name of the place)

    Tangle Blue at 130 Nugget Ln. is one of three local ‘watering holes’ (not to be confused with the actual Water Bar highlighted above). It, by far, has the coolest name out of the three and the coolest bartenders who will hook you up with your favorite libation and one of their artisan pizzas

    Stay safe

    Has a very low crime rate does not have a police department but does have a County Sherriff and the California Highway Patrol. Also of note there are few gas stations outside of Weaverville so it would be wise to get gas there, as cell phone service is spotty as you are in the mountains and would not want to be stuck for a long period of time. Also be wary in the wintertime as it does snow in this area and usually the roads are cleaned up rather quickly, best advice would to bring chains for your vehicles tires just in case
