    Understand

    Bellville is known for a downtown area that still features old-timey storefronts and its historic Bellville Gazebo Bandstand, which was built in 1879.

    Get in

    By car

    • Interstate 71, exit 165 at State Route 97 (Lexington-Bellville exit), go south on State Route 97, your right in Bellville. To get to the central business district, go south on State Route 97 to State Route 13.
    • Interstate 71, exit 169 at State Route 13 (Mansfield-Bellville exit), go south on State Route 13 to Bellville.

    See

    See Louis Bromfield's Malabar Farm (Malabar Farm State Park)

    In 1976, Malabar Farm became an official Ohio State Park. Dedicated to perpetuating Bromfield's farming philosophies, preserving the Big House artifacts, providing a place where visitors can explore life on the farm and beauty of nature. Visit the livestock in the main barn. Camping, fishing, hiking. Horses and pets permitted. Farm tours cost $3 per person, and house tours are $6.

    📍 4050 Bromfield Rd, Lucas~10 miles east of town☎ +1 419-892-2784Checked Oct 2026

    Do

    Do Richland B&O Bike Trail

    The trail runs mostly east–west along the north end of the main downtown area. In total, it is 18 miles long and connects four different towns along a former railroad track. It is paved and mostly flat, so you can walk, run, skate, or bike. Dogs only if leashed. No horses.

    Sleep

    Most of the hotels are on the north end of town, at the intersection of Interstate 71 and State Highway 97.

    Stay Comfort Inn Splash Harbor

    An otherwise ordinary hotel lifted into the dreams of traveling children by a busy indoor pool with a large water slide and other amusement-park-style water features. Grab a hot breakfast (included) and then let the kids wear themselves out in the pool area before checking out by 11AM sharp (they fine anyone who overstays their time in the pool) and resuming your drive. Arcade games, laundry room, exercise room. Some rooms permit dogs. Some rooms have three queen beds, to accommodate a family of six.

    📍 855 Comfort Plaza Dr💲 $100+Check-in 4PM · out 11AM☎ +1 419-886-4000Checked Oct 2026
    MapsWebsite

    Stay Days Inn by Wyndham Bellville Mansfield (formerly Mid Ohio Inn)

    A less-expensive option if you're passing through. It's right next to the highway, and the traffic noise is a bit less if you can get a room on the far side of the building. Cold breakfast included.
