Best things about Hamilton

Pyramid Hill Sculpture Park

A 300-acre sculpture park. Has an ancient sculpture museum on site.

Fernald Preserve

A reclaimed site on top of a former nuclear material processing plant. The visitors center has information on area history.

Miami University Hamilton

Butler County Courthouse

A historic courthouse, built in 1885 in the Second Empire architectural style. The large octagonal stone clocktower on the roof makes this building easy to identify.

Hollow Earth Monument

A monument to area local John Symmes Jr. who proposed an updated Hollow Earth model in 1818. The monument features a stone carving of his model, which features large holes to the inner Earth at the poles.

Get in

By plane

The nearest commercial passenger air service to Hamilton is at either Cincinnati-Northern Kentucky International Airport (CVG) or Dayton International Airport (DAY), both located approximately 50 miles away.

Go Butler County Regional Airport (HAO, Hogan Field)

Located in Hamilton, provides General aviation services for small private aircraft. Charter flights are available here. Offers free Wi-Fi at the terminal.

By train

There are no scheduled passenger rail service to Hamilton or Butler County, although Amtrak runs its Cardinal train through the county three times per week. The nearest Amtrak station is Union Terminal in Cincinnati.

By car

US 127 runs north-south through Hamilton, connecting the city with Eaton and Greenville to the north and Fairfield and Cincinnati to the south. Ohio 129 crosses Hamilton west to east, with the portion east of the city constructed as a limited-access highway, providing a direct connection to Interstate 75 near West Chester.

Other state highways in Hamilton include Ohio 4 to Fairfield and Middletown, Ohio 128 southwest to Ross and Cleves, and Ohio 177 northwest to Richmond, Indiana.
