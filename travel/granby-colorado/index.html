Overview

Granby is a town in the Rocky Mountains in Colorado. It is home to the ski resort, SolVista.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Jun–Sep. Summer highs reach about 78°F; winter lows dip to about 15°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: May and Apr. (climate data from nearby Rocky Mountain National Park)

    Understand

    Granby and SolVista try very hard, and often succeed, in catering to families. There are programs for every age that should keep everyone in the family happy.

    Get in

    Go Granby train station

    Amtrak serves Granby with the California Zephyr, which runs daily between Emeryville (in the San Francisco Bay Area) and Chicago.
