Overview

Greenwater is a small town in Washington state, immediately north of Mount Rainier. This small community of less than 100 residents, along with the nearby Crystal Mountain ski resort, provides the only services on the east side of Mount Rainier National Park.

Get in

State Route 410 (WA-410), the Chinook Pass Highway, runs through Greenwater, connecting Tacoma and the Puget Sound region over Chinook Pass to Yakima and eastern Washington.

Chinook Pass closes for the winter, south of Crystal Mountain. It usually closes in November and reopens in June, but is variable based on snowpack. Check the Washington State Department of Transportation for winter travel information.

See and do

Greenwater is near the Sunrise entrance of Mount Rainier National Park.

Hiking

  • The Pacific Crest Trail crosses WA-410 at Chinook Pass. Most trails east of WA-410 eventually connect to it.

Camping

If you're not able to snag a campsite within the national park, the Forest Service campgrounds along WA-410 are great alternatives.

Stay The Dalles Campground

45 tent or trailer units, five sites are barrier free and accessible. The campground is located along the White River.

Explore the other ski resorts in the Washington Cascades:

  • Mount Baker — holder of the world record for most snowfall. Elevation 3,500–5,089 ft
  • Stevens Pass — the biggest terrain within two hours of Seattle. Elevation 3,500–5,089 ft
  • Mission Ridge — on the eastern slopes, with more sunshine than the other resorts. Elevation 4,570-6,820 ft
  • Snoqualmie Pass — the closest ski resort to Seattle. Elevation 2,610–5,420 ft
  • White Pass — a relaxed atmosphere for all abilities. Elevation 4,500-6,550 ft
