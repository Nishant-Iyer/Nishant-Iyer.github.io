Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Oct. Summer highs reach about 84°F; winter lows dip to about 16°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Aug and Sep. (climate data from nearby Ypsilanti)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).
