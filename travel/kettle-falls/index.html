Best things about Kettle Falls

St. Paul's Mission

This 1846 church is one of the oldest in Washington. A trail with exhibits is nearby.

China Bend Winery

Kettle Falls Historical Centre

An interpretive center and other exhibits allow you to learn about 9,000 years of human history. Exhibits range from the Native American fishermen to white settlers.

Bull Hill Ranch

Enjoy the scenic horseback riding trails of the area

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Sep. Summer highs reach about 87°F; winter lows dip to about 26°F. Cold months (Jan, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Dec and Jan. (climate data from nearby Spokane)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 1,880 miles, roughly 32 h 30 min without stops. Flying is usually faster.
  • GEG Spokane International Airport, Spokane — 73 mi away (major hub). Directions
  • EAT Pangborn Memorial Airport, Wenatchee — 130 mi away. Directions

Kettle Falls is at the junctions of US Highway 395 (US-395), State Route 20 (WA-20), and State Route 25 (WA-25), about 80 mi northwest of Spokane and 41 mi south of the Canadian border.
