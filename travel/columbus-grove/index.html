Best things about Columbus Grove

Bridenbaugh District No. 3 Schoolhouse

Columbus Grove Swimming Pool

A historic and uniquely castle like pool built during the Great Depression of the 1930s. There is also an emerald green fishing pond in front of the pool, as well as picnic space next to the entrance.
