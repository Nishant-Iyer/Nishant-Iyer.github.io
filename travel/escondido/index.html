Best things about Escondido

San Diego Zoo Safari Park

This sibling to the San Diego Zoo is a few miles east of town, in the San Pasqual Valley of North San Diego.

San Diego Archaeological Center

Dedicated to "the care, management, and curation of archaeological artifacts discovered in the San Diego region" the site features over 500 collections as well as a public lecture series for visitors.

California Center for the Arts

Excellent local playhouse.

Cruisin Grand

On Fridays from April 30 to September 18, Grand Avenue downtown becomes a classic automobile showcase with a different theme every night.

Lake Hodges, Lake Dixon and Lake Wolford

Offer fishing, boating and other recreational activities.

North Shore Lake Hodges Trail

A 0.6-mile trail which runs along Interstate 15, beginning at Sunset Drive (Escondido), and is an excellent and moderate hiking, biking, and equestrian trail. For mountain bikers, there is a trail that continues west.

    Get in

    Escondido is located 30 miles north of San Diego on I-15, and 20 miles east/inland of Oceanside on State Route 78. The nearest major airport is the San Diego International Airport in San Diego. The nearest general aviation airport is Palomar Airport in Carlsbad.

    The North County Transit District (NCTD) operates bus routes throughout northern San Diego County, and the Sprinter, a light rail line running along Route 78 between Escondido and Oceanside.

    Buses and the Sprinter train call at Escondido Transit Center, at 700 W Valley Pkwy.

    Drink Stone Brewing Company

    American

    The Escondido location is Stone Brewing Company's headquarters. Stone brews a variety of regular and seasonal beers, with a particular strength in IPAs; its most famous beer is "Arrogant Bastard," an American Strong Ale, but Stone brewery has an expansive. For visitors, the brewery has a company store and an extensive restaurant and beer garden. Growlers and growler fills, including many limited special release beers, are available in the company store. Brewery tours are offered several times daily, which end in a beer tasting.
