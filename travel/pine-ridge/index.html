Overview

Pine Ridge is a town in South Dakota and the tribal headquarters of the Pine Ridge Indian Reservation.

2 sights · 1 places to eat · 1 places to stay

Best things about Pine Ridge

Wounded Knee Battlefield

The site of the Wounded Knee Massacre in 1890; over 250 Sioux were killed. There is a Wounded Knee Museum in nearby Wall.

The Heritage Center at Red Cloud Indian School

A museum of Lakota and other Native American cultures, art gallery, and gift shop.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Sep. Summer highs reach about 85°F; winter lows dip to about 15°F. Cold months (Jan, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: May and Jun. (climate data from nearby Wind Cave National Park)
