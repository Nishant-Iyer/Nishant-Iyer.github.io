Best things about Marshalltown

Matthew Edel Blacksmith Shop

The house and shop of Matthew Edel, a German immigrant who died in 1940, are listed on the National Register of Historic Places. The shop was built in 1883.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Oct. Summer highs reach about 83°F; winter lows dip to about 10°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Jun and May. (climate data from nearby Grinnell)

Getting there

  • From you: to see whether to drive or fly, and how long it takes.
  • ALO Waterloo Regional Airport, Waterloo — 44 mi away. Directions
  • DSM Des Moines International Airport, Des Moines — 52 mi away (major hub). Directions

There is no commercial aviation to Ames; the nearest major airport is located in Des Moines. After landing at Des Moines, rent a car or take the Burlington Trailways bus to Marshalltown. The Trailways bus stop is at 1501 2nd Ave, a few blocks north of East Grand Ave in downtown. You can take the free D-Line bus to 2nd Ave and East Grand Ave, then walk north.
