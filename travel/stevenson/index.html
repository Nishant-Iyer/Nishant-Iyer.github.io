Overview

Stevenson is a city in southwest Washington, in the Columbia Gorge. It is the capital of Skamania County.

Get in

Stevenson is 45 minutes outside of Portland and 3 ½ hours from Seattle.

By car

State Route 14 passes through Stevenson.

Interstate 84 is on the Oregon side of the river, across the Bridge of the Gods.

By plane

Fly into Portland International Airport (PDX) and hop onto I-84 East.

If you fly into Seattle-Tacoma International Airport, take I-5 South to SR-14.

Hiking

Do Beacon Rock State Park

This giant monolith, the second-largest free-standing monolith in the world (after the Rock of Gibraltar), was once the core of an ancient volcano. It acquired its name from Lewis and Clark during their expedition west. A relatively short, but moderately vertical hike up the 1 mi trail to the top of this old volcanic core rewards you with spectacular views of the Gorge, the river, and wildlife. An average hiker can reach the top in less than thirty minutes. The park also offers camping and more hiking trails, if you feel so inclined or you wish to avoid other hikers.

Do Hamilton Mountain

For all hikers there is the Hamilton Mountain trail. There is a 7.5 mi loop trail for more experienced hikers but families and beginners can walk a mere 1.25 mi up the trail to view Rodney Falls and the Pool of the Winds.

Drink Walking Man Brewery

Walking Man Brewery, Stevenson’s award-winning craft brewery, is located just a block off of the riverfront. There is a reason this brewery has fans around the world; it has an all-star selection of brews and a great northwest vibe. If you can’t make it to the brewery, their beers are offered in nearly every restaurant in town.

Stay The Columbia Gorge Riverside Lodge

If the Gorge winds are what pulled you to Stevenson, try out the Columbia Gorge Riverside Lodge conveniently located on Bob’s Beach. It’s a popular launch point for any windsurfer or kite boarder (and it beats paying fees to launch on the Oregon side). If you have your gear, the only effort needed to get out on the river is to open your front door. The Columbia Gorge Riverside Lodge offers very competitive room rates and several rooms are equipped with hot tubs built-in to their font decks –not too shabby!
