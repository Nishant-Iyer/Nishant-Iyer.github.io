Overview

Westlake is a city in Cuyahoga County, part of Greater Cleveland in Ohio. With a population of just over 32,000 in 2019, it's often regarded as a suburb of Cleveland (whose downtown is 12 miles east) but is a city in its own right. It's on the shore of Lake Erie and its main occupation is software development and the university hospital.

Best things about Westlake

Bradley Woods Reservation

Nature preserve. Waystation for Monarch Butterflies during their migration. Bunns Lake offers birdwatching and fishing.

Sky Zone Westlake

Westlake Recreation Center

Includes fishing ponds and sports pitches. Indoors it includes gym rooms, and an aquatics center with pools, slides, and a lazy river.
