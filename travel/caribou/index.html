Overview

Caribou is the most northeastern city in the United States and located just 10 minutes from the Canadian border. The community was incorporated in 1859. The community has a rich agricultural history and is known for some of the finest snowmobiling in the Northeast.

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 1,250 miles, roughly 21 h 30 min without stops. Flying is usually faster.
  • PQI Presque Isle International Airport, Presque Isle — 12 mi away. Directions
  • BGR Bangor International Airport, Bangor — 148 mi away. Directions
  • PWM Portland International Jetport, Portland — 249 mi away (major hub). Directions

Caribou is 1 hour north of Houlton on U.S Route 1. Travel times are approximately 3 hours north of Bangor, Maine, and 5 hours north of Portland, Maine. The community is 4 hours east of Quebec City and 2 hours north of Fredericton, New Brunswick.
