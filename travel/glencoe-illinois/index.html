    See Chicago Botanic Garden

    Glencoe (25 miles north of downtown) - Daily 8AM-sunset, except Christmas Day (25 Dec). Amazingly beautiful, and completely diverse. The 385-acre park, research, and art facilities are open year-round for your visiting pleasure. Admission is free, though a $10 per vehicle fee charged for parking.
