Do

  • If you have a bicycle, there are two bike/walk paths in Somers Point. One of them starts a few blocks north of Somers Mansion (near the fire station) and goes north through Somers Point, Linwood, Northfield, and ends in Pleasantville. The other one is the Ocean City Bike Path, which ends on the Somers Point side of the 9th St. Bridge and leads into Ocean City.
