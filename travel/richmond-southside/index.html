See

See Belle Isle

In the middle of the James River, this island includes a mountain-biking trail. Fans of the movies Cold Mountain, Hannibal, and The Jackal may recognize the area. It offers great views of the falls and the river, but be wary of copperheads, which abound on the island. Also many teenagers enjoy swimming and laying out on the rocks. There are even rope swings set up on the bridges nearby. It is a great hangout for teenagers and young adults. The rocks can get overcrowded during the warmer months on the weekend, so plan accordingly. Also, in mainly July and August when the James River water levels are low, the water can get bath-water hot.

Shop Stony Point Fashion Park

Stony Point Fashion Park is just south of the James River. This open air shopping mall hosts over 100 shops and restaurants including Dillard's department store and upscale shops like Saks Fifth Avenue, Coach, Ann Taylor and Sur La Table. You'll also find many restaurants at the shopping center including CineBistro luxury dinner-and-a-movie theater.

Drink O'Toole's Restaurant (Irish Pub)

O'Toole's Irish Pub, in the quiet neighborhood of Westover Hills, serves reuben and onion rings, prime rib and mashed sweet potatoes, Irish beers on tap and homemade bread pudding. There are authentic family crests along the walls and live music every weekend, a dart room and private banquet rooms.
