    Get in

    Greyhound bus lines offers a daily bus from Little Rock to El Dorado.

    The South Arkansas Regional Airport at Goodwin Field (ELD) has flights are operated by Southern Airways Express from Dallas-Fort Worth International Airport and Memphis.

    Get around

    There is no public transportation in El Dorado, but there are some car rental agencies in town. Traffic runs fairly smoothly, so anyone used to driving in small- to medium-sized cities will have no problems getting around.
