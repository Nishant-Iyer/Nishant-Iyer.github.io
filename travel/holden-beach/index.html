How is the food here?

Betty's Waterfront Restaurant is amazing, along with the Seafood Barn.

The best place for ice cream is The Scoop, and if you want coffee you have to check out Cappuccino by the Sea, it is fantastic.

Get around

Try renting bikes. There's a place right before you get on the island called Boomers Rentals, Holden Beach has many bike friendly roads and even a self-guided tour.
