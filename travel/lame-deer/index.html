Overview

Lame Deer is in southeast Montana. It is on the Northern Cheyenne Indian Reservation and serves as the tribal headquarters. About 1,900 people live there.

1 things to do

Best things about Lame Deer

Northern Cheyenne Pow Wow

Traditional pow wow plus parade, competitive dancing and other events celebrating the Northern Cheyenne people. Camping available.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Sep. Summer highs reach about 86°F; winter lows dip to about 16°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: May and Jun. (climate data from nearby Gillette)

Understand

As of 2023, Lame Deer has not been Google-mapped. Once in town however, it is easy to see locations, or ask at the gas station.

Chief Dull Knife College, a small two-year college, is located in town.

Get in

Lame Deer is on State Highway 212 (MT-212).

The nearest commercial airport is Billings Logan International Airport (BIL).

Get around

You will likely arrive by car, which is the easiest way to get around. Walking or biking once in town is also possible.
