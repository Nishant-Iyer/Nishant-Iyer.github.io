Overview

Mims is a city in Brevard County between Oak Hill to the northeast and Titusville to the south. Mims' northern suburb, Scottsmoor, is also covered in this article.

    See

    See Scottsmoor Flatwoods Sanctuary East

    A wildlife preserve between US-1 and I-95 north of Mims (near Scottsmoor) with about 10 mi of trails. These trails are accessible via Parrish Park, where there are restrooms and a parking lot. The park features the pine flatwood ecosystem native to Florida and contains a variety of wildlife including sandhill cranes and birds of prey. Wild pigs also inhabit the park. Some trails may be flooded after heavy rain. Scottsmoor Flatwoods Sanctuary West can be accessed from the southern fork of the East Central Regional Rail Trail.
