    Get in

    Given that Watertown is relatively close to Boston, please refer to the Get In and Get Around sections of that article for general transportation details.

    By plane

    Logan International Airport in Boston.

    By bus

    MBTA provides bus service. The 70 and 71 can be accessed from Central and Harvard, respectively. The 57 bus from Newtown Corner and Brighton. The 52 and 59 from Dedham and Needham, respectively, via Newton. From Waltham, the aformentioned 70 bus. The MBTA's 504 route operates express trips from downtown all day weekdays and much of Saturday.

    By car

    Interstate 90 (the Massachusetts Turnpike), Exit 17.

    Arsenal Street / Western Ave and Route 20 exits off Soldiers Field Road.

    Get around

    By car

    Traffic can get heavy during the morning and evening rush hours. Route 16 and US Route 20 cuts across Watertown.

    By public transit

    The MBTA's 71 bus is the most frequent of the routes that serve Watertown. Routes 554 and 73 run along the northern border of Watertown. The 70 traverses the entire town west to east. Watertown Square is the main bus hub, but note that the 504, 57, and 52 routes terminate at the former bus yard across the Charles from Watertown Square.

    By bike

    Watertown has several Bluebikes bike rental stations.

    Shop Armenian markets (Arax Market, Massis Bakery, Sevan Bakery)

    Watertown has the third-largest Armenian population of any U.S. city, and as such supports several authentic ethnic markets, three of which are clustered in the space of a single block. They are packed with interesting culinary finds, many of which are made in-house.
