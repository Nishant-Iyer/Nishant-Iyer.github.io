Overview

Universal Orlando Resort is a theme park resort in Orlando, Florida, composed of three theme parks, an entertainment district with nightlife and restaurants, a waterpark, and several hotels. Many visitors describe it as a place of fun, action, magic, excitement, humor, wonder, fantasy and attention to detail.

    Understand

    The Universal Orlando Resort opened in 1990 and is one of the biggest and most visited theme park complexes in the world and the second in Orlando after Walt Disney World. It is composed of three theme parks, an entertainement district, and 10 hotels:

    • Universal Studios Florida is the resort's original theme park, opening in 1990 with a focus on movies and TV and has 8 themed areas with an array of rides, shows, and other attractions. Visitors can also enjoy themed dining and shopping, a variety of special events throughout the year. The themes of Universal Studio's are targeted at making guests feel like they are in a movie set with rides, shows, and attractions that are inspired by popular film, television, and music productions.
    • Islands of Adventure is a theme park composed of seven distinct "islands" that are themed to various forms of adventures. Visitors start off in the Port of Entry and make their way through the various islands – Marvel Super Hero Island, Toon Lagoon, Jurassic Park, The Wizarding World of Harry Potter, The Lost Continent, and Seuss Landing. The Wizarding World of Harry Potter, based on the Harry Potter franchise, is the only island added after the park opened, in 2010.
    • Epic Universe is Universal Orlando's third theme park. It is to the southeast of the other parks some distance away, about a twenty minute drive, and it has 4 universes inspired by Nintendo, How to Train Your Dragon, Harry Potter, and Universal's monsters.
    • Universal CityWalk Orlando acts as the gateway to the parks, featuring shopping, nightclubs, dining venues, and a movie theater. There are several nightclubs in city walk as well as live shows and stores with universal-themed merchandise.
    • Volcano Bay is a water-park that replaced "Wet 'n Wild". The park is centered on a 200-foot-tall (61-m) volcano named "Krakatau" (see Krakatoa for information on the real Krakatau). Volcano Bay's attractions are located in four themed areas, each inspired by various Polynesian islands and cultures.

    When to visit

    Like all of Central Florida, the climate at Universal Orlando is humid and subtropical. Summer (May–October) is hot and sticky and winter (November–April) is mild and drier. Visitors from higher latitudes may be surprised by the intensity of the sun in the summer. In June through September, late afternoon thundershowers are common and the hurricane season runs from late August to late October (the parks will close if there is hurricane warning for the Orlando area). Keep in mind that inclement weather can often lead to the temporary closure of outdoor rides and live performances. Rides will re-open after the weather improves and live performances may be either delayed or canceled outright.

    Determining when to visit the resort should also factor in crowds, prices and special events. Crowds tend to be heavy during national and school holidays like Christmas, Thanksgiving, Spring Break, Halloween, Summer etc. Prices for tickets to the parks and hotels also tend to fluctuate throughout the year but are typically higher during the peak seasons. If you are visiting on a tight budget or don't like crowds it's better to visit in during the off-season but keep in mind that some attractions may be closed for seasonal maintenance and that it may be rainy or chilly. Various special events like the Mardi Gras Festival or Halloween Horror Nights also tend to attract higher crowds and raise prices since they are very popular with park-goers.

    Get in

    By plane

    Most visitors fly into Orlando International Airport (MCO), +1 407-825-2001. It's served by every major US airline and a wide variety of international carriers. Once there, you can rent a car, take a taxi, or order a limo or town car. If you're staying at an on-site Universal hotel, another option is Universal's Superstar Shuttle, a bus service from the airport to all ten resort hotels. Prior reservations are required. If driving yourself, Florida State Road 528 links the airport with Interstate 4, and heading northbound on that highway leads directly to Universal.

    Orlando-Sanford International Airport (SFB) is a bit farther from Universal than Orlando International. Aside from domestic flights on Allegiant Air, the airport primarily handles flights from the United Kingdom and Iceland, and Universal's Superstar Shuttle is not available here. Florida State Road 417 connects the airport to Interstate 4.

    By car

    Interstate 4 is the most direct driving route to Universal Orlando, passing just south of the resort. Depending on your point of origin, Florida's Turnpike can be quite useful, particularly if you're driving north from the Miami area. If you're coming from a cruise at Port Canaveral, Florida State Road 528 gets you to I-4, with Orlando International Airport and the Turnpike en route.

    By train

    Amtrak's Silver Service Miami–New York City routes serve Orlando four times daily, with two trains in either direction. Northbound trains from Miami arrive at the Orlando station at 1:43PM and 7:08PM; southbound trains from NYC arrive at 10:17AM and 12:55PM. The Auto Train, which can carry both passengers and automobiles, serves nearby Sanford non-stop from Lorton, Virginia (near Washington, D.C.).

    Brightline operates a train service between Miami and the Orlando International Airport, with stops in Fort Lauderdale, Boca Ranton, Aventura and West Palm Beach. The journey from downtown Miami takes about 3,5 hours and the trains run every hour between 6:50AM and 8:50PM. Tickets from Miami start at $39.

    If you are coming from Volusia, Seminole, or northern Osceola counties on a weekday and want to avoid I-4 traffic, your best option is SunRail, which runs between DeBary and Poinciana. There is no service on weekends and some federal holidays (for example, Thanksgiving and Christmas Day), and the trains run every 30 minutes during rush hour periods, and every 2 hours during off peak periods. The closest station to Universal is at Sand Lake Road in Orlando, however, a taxi or ridesharing service will be needed to complete the journey. For convenience, take the train to Lynx Central Station in downtown Orlando and take Lynx bus routes 21, 38, or 40 to Universal. You may also disembark at the Orlando Health/Orlando Amtrak station and board Lynx bus route 40 from there.

    By bus

    Universal's Superstar Shuttle is a very popular option for guests staying at Universal resort hotels; it provides transportation to and from Orlando International Airport. Be sure to book service ahead of time! It costs about $39 per adult, each way. The experience of Universal's Superstar Shuttle is mixed. Some describe it as quick while others describe it as an arduous journey stopping at different terminals of the airport and multiple hotel stops. Unless you are a solo traveller, it is cheaper and quicker to order a taxi or rideshare at the airport to bring your entire group directly to the hotel.

    If you're not staying at a Universal resort, or aren't coming in via Orlando International, you do have some other choices. Mears Transportation is the main player in the region; they have a fleet of shuttle buses and town cars that can take you to any of the local attractions or hotels.

    Long-distance bus company Greyhound serves Orlando at their Orlando Terminal, +1 407-292-3424.

    LYNX is the public bus service of the Central Florida Regional Transportation Authority. Four LYNX routes provide service between Universal Orlando and the surrounding area. All pick-up and drop-off from the Universal Orlando Parking Garage bus stop, next to the guest drop-off loop on the walkway between the parking garages and CityWalk.

    • Regular routes:
    • Route 21, from Universal inbound to Lynx Central Station in downtown Orlando and outbound to Sand Lake Commons. This is the one of four connectors to SunRail at Lynx Central Station.
    • Route 37, from Universal northbound to Park Promenade Plaza near and southbound to the Florida Mall.
    • Route 38, from Universal southbound to International Drive and Destination Parkway, and Northbound non-stop express to Lynx Central Station in downtown Orlando.
    • Route 40, from Universal inbound to Lynx Central Station. This is the other connector to SunRail. (The outbound route for this line only goes one stop further, to the Universal Employee Center; make sure you don't go to this stop!)
    • Route 303, is a commuter route with very limited service, running from Universal to different locations throughout Orlando and Walt Disney World, specifically servicing Disney Springs.

    Get around

    Universal Studios Florida, Islands of Adventure and City Walk are all within walking distance from each other and from most hotels. Epic Universe and its adjacent hotels (Stella Nova, Terra Luna and Helios Grand Hotel) are located about 7 miles to the south and also within walking distance of each other. The Universal Shuttle Bus picks up guests from all hotels and drops them off at the entrance of CityWalk and Volcano Bay. There are also water taxis that run between the Portofino Bay, Royal Pacific, Hard Rock and Sapphire Falls hotels to the entrances of Universal Studios Florida and Islands of Adventure. Both the shuttle bus and the water taxis are free. Epic Universe will also be connected by shuttle bus to the resorts and the other parks when it opens.

    Concerts

    The Universal Music Plaza Stage live concerts:

    • Rock the Universe is the park's Christian music festival, featuring two days in September of concerts with leading contemporary Christian, Christian rock, and Christian rap artists live at The Universal Music Plaza Stage in the Production Central.
    • Summer Concert Series occurs annually and features a variety of popular music acts performing live at The Universal Music Plaza Stage in the Production Central.

    Parades

    • Macy's Holiday Parade brings some authentic balloons from the Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade to Orlando in a month-long event in December that highlights the Christmas celebrations at the park.
    • Mardi Gras Generally in February through to April, a parade and concert series inspired by New Orleans' Fat Tuesday party is held at the park.
    • Universal's Superstar Parade is a daily parade at Universal Studios Florida; it features a lot of kid-friendly characters such as SpongeBob, Dora, Minions, etc.

    Halloween

    Halloween Horror Nights turns the park into a giant haunted playground, including scare zones, numerous haunted houses and special themed shows. The event is run annually in late September and October.

    Universal Studios Florida

    Universal Studios Florida features seven themed areas, all situated around a large lagoon. Clockwise from the entrance they are Production Central, New York, San Francisco, The Wizarding World of Harry Potter – Diagon Alley, World Expo, Springfield: Home of the Simpsons, Dreamworks and Hollywood. Each area features a combination of rides, shows, attractions, character appearances, dining outlets and merchandise stores.

    Production Central

    The main entryway, with a stately movie studio arch standing as grand entrance to the central area of the park. Stores line the pathway in the front half of the land, while large-scale motion simulator and "4-D" multisensory rides dominate the back half.

    New York

    An extremely detailed "backlot" replica of New York City circa 1940. Wander the streets and alleyways of this neon-covered land and peer in the shop windows to find interesting surprises. The Blues Brothers arrive in the iconic "Bluesmobile" and perform multiple times a day from their stage on one of the land's streets.

    Drink

    When creating the Wizarding World of Harry Potter, the folks at Universal had to turn the fictional drink of butterbeer into something real. Fortunately, they did an excellent job, and the butterbeer sold in the park is a unique and deliciously sweet non-alcoholic drink. (The hot version has dairy in its base and can't be veganized.)

    Sleep

    One of the best perks to staying on site is that shuttles and ferries to and from Universal Studios, Islands of Adventures, and Universal's CityWalk are available free of charge! And another great perk of staying on the grounds is that you get admission one hour early to one of the parks (either Universal Studios or Islands of Adventure depending on the day), so you can get on the rides when the lines are short before the parks open to the rest of the public.

    Universal Orlando Resort offers seven on site hotels, with two more opening in 2025. They are split into categories ranging from Value to Premier.

    The three "Premier" hotels (Loews Royal Pacific Resort, Hard Rock Hotel, and Loews Portofino Bay Hotel) also include free Universal Express Unlimited for shorter lines in the parks. For some odd reason, the hotels in "Value" and "Budget" category offer more frequent shuttle buses (every 15 mins) than the "Preferred" and "Premier" hotels (every 30 mins).

    Budget

    Universal classifies these hotels as Value Inns and Suites. All fairly new and lightly themed, these options are great for families and guests who want less of a full resort atmosphere and more of a convenient place to relax and sleep after a long day in the theme parks. Though less expensive than the other on-site hotels, these are still well-equipped and have some resort-style facilities.

    Tickets

    A Universal vacation can be expensive proposition, but with some planning you can determine just how expensive. Tickets are sold in options ranging from one to five days. While single-day tickets are costly, buying multiple-day tickets makes for a much lower price per-day.

    En route to Hogwarts

    The Hogwarts Express is an immersive train ride that connects the Hogsmeade section of the Wizarding World of Harry Potter in Islands of Adventure with the Diagon Alley section in Universal Studios Florida. As it links both theme parks, a 2-Park ticket is required to ride the train, and guests with just a single-park ticket will be turned away!

    Two kinds of tickets are offered: One Park tickets and Multiple Park tickets. One Park tickets allow for entry only on one theme park for one day. Multiple Park tickets allow for entry to more than one park and are split into tickets including only Universal Studios Florida and Islands of Adventure and the ones that also include Volcano Bay. They can be purchased for up to 5 days. Multiple Park tickets are very cost effective if your vacation is for more than 1-2 days and are a great way to maximize your time and experience. Multiple-day tickets do not have to be used on consecutive days, however, these tickets will expire 6 days after they are first used (7 days total). Unused tickets expire 12 months from the order date. Buying online through the Universal Orlando website can save $20 off the price of tickets bought at the gate.

    One-day tickets for Epic Universe are now available as of Oct 2025.

    There are multiple add-on options when purchasing your tickets like the My Universal Photos package, which allows unlimited downloads of all photos and videos taken at participating locations in both theme parks and the water park, Marvel Character Dinner, where you dine with one or more Marvel characters and a VIP Experience that includes a 5-hour tour of both parks without waiting in lines and backstage access. For more information always visit the official Universal Orlando website.

    The CityWalk Party Pass gets rid of the individual cover charge at restaurants and entertainment venues and runs $12 per pass, or $15 if you want to include a movie at the AMC Cineplex. The pass can be purchased online , at the Plaza located near The Groove Discotheque or the Guest Services window

    Children under age three are admitted for free. The prices below are accurate as of Jan 2026 (Florida sales tax not included):

    Volcano Bay water park has a different single-day, one-park admission price: $80 ($75 for ages 3–9).

    Florida residents who provide proof of residence get discounts of varying degrees with some blockout dates, and they can't purchase beyond a 3-day ticket. Annual passes are also available for frequent visitors or residents.

    Express Pass

    An Express Pass is a purchased add-on to your ticket that allows its users to bypass the regular queue in a ride, and be granted priority when boarding. The Express Pass is not included in park admission. There are two types of passes: the regular that can be used once on each ride and the unlimited that can be used without any restrictions. There is a limited number of them available each day and may be sold out in advance on busier days. The cost of the pass varies based on how many days and for which parks it is used for.

    If staying at Portofino Bay, Royal Pacific Resort or the Hard Rock Hotel then free Express Passes are available at kiosk in the lobby.
