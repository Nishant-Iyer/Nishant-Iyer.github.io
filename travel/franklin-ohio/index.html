Best things about Franklin

Franklin Historical Society Museum

Built in 1901, this former residence of General E. Forrest Harding now houses a museum dedicated to the history of Franklin.

Old Franklin Post Office

A historical log post office that was commissioned by Thomas Jefferson in 1805.
