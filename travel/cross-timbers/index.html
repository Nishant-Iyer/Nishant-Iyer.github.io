Overview

The Cross Timbers are the northwestern band of Texas' Prairies and Lakes region.

Plan your trip

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Mar–May, Sep–Nov. Summer highs reach about 96°F; winter lows dip to about 35°F. Very hot (Jun, Jul, Aug) — plan outdoor time for mornings. Wettest: Oct and May. (climate data from nearby Fort Worth)

Understand

The Cross Timbers extend south from the Oklahoma border as far east as Lake Texoma and down south of Fort Worth. They wrap around the west side of the Dallas-Fort Worth Metroplex, bound on the west by the North Central Plains and on the east and south by the Blackland Prairie region.

Cities

  • Bridgeport
  • Cleburne
  • Decatur has one of the best 19th century courthouses in the state.
  • Denton - Eclectic home of the University of North Texas, Texas Women's University, and a vibrant music scene.
  • Gainesville
  • Glen Rose , gateway to Dinosaur Valley State Park.
  • Granbury
  • Mineral Wells
  • Muenster - A small German settlement with a great Oktoberfest celebration.
  • Nocona - Small town famous for producing cowboy boots.
  • Stephenville
  • Weatherford
