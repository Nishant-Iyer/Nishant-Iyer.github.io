Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Jun–Oct. Summer highs reach about 67°F; winter lows dip to about 39°F. Wettest: Dec and Jan. (climate data from nearby Coos Bay)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 2,270 miles, roughly 39 h without stops. Flying is usually faster.
  • OTH Southwest Oregon Regional Airport, North Bend — 41 mi away. Directions
  • CEC Jack Mc Namara Field Airport, Crescent City — 73 mi away. Directions
  • PDX Portland International Airport, Portland — 208 mi away (major hub). Directions

Sample 3-day trip

  1. Day 1: Gold Beach — Pistol River State Scenic Viewpoint, Curry Historical Museum.
  2. Day 2: Brookings-Harbor — Alfred A. Loeb State Park, Crissey Beach, Harris Beach.
  3. Day 3: Bandon — Face Rock State Scenic Viewpoint, Face Rock Creamery, Bandon Dunes Golf Resort.
