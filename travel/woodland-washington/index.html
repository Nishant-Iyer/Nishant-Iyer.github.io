Get in

By car

  • Interstate 5 (I-5) runs through Woodland off of Exit #21 (SR-503 Woodland Cougar)
  • to & State Route 503 (SR-503) connects Woodland to Mt St Helens via Cougar. From Cougar the road becomes Forest Rd 90 which skirts the south of Mt St Helens to its junction with Forest Rd 25. Go north on Forest Rd 25 which goes between Mt St Helens and Mt Adams. Access to Mt St Helens from Windy Ridge on FR 99 from FR 25. SR-503 goes through town as Lewis River Rd.

By bus

Go Lower Columbia CAP

Rural public bus system serving Longview from Kalama, Woodland, and Vancouver 99th St Station (north of Vancouver, WA). Nearest transfer point to/from the C-Tran buses at the 99th St Station to other places in Vancouver. The #105 bus goes from the 99th St Station to Portland. Nearest Connections to Greyhound and Amtrak are in Kelso/Longview and Portland.
