Overview

The Baraboo River Valley is a region in Southwest Wisconsin comprised of Sauk and Juneau Counties. The region is anchored by Baraboo and centered around the Baraboo River, a tributary of the Wisconsin River.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Sep. Summer highs reach about 82°F; winter lows dip to about 12°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Jun and Jul. (climate data from nearby Madison)

Cities

  • Baraboo : The county seat of Sauk County and the primary economic driver of the region.
  • Camp Douglas : A small village in Juneau County.
  • Elroy : A small village in Juneau County along the Elroy-Sparta State Trail.
  • Lake Delton : A smaller community in northern Sauk County, it has a number of resorts and might be considered a sister community to Wisconsin Dells.
  • Mauston : The county seat of Juneau County.
  • Necedah : A small village in Juneau County.
  • Prairie du Sac : A small village in Sauk County.
  • Reedsburg : A large, rural community in Sauk County.
