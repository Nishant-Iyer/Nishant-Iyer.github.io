    Get in

    By plane

    • Tweed New Haven Regional Airport (HVN), 155 Burr Street, Phone: +1 203 466-8833. Served by American Airlines.
    • Bradley International Airport (BDL) in Windsor Locks (near Hartford), from which I-91 leads straight to New Haven, is a much larger airport with flights nation and world wide. It is about an hour away.
    • Laguardia Airport is another major airport in the area, in nearby Queens. It has flights nation and worldwide, and is about 1 1/2 hours away

    Eat The Place

    Seafood

    Tree stumps for seats, spools for tables, and the smell of clam-bake in the air, it could only be The Place. The whole restaurant is outdoors, so getting a bite here is only possible during the warmer months. It's kind of pricey so packing a cooler with drinks and appetizers may be wise, however the food is delicious!
