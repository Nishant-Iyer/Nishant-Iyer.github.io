    South Pasadena is located between downtown Los Angeles and Pasadena, accessible via I-110.

    The Los Angeles Metro A Line passes through and has a stop in South Pasadena at Meridian Avenue and Mission Street.

    Shop South Pasadena Farmers' Market

    Dozens of local vendors sell fruit, vegetables, baked goods, fresh flowers, and more. A variety of food trucks and other ready-to-eat sellers are there, so you can get dinner at the market.
