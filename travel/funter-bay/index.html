Overview

Funter Bay is on the northwest side of Admiralty Island in Southeast Alaska, approximately 12 air miles from Juneau, or 25 miles from Auke Bay by boat. It is a protected anchorage and affords two state-maintained floats for boat tie-up.

Best time to visit & weather
