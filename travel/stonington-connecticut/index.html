    Do Johnathan Edwards Winery

    Wine,food,friends and music, there is no better time than a summer event at Johnathan Edwards Winery. The Winery has a typical New England style with that Napa Valley feel. One event you shouldn't miss is the traditional New England Lobster bake held in July. There is great New England seafood, wine, dancing and music. There is also Wine tasting year round.

    📍 74 Chester Maine Road

    Eat Cove Fish Market

    SeafoodAmerican

    The Cove Fish Market is a local favorite. It features outdoor seating on red picnic table as well as indoor seating. It is open year round. The featured seafood is fantastic and one dish was even featured in Esquire Magazine in 2008 voted as one of the best sandwiches in America. Not only is there dining but there is also a fish market with fresh local seafood caught in Stonington Waters.

    📍 20 Old Stonington Rd☎ +1 860 536-0061

    Bakery / Café
