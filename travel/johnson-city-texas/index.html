Overview

Johnson City is a city in the German Hill Country of Texas. It is the hometown of President Lyndon B. Johnson (the town was named after his grandfather).

4 sights · 1 places to stay

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Oct–Apr. Summer highs reach about 98°F; winter lows dip to about 42°F. Very hot (Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep) — plan outdoor time for mornings. Wettest: May and Oct. (climate data from nearby Austin)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 1,220 miles, roughly 21 h without stops. Flying is usually faster.

  • SAT San Antonio International Airport, San Antonio — 51 mi away (major hub). Directions

Johnson City is located on the intersection of US Highway 290 and US Highway 281. Johnson City is about 48 miles west of Austin and about 64 miles north of San Antonio.
