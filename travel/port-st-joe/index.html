Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Oct–May. Summer highs reach about 92°F; winter lows dip to about 40°F. Very hot (Jun, Jul, Aug) — plan outdoor time for mornings. Wettest: Jun and Aug. (climate data from nearby Florida Panhandle)

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 1,030 miles, roughly 17 h 30 min without stops. Flying is usually faster.
  • ECP Northwest Florida Beaches International Airport, Panama City Beach — 48 mi away. Directions
  • TLH Tallahassee International Airport, Tallahassee — 69 mi away. Directions
  • PNS Pensacola International Airport, Pensacola — 122 mi away (major hub). Directions

The closest airports in the area are the Apalachicola Municipal airport (AAF) located about 17 miles southeast of Port St Joe in Apalachicola, Florida, the Panama City Northwest Florida International Airport (ECP) located approximately 60 miles northwest of Port St Joe in Panama City, Florida, the Tallahassee Regional airport (TLH) located roughly 69 miles northeast of Port St Joe in Tallahassee, Florida, and finally the Marianna Municipal airport (MAI) located around 71 miles north of Port St Joe in Marianna, Florida.

Stay The Port Inn

Port Inn is a restored 21-room hotel located in the small coastal town of Port St. Joe on Florida's quiet Gulf Coast, less than twenty minutes from Apalachicola and Mexico Beach. The Inn sits just across the street from St. Joseph Bay and Frank Pate Park with public boat launching facilities, and just three blocks from a new, state-of-the-art marina offering fishing charters and waterfront dining.
