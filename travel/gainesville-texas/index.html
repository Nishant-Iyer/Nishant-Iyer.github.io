Overview

Gainesville is a city in the state of Texas. It is the county seat of Cooke County.

5 sights · 1 things to do · 7 places to eat · 2 places to stay

Best things about Gainesville

Frank Buck Zoo

One of the better small-town zoos in the state.

Historic Victorian homes

On brick-paved Denton, Lindsay, and Church streets.

Morton Museum of Cooke County

Local history museum, kind of sleepy.

Santa Fe Depot Museum

Usually has some photography or art exhibits on display, like of the Old West.

The Great Hanging Memorial

Where some individuals were disturbingly hung during the Civil War for not wanting to participate in Confederate conscription. There's a marker plus a tree.

Scenic drives

On Farm and Market roads 372, 678, 902 east and south of town. These roads give some great panoramic views of the rolling Cross Timbers landscape.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Mar–May, Sep–Nov. Summer highs reach about 96°F; winter lows dip to about 35°F. Very hot (Jun, Jul, Aug) — plan outdoor time for mornings. Wettest: Oct and May. (climate data from nearby Fort Worth)

Getting there

  • DAL Dallas Love Field, Dallas — 57 mi away (major hub). Directions

Gainesville is on I-35 near the Oklahoma border, about 30 minutes north of Denton, and US Highway 82, 30 minutes east of Sherman. It is also served by Amtrak and its Heartland Flyer service. The Heartland Flyer goes between Fort Worth and Oklahoma City once each day in both directions, south in the morning and north in the afternoon. Gainesville Station is located at 605 E California St, just west of downtown.
