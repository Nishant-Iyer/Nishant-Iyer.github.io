Understand

Brawley is a farming community in the heart of the Imperial Valley. It is best known for winter vegetables and cattle feedlots.

Get in

State Route 86 passes through Brawley as the town's Main Street. State Route 78 bypasses the city on the newly-built Brawley Bypass.

Imperial County Airport (IPL) at Imperial has a few scheduled commercial flights as well as general aviation.
