Overview

Brighton is a city in southeast Livingston County featuring a tranquil riverside boardwalk, the Mill Pond, historic cemeteries, local restaurants and art galleries.

Drink Brewery Becker

Brewery Becker is an atmospheric local brewery in Brighton, boasting its tasty beers and historic building that can be rented as a venue. The building, built in 1873, used to be called the Western House. It was built by a local farmer, and used to be a popular hotel in the area.
