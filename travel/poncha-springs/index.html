Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Jun–Aug. Summer highs reach about 79°F; winter lows dip to about 5°F. Cold months (Jan, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Aug and Jul. (climate data from nearby Florissant Fossil Beds National Monument)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

  • COS City of Colorado Springs Municipal Airport, Colorado Springs — 77 mi away (major hub). Directions

Poncha Springs is located near the geographic center of Colorado and being "The Crossroads of the Rockies" can be easily accessed from every direction by automobile. The Town is bisected by the north-south running US-285, and east-west running US-50.

Sample 3-day itinerary

Day 1

  • Morning Hutchinson Homestead, Historic Jackson Hotel, O'Haver Lake
  • Lunch Suzi's Out West
  • Afternoon Rafting/Kayaking, Disc Golf Course
  • Dinner A Mini Thai Cafe
  • Evening Elevation Beer Company

Day 2

  • Morning Town Hall, Ski Monarch, Marshall Pass
  • Lunch Grimo's Italian
  • Afternoon Trout Fishing, Chaffee County Fair and Rodeo
  • Dinner High Valley Center's Pub & Grub

Day 3

  • Morning Community Garden, Monarch Crest Trail
  • Afternoon Motocross/OHV Track, Climbing the 14ers
