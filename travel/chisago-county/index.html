Overview

Chisago County is a county in the Twin Cities region of Minnesota.

Its principal settlements are:

  • Taylors Falls, which abuts the St. Croix River which separates Minnesota and Wisconsin.
  • North Branch
  • Chisago City, a northern suburb of the Twin Cities in the Chisago Lakes area, comprising Lindstrom and Center City.

    Understand

    Chisago County lies on Minnesota's eastern border, abutting the western border of Wisconsin (across the Saint Croix River). The Saint Croix flows south-southeast along the county's eastern border. The Sunrise River flows north through the county's central part, collecting the waters of the North Branch Sunrise River and Hay Creek before discharging into the St. Croix at the county's eastern boundary. The county terrain consists of rolling hills, devoted to agriculture.

    Chisago County is strongly influenced by the German, Swedish and Norwegian immigrants who settled there in the mid-19th century.
