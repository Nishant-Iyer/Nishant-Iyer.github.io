Overview

Spencer is a small town in the Worcester Hills of Central Massachusetts near Worcester. As a rural community, there is not much in the way of nightlife but there are many opportunities for outdoor exploration and recreation.

Get in

There are no major highways through Spencer, so you will need to take either the state routes or backroads. MA Route 9 runs east-west through the center of Spencer, and MA Route 31 runs through the center of Spencer north-south. If you take MA Route 49 northbound from Sturbridge, you will intersect with Route 9 in the western part of Spencer.

Get around

Traveling by car is the most practical way to navigate Spencer. A bus line does go through the town center, but outside the town center places can be far-flung.

See

See Saint Joseph's Abbey

A Trappist monk community. Visitors are welcome to walk the areas in front of the church entrance but should not enter the areas of enclosure. The monks are happy to talk to visitors, and pray with or for them if asked. Guests are also allowed to join in with mass and prayer services, and are welcome to visit the gift shop. Check the website for the hours for the gift shop, mass and prayer services, and the showroom hours for the Holy Rood Guild.

Nearby attractions

  • The Old Sturbridge Village living history museum is nearby in Sturbridge.
  • Six Flags New England amusement park is an hour away in Agawam, south of Springfield
  • Rock House Reservation is in the nearby town of West Brookfield. Outdoor Trustees' of Reservations land that historically was a Nipmuc gathering site. Has some beautiful geologic deposits from the last ice age.
