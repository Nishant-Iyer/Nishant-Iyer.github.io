Overview

Charlemont is a town in Franklin County, in the Pioneer Valley region of Massachusetts.

2 sights · 3 things to do

Best things about Charlemont

Mohawk Park

Roadside park featuring the monument Hail To The Sunrise, a bronze statue depicting a Native American looking East in the direction of the rising sun.

Bissell Covered Bridge

Covered Bridge over the Mill Brook with viewing area.

Berkshire East Ski Resort

Ski resort offering 45 ski runs, snow tubing, Zip Lines, downhill mountain bike park, and an Alpine Coaster.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Oct. Summer highs reach about 84°F; winter lows dip to about 16°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Jul and Jun. (climate data from nearby Albany)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 930 miles, roughly 16 h without stops. Flying is usually faster.
  • BDL Bradley International Airport, Hartford — 45 mi away (major hub). Directions
  • ORH Worcester Regional Airport, Worcester — 47 mi away. Directions
