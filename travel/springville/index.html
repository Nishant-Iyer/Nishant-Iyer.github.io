Best things about Springville

Springville Museum of Art

Museum of art featuring both local artists and more-known figures. Also features a statue garden and a large collection of Russian artwork.

Springville-Mapleton Pioneer Museum

Collection of pioneer artifacts housed by the Daughters of the Utah Pioneers for the Springville and Mapleton communities.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Apr–Jun, Sep–Oct. Summer highs reach about 95°F; winter lows dip to about 24°F. Very hot (Jul, Aug) — plan outdoor time for mornings. Wettest: Jan and May. (climate data from nearby Provo)

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 1,530 miles, roughly 26 h 30 min without stops. Flying is usually faster.
