Understand

Monroe County was created on April 1, 1836, from parts of Northampton and Pike Counties and named for President James Monroe. At that time there was no county seat. Stroudsburg was finally selected to be the county seat on August 31, 1837. It was named after Jacob Stroud who was an original settler of the area.Today, Monroe County is comprised of 16-second class townships and 4 incorporated boroughs with a population of about 139,000 residents. Three of the boroughs and all of the townships were settled in the 18th century. The oldest of all the municipalities are Smithfield Township and the borough of East Stroudsburg.

Get in

By car

The easiest way to get to East Stroudsburg by car is from Interstate 80. It is one of the last exits on the interstate before entering into New Jersey.

By bus

Greyhound provides one round-trip per day on a route between Scranton and Philadelphia. The Martz bus station, where the Greyhound buses make their stops, is located only a few miles away from Main Street.

PO Box 1291 Stroudsburg, PA 18360 Martz Trailways Rail Road 1 +1 570-421-3040, +1-800-454-2487

By van

Uptown Vans has several carriers operating to/from different stops in Downtown Stroudsburg and East Stroudsburg on routes traveling from Paterson and New York City. These stops are centrally located, though advance reservations are needed as the vans often sell out quickly.
