Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Oct–May. Summer highs reach about 91°F; winter lows dip to about 39°F. Very hot (Jul, Aug) — plan outdoor time for mornings. Wettest: Aug and Jul.

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).
