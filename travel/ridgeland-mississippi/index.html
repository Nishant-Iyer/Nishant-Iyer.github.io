Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May, Oct–Nov. Summer highs reach about 91°F; winter lows dip to about 38°F. Very hot (Jun, Jul, Aug) — plan outdoor time for mornings. Wettest: Jan and Jul. (climate data from nearby Gulf Coast)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).
