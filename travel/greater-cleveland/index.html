Overview

Greater Cleveland is a five-county region centered around the city of Cleveland, Ohio, USA. It is a core component of the 13-county region of Northeast Ohio.

Plan your trip

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Oct. Summer highs reach about 84°F; winter lows dip to about 22°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Sep and Apr. (climate data from nearby Cleveland)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 380 miles, roughly 6 h 30 min without stops.
