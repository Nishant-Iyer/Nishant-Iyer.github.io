Overview

Leadville is a city in Lake County, Colorado that is about tourism, especially outdoor recreation. In the winter, multiple ski areas are within driving distance. In the summer, hikes abound. The Continental Divide Trail and the Colorado Trail pass near enough to Leadville that through hikers can rest and receive supplies here. It is the highest city in the continental United States, at 10,200ft, and is in the heart of the Sawatch Range. Offering access to the highest 14,000ft peak in Colorado Mt Elbert and Mt Massive.

Understand

Leadville is a former silver mining town that lies near the headwaters of the Arkansas River in the heart of the Rocky Mountains. Leadville is the highest incorporated city and the second highest incorporated municipality in the United States. It was the second-largest city in Colorado in the late 19th century, so its Historic District has beautiful late 19th-century buildings that are well worth your viewing if you are in the area.

Get in

As Leadville is a small town set deep in the Rocky Mountains, driving is the only practical way in or out.

Go Lake County Leadville Airport (KLXV)

KLXV is famous among general aviation in North America for being the highest-altitude airport open to the public in North America. At 9,934 feet above Mean Sea Level, flights here start at altitude which many recreational aircraft from the flatlands can barely reach. There is no scheduled airline service, but pilots with their own aircraft are welcome. It is wise to be familiar with mountain flying, prepared for cold weather, and have an aircraft equipped for the altitude. When you land here, the FBO here will be happy to issue you a certificate commemorating your accomplishment.

Get around

The heart of tourist-centric Leadville is only a kilometre across, so distances are walkable. However, with the altitude, you may find a gentle uphill stroll to be more of a strain than you expect. Sidewalks and streets are narrow, not offering much room for bikes. Cars, however, will find roads and plenty of parking.

See

Leadville Historic District includes 67 historic mines to the east of town and a number of striking late 19th- and early 20th-century buildings in Downtown Leadville.

Do

Do Leadville Railroad

A special sightseeing train with open cars runs out from Leadville, through rugged mountain scenery, and returns 2.5 hours later. Packages combine the trip with zip line or rafting adventures. The railway line is a lingering remnant of what started in 1884 as the Denver South Park & Pacific Railroad "highline".

📍 326 E 7th St🕒 Trips generally 10AM, 2PM and last 2.5 hours; season late May–early Nov💲 Adult $59, child $36, more for lounge car

Do Leadville Scenic Flight Tours (Leadville Flying Club)

Fly in a small Cessna 182 single-engine propeller plane on a circular tour near Leadville. See the Climax Mine, Mount Elbert, Independence Pass, the Copper Mountain ski area, and more rocky, mountainous peaks than you can count. The local flying club includes pilots who are certified to conduct the tours.

Sleep

There are many listings for whole-house rentals in Leadville on AirBNB, VRBO, and the like. Perhaps this reflects the popularity of groups renting a house as a base for trips to local ski areas during the winter.

Stay Inn the Clouds Hostel & Inn

What looks like an enlarged, rambling ranch house contains a selection of rooms with private bath, rooms with shared bath, and dorm rooms for mixed or single gender with shared bath. A generous all-access kitchen offers a fridge to store groceries and stoves and utensils to cook. A large dining room table, and some outdoor tables, offer places to eat in the company of other travellers. They are particularly welcoming to hikers from the nearby Continental Divide Trail. The management has a friendly, family feel. Some of the infrastructure (plumbing, air conditioning) is straining from the load. Free parking in front of the inn, or on the street nearby.
