Overview

Garibaldi is a quiet coastal town on the Northern Oregon Coast. It is on the mouth of the Tillamook Bay and an excellent hub of crabbing, fishing, clamming and several other sea activities.

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 2,210 miles, roughly 38 h without stops. Flying is usually faster.
  • SLE Salem-Willamette Valley Airport/McNary Field, Salem — 63 mi away. Directions
  • PDX Portland International Airport, Portland — 64 mi away (major hub). Directions

To get to Garibaldi take highway 101 from the North or South or highway 6 from the East. Located 75 miles West of Portland and 8 miles North of Tillamook.

Stay Harborview Inn & RV Park

A locally-owned and -operated motel and RV park located 50 yards from the boat launch and marina.
