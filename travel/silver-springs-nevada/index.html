Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Apr–Jun, Sep–Oct. Summer highs reach about 94°F; winter lows dip to about 26°F. Very hot (Jul, Aug) — plan outdoor time for mornings. Wettest: Jan and Dec. (climate data from nearby Reno)
