Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Sep. Summer highs reach about 80°F; winter lows dip to about 24°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Mar and Oct. (climate data from nearby Newport)

Sample 3-day trip

  1. Day 1: Newport — Newport Cliff Walk, Fort Adams, Fort Adams State Park.
  2. Day 2: Jamestown — Beavertail State Park and Lighthouse Museum, Fort Getty Recreational Area, Fort Wetherill State Park.
