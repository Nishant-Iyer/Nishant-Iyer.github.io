Overview

Paintsville is a city in the Kentucky Appalachians. It's one of those eastern Kentucky towns that is feeling the effects of the downturn in the coal industry.

4 sights · 2 things to do · 1 places to stay

Best things about Paintsville

U.S. 23 Country Music Highway Museum

Covers the country music "legends" like Loretta Lynn, Dwight Yoakum, Hylo Brown, Crystal Gayle, and the Judds from this area of Appalachia.

Loretta Lynn HomePlace

Relive the experience after seeing Coal Miner's Daughter by visiting Loretta Lynn's childhood home in person.

Van Lear Historical Society

A little museum that will brief you on what life was like in coal mining country.

Mayo Memorial United Methodist

Organ was donated by Andrew Carnegie.

Paintsville Golf Course

Founded in 1929, this 18 hole golf course is one of the oldest in eastern Kentucky.

Paintsville Lake State Park

A Kentucky state park located on a 1140 acre lake. The park offers a campground, marina, and boat rentals.
