Get around

Cars are by far the best way to get around. Public transport has buses that run once every 40 minutes. Routes are on Porterville city website. In addition they have vehicles formerly run by the city, now part of Uber. Fare is $3, twice city bus fare, and carry one fare at a time so there will be delays, and require smart phones.
