Overview

North Central Florida includes Alachua, Baker, Bradford, Columbia, Gilchrist, Hamilton, Lafayette, Madison, Marion, Putnam, Sumter, Suwannee, and Union counties.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Oct–May. Summer highs reach about 92°F; winter lows dip to about 42°F. Very hot (Jun, Jul, Aug) — plan outdoor time for mornings. Wettest: Jun and Sep. (climate data from nearby North Florida)

Sample 3-day trip

  1. Day 1: Gainesville — Devil's Millhopper Geological State Park, Curtis M. Phillips Center for the Performing Arts, Harn Museum of Art.
  2. Day 2: Palatka — Palatka Historic Union Depot, Bronson-Mulholland House, Historic Lemon Street.
  3. Day 3: The Villages — Spanish Springs Town Square, Lake Sumter Landing Market Square, Brownwood Paddock Square.
