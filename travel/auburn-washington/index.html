Get in

By car

  • State Route (SR) 18 serves as a major east-west freeway between I-5 (exit 142A) in Federal Way and I-90 (exit 25) near Snoqualmie. It goes through Auburn, Covington, Maple Valley and Issaquah in the east/southeastern regions of King County.
  • Auburn Way S (SR-164) becomes Auburn-Enumclaw Hwy, outside the city, to connect Auburn to Enumclaw.
  • Valley Freeway (SR-167) serves as the main north-south highway from I-405 (exit 2) in Renton to Puyallup through Kent, Auburn, and Sumner in the Kent Valley.

By train

  • Sound Transit Sounder operates the Sounder Train between King Street Station in downtown Seattle and Lakewood Station (south of Tacoma) via Tukwila, Kent Station, Auburn, Sumner, Puyallup, Tacoma-Dome, S Tacoma (S Washington Ave between S 57th & 58th). Nearest transfer stations to Amtrak trains are in Tacoma-Dome Station and Seattle King Street Station. Connections to King County Metro and Pierce Transit buses are at each station. Check schedules as trains only go north in the mornings, south in the afternoons and on Seattle Seahawks and Mariners game days. Check schedules.

By bus

Nearest connections to Greyhound, Flixbus, and other intercity bus companies are in Seattle and the Tacoma Dome Station.

By plane

The Auburn Municipal Airport (S50) (for general aviation only) is two miles north of the city.

Seattle-Tacoma International Airport (SEA), the nearest commercial airport to Auburn, is 9 mi away. Depending on traffic, you can go north on SR-167 to I-405 in Renton, and go west on I-405 which becomes SR-518 towards the airport. Or one can go west SR-18 to Federal Way and then north on I-5 to the I-450/SR-518 junction in Tukwila.

Get around

By car

By public transit

Auburn Station on A st & 2nd St SW in downtown Auburn is the transportation hub (bus station). The Auburn Station offers routes with King County Metro and Sound Transit service to Kent (160, 566); Enumclaw (915); Federal Way (181, 578); Kent (160, 566) Redmond (566); Bellevue (566); Renton (160, 566); Downtown Seattle (578); Puyallup (578); Algona/Pacific (917), and local service around town (181, 184, 915, 917).

Gambling

If you win...

Chances are if you win it big in Auburn and you are a foreigner you will get dinged by the 30% withholding tax. That $10,000 slot winning can dwindle quite quickly if that is taken off the top. Not to worry though, you can reclaim your gambling winnings tax through a 1042-S form. You should get this from the casino so don't lose it... it is your starting ticket to getting your gambling winnings back.

The Muckleshoot Indian Reservation lies just southeast of the city.
