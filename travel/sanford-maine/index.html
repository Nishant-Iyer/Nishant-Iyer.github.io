Overview

Sanford is a small town is southern Maine. It includes the village of Springvale.

Sanford Mainers

McDougal Orchards

Province Lake Golf

18 holes for all ages. From 2000 yards to 6300 yards.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Sep. Summer highs reach about 80°F; winter lows dip to about 16°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Oct and Dec. (climate data from nearby Seacoast)
