Overview

Lanesboro is a quaint tourist destination in Southern Minnesota nestled in a deep valley of the Root River.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Oct. Summer highs reach about 85°F; winter lows dip to about 11°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Jun and May. (climate data from nearby Waterloo)

Eat Riverside on the Root

Lunch and dinner. Casual dining with good-sounding, but bland-tasting, thus over-prized entrées with rushed and slightly grumpy service, role-modeled by the owners. But the location and atmosphere is grand (if you can get a table on the river patio or balcony) and if you don't have too high expectations about food quality and service, it's worth a visit.

📍 109 S. Parkway Avedowntown

Eat Spud Boy Diner

Breakfast and lunch. Circa 1925 diner car refurbished and placed on a small lot on Lanesboro's main street. Great food and excellent coffee. Booth seating for ladies.
