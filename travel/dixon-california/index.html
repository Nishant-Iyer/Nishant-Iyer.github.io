Best time to visit & weather

* Dixon is about 65 mi northeast from San Francisco and 22.5 mi southwest of Sacramento (via Davis 9 mi east). Dixon is accessed from Exit 60 (Midway Rd, Lewis Rd) through Exit 67 (Pedrick Rd) of I-80. * to 1st St, Rio Dixon Rd goes south from I-80, through town as 1st St and becomes Rio Dixon Rd south of town to its intersection with CA-Hwy 12 21 mi later. Going north CA-Hwy 113 runs concurrent on I-80 from Exit 66A to Exit 70 and 13 mi north to I-5 in Woodland (exit 537). * Pedrick Rd from & . Road 98 goes 21 mi from Exit 541 of I-5 as CA-16, along the westside of Woodland, to I-80 as Pedrick Rd (exit 67). This road serves as the most direct route into Dixon from I-5 north/west of Woodland or anywhere further north without into & through Sacramento.

    Do

    Do Jepson Prairie Reserve (Dixon Vernal Pools)

    The Reserve is one of the few remaining vernal pool habitats and native bunchgrass prairies in California and is a National Natural Landmark. There is a 1/2 mile self-guided nature trail and docent-led walks are given Sa and Su at 10AM from mid-March to early May.
