Best things about John Day

Kam Wah Chung State Heritage Site

An 1865 stagecoach stop and depot that was purchased by 2 Chinese immigrants in 1887 and functioned as a clinic, general store, and community center for the Chinese community until the 1940s. It is now a museum.

    Camping

    Stay Clyde Holliday State Recreation Site

    Seasonal campground (March 1- Nov 30) and year-round picnic area amidst cottonwood trees along the John Day River. 31 electrical sites with water, hiker-biker camp, and 2 tepees with space heater and sleeping pads for 8 people.

    ☎ +1 541-932-4453⚠ Last checked Mar 2021 — confirm before you go
    MapsWebsite

    Stay Grant County Fairgrounds RV Park

    Full hook up sites with picnic table, showers, and fire ring, with a walking path by the John Day River. RVs can not be more than 15 years old. Pet friendly.
