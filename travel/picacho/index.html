Do

Do Picacho Peak State Park

Directly along I-10 between Tucson and Casa Grande, this eroded volcanic outcropping is impossible to miss. The peak affords excellent views, and can be accessed via two difficult via ferrata routes, or by a strenuous hike. From mid-March until early April it is particularly photogenic with spring wildflowers following a good winter rainy season. In early March there is an annual Civil War re-enactment commemorating the Battle of Picacho Peak, one of several Civil War engagements in what was then Confederate Arizona Territory.

📍 I-10 Exit 219, Eloy🕒 5AM-10PM mid-September to late May☎ +1 520-466-3183

Do Rooster Cogburn Ostrich Ranch

Long-standing roadside attraction where you can walk up and feed various critters such as ostriches, lorikeet, deer, tortoises and stingrays. There is also shopping for ostrich products. Monster truck tours of the ranch are held on weekends and holidays.

📍 17599 E Peak LnI-10 north of Tucson, #219, take the frontage road SE🕒 8AM-2PM May through September, 9AM-5PM October through April. Admission ends 30 minutes before closing time💲 $12-15, $25 for the monster truck tour☎ +1 520-466-3658
