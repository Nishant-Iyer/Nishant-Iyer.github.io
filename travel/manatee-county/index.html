Overview

Manatee County is in the Tampa Bay Area, a metropolitan area on the Florida West Coast.

Plan your trip

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Oct–May. Summer highs reach about 92°F; winter lows dip to about 52°F. Very hot (Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep) — plan outdoor time for mornings. Wettest: Aug and Jul. (climate data from nearby Sarasota)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 1,270 miles, roughly 22 h without stops. Flying is usually faster.
  • SRQ Sarasota Bradenton International Airport, Sarasota/Bradenton — 13 mi away (major hub). Directions
  • TPA Tampa International Airport, Tampa — 36 mi away (major hub). Directions

Sample 3-day trip

  1. Day 1: Anna Maria Island — Anna Maria Island Historical Society, Leffis Key Preserve, Fun and More Rentals.
  2. Day 2: Bradenton — Lake Manatee State Park, Bishop Museum of Science and Nature, De Soto National Memorial.
  3. Day 3: Longboat Key — Rufus P Jordan House, Bayfront Park.

  • Bradenton — main city in the county; on the mainland

  • Palmetto — town across the river from Bradenton

Other destinations

  • Anna Maria Island — contains the cities of Anna Maria, Holmes Beach, and Bradenton Beach

Get around

By bus

  • Manatee County Area Transit (MCAT) serves the south Bay areas of Bradenton, W Bradenton, Palmetto in Manatee County.
