Best things about Kelseyville

Clear Lake State Park

Clear Lake State Park is on the shores of California's largest freshwater lake. The area is popular for all kinds of water recreation, including swimming, fishing, boating and water-skiing.

Bass Fishin' with Richard Pounds

Year Round Guide Service Exclusively at Clear Lake averaging 200 guide trips per year! 25 years fishing experience on Clear Lake. Richard Promotes and Practices Catch &amp; Release.
