Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Sep. Summer highs reach about 83°F; winter lows dip to about 19°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Apr and May. (climate data from nearby Grand Rapids)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 170 miles, roughly 3 h without stops.
  • GRR Gerald R. Ford International Airport, Grand Rapids — 10 mi away (major hub). Directions
  • LAN Capital Region International Airport, Lansing — 40 mi away. Directions

M-21 runs east west through downtown. Drive I-96 exit 52, then north on Alden Nash Ave for about 4.5 miles.
