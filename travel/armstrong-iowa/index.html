Best things about Armstrong

Armstrong Heritage Museum

Country Greens Golf Course

Par-36, 9-hole golf course. It's usually possible to reserve a tee time online on short notice.

Armstrong City Park

City park with playground. Also the home of the county's "Freedom Rock", which is a boulder that is painted as a memorial to veterans. The municipal swimming pool is across the street.
