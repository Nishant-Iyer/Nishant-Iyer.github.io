Overview

Huntsville is a small town in the Ozarks region of Arkansas. While there is not much for the traveller, it can provide a meal if you are passing through the area.

Do Withrow Springs State Park

State park that features camping, hiking, fishing, fishing and boating (including canoe and kayak rentals); there are also baseball fields, tennis courts, playground and gift shop. There is also a pool with snack bar open in the summer months. Located five miles north of Huntsville.
