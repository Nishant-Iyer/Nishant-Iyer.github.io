Overview

Colesville-Burtonsville, sometimes called East County, is a suburban area in the eastern part of Montgomery County, Maryland. Other neighborhoods within this region include White Oak, Hillandale, Cloverly, Fairland, Briggs Chaney, Spencerville, and Ashton. Residents of these neighborhoods often consider themselves to be part of Silver Spring while being several miles away from Downtown Silver Spring.

    Get in

    Most people will arrive by car. New Hampshire Avenue (Route 650) and Columbia Pike (Route 29) are the two major north-south roads. The Intercounty Connector (Route 200) is the major highway going east and west.

    There are public buses that travel New Hampshire Avenue and Columbia Pike.

    The nearest Metrorail station is Glenmont, the last stop on the Red line. From Glenmont, take a taxi from the taxi stand to your destination.

    Get around

    While there are public buses in this area, getting from place to place may take a transfer or two. Many people will find that a car will be helpful here.

    Southbound traffic is typically heavy during weekday mornings, while northbound traffic is often heavy during weekday evenings. Other times and days are generally much lighter.
