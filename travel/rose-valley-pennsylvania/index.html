    Understand

    Rose Valley was founded by William Price in 1901, and was created as part of an imagined utopia following the Arts and Crafts movement.

    Get in

    By car

    Rose Valley is located south of the county seat of Delaware County, Media. Rose Valley/Manchester Road is the major road into Rose Valley

    By train

    SEPTA Regional Rail trains (Media/Elwyn Line) stop at Moylan-Rose Valley Station, located just outside the borough limits to the north.

    Go Moylan–Rose Valley railway station

    Maps

    By plane

    Philadelphia International Airport (PHL) is minutes from Rose Valley and is served by taxis and the SEPTA R1 Regional Rail Line.

    Get around

    Rose Valley has no sidewalks; the easiest option is to use a car.
