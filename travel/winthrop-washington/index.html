Overview

Winthrop is a town in the Methow Valley, in the North Central Cascades of Washington State. This eastern gateway to North Cascades National Park has retained its historic frontier-town roots.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Sep. Summer highs reach about 87°F; winter lows dip to about 19°F. Cold months (Jan, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Dec and Jan. (climate data from nearby Leavenworth)

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 2,000 miles, roughly 34 h 30 min without stops. Flying is usually faster.

  • PAE Seattle Paine Field International Airport, Everett — 104 mi away. Directions
  • SEA Seattle–Tacoma International Airport, Seattle — 121 mi away (major hub). Directions

State Route 20 (WA-20), known as the North Cascades Highway, passes through town, from Twisp to the east and North Cascades National Park to the west. WA-20 closes during the winter west of Winthrop, and is also subject to temporary winter storm closures.
