Overview

Bentonville is a small town in the US state of Virginia's Shenandoah Valley. It is located near both the George Washington National Forest and the Shenandoah National Park in Warren County.

1 things to do

Best things about Bentonville

Downriver Canoe Company

Rain or shine, jump into a canoe, kayak, tube, or raft for a trip down the South Fork of the Shenandoah river. Between $14-120 depending on day of week (cheaper during the week) and vehicle choice.
