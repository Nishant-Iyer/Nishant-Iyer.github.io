    Understand

    Short Hills is in Essex County, about 20 miles from Manhattan.

    Get in

    By plane

    Newark Airport, in Newark, NJ, is approximately a 20-minute drive from Short Hills.

    LaGuardia Airport, in Flushing, NY, is approximately a 50-minute drive to Short Hills.

    By train

    Go Short Hills Station

    If you are coming from New York City, the easiest way to get to Short Hills is by train. You can take it from Penn Station straight into Short Hills, which is about a 45-minute ride. The train is the easiest way in from the city. If you are farther than New York City, take the Amtrak from anywhere on the east coast to Newark Penn Station which is about 15-minutes from Short Hills.

    By bus

    There is an hourly bus from Penn Station to Short Hills, which is about an hour long and only costs around $5.

    Get around

    Short Hills is a pedestrian-friendly town. It is small enough that you can walk from one edge of town to the other in about an hour and half. If you don't feel like walking you can take a car or bike.

    See

    • Downtown Short Hills- The downtown part of Short Hills is called Millburn. Although this may be confusing, it is the same town. There are many things to do in Millburn. You can eat at a range of restaurants from pizza places to hibachi places and everything in between. There are plenty of parks as well that you can check out such as Taylor Park in the heart of the downtown area.
    • South Mountain Reservation- The reservation is right in downtown Short Hills. It is a huge reservation spanning through 5 towns. If you walk your way to the top of the reservation you will be able to find yourself with a view of the whole downtown of Short Hills. Also, at night you will get a spectacular view of New York City.

    See Cora Hartshorn Arboretum and Bird Sanctuary

    Its trails are open to the public from dawn to dusk without charge. Programs are offered for children, families, and adults. The arboretum contains 45 species of trees, including 275-year-old tulip trees, as well as rare ferns, over 150 species of wildflowers, and 100 species of birds and represents a critical habitat for plants and wildlife. The Stone House has a reception area, and a bird observatory,

    Buy

    Shop The Mall at Short Hills

    One of the most popular destinations in New Jersey. The Mall at Short Hills is known for its high-class luxurious stores such as Cartier, Brooks Brothers, Polo, and many more. Also, it has an array of restaurants from seafood to steakhouses. Another plus is the celebrities you may run into such as Britney Spears, Nicki Minaj, Snooki, and Kirsten Dunst. Anchors include Macy's, Bloomingdale's, Nordstrom, Neiman Marcus, and Indigo Books & Music.

    • Livingston Mall- The mall is just a town over and less than five minutes away. It has many stores for children such as Gamestop and toy stores. Also, it has a great food court for cheap eats.
    • Downtown Short Hills- Downtown Short Hills has many great places to shop such as Go! Calendars, Games & Toys, fashion from furs to elegant rugs. Anchors include Macy's, Lord & Taylor, Sears, Old Navy, and Barnes & Noble.

    Eat

    • Legal Seafood- Located in the Short Hills Mall, it is one of the best seafood restaurants in the town. With a very elegant atmosphere, the food is great with a great price, and the service is quick and nice, nothing beats this place for seafood.
    • Joes American Bar & Grill- Located in the Short Hills Mall, it is one of the best places to grab a steak or a burger. There is sometimes a wait, but it is definitely worth it for the warm bread and juicy meats you will soon be devouring.
    • Benihanna- Benihanna is one of the best hibachi places in town. Although it is slightly expensive, the food that you will be served along with the show they perform while the cooking of your food, every dollar will be worth spent.
    • Samauri Sushi- If you are looking for a great sushi place, look no further. Samauri Sushi is a great place to eat. Located in the heart of downtown Short hills, you can spend a night on the town after eating amazing sushi.

    Drink

    Martinis- This chic place in downtown Short Hills is a great place to stop and get a drink and some good food. Also, it has a place to eat in the back so you can take your kids as well if you choose!

    Sleep

    Hilton Short Hills- Located across the street from The Mall at Short Hills, this is a very elegant hotel.

    Go next

    • New York City- Take the train, which is less than one hour from the big apple. You can see all the extravagant NYC sites such as Time Square where you where you will be when you get off the train.
