Overview

Whitefish is a small town in the northern Flathead Valley of northwest Montana, on the southern end of Whitefish Lake. Whitefish Mountain Resort north of town offers excellent skiing, and Glacier National Park to the east is a major hiking and camping destination.

    Understand

    Whitefish was founded in 1905 as a stopover for the Great Northern Railroad. Traditional industries in Whitefish have included logging, ranching, and farming. The population was about 8,300 in 2019.

    Get in

    By car

    Whitefish is on U.S. Route 93 (US-93).

    From the south, take Exit 96 on Interstate 90 near Missoula and head north on US-93.

    From Calgary, the fastest route is to go southwest on the Crowsnest Highway 3, passing through Fernie. Continue south on Hwy 3 until it crosses with US-93. Take that south through the Roosville border crossing and the town of Eureka until hitting Whitefish. Driving time is about 5 hours.

    From Regina, take the Trans-Canada Hwy 1 west to Calgary and then follow the route explained above, which would take about 12-13 hours. Another way is to take Hwy 6 south to Plentywood, and take Hwy 16 south to Culbertson. Take US-2 west through Glasgow and Havre until eventually hitting Whitefish. This takes about 13 hours.

    By plane

    For commercial flights, the nearest airport is Glacier Park International Airport (FCA), roughly 11.5 mi southeast of town on US-2 towards Kalispell. There is no public transportation available from the airport into town. It's either by rental car, taxi or ride sharing.

    Do Whitefish Mountain Resort (Big Mountain)

    In the winter, offers a wide variety of Skiing and snowboarding terrain, from moguls to powder to bunny hills. In the summer, there's golfing, hiking, biking, and ziplines.

    📍 1015 Glades Dr☎ +1 406 862-2900Checked Jul 2023
    MapsWebsite
    • Hiking and Biking: Glacier National Park has 735 miles (1180 km) of trails for day-hiking and multi-day backpacking adventures.
    • Fishing: Fly fishing is the choice in and around Whitefish. Close to the Flathead River, trout fishing is ample during summer and fall. There are two fly shops in Whitefish. There are many lakes in the area that support fishing, though rivers are better.
    • Boating: Water sports form kayaking down the lazy flowing Whitefish River to water skiing across Whitefish Lake can be enjoyed. Boats can be rented from local business.

    There are many lodging options available in and around Whitefish. They range from four star luxury, to budget, to vacation homes and condominiums.

    The Best Western Rocky Mountain Lodge offers rooms with king or queen sized beds. Suites-featuring a fire place, hot tub, and wet bar- are also available. This pet-friendly hotel has specially designated rooms available for guests who wish to travel with their dogs and/or cats.

    Stay The Lodge at Whitefish Lake

    The Lodge is a full-service resort. There is an outdoor pool and hot tub, full service day spa, concierge service, marina and private beach. The Boat Club restaurant is fine dining and there is also a full service bar, both offer spectacular views of the lake.

    Go next

    • Glacier National Park: Many backpackers and adventurers use Whitefish as a staging point for expeditions into the park. 27 miles (43 km) east along US-2 to West Glacier.
    • Columbia Falls and Hungry Horse Dam: Visit one of the largest concrete dams in the U.S. 20 miles east.
    • Flathead Lake to the south past Kalispell is the largest freshwater lake west of the Mississippi and features many opportunities for water sports and boating.
    • Flathead National Forest: Over 2000 miles of trails, lakes, campgrounds, and ski areas. 16 miles east.
