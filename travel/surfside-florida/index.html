Overview

Surfside is a town in Miami-Dade County, Florida. It is just north of Miami Beach, south of Bal Habour, & east of Bay Harbor Islands.

Its business district is between 96th Street & 95th Street on Harding Avenue, while it's many hotels are located between Harding Avenue to the beachfront from 96th Street to 88th Street.

Best time to visit & weather

Get in

  • Drive via Florida State Road 922 to arrive from the west. Note of a vehicle toll ($2.25 SunPass; 5.25 toll-by-plate)when using the Shepard Broad Causeway to cross Biscayne Bay.
  • Drive via Florida State Road A1A to arrive from either the north or south.
  • Take Miami-Dade County's Transit Metrobus Route 125 to comes from the west and Route 100 to come from either north or south.
