    Do

    While you are in Talihina, be sure and visit the Historical Downtown. Visit Cranford's Boots for a true old fashioned boot store with some of the best prices around. Nothing fancy, just good boots at great prices! Another old time treat is "Treats and Treasures Old Fashioned Soda Fountain". This 1940s Soda Fountain is like stepping back into time. They have the best milkshakes and malts in town and still make ice cream sodas. Some say their hamburgers are the best in town.
