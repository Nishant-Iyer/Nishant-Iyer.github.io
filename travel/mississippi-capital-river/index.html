Overview

The Mississippi Capital-River region is in the southwest of Mississippi.

Plan your trip

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Oct–May. Summer highs reach about 92°F; winter lows dip to about 42°F. Very hot (Jun, Jul, Aug) — plan outdoor time for mornings. Wettest: Jun and Jan. (climate data from nearby Baton Rouge)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 860 miles, roughly 15 h without stops. Flying is usually faster.
  • JAN Jackson-Medgar Wiley Evers International Airport, Jackson — 42 mi away. Directions
  • PIB Hattiesburg Laurel Regional Airport, Moselle — 80 mi away. Directions
  • MSY Louis Armstrong New Orleans International Airport, New Orleans — 133 mi away (major hub). Directions

  1. Day 1: Jackson — Old Capitol Museum, New State Capitol, Supreme Court Building.
  2. Day 2: Natchez — Longwood, Natchez Pilgrimage, Historic Natchez Pageant.

Cities

  • Jackson – the state capital, with an attractive state capitol building and other attractions
  • Brookhaven
  • Clinton
  • McComb
  • Monticello
  • Natchez – a beautiful antebellum city with many lovely southern mansions, especially beautiful in the evening and night
  • Pearl
  • Port Gibson
  • Ridgeland
  • Vicksburg – home of a national military park, as a very important civil war siege took place here, and there are many memorials in a beautiful location overlooking the Mississippi River.

Other destinations

  • Natchez Trace Parkway - This 444 mile (715 km) long parkway is administered by the National Park Service and follows a route dating back hundreds of years. Hiking, Indian mounds, natural history exhibits, and interpretive exhibits make this is leisurely and enjoyable route for motorists.
