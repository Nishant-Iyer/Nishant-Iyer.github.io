Best things about Heath

Great Circle Earthworks

Davis-Shai House

a restored 1800s farmhouse, owned by the city, that hosts various tea and luncheon events.

A pool complex with a lazy river, water slides, splash playground, and other facilities.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Apr–Oct. Summer highs reach about 85°F; winter lows dip to about 22°F. Cold months (Jan) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Jul and Jun. (climate data from nearby Columbus)

    Get around

    By car

    Heath has no mass transit, so a vehicle will be needed to travel in the city.

    By bike

    There are two bike trails in the city. The Buckeye Scenic Trail connects with Newark.
