Overview

Crosby is a town in Western North Dakota with a population of just over a thousand. Crosby's elevation is 1,964 feet. Like much of this part of the Dakotas, it's not exactly a scenic wonderland or a thrill a minute.

Best things about Crosby

Divide County Museum

25 pioneer buildings including a bank, printing press, general store and dwelling shacks. Open Memorial Day until Labor Day.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Sep. Summer highs reach about 84°F; winter lows dip to about 4°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Mar, Nov, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Jun and May. (climate data from nearby Minot)

    • By car: about 125 miles northwest of Minot. On US highway 52, travel northwest for about 88 miles, then west on state highway 5 for another 36 miles.
