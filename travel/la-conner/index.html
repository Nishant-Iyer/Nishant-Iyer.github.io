Overview

La Conner is a town in northwest Washington state, on the Swinomish Channel. Its charming waterfront is tucked away amongst the farmland and tulip fields of the Skagit Valley. The protected farmland around the town is said to be some of the richest in the world.

Get in

By car

La Conner is not on any major highways, making it slightly tedious to get to.

It is about 10 mi west of Interstate 5.

  • From the south, exit in Conway for WA-534 West. Stay on the arterial as it turns onto Best Rd., then Chilberg Rd.
  • From the north, exit in Burlington for WA-20 West. Take a left onto La Conner Whitney Road.

Either way, the drive through the farmland of the Skagit Valley is especially beautiful.

By bus

Go Skagit Transit

Operates bus service within Skagit County including within and between Anacortes, Burlington, La Conner, Mount Vernon, and Sedro-Woolley. The buses do not run at night. Also offers connecting service from other counties, including route 80X traveling south from Bellingham and route 90X traveling north from Everett. Routes 80X and 90X stop in Burlington and Mount Vernon.

☎ +1 360-757-4433

Get around

La Conner is a great town to walk, with restaurants, museums, artist studios, and boutique retailers all packed into a few blocks. First Street follows the waterfront and intersects Morris Street, which runs the length of town.

Away from the town center, you'll need a car, or a boat, as the valley is spread out and there is little public transportation.
