Best things about Minneapolis

Rock City

A park located 3.6 miles south of town famous for its rock formations. Rock City contains 200 spherical boulders in three clusters. It is a registered national natural landmark.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Apr–May, Sep–Oct. Summer highs reach about 93°F; winter lows dip to about 22°F. Very hot (Jul, Aug) — plan outdoor time for mornings. Wettest: May and Jun. (climate data from nearby Wichita)
