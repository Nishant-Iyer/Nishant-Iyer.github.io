Overview

Tustin is a suburban city in Orange County, California. It is located on I-5 3 miles southeast of Santa Ana, 9 miles southeast of Disneyland, 6 miles north of John Wayne Airport.

Understand

Tustin used to be a community that supported Tustin and El Toro Marine bases, but they are closed now. Nearly all of the city was built since 1960 and most since 1980. Tustin Legacy, on the site of the former Marine Base, was built since 2006.
