Overview

Pinellas County is in the Tampa Bay Area on the Florida West Coast.

Plan your trip

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: Oct–May. Summer highs reach about 90°F; winter lows dip to about 54°F. Very hot (Jul, Aug) — plan outdoor time for mornings. Wettest: Aug and Sep. (climate data from nearby Largo)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 1,230 miles, roughly 21 h without stops. Flying is usually faster.
  • PIE St. Petersburg Clearwater International Airport, Pinellas Park — 3 mi away (major hub). Directions
  • TPA Tampa International Airport, Tampa — 14 mi away (major hub). Directions

Sample 3-day trip

  1. Day 1: Saint Petersburg — Salvador Dali Museum, Sunken Gardens, Florida Holocaust Museum.
  2. Day 2: St. Pete Beach — Fort De Soto Park, Gulf Beaches, Gulf Beaches Historical Museum.
  3. Day 3: Dunedin — Honeymoon Island State Park, Caladesi Island State Park, Dunedin Historical Society.
