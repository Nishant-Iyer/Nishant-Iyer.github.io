Overview

Medfield is a town of 13,000 people (2020) in Massachusetts. Medfield was first settled by Anglo-Americans in 1649 after the land was taken from the Neponset Native Americans. It is home to one of the oldest houses in the U.S., and to several other historic buildings.

Dover population was about 6k in the 2020 census. For Cryptozoologists and other curious bystanders, it is said to be the home of the mysterious creature called the "Dover Demon."

Do Noon Hill

is the highest point in Medfield at 370 feet with a trail to its peak. There are a 4½ miles of trails around the hill and offer views of the hills of Walpole and Norfolk.

Do Charles River

One of the longer river in the state of Massachusetts. The river winds through the town providing many areas the opportunity to go fishing. The Charles River is also a popular place to go canoeing. Best boating entrance located on Rt. 27 on the Medfield-Sherborn border.
