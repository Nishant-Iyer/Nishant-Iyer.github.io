Overview

New Town is a city in Western North Dakota. It is situated on the shore of Lake Sakakawea and on the north end of the Fort Berthold Indian Reservation. It is a popular destination for tourists, mostly from within the state, who visit for fishing, boating, and gambling. The city became well known after the oil boom around 2006. As a result, many people came to the town for work, and there was a rapid development of trailers and vinyl houses. The town is a common jumping-off point for hunting and fishing.

    Understand

    The town got its name after the Missouri river was dammed. The resulting flood created Lake Sakakawea and flooded the town of Van Hook. The residents of the community needed a new place to live, and founded New Town as a new town. The town is predominantly Native American, mostly of the Mandan, Hidatsa, and Arikara tribes, called MHA or Three Affiliated Tribes. Since the oil boom, the town had a resurgence and has changed drastically, with many people flocking for work in the oil fields.

    Get in

    The most common way to enter is to take the 23 highway from east or west. There's also the 1804 if you are coming from the north. There is no airport.

    Get around

    There is no public transportation. A car, truck, or side-by-side is necessary. You can try ridesharing apps but these may be undependable.
