Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Sep. Summer highs reach about 80°F; winter lows dip to about 16°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Oct and Dec. (climate data from nearby Seacoast)

    Understand

    Formerly a farming and mill town, modern Amesbury is for the most part residential. It is one of two northernmost towns in Massachusetts, the other being neighboring Salisbury.

    Get in

    There are daily buses from Haverhill and from Salisbury via Newburyport. Buses stops at Nicholas J. Costello Transportation Center, which is just a few hundred meters from Main street.
