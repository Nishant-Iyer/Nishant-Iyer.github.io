Overview

Brick Township, commonly known as Brick or Bricktown, is a township in Ocean County, New Jersey.

1 sights · 1 things to do · 3 places to stay

Best things about Brick Township

Havens Homestead Museum

The museum and other buildings in the vicinity is a time capsule of the 19th century.

Pirate Adventures

A pirate-themed adventure on the Sea Gypsy lasting over an hour. Requires reservation.

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Oct. Summer highs reach about 85°F; winter lows dip to about 22°F. Wettest: Mar and Aug. (climate data from nearby Toms River)
