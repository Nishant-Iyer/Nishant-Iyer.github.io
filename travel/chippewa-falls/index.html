Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Sep. Summer highs reach about 83°F; winter lows dip to about 9°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: Jun and Aug. (climate data from nearby Saint Paul)

    Get in

    By bus

    Chippewa Falls is served by Jefferson Lines on the route between Milwaukee and Minneapolis (via Green Bay).

    By plane

    Chippewa Valley Regional Airport (EAU) is 8 miles from the town. As of March 2016 a twice daily (once on Saturdays) service is available to Chicago O'Hare.
