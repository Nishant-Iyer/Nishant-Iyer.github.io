Overview

Wayne County is in Southeast Michigan.

Plan your trip

Best time to visit & weather

Most comfortable: May–Oct. Summer highs reach about 84°F; winter lows dip to about 19°F. Cold months (Jan, Feb, Dec) can bring snow and ice; check road conditions. Wettest: May and Jul. (climate data from nearby Detroit)

Blue bars: average daily high and low (°F). Purple columns: average monthly rain and melted snow (inches).

Getting there

  • Drive from Chicago: about 280 miles, roughly 4 h 30 min without stops.
  • DTW Detroit Metropolitan Wayne County Airport, Detroit — 7 mi away (major hub). Directions
  • FNT Bishop International Airport, Flint — 54 mi away. Directions

Sample 3-day trip

  1. Day 1: Detroit — explore the town.
  2. Day 2: Dearborn — Automotive Hall of Fame, Arab American National Museum, Henry Ford Estate.
  3. Day 3: Canton — Cherry Hill, Michigan, Canton Historical Museum, The Village Theater at Cherry Hill.
