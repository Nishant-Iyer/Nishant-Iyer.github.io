How it works

  • The model: predicts the chance a game goes over the posted total from Elo team strength, recent scoring form, rest, travel and game-day weather.
  • No peeking: the model and its settings were chosen on games before December 2003. Each later stretch of games was predicted by a model trained only on what came before it.
  • Try: raise the edge to bet only the games the model is surest about, switch to ½ Kelly to see how sizing changes the risk, or move the start year to see how much the result depends on when you began.

Data: NFL games with closing Over/Under lines, joined with stadium weather. Pushes return the stake. Read the case study or the code.
