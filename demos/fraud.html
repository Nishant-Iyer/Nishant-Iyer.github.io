How it works

  • The model: an autoencoder trained only on legitimate purchases measures how unusual each transaction looks. That score joins the card features in XGBoost and LightGBM, and a logistic regression combines the two.
  • The cost: every alert costs a review, and every missed fraud costs its amount. The cheapest threshold balances the two, and it moves when reviews get more expensive.
  • Try: take a fraud and slide "How unusual it looks" toward 0% to see where the model stops flagging it, or raise the review cost and watch the cheapest threshold climb.

Data: the public European cardholder dataset (284,807 transactions, 0.17% fraud). The threshold was chosen on a validation split; costs here are on the separate test set. Read the case study or the code.
